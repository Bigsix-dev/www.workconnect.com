Property Inspections Team Coordinator

Job Description

Beresfords is seeking an organised Property Inspections Team Coordinator to coordinate inspection activities and provide administrative support to the property team. The successful candidate will manage inspection schedules, communicate with tenants, landlords, and inspectors, maintain accurate records, and ensure inspection processes run smoothly.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate and schedule property inspections.
  • Manage inspection diaries and allocate appointments efficiently.
  • Communicate with tenants, landlords, inspectors, and contractors.
  • Prepare inspection schedules and relevant documentation.
  • Monitor completed inspections and ensure reports are processed promptly.
  • Maintain accurate property and inspection records.
  • Identify and record maintenance or property issues raised during inspections.
  • Follow up outstanding inspection actions and communicate updates.
  • Assist the property team with general administration.
  • Ensure customers receive professional and timely communication.

Requirements

  • Previous property administration or coordination experience is desirable.
  • Excellent organisational and diary management skills.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills.
  • Good attention to detail and accurate record-keeping abilities.
  • Confident using computer systems and Microsoft Office.
  • Ability to coordinate multiple appointments and priorities.
  • Professional and customer-focused approach.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Hybrid working arrangement.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £27,000–£28,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Hybrid working opportunity.
  • Professional property environment.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Opportunity to develop property coordination and administration skills.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
