Patient Transport Driver

Job Description

EMED Group, originally formed through the merger of ERS Medical and E-zec Medical, is a leading UK healthcare logistics and specialist patient transport provider delivering safe, reliable, and compassionate transport services across the country.

As a Patient Transport Driver, you will ensure patients are transported safely and comfortably to hospitals, clinics, and other healthcare appointments while providing professional, respectful, and person-centred support throughout every journey.

Key Responsibilities

  • Transport patients safely to and from medical appointments and healthcare facilities.
  • Assist passengers with boarding, exiting, and the safe handling of mobility equipment where required.
  • Carry out daily vehicle inspections and maintain cleanliness and roadworthiness.
  • Complete transport records and documentation accurately.
  • Communicate effectively with patients, healthcare professionals, and dispatch teams.
  • Adhere to all health and safety, safeguarding, and infection control procedures.
  • Deliver excellent customer service while maintaining patient dignity and confidentiality.

Requirements

  • Full, valid UK driving licence with a clean or acceptable driving record.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to remain calm, compassionate, and professional under pressure.
  • Basic literacy and numeracy skills for record-keeping.
  • Willingness to complete a DBS check and mandatory training.
  • Previous experience in patient transport, healthcare, or customer service is beneficial but not essential.

What We Offer

  • Competitive salary of £24,000–£29,000 per year.
  • Full-time employment with consistent working hours.
  • Comprehensive induction and ongoing professional training.
  • Company pension scheme and paid annual leave.
  • Career development opportunities within a national healthcare transport organisation.
  • Supportive team environment with a strong focus on patient care and safety.
  • The opportunity to make a meaningful impact by helping patients access essential healthcare services.
