Night Security Officer

Job Description

Commercial Security Company provides professional security services to offices, warehouses, commercial properties, and other business premises. The company is seeking a dependable Night Security Officer to protect sites outside normal business hours. You will conduct overnight patrols, monitor security systems, control access, respond to alarms, and ensure that premises remain secure throughout the night.

Responsibilities

  • Provide overnight security coverage at assigned commercial premises.
  • Conduct regular internal and external patrols throughout the site.
  • Monitor CCTV, alarms, access control systems, and other security equipment.
  • Control access to the premises during night operations.
  • Check doors, windows, gates, equipment, and restricted areas during patrols.
  • Respond appropriately to alarms, incidents, and suspicious activity.
  • Contact emergency services or management when escalation is required.
  • Maintain detailed night shift logs and incident reports.

Requirements

  • Valid SIA licence where required for the role.
  • Previous security, night security, facilities, or guarding experience is desirable.
  • Strong observation and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work independently during overnight shifts.
  • Excellent communication and incident reporting skills.
  • Ability to remain alert and focused throughout night shifts.
  • Good understanding of security and emergency procedures.
  • Reliable, punctual, and professionally presented.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Night shift working, including weekends and public holidays where required.

Salary

  • £27,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive night security salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Night security and incident response training.
  • Opportunities for additional security qualifications.
  • Stable commercial security environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
