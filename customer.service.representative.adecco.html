Customer Service Representative

Job Description

Adecco is seeking a confident Customer Service Representative to provide excellent support and assistance to customers. The role involves handling enquiries, resolving customer issues, updating records, and ensuring customers receive accurate and helpful information throughout their interactions.

Responsibilities

  • Handle customer enquiries by telephone, email, and other channels.
  • Provide clear, accurate, and professional information to customers.
  • Identify customer needs and provide suitable solutions.
  • Resolve customer complaints and service issues where possible.
  • Record customer interactions and update relevant systems.
  • Process customer requests accurately and efficiently.
  • Liaise with other departments to resolve complex enquiries.
  • Follow customer service procedures and company standards.
  • Maintain positive relationships with customers.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service experience is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong problem-solving abilities.
  • Good computer and administrative skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional when handling difficult customers.
  • Strong attention to detail.
  • Ability to manage multiple customer requests efficiently.
  • Positive and customer-focused attitude.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • Salary negotiable.

Benefits

  • Negotiable salary package.
  • Professional working environment.
  • Customer service training and development.
  • Opportunities to develop professional experience.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
