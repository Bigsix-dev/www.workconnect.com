Logistics Operative

Job Description

CEVA Logistics Limited, part of CEVA Logistics Group, is a global logistics and supply chain company providing warehousing, transportation, and distribution solutions for businesses worldwide. CEVA Logistics is seeking a reliable and hardworking Logistics Operative to support its warehouse and distribution operations. In this role, you will assist with receiving, storing, picking, packing, and dispatching goods while ensuring accuracy, efficiency, and compliance with company safety standards.

Responsibilities

  • Receive, check, and process incoming goods and deliveries.
  • Pick, pack, and prepare customer orders for dispatch.
  • Load and unload vehicles safely and efficiently.
  • Operate warehouse equipment and handheld scanning systems.
  • Move stock and materials within the warehouse as required.
  • Assist with stock checks and maintain accurate inventory records.
  • Maintain a clean, organised, and safe working environment.
  • Follow company procedures, warehouse standards, and health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous warehouse or logistics experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment.
  • Good attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to lift, carry, and move goods safely.
  • Experience using handheld scanners or warehouse systems is an advantage.
  • Strong teamwork and communication skills.
  • Reliable, punctual, and committed to meeting operational targets.
  • Flexible availability for shift patterns when required.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, nights, and weekends.

Salary

  • £13.25–£15.25 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Comprehensive training and development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts and reward programmes.
  • Health and wellbeing support services.
  • Modern warehouse facilities and equipment.
  • Career progression opportunities within CEVA Logistics.
