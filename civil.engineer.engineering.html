Civil Engineer

Job Description

Civil Engineering Company delivers professional infrastructure, construction, and engineering solutions across a range of projects. The company is seeking a skilled and motivated Civil Engineer to support the design, development, and delivery of civil engineering projects. In this role, you will work on infrastructure improvements, technical designs, site assessments, and project coordination while ensuring all work meets industry standards and client requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Design and develop civil engineering solutions for construction and infrastructure projects.
  • Prepare technical drawings, reports, calculations, and project documentation.
  • Carry out site inspections, surveys, and engineering assessments.
  • Support project planning, construction activities, and quality control.
  • Collaborate with contractors, engineers, architects, and project managers.
  • Monitor project progress and ensure compliance with engineering standards.
  • Identify technical issues and provide practical engineering solutions.
  • Ensure projects meet health, safety, and environmental requirements.

Requirements

  • Degree in Civil Engineering or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience working as a Civil Engineer is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of civil engineering principles and construction processes.
  • Experience with engineering software and technical drawing tools.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong communication and teamwork abilities.
  • Ability to manage engineering tasks and project deadlines.
  • Knowledge of health and safety regulations within construction environments.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £35,000–£55,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Professional development and technical training.
  • Opportunities to work on major engineering projects.
  • Supportive engineering team environment.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
