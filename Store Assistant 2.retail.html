Store Assistant

Matalan Retail Limited is a leading UK value fashion and homeware retailer, offering affordable clothing, footwear, and household products for the whole family. Matalan is seeking enthusiastic and customer-focused Store Assistants to join its retail team. In this role, you will help deliver excellent customer service, maintain stock availability, support merchandising, and ensure the store remains clean, organised, and welcoming. This position is ideal for individuals who enjoy working in a fast-paced retail environment and take pride in supporting a positive shopping experience.

Responsibilities

  • Provide friendly and helpful customer service at all times.
  • Assist customers with product selection and store enquiries.
  • Operate tills and process transactions accurately.
  • Replenish stock and ensure shelves are fully stocked and well-presented.
  • Assist with deliveries, unpacking, and stock organisation.
  • Maintain clean, safe, and tidy store and stockroom areas.
  • Support merchandising and promotional displays.
  • Follow company procedures and health and safety guidelines.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly, reliable, and team-oriented attitude.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a busy retail environment.
  • Good attention to detail and organisational skills.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling ability.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.
  • Previous retail experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns, including weekends and public holidays.

Salary

  • £12.25–£13.40 per hour.
  • Overtime opportunities may be available.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discount on Matalan products.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Opportunities for progression within retail.
