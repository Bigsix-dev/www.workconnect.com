Logistics Coordinator

Job Description

DB Schenker UK Limited is one of the world's leading logistics and supply chain providers, offering global freight, transport, and warehousing solutions to businesses across multiple industries. DB Schenker is seeking an organised and detail-focused Logistics Coordinator to support its daily logistics operations. In this role, you will coordinate shipments, manage transport activities, communicate with customers and suppliers, and help ensure goods are delivered efficiently and on schedule.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate daily logistics operations and shipment activities.
  • Monitor deliveries and ensure transport schedules are completed efficiently.
  • Communicate with customers, suppliers, drivers, and internal teams.
  • Prepare and maintain accurate transport documentation and records.
  • Track shipments and resolve delivery issues promptly.
  • Update logistics systems with shipment information and operational data.
  • Support warehouse and transport teams to improve service performance.
  • Ensure compliance with company procedures, customer requirements, and health and safety standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in logistics, transport coordination, or supply chain operations.
  • Strong organisational and administrative skills.
  • Excellent communication and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Good knowledge of logistics processes and transport operations.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy when handling documentation.
  • Experience using logistics management systems is desirable.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday schedule with flexibility depending on operational requirements.

Salary

  • £28,000–£34,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary package.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Employee benefits and reward programmes.
  • Health and wellbeing support.
  • Opportunities for career progression within DB Schenker.
  • Experience working with a global logistics organisation.
