Fleet Administrator

Job Description

Enterprise Mobility is one of the world's leading vehicle rental, fleet management, and mobility solutions providers, operating an extensive network across the United Kingdom and internationally. Enterprise Mobility is seeking a highly organised and detail-oriented Fleet Administrator to support the efficient management of its vehicle fleet. In this role, you will maintain fleet records, coordinate vehicle servicing and maintenance, process administrative documentation, and ensure fleet operations comply with company standards and legal requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain accurate fleet records and vehicle documentation.
  • Schedule vehicle servicing, MOTs, inspections, and maintenance appointments.
  • Process fleet-related invoices, purchase orders, and administrative paperwork.
  • Monitor vehicle availability and update fleet management systems.
  • Coordinate with suppliers, garages, and internal departments regarding fleet activities.
  • Assist with vehicle licensing, insurance, and compliance documentation.
  • Prepare reports on fleet performance, maintenance, and operational activities.
  • Ensure compliance with company policies and transport regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in administration, fleet management, or transport operations is desirable.
  • Strong organisational and administrative skills.
  • Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Good communication and customer service skills.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel and Outlook.
  • Experience using fleet management or business administration systems is an advantage.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Professional, reliable, and able to work effectively within a team.

Working Hours

  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with occasional flexibility based on business needs.

Salary

Benefits
