Carpenter / Joiner

Job Description

Property Services Contractor is seeking an experienced Carpenter / Joiner to undertake carpentry and joinery work across properties in Chelmsford. The successful candidate will be responsible for delivering accurate, high-quality work while meeting project and maintenance requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out first and second fix carpentry and joinery works.
  • Install doors, frames, skirting, architraves, and other timber components.
  • Repair and replace damaged timber fixtures.
  • Measure, cut, and install materials accurately.
  • Complete maintenance and refurbishment projects.
  • Maintain safe and tidy working areas.

Requirements

  • Proven experience as a Carpenter or Joiner.
  • Strong knowledge of carpentry tools and materials.
  • Ability to read measurements and technical drawings.
  • Good attention to detail and finishing standards.
  • Reliable and professional approach to work.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Based in Chelmsford.

Salary

  • £30,000–£40,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary.
  • Steady property services work.
  • Opportunities for varied carpentry projects.
  • Supportive working environment.
