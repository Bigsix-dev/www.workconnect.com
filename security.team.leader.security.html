Security Team Leader

Job Description

Security Services Company provides professional security guarding, monitoring, and protective services to a range of commercial and industrial clients. The company is seeking an experienced Security Team Leader to supervise security personnel, coordinate daily duties, and ensure high standards of site security. You will support security officers, communicate with clients and management, and respond to incidents while ensuring operational procedures are consistently followed.

Responsibilities

  • Lead and supervise security officers during assigned shifts.
  • Allocate duties, patrols, access control responsibilities, and other security tasks.
  • Ensure team members follow site security procedures and operational instructions.
  • Conduct regular site inspections and monitor security standards.
  • Respond to incidents and coordinate the team's response when required.
  • Maintain and review security logs, incident reports, and shift records.
  • Act as a point of contact between security officers, clients, and site management.
  • Provide guidance, coaching, and support to members of the security team.

Requirements

  • Valid SIA licence appropriate to the role.
  • Previous security experience with team-leading or supervisory responsibility.
  • Strong knowledge of security procedures and incident management.
  • Good leadership and people management skills.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal abilities.
  • Strong decision-making and problem-solving skills.
  • Good administrative and report writing skills.
  • Reliable, confident, professional, and capable of leading a team effectively.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working may include nights, weekends, and public holidays.

Salary

  • £30,000–£35,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive security team leader salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Leadership and security management training.
  • Opportunities for additional professional security qualifications.
  • Supportive security operations environment.
  • Career progression into security supervisory and management roles.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
