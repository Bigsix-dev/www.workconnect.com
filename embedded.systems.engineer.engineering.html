Embedded Systems Engineer

Job Description

Technology Company develops innovative electronic and software-based products for modern technology applications. The company is seeking an Embedded Systems Engineer to design, develop, test, and maintain embedded software and hardware solutions. You will collaborate with hardware, software, and product teams to create reliable embedded systems that meet performance and product requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Design and develop embedded software for electronic devices and systems.
  • Develop, test, and debug firmware for embedded platforms.
  • Work with hardware engineers to integrate software and electronic components.
  • Analyse system requirements and translate them into technical solutions.
  • Carry out debugging, testing, and performance analysis of embedded systems.
  • Develop and maintain technical documentation and software specifications.
  • Investigate system faults and implement reliable corrective solutions.
  • Contribute to product development and continuous improvement projects.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Electronic Engineering, Embedded Systems, Computer Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Experience developing embedded systems or firmware is desirable.
  • Good knowledge of programming languages such as C or C++.
  • Understanding of microcontrollers, communication protocols, and embedded hardware.
  • Experience with debugging and testing embedded systems.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to work effectively with multidisciplinary engineering teams.
  • Strong attention to detail and technical accuracy.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £45,000–£70,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunities to work with modern technologies.
  • Collaborative technology and engineering environment.
  • Career progression and professional development opportunities.
