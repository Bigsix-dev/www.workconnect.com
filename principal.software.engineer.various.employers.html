Principal Software Engineer

Job Description

Various Employers across Chelmsford are looking for Principal Software Engineers to provide senior technical leadership across software development projects. The position involves shaping technical direction, designing scalable solutions and supporting engineering teams.

Responsibilities

  • Provide technical leadership across software engineering projects.
  • Define and review software architecture and technical solutions.
  • Lead the design and development of complex applications and systems.
  • Mentor and support software engineers across development teams.
  • Identify opportunities to improve development standards and engineering practices.
  • Collaborate with technical and business stakeholders on strategic initiatives.

Requirements

  • Extensive professional experience in software engineering.
  • Strong knowledge of software architecture and design principles.
  • Excellent programming and technical problem-solving skills.
  • Experience providing technical leadership and mentoring engineers.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working arrangements may vary depending on the employer.

Salary

  • Around £68,198 per year average.

Benefits

  • Senior technical leadership opportunities.
  • Access to challenging and strategic software projects.
  • Professional development and learning opportunities.
  • Opportunity to influence engineering standards and technology decisions.
