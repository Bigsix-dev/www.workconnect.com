Commercial Property Secretary

Job Description

Legal Firm is seeking an organised Commercial Property Secretary to provide efficient administrative and secretarial support to the commercial property team. The successful candidate will assist solicitors with correspondence, documentation, diary management, client communication, and day-to-day administration while ensuring matters are handled accurately and professionally.

Responsibilities

  • Provide secretarial and administrative support to commercial property solicitors.
  • Prepare, format, and process legal correspondence and documentation.
  • Maintain accurate client and matter files.
  • Manage diaries, appointments, meetings, and deadlines.
  • Handle telephone calls and correspondence from clients and professional contacts.
  • Assist with property transaction administration and document management.
  • Update case management systems and maintain accurate records.
  • Arrange meetings and liaise with clients, agents, lenders, and other professionals.
  • Monitor outstanding tasks and assist solicitors with transaction progression.
  • Provide general administrative support to the commercial property department.

Requirements

  • Previous legal secretarial, property, or administrative experience is desirable.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Confident using Microsoft Office and legal case management systems.
  • Ability to manage confidential information professionally.
  • Ability to prioritise multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Professional and client-focused approach.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday, 9:00am–5:00pm.
  • Full-time position.

Salary

  • £24,500 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional legal working environment.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Opportunity to develop specialist property administration skills.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
