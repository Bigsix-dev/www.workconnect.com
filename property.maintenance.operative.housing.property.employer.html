Property Maintenance Operative

Job Description

A Housing/Property Employer is seeking a practical and dependable Property Maintenance Operative to undertake repairs, maintenance, and improvement work across residential properties. The role requires a broad range of practical skills, a commitment to quality workmanship, and the ability to respond efficiently to maintenance requirements while maintaining safe working practices.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out general repairs and property maintenance tasks.
  • Complete minor carpentry, plumbing, decorating, and building repairs.
  • Investigate maintenance issues and identify suitable repair solutions.
  • Complete planned and reactive maintenance work.
  • Use tools and equipment safely and correctly.
  • Maintain clean and safe working areas.
  • Report major defects or specialist work requirements.
  • Communicate professionally with tenants, residents, property managers, and colleagues.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in property maintenance, construction, repairs, or a related trade.
  • Good practical and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to carry out a variety of maintenance tasks.
  • Strong attention to detail and quality standards.
  • Knowledge of basic health and safety procedures.
  • Good communication and customer service skills.
  • Reliable, punctual, and well organised.
  • Relevant trade qualifications are desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours based around maintenance schedules and service requirements.
  • Occasional emergency or out-of-hours work may be required.

Salary

  • Salary not stated; dependent on experience and qualifications.

Benefits

  • Stable property-sector employment.
  • Varied maintenance and repair work.
  • Training and professional development opportunities.
  • Opportunity to develop a broad range of practical skills.
  • Supportive working environment.
  • Potential for career progression within property maintenance.
