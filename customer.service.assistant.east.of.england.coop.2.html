Customer Service Assistant

Job Description

East of England Co-op is looking for a friendly Customer Service Assistant to help deliver a positive experience for customers while supporting daily store operations. You will assist customers, process transactions, replenish stock, maintain store standards, and work closely with colleagues to ensure the store operates efficiently.

Responsibilities

  • Provide friendly and helpful customer service.
  • Assist customers with product enquiries and requests.
  • Process purchases and other customer transactions accurately.
  • Replenish shelves and maintain product availability.
  • Maintain clean and organised store areas.
  • Assist with deliveries, stock handling, and other operational duties.
  • Monitor product presentation and store standards.
  • Respond professionally to customer concerns.
  • Follow company health, safety, and security procedures.
  • Support colleagues during busy periods.

Requirements

  • Previous retail or customer service experience is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly, approachable, and helpful manner.
  • Good attention to detail.
  • Reliable and punctual approach to work.
  • Ability to work effectively within a team.
  • Willingness to undertake a variety of store duties.
  • Flexibility to work the required part-time shifts.

Working Hours

  • 4–12 hours per week.
  • Part-time position.

Salary

  • £13.23 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly rate.
  • Employee benefits associated with the organisation.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Supportive retail environment.
  • Opportunity to gain valuable customer service experience.
