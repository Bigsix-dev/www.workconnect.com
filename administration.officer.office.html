Administration Officer

Job Description

Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) is the United Kingdom's largest government department, responsible for welfare, pensions, and employment services. The Department for Work and Pensions is seeking a dedicated and organised Administration Officer to support the delivery of high-quality public services. In this role, you will provide administrative support, process documentation, maintain accurate records, and assist with delivering efficient services while ensuring compliance with government policies and procedures.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to departmental teams and managers.
  • Process applications, forms, correspondence, and official documentation accurately.
  • Maintain electronic and paper-based records in accordance with DWP procedures.
  • Handle telephone, email, and written enquiries from customers and stakeholders.
  • Prepare reports, letters, and administrative documents.
  • Schedule meetings, appointments, and maintain team calendars.
  • Support case management and document processing activities.
  • Ensure compliance with government regulations, data protection legislation, and confidentiality requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in administration, customer service, or office support is desirable.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Ability to manage confidential information professionally.
  • Strong problem-solving and teamwork skills.
  • Commitment to delivering excellent public service.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard government office hours.

Salary

  • £25,000–£29,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive Civil Service salary.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Civil Service Pension Scheme.
  • Professional learning and development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Flexible and hybrid working opportunities where applicable.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within the Department for Work and Pensions.
