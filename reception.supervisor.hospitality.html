Reception Supervisor

Job Description

Premier Inn, part of Whitbread PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's leading hotel brands, renowned for providing comfortable accommodation and outstanding customer service. Premier Inn is seeking an experienced and customer-focused Reception Supervisor to lead the front desk team and ensure guests receive a warm welcome and exceptional service throughout their stay. In this role, you will oversee reception operations, support the Reception Manager, and maintain the highest standards of guest satisfaction.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise the daily operation of the hotel reception and front desk team.
  • Welcome guests and oversee smooth check-in and check-out procedures.
  • Support, coach, and motivate reception team members to deliver outstanding customer service.
  • Handle guest enquiries, complaints, and special requests professionally.
  • Manage room reservations, guest records, and payment transactions accurately.
  • Prepare daily reports and support administrative duties.
  • Assist with staff scheduling and shift coordination.
  • Ensure compliance with hotel policies, health and safety regulations, and company standards.

Requirements

  • Previous supervisory experience in hotel reception or front office operations.
  • Excellent leadership and customer service skills.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal abilities.
  • Good organisational, administrative, and problem-solving skills.
  • Experience using hotel reservation or property management systems is desirable.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced hospitality environment.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends, evenings, and public holidays.
  • Professional appearance and commitment to providing exceptional guest experiences.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible rotating shifts, including mornings, evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £14.50–£16.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts on Premier Inn stays and Whitbread brands.
  • Comprehensive leadership training and career development programmes.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression within Premier Inn and Whitbread.
