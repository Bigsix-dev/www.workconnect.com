Training Administrator

Job Description

Lifetime Training Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading training providers, helping individuals and organisations develop skills through apprenticeships, professional qualifications, and workplace learning programmes. Lifetime Training is seeking a highly organised and supportive Training Administrator to assist with the coordination and delivery of training activities. In this role, you will manage learner records, coordinate training schedules, support trainers, and ensure accurate administration of learning programmes.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support for training programmes and learning activities.
  • Maintain accurate learner records, attendance data, and training documentation.
  • Schedule training sessions and coordinate with learners, trainers, and employers.
  • Prepare reports, certificates, and training-related documents.
  • Monitor learner progress and update training systems.
  • Respond to enquiries from learners, employers, and internal teams.
  • Support onboarding of new learners onto training programmes.
  • Ensure compliance with training procedures, quality standards, and data protection requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in training administration, education administration, or office support.
  • Excellent organisational and coordination skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accurate record keeping abilities.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Experience using learning management systems is desirable.
  • Professional, approachable, and committed to supporting learners.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard business hours.

Salary

  • £25,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Flexible working opportunities where applicable.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Lifetime Training.
