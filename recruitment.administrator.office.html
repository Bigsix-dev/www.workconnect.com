Recruitment Administrator

Job Description

Hays Specialist Recruitment Limited is one of the world's leading recruitment and workforce solutions companies, connecting skilled professionals with employers across multiple industries. Hays Recruitment is seeking a highly organised and proactive Recruitment Administrator to support recruitment consultants and ensure a smooth hiring process. In this role, you will coordinate interviews, manage candidate records, prepare recruitment documentation, and provide administrative support while delivering an excellent experience for both clients and candidates.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to recruitment consultants and hiring teams.
  • Schedule interviews and coordinate communication with candidates and clients.
  • Maintain accurate candidate records and recruitment databases.
  • Prepare employment documents, contracts, and onboarding paperwork.
  • Screen applications and organise candidate documentation.
  • Respond to candidate and client enquiries professionally.
  • Monitor recruitment progress and ensure compliance with hiring procedures.
  • Maintain confidentiality and comply with employment legislation and company policies.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in recruitment administration, HR administration, or office administration is desirable.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Strong customer service and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to manage confidential information professionally.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced recruitment environment.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard business hours.

Salary

  • £25,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Performance-related rewards and incentives.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Hays Recruitment.
