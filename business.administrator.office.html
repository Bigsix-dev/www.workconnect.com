Business Administrator

Job Description

Capita PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading business process outsourcing and professional services companies, providing administrative, digital, and consulting solutions to public and private sector organisations. Capita is seeking an organised and proactive Business Administrator to support day-to-day business operations. In this role, you will provide administrative support, maintain business records, coordinate communications, and assist departments in delivering efficient and high-quality services.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to managers and business teams.
  • Prepare reports, correspondence, and business documentation.
  • Maintain accurate records, databases, and filing systems.
  • Coordinate meetings, appointments, and team schedules.
  • Handle telephone calls, emails, and customer enquiries professionally.
  • Assist with document processing, data entry, and administrative projects.
  • Support the implementation of business procedures and operational improvements.
  • Ensure compliance with company policies, confidentiality requirements, and data protection regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in business administration, office administration, or a similar role.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, Outlook, and PowerPoint.
  • Strong problem-solving and administrative skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Professional attitude with excellent teamwork and customer service skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard business hours.

Salary

  • £26,000–£31,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional training and career development programmes.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Flexible and hybrid working opportunities where applicable.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Capita.
