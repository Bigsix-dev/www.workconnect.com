Service Advisor

Job Description

theo is seeking a customer-focused Service Advisor to manage customer service requirements and coordinate vehicle servicing and maintenance activities. You will act as a link between customers and technical teams, ensuring that customer concerns are understood, work is scheduled efficiently, and customers receive clear updates throughout the service process. The role requires excellent communication, organisation, and commercial awareness.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide a professional and friendly service.
  • Arrange vehicle servicing, maintenance, repairs, and inspection appointments.
  • Listen to customer concerns and accurately record required work.
  • Explain recommended work, service requirements, costs, and estimated completion times.
  • Coordinate with technicians and workshop teams to ensure work is completed efficiently.
  • Keep customers informed about vehicle progress and any changes to the agreed work.
  • Process service documentation, estimates, invoices, and customer records.
  • Follow up with customers and support high levels of customer satisfaction.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Service Advisor or within an automotive customer service environment is desirable.
  • Good understanding of vehicle servicing and repair processes.
  • Excellent communication, listening, and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong customer service and relationship-building abilities.
  • Good organisational and administrative skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to manage competing priorities.
  • Confident discussing technical information and service costs with customers.
  • Commercially aware, proactive, and motivated to achieve excellent customer outcomes.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday, 8:00am–6:00pm.
  • Full-time working schedule.
  • Additional flexibility may be required according to business needs.

Salary

  • £41,500–£52,500 OTE.

Benefits

  • Competitive OTE package.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Automotive service and product training.
  • Professional working environment.
  • Opportunities to develop technical and customer service knowledge.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
