Housing Options Triage Officer

Job Description

Braintree District Council is seeking a Housing Options Triage Officer to provide an effective first point of contact for residents seeking housing advice and assistance. The role involves assessing housing circumstances, identifying appropriate options, providing information, and referring cases to specialist services where required. The successful candidate will demonstrate empathy, sound judgement, and a strong understanding of housing-related support.

Responsibilities

  • Provide initial housing advice and assistance to residents.
  • Assess housing circumstances and identify immediate needs and risks.
  • Gather accurate information to support housing assessments.
  • Explain available housing options and relevant procedures.
  • Identify cases requiring specialist housing or support intervention.
  • Make appropriate referrals to internal and external services.
  • Maintain accurate and confidential case records.
  • Work with housing colleagues and partner agencies to support residents.

Requirements

  • Experience in housing, advice, customer service, social care, or a related environment.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to handle sensitive and potentially challenging situations professionally.
  • Good understanding of housing-related issues and support services.
  • Strong assessment and decision-making skills.
  • Ability to maintain accurate confidential records.
  • Excellent organisational and administrative skills.
  • Commitment to providing fair and respectful services to residents.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Standard professional working hours.
  • Some flexibility may be required depending on service requirements.

Salary

  • £28–£30 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive public-sector hourly rate.
  • Professional development opportunities.
  • Public-sector working environment.
  • Training and support.
  • Opportunities to develop housing and community-support expertise.
  • Meaningful work supporting local residents.
