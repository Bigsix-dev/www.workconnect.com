Software Engineer

Job Description

Engineering Technology Firm develops software solutions and digital technologies for engineering and industrial applications. The company is seeking a skilled Software Engineer to design, develop, test, and maintain software systems that support engineering products and business operations. In this role, you will work closely with engineers, developers, and technical teams to deliver reliable and efficient software solutions.

Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, test, and maintain software applications and systems.
  • Write clean, reliable, and maintainable code.
  • Analyse software requirements and translate them into technical solutions.
  • Identify and resolve software defects, bugs, and performance issues.
  • Work with engineering teams to integrate software with technical systems and products.
  • Participate in code reviews and software development activities.
  • Maintain technical documentation and development records.
  • Contribute to software improvement and new technology development projects.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience in software development or engineering is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of at least one programming language.
  • Good understanding of software development principles and testing practices.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to understand technical requirements and develop effective solutions.
  • Good attention to detail and commitment to producing high-quality software.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £45,000–£75,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering technology salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Software development and technical training.
  • Opportunities to work with modern engineering technologies.
  • Collaborative technology and engineering environment.
  • Career progression and professional development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
