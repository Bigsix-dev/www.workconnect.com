Custody Officer

Job Description

Public Services Contractor provides operational and custodial support services to public-sector organisations. The company is seeking a responsible Custody Officer to support the safe and secure management of individuals within a custodial environment. You will maintain security, supervise individuals in custody, complete required documentation, and respond appropriately to incidents while following strict operational procedures.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain the safety, security, and welfare of individuals within custody.
  • Supervise individuals in accordance with custody procedures and operational policies.
  • Carry out appropriate searches, security checks, and monitoring activities.
  • Maintain accurate custody records, logs, and incident reports.
  • Monitor custody areas and identify potential security or welfare concerns.
  • Respond appropriately to incidents, emergencies, and challenging behaviour.
  • Communicate effectively with colleagues, visitors, professionals, and relevant authorities.
  • Ensure all custody procedures are followed fairly, professionally, and consistently.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in security, custody, public service, or a similar environment is desirable.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional under pressure.
  • Strong observation and situational awareness.
  • Ability to follow strict procedures and maintain accurate records.
  • Good judgement and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong personal integrity and professional conduct.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a custody and security team.

Working Hours

  • Shift-based working schedule may apply.
