Activities Coordinator

Job Overview

As an Activities Coordinator, you will be responsible for planning and delivering engaging activities that improve the physical, emotional, and social wellbeing of residents living in the care home. You will encourage participation, promote independence, and create enjoyable experiences tailored to residents' interests and abilities.

Key Responsibilities

  • Plan and organise a varied programme of daily and weekly activities.
  • Encourage residents to take part in social, recreational, and therapeutic events.
  • Arrange group activities such as games, quizzes, music sessions, arts and crafts, exercise classes, and celebrations.
  • Organise special events, themed days, and community outings where appropriate.
  • Build positive relationships with residents, families, and colleagues.
  • Adapt activities to meet the needs of individuals with dementia or mobility limitations.
  • Maintain activity records and gather feedback to improve programmes.
  • Assist with decorating and preparing communal areas for events and seasonal celebrations.
  • Work closely with the care team to ensure residents receive person-centred support.
  • Follow health and safety procedures during all activities.

About You

The ideal candidate will:

  • Have a friendly, enthusiastic, and compassionate personality.
  • Enjoy working with older people and helping them stay active and engaged.
  • Possess good communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Be creative and able to develop fun, meaningful activities.
  • Be organised and capable of managing multiple events and schedules.
  • Be flexible and reliable, especially in a bank or as-needed role.
  • Previous experience in care, hospitality, education, or activity coordination is beneficial but not always essential.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay of £12.75 per hour.
  • Flexible bank shifts that fit around your availability.
  • Paid training and ongoing learning opportunities.
  • Supportive and welcoming working environment.
  • Opportunities to gain experience in elderly care and wellbeing services.
  • Workplace pension scheme (where eligible).
  • Holiday pay and statutory employment benefits.
  • Chance to make a meaningful difference in residents' quality of life.
  • Potential opportunities for permanent employment and career progression.
