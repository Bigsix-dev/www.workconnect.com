Hospice Support Worker

Job Description

Farleigh Hospice is seeking a compassionate and dedicated Hospice Support Worker to provide high-quality care and emotional support to individuals living with life-limiting illnesses. Working as part of a multidisciplinary team, you will assist patients with personal care, promote comfort and dignity, monitor their wellbeing, and support families during challenging times. This role is ideal for someone who is empathetic, reliable, and committed to delivering person-centred palliative care.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist patients with personal care, mobility, and daily living activities.
  • Support nurses and clinical staff in delivering palliative and end-of-life care.
  • Monitor and report changes in patients' physical or emotional condition.
  • Provide emotional reassurance and companionship to patients and their families.
  • Maintain accurate care records and follow care plans.
  • Help create a safe, clean, and comfortable environment for patients.
  • Adhere to safeguarding procedures, infection prevention standards, and hospice policies.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in healthcare, hospice, residential care, or a support worker role is desirable.
  • A caring, respectful, and patient-focused approach.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to work flexible shifts, including weekends and holidays when required.
  • Basic literacy and IT skills for maintaining care documentation.
  • Relevant health and social care qualifications (or willingness to work towards them) are advantageous.

What Farleigh Hospice Offers

  • Competitive salary with opportunities for progression.
  • Comprehensive induction and ongoing professional training.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement and pension scheme.
  • Employee wellbeing initiatives and counselling support.
  • The opportunity to make a meaningful difference in the lives of patients and their loved ones every day.
