Events Coordinator

Job Description

The County Hotel is a respected hospitality venue known for hosting conferences, business meetings, weddings, banquets, and private events. The hotel is seeking a highly organised and customer-focused Events Coordinator to manage the planning and successful delivery of a wide range of events. In this role, you will work closely with clients, suppliers, and hotel departments to ensure every event is delivered professionally, efficiently, and to the highest standard of customer satisfaction.

Responsibilities

  • Plan, organise, and coordinate conferences, weddings, corporate events, and private functions.
  • Meet with clients to discuss event requirements, budgets, and schedules.
  • Coordinate with catering, housekeeping, reception, and maintenance teams.
  • Prepare event schedules, function sheets, and operational plans.
  • Monitor event setup and ensure all arrangements meet client expectations.
  • Manage supplier bookings, equipment hire, and event logistics.
  • Handle client enquiries, resolve issues promptly, and ensure exceptional customer service.
  • Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations and hotel policies throughout all events.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in event coordination, hospitality, or hotel operations.
  • Excellent organisational and project management skills.
  • Outstanding communication and interpersonal abilities.
  • Strong customer service and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple events and deadlines simultaneously.
  • Good computer skills, including Microsoft Office applications.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays when required.
  • A professional attitude with excellent attention to detail.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts based on event schedules and business requirements.

Salary

  • £15.00–£18.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Complimentary or discounted staff meals during shifts.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts on hotel services.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive working environment with excellent opportunities for career progression.
