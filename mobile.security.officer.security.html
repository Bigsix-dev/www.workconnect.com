Mobile Security Officer

Job Description

Security Services Company provides mobile security and patrol services to businesses, properties, and organisations across multiple locations. The company is seeking a dependable Mobile Security Officer to conduct scheduled patrols, respond to alarms, inspect properties, and provide security support across assigned sites. The role requires a proactive approach, strong situational awareness, and the ability to work independently while maintaining professional communication with clients and colleagues.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct scheduled mobile patrols of assigned properties and locations.
  • Respond promptly to alarm activations, incidents, and security requests.
  • Inspect buildings, gates, doors, windows, and other security points during patrols.
  • Identify suspicious activity, damage, safety concerns, and potential security risks.
  • Secure properties and premises in accordance with client and company procedures.
  • Maintain accurate patrol logs, incident reports, and security records.
  • Communicate effectively with control rooms, clients, emergency services, and colleagues.
  • Ensure company security vehicles and equipment are used responsibly and maintained appropriately.

Requirements

  • Valid SIA Security Guard or Door Supervisor licence as required for the role.
  • Previous mobile security or patrol experience is desirable.
  • Full UK driving licence is desirable and may be required for the position.
  • Strong observation and situational awareness skills.
  • Ability to work independently and make responsible decisions.
  • Good communication and incident reporting skills.
  • Reliable, punctual, and flexible approach to shift work.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional when responding to incidents.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working schedule may include nights and weekends.

Salary

  • £28,000–£34,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive mobile security salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Mobile security and incident response training.
  • Company vehicle or vehicle support where applicable.
  • Uniform and required security equipment.
  • Career progression opportunities within security operations.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
