Clinic Administrator

Job Description

Spire Healthcare Group PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading independent healthcare providers, operating hospitals and clinics that deliver high-quality medical care across a wide range of specialties. Spire Healthcare is seeking a professional and organised Clinic Administrator to support the smooth operation of clinical services. In this role, you will coordinate patient appointments, maintain medical records, assist clinical teams, and provide excellent administrative support while ensuring a positive patient experience.

Responsibilities

  • Manage patient appointments, clinic schedules, and consultant diaries.
  • Maintain accurate patient records and update healthcare databases.
  • Prepare clinic documentation, correspondence, and administrative reports.
  • Welcome patients and respond to enquiries by telephone, email, and in person.
  • Coordinate referrals, admissions, and follow-up appointments.
  • Support clinicians and healthcare professionals with administrative tasks.
  • Process invoices, medical documentation, and clinic paperwork.
  • Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations, GDPR, and confidentiality requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in healthcare administration, clinic administration, or office administration.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong communication and customer service abilities.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Experience using healthcare or patient management systems is desirable.
  • Ability to manage confidential patient information professionally.
  • Friendly, professional, and able to work effectively within a multidisciplinary team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with occasional flexibility depending on clinic requirements.

Salary

  • £25,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Private healthcare and wellbeing support.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Spire Healthcare.
