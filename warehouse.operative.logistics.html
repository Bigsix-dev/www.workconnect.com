Warehouse Operative

Job Description

Amazon UK Services Limited is one of the world's leading e-commerce and logistics companies, operating advanced fulfilment centres that process millions of customer orders every day. Amazon UK is seeking a hardworking and reliable Warehouse Operative to join its warehouse team. In this role, you will receive, pick, pack, and dispatch customer orders while maintaining high standards of accuracy, safety, and productivity within a fast-paced warehouse environment.

Responsibilities

  • Receive, unload, and organise incoming deliveries.
  • Pick, pack, and prepare customer orders accurately for dispatch.
  • Operate handheld scanners and warehouse management systems.
  • Inspect products for quality and report damaged items.
  • Maintain a clean, organised, and safe working environment.
  • Follow warehouse procedures and productivity targets.
  • Work collaboratively with team members to achieve daily operational goals.
  • Comply with company policies and health and safety regulations at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous warehouse experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Ability to lift, carry, and move products safely.
  • Good attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment.
  • Basic computer and handheld scanner skills.
  • Strong teamwork and communication skills.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and overtime when required.
  • Commitment to maintaining high safety and quality standards.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5–40 hours per week.
  • Day, evening, night, and weekend shifts may be required.

Salary

  • £13.00–£14.80 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Comprehensive training and onboarding programme.
  • Employee discounts on selected Amazon products and services.
  • Health and wellbeing support programmes.
  • Modern warehouse facilities and equipment.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression within Amazon UK.
