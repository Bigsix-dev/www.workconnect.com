Bank Care Assistant

Job Description

As a Bank Care Assistant at Windle Court, you will provide compassionate and person-centred care to residents on an as-needed basis. Working flexible shifts, you will support individuals with their daily routines, promote independence, and help maintain a safe, comfortable, and welcoming environment.

This role is ideal for someone looking for flexible working arrangements while making a positive difference in the lives of elderly or vulnerable people.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist residents with personal care, including washing, dressing, and grooming.
  • Support with mobility and safe transfers using appropriate equipment where required.
  • Help residents during meal times and encourage good nutrition and hydration.
  • Provide companionship and emotional support to promote wellbeing.
  • Monitor residents' health and report any concerns to senior staff.
  • Assist with recreational activities and encourage social interaction.
  • Maintain accurate care records and follow care plans.
  • Ensure a clean, safe, and respectful living environment.
  • Follow safeguarding procedures and infection prevention guidelines.
  • Work collaboratively with colleagues, healthcare professionals, and families.

About You

To be successful in this role, you should:

  • Be caring, patient, and empathetic.
  • Have excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Be reliable and able to work flexible bank shifts, including weekends or evenings when needed.
  • Be willing to learn and adapt in a fast-paced care environment.
  • Be able to work effectively both independently and as part of a team.
  • Previous experience in care is beneficial but not always essential, as training may be provided.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay of £12.72 per hour.
  • Flexible bank shifts to fit around your availability.
  • Paid training and opportunities for career development.
  • Supportive and friendly working environment.
  • Holiday pay in line with bank worker arrangements.
  • Access to employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Pension scheme (where eligible).
  • Opportunity to gain valuable experience in the healthcare and social care sector.
  • Uniform provided (where applicable).
  • Chance to make a meaningful impact on the lives of residents every day.
