Restaurant Assistant

Job Description

Harvester, part of Mitchells & Butlers PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's most popular family restaurant chains, known for its freshly grilled meals, unlimited salad bar, and welcoming dining experience. Harvester is looking for enthusiastic and customer-focused Restaurant Assistants to join its team. In this role, you will help deliver outstanding customer service, support the smooth day-to-day running of the restaurant, and ensure every guest enjoys a memorable dining experience.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome guests in a friendly and professional manner.
  • Prepare dining areas before opening and throughout service.
  • Assist with taking food and drink orders when required.
  • Serve meals and beverages efficiently and accurately.
  • Maintain cleanliness of tables, dining areas, and service stations.
  • Restock supplies, cutlery, napkins, and condiments.
  • Support kitchen and front-of-house staff during busy periods.
  • Follow food hygiene, health and safety, and company policies.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Friendly, positive, and professional attitude.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced restaurant environment.
  • Strong teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Good attention to detail.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Previous hospitality experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts, including evenings and weekends.

Salary

  • £12.30–£13.60 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Discounted meals while on duty.
  • Employee discounts across Mitchells & Butlers restaurants.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
