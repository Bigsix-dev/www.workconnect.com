Retail Merchandiser

DCK Group is a leading retail service provider specialising in jewellery and accessories merchandising across major UK retailers. The company is seeking motivated and detail-oriented Retail Merchandisers to join its field-based retail team. In this role, you will be responsible for ensuring products are attractively displayed, well-stocked, and aligned with brand standards across multiple retail locations. This position is ideal for individuals who enjoy working independently, have an eye for detail, and take pride in maintaining high visual merchandising standards.

Responsibilities

  • Visit assigned retail stores to maintain and update product displays.
  • Ensure merchandise is presented according to brand guidelines.
  • Replenish stock and ensure products are available on the shop floor.
  • Install and adjust display fixtures and promotional materials.
  • Monitor stock levels and report shortages or issues.
  • Maintain accurate records of store visits and merchandising activities.
  • Build and maintain good relationships with store managers and staff.
  • Follow company procedures, health, and safety standards.

Requirements

  • Strong attention to detail and organisational skills.
  • Ability to work independently and manage time effectively.
  • Good communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Reliable, punctual, and self-motivated attitude.
  • Ability to travel between multiple store locations.
  • Basic understanding of retail or merchandising is beneficial but not essential.
  • Flexible schedule to meet store visit requirements.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35 hours per week.
  • May include flexible scheduling depending on store locations and merchandising needs.

Salary

  • £24,000–£28,000 per year.
  • Travel allowances may be included depending on assignments.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Travel expense support (where applicable).
  • Comprehensive training and development opportunities.
  • Career progression within retail merchandising.
  • Employee support programmes.
  • Flexible and independent working environment.
