Estate Agent Trainee Sales Negotiator

Job Description

Kings Permanent Recruitment is seeking a motivated Estate Agent Trainee Sales Negotiator to begin a career in residential property sales. The successful candidate will receive training while assisting customers, arranging property viewings, generating sales opportunities, and supporting the sales team through the property transaction process.

Responsibilities

  • Respond to enquiries from prospective buyers and property sellers.
  • Learn how to qualify buyers and understand their property requirements.
  • Arrange and assist with property viewings.
  • Support the marketing and promotion of properties for sale.
  • Develop relationships with buyers, sellers, and other property professionals.
  • Assist experienced Sales Negotiators with negotiations and offers.
  • Maintain accurate customer and property records.
  • Follow up with prospective buyers and progress sales opportunities.
  • Assist with sales administration and property documentation.
  • Work towards developing sales skills and achieving agreed targets.

Requirements

  • No previous estate agency experience is necessarily required.
  • Strong interest in property and residential sales.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Confident and enthusiastic approach to customer interaction.
  • Good organisational and time management abilities.
  • Commercially aware and motivated to develop a career in sales.
  • Positive attitude with a willingness to learn.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a busy estate agency team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £16,000–£35,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Structured training and development.
  • Opportunity to start a career in estate agency.
  • Professional property sales environment.
  • Practical experience in residential property sales.
  • Career progression opportunities.
