Support & Care Worker

Full Job Description

As a Support & Care Worker, you will provide compassionate, person-centred care to individuals who may require assistance due to age, illness, disability, or other support needs. Working as part of a dedicated care team, you will help clients maintain their independence, dignity, and quality of life in residential settings or within the community.

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide personal care, including assistance with bathing, dressing, and grooming.
  • Support clients with mobility and daily living activities.
  • Assist with meal preparation and encourage healthy nutrition.
  • Administer or prompt medication in accordance with care plans and training.
  • Offer emotional support and companionship to clients.
  • Record observations and maintain accurate care documentation.
  • Follow safeguarding procedures and report any concerns promptly.
  • Work collaboratively with healthcare professionals, families, and colleagues.
  • Promote independence while ensuring clients' safety and wellbeing
  • Maintain confidentiality and comply with health and safety regulations.

About You

The ideal candidate should:

  • Be caring, patient, and compassionate.
  • Have excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Be reliable, trustworthy, and flexible with working hours.
  • Be able to work independently as well as part of a team.
  • Have a willingness to complete mandatory training.
  • Previous experience in care is beneficial but may not always be essential.
  • Hold the legal right to work in the UK and, where required, possess a valid driving licence and access to transport.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay of £12.21–£14.00.
  • Flexible part-time working arrangements.
  • Paid training and opportunities for professional development.
  • Ongoing support from experienced supervisors and management.
  • Holiday entitlement and workplace pension (subject to eligibility).
  • Potential overtime and additional shift opportunities.
  • Rewarding work that makes a meaningful difference in people's lives.
  • Career progression opportunities within the health and social care sector.
