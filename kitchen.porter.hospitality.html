Kitchen Porter

Job Description

PizzaExpress Restaurants Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading casual dining restaurant chains, famous for its handcrafted pizzas, Italian-inspired dishes, and welcoming dining experience. PizzaExpress is looking for hardworking and dependable Kitchen Porters to support the kitchen team by maintaining high standards of cleanliness, assisting with food preparation tasks, and ensuring the kitchen operates efficiently. This role is essential in helping chefs deliver high-quality meals while maintaining strict food hygiene and safety standards.

Responsibilities

  • Wash and sanitise kitchen equipment, utensils, crockery, and cookware.
  • Maintain cleanliness of kitchen workstations, storage areas, and food preparation surfaces.
  • Assist chefs with basic food preparation, including washing and preparing ingredients.
  • Receive, unpack, and correctly store food and kitchen deliveries.
  • Dispose of waste and recycling safely in accordance with company procedures.
  • Restock cleaning supplies, kitchen equipment, and food storage areas.
  • Support the kitchen team during busy service periods.
  • Follow food hygiene, health and safety, and company policies at all times.

Requirements

  • Strong work ethic and willingness to learn.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced kitchen environment.
  • Good teamwork and communication skills.
  • Excellent attention to cleanliness and hygiene.
  • Physically capable of standing for long periods and lifting kitchen supplies when required.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Previous kitchen or hospitality experience is advantageous but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £12.25–£13.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted meals during shifts.
  • Employee discounts at PizzaExpress restaurants.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive, inclusive workplace with opportunities for career progression.
