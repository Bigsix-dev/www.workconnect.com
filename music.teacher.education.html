Music Teacher

Job Description

Chelmsford Schools are dedicated to providing a broad and balanced education that encourages creativity, confidence, and academic achievement. Schools across Chelmsford are seeking a talented and enthusiastic Music Teacher to inspire students through music education and performance. In this role, you will deliver engaging lessons, develop students' musical abilities, and encourage participation in performances, ensembles, and extracurricular activities.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and deliver engaging Music lessons in accordance with the national curriculum.
  • Teach music theory, performance, composition, listening skills, and appreciation.
  • Support students in developing vocal and instrumental performance skills.
  • Prepare lesson plans, assessments, and teaching resources.
  • Assess student progress and provide constructive feedback.
  • Organise concerts, school performances, choirs, bands, and musical productions.
  • Maintain musical instruments, equipment, and classroom resources.
  • Participate in school events, parents' evenings, and professional development activities.

Requirements

  • Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) is required.
  • Bachelor's degree in Music or a related subject.
  • Previous experience teaching Music at secondary school level is desirable.
  • Strong practical knowledge of music theory and performance.
  • Ability to play one or more musical instruments proficiently.
  • Excellent classroom management and communication skills.
  • Commitment to safeguarding and promoting student wellbeing.
  • Strong organisational, planning, and assessment skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 32.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £34,000–£48,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive teacher salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme.
  • Paid school holidays and generous annual leave.
  • Professional development and continuous training opportunities.
  • Access to music facilities, instruments, and teaching resources.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Career progression opportunities within education.
  • Supportive and collaborative teaching environment.
