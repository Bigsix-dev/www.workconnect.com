Door Supervisor

Job Description

Event Security Company provides professional security services for events, entertainment venues, hospitality businesses, and public gatherings. The company is seeking responsible Door Supervisors to help maintain a safe and welcoming environment for customers, staff, and visitors. You will control access, monitor venue activity, prevent disorder, and respond professionally to security incidents.

Responsibilities

  • Control access to venues and check visitors in accordance with site procedures.
  • Monitor entrances, exits, and public areas for potential security risks.
  • Check identification and entry requirements where required.
  • Help prevent disorder, antisocial behaviour, and unauthorised access.
  • Respond calmly and professionally to incidents and emergencies.
  • Work closely with venue management, event staff, and emergency services when required.
  • Maintain accurate records of incidents and security-related activities.
  • Provide a professional and approachable security presence throughout events.

Requirements

  • Valid SIA Door Supervisor licence.
  • Previous experience in event, hospitality, or venue security is desirable.
  • Good communication and conflict management skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional under pressure.
  • Good observation and situational awareness.
  • Professional appearance and responsible attitude.
  • Ability to work flexible hours, including evenings and weekends.
  • Reliable, punctual, and able to work effectively as part of a security team.

Working Hours

  • Part-time or full-time position.
  • 20–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts, including evenings and weekends.

Salary

  • £12.50–£15.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly rate.
  • Flexible working opportunities.
  • Access to security and professional development training.
  • Opportunities to work at a variety of events and venues.
  • Uniform and required security equipment.
  • Opportunities to gain additional industry experience.
  • Potential for regular event and venue assignments.
  • Supportive security team environment.
