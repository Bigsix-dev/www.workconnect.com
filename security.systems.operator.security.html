Security Systems Operator

Job Description

Security Technology Company provides electronic security and monitoring solutions to commercial, industrial, and public-sector organisations. The company is seeking a technically minded Security Systems Operator to monitor and operate security technology, identify system alerts, and support the effective operation of CCTV, access control, alarm, and related systems. You will work closely with security teams, technical engineers, and clients to maintain effective security operations.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor CCTV, access control, intruder alarms, and other security systems.
  • Operate security monitoring platforms and respond to system alerts.
  • Identify faults, unusual activity, and potential security incidents.
  • Escalate incidents and technical issues to appropriate personnel.
  • Maintain accurate records of system activity, incidents, and responses.
  • Assist engineers and technical teams with system checks and troubleshooting.
  • Communicate with clients and security personnel regarding system events.
  • Ensure security systems are operated in accordance with established procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous experience operating CCTV, access control, alarm, or security monitoring systems is desirable.
  • Good understanding of electronic security systems and monitoring procedures.
  • Strong technical aptitude and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent attention to detail and situational awareness.
  • Good computer and communication skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and focused when responding to incidents.
  • Good administrative and report writing abilities.
  • Ability to work effectively independently and within a technical security team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working may include evenings, nights, weekends, and public holidays.

Salary

  • £28,000–£35,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive security technology salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Security systems and technology training.
  • Opportunities to gain specialist technical qualifications.
  • Modern technology-focused working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
