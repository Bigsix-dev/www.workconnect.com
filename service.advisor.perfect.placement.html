Service Advisor

Job Description

Perfect Placement is seeking a professional Service Advisor to provide excellent customer service and coordinate vehicle servicing and repair requirements. You will act as the main point of contact between customers and the workshop team, ensuring customer concerns are accurately recorded, work is arranged efficiently, and customers receive regular updates throughout the service process.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide a professional and friendly service.
  • Book vehicles into the workshop for servicing, maintenance, inspections, and repairs.
  • Listen to customer concerns and accurately record required work.
  • Prepare and explain estimates, repair costs, and service recommendations.
  • Coordinate with technicians and workshop staff to ensure work is completed efficiently.
  • Keep customers informed about vehicle progress, costs, and completion times.
  • Maintain accurate customer and vehicle records.
  • Process relevant service documentation and support customer follow-up activities.

Requirements

  • Previous automotive service advisor, dealership, or customer service experience is desirable.
  • Good understanding of vehicle servicing and repair processes.
  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Strong organisational and administrative abilities.
  • Good attention to detail and ability to manage multiple priorities.
  • Confident discussing technical information and service costs with customers.
  • Good computer skills and accurate record-keeping abilities.
  • Ability to work effectively with customers, technicians, and management.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday working schedule.
  • Saturday rota.
  • Full-time position.

Salary

  • Up to £32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive automotive service salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Automotive service and customer care training.
  • Professional dealership environment.
  • Opportunities to develop technical and customer service knowledge.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
