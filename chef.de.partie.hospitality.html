Chef de Partie

Job Description

Pontlands Park Hotel is a prestigious country house hotel in Essex, renowned for its luxury accommodation, award-winning dining, weddings, conferences, and private events. The hotel is seeking a talented and passionate Chef de Partie to join its professional kitchen team. In this role, you will take responsibility for a designated section of the kitchen, preparing and presenting high-quality dishes while maintaining exceptional food safety, hygiene, and presentation standards. You will work closely with the Head Chef and Sous Chef to deliver outstanding culinary experiences for hotel guests and event attendees.

Responsibilities

  • Manage and oversee an assigned section of the kitchen during food service.
  • Prepare, cook, and present dishes to the hotel's quality and presentation standards.
  • Assist with menu preparation, recipe development, and daily food production.
  • Monitor food quality, portion control, and minimise food waste.
  • Supervise and support Commis Chefs and Kitchen Assistants when required.
  • Ensure kitchen equipment is used safely and maintained correctly.
  • Monitor stock levels and assist with ordering and stock rotation.
  • Comply with food hygiene regulations, HACCP procedures, and health and safety standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Chef de Partie or an experienced Commis Chef ready for progression.
  • Professional culinary skills with excellent attention to detail.
  • Knowledge of food safety, HACCP, and kitchen hygiene procedures.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced professional kitchen.
  • Excellent teamwork, communication, and organisational skills.
  • Passion for producing high-quality food and maintaining presentation standards.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Professional culinary qualifications are desirable but not essential.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40–45 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £15.00–£18.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Complimentary or discounted staff meals during shifts.
  • Professional chef training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts on hotel services.
  • Supportive and professional working environment.
  • Opportunities for career progression within a luxury hospitality setting.
