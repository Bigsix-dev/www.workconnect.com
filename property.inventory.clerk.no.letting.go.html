Property Inventory Clerk

Job Description

No Letting Go is seeking a reliable Property Inventory Clerk to carry out professional property inspections and prepare accurate inventory and condition reports. The role requires excellent attention to detail, strong written communication, and the ability to identify and document the condition of property fixtures, fittings, furnishings, and equipment.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct property inventories and inspection appointments.
  • Record the condition of rooms, fixtures, fittings, furnishings, and appliances.
  • Take clear and accurate photographic evidence where required.
  • Prepare detailed inventory and condition reports.
  • Record meter readings and relevant property information.
  • Complete check-in and check-out inspections professionally.
  • Communicate effectively with landlords, tenants, and property agents.
  • Submit completed reports within agreed deadlines.

Requirements

  • Strong attention to detail and observation skills.
  • Good written English and report-writing ability.
  • Professional communication and customer service skills.
  • Ability to work independently and manage appointments.
  • Reliable, organised, and punctual approach.
  • Comfortable using mobile devices and digital reporting systems.
  • Previous property, inventory, inspection, or customer service experience is desirable.
  • Ability to travel between properties as required.

Working Hours

  • Part-time or freelance position.
  • Appointments may be scheduled around property availability.
  • Flexible working arrangements may be available.

Salary

  • Salary not stated; dependent on assignments and working arrangement.

Benefits

  • Flexible freelance or part-time working opportunities.
  • Varied property inspection work.
  • Opportunity to develop property-sector experience.
  • Independent working environment.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
