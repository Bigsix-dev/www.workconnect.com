Security Receptionist

Job Description

Corporate Security Company provides professional security and front-of-house services to corporate offices and commercial facilities. The company is seeking a professional Security Receptionist to provide a welcoming reception service while maintaining strong access control and site security. You will greet visitors, manage entry procedures, monitor security systems, and assist employees and guests while ensuring company security policies are followed.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome and assist visitors, employees, contractors, and guests professionally.
  • Manage reception and building access procedures in accordance with site policies.
  • Verify visitor identification and maintain accurate visitor records.
  • Issue visitor passes and control access to restricted areas.
  • Monitor CCTV, alarms, access control systems, and other security equipment where required.
  • Respond appropriately to security incidents, emergencies, and unusual activity.
  • Maintain accurate reception, security, and incident records.
  • Provide professional customer service while maintaining a strong security presence.

Requirements

  • Previous reception, security, customer service, or front-of-house experience is desirable.
  • Valid SIA licence where required for the assigned duties.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Professional appearance and confident manner.
  • Strong attention to detail and organisational skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional when dealing with difficult situations.
  • Good computer and administrative skills.
  • Reliable, punctual, and able to work independently.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £26,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive corporate security salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Security and customer service training.
  • Professional corporate working environment.
  • Opportunities for additional security qualifications.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
