Higher Level Teaching Assistant

Job Description

Bridge Academy Trust is a collaborative education organisation committed to providing high-quality learning experiences and supporting schools to deliver excellent outcomes for children and young people. Bridge Academy Trust is seeking an experienced and enthusiastic Higher Level Teaching Assistant to support teaching delivery and provide advanced classroom support. In this role, you will lead learning activities, support pupils with their development, and work closely with teachers to create an engaging and effective learning environment.

Responsibilities

  • Lead classroom activities and support learning under teacher guidance.
  • Deliver planned lessons and learning sessions when required.
  • Provide targeted support to individuals and small groups of pupils.
  • Assist teachers with lesson preparation and classroom organisation.
  • Support pupils with additional educational needs and learning challenges.
  • Monitor pupil progress and provide feedback to teaching staff.
  • Support behaviour management and maintain a positive classroom environment.
  • Contribute to school activities, meetings, and professional development.

Requirements

  • Previous experience working as a Teaching Assistant or Higher Level Teaching Assistant.
  • Relevant Level 3 Teaching Assistant qualification or equivalent experience.
  • Strong understanding of classroom support and learning strategies.
  • Excellent communication and leadership skills.
  • Ability to work independently and support teaching staff effectively.
  • Strong organisational and planning abilities.
  • Knowledge of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Commitment to supporting children's educational development.

Working Hours

  • Part-time position.
  • 25 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £26,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme or workplace pension.
  • Professional development and training opportunities.
  • Supportive education team environment.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for career progression within Bridge Academy Trust.
  • Paid annual leave and school holiday entitlement.
  • Rewarding role supporting pupil achievement.
