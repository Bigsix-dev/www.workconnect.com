Residential Surveyor

Job Description

GCB Recruitment is seeking an experienced Residential Surveyor to provide professional surveying and property assessment services. The successful candidate will inspect residential properties, assess their condition and value, prepare detailed reports, and provide clients with clear professional advice.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct residential property surveys and inspections.
  • Assess the condition and construction of residential properties.
  • Identify defects, maintenance requirements, and potential risks.
  • Prepare accurate and comprehensive survey reports.
  • Provide professional advice to clients based on survey findings.
  • Carry out property valuations where required within the scope of the role.
  • Maintain accurate records of inspections and survey findings.
  • Communicate findings clearly with clients and relevant stakeholders.
  • Manage survey appointments and workload effectively.
  • Maintain professional standards and comply with relevant surveying requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Residential Surveyor or in a related property surveying role.
  • Strong knowledge of residential property construction and defects.
  • Excellent inspection, reporting, and analytical skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • Excellent attention to detail.
  • Ability to manage an independent workload effectively.
  • Professional approach to client service.
  • Relevant surveying qualifications or professional experience are desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Remote working arrangement.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £50,000–£60,000 per year.
  • OTE available.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Remote working opportunity.
  • Professional surveying environment.
  • Opportunities for professional development.
  • Career progression opportunities.
