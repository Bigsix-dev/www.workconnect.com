Sales Assistant

Footasylum PLC (Public Limited Company) is a leading UK fashion and sportswear retailer specialising in trainers, footwear, clothing, and accessories from globally recognised brands. Footasylum is looking for energetic and customer-focused Sales Assistants to deliver outstanding customer service, support daily store operations, and help customers find products that match their style and needs.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide exceptional customer service.
  • Assist customers in selecting footwear, clothing, and accessories.
  • Operate tills and process payments accurately.
  • Maintain attractive product displays and visual merchandising standards.
  • Replenish stock and organise the sales floor.
  • Promote current offers, new arrivals, and loyalty programmes.
  • Support stock deliveries and inventory management.
  • Follow company policies and health and safety procedures.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Passion for fashion, trainers, and sportswear.
  • Friendly, confident, and enthusiastic attitude.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced retail environment.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Flexible availability including weekends and bank holidays.
  • Previous retail experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns including evenings and weekends.

Salary

  • £12.25–£13.40 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discount on Footasylum products.
  • Training and career progression opportunities.
  • Performance incentive programmes.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing support.
  • Supportive and inclusive team environment.
