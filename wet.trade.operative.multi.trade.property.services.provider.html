Wet Trade Operative / Multi-Trade

Job Description

A Property Services Provider is seeking a skilled Wet Trade Operative / Multi-Trade professional to undertake property maintenance and refurbishment work across Chelmsford and Essex. The role requires strong wet trade skills together with the ability to complete additional maintenance tasks.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out plastering, rendering, screeding and other wet trade work.
  • Complete general property maintenance and refurbishment tasks.
  • Prepare surfaces and deliver high-quality finishes.
  • Identify defects and carry out appropriate repairs.
  • Work alongside other trades to complete projects efficiently.
  • Maintain safe and professional working practices.

Requirements

  • Strong experience in wet trades and property maintenance.
  • Additional multi-trade skills are advantageous.
  • Good practical workmanship and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work independently and manage a varied workload.
  • Reliable, professional and safety-conscious approach.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.

Salary

  • £35,000–£40,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary.
  • Varied property maintenance and refurbishment work.
  • Opportunity to use a broad range of trade skills.
  • Professional working environment.
