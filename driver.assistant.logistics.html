Driver Assistant

Job Description

Currys PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading retailers of electrical products and technology solutions, delivering appliances and electronics to customers nationwide. Currys is seeking a dependable and customer-focused Driver Assistant to support its home delivery operations. In this role, you will assist delivery drivers with loading, transporting, delivering, and installing products while ensuring customers receive a professional and friendly delivery experience.

Responsibilities

  • Assist the delivery driver with loading and unloading products safely.
  • Help deliver appliances, electronics, and furniture to customers' homes.
  • Carry large and heavy items into customer properties with care.
  • Support the installation and positioning of products where required.
  • Provide excellent customer service and answer basic delivery enquiries.
  • Complete delivery paperwork and obtain proof of delivery.
  • Maintain cleanliness and organisation of the delivery vehicle.
  • Follow company procedures and health and safety regulations at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous delivery, warehouse, or customer service experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Good physical fitness and ability to lift heavy items safely.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
  • Friendly, professional, and customer-focused attitude.
  • Reliable, punctual, and able to work efficiently.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced delivery environment.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and bank holidays when required.
  • Commitment to delivering excellent customer service.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including weekends and public holidays.

Salary

  • £12.40–£13.90 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts on Currys products and services.
  • Comprehensive training and development programmes.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing support.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Currys.
