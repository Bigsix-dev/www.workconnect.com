Multi-Trade Plumber

Job Description

MMP Consultancy is seeking a Multi-Trade Plumber to carry out plumbing, maintenance and associated property repair work across Chelmsford and Essex. The successful candidate will be able to work across several trades while maintaining high-quality standards.

Responsibilities

  • Complete plumbing installations, repairs and maintenance.
  • Carry out general multi-trade property maintenance.
  • Identify faults and complete appropriate repairs.
  • Support refurbishment and improvement projects.
  • Ensure all work complies with safety and quality requirements.
  • Maintain professional communication with customers and site teams.

Requirements

  • Experienced plumber with additional multi-trade skills.
  • Strong knowledge of plumbing systems and property maintenance.
  • Good practical and fault-finding abilities.
  • Ability to work independently and manage assigned jobs.
  • Reliable and committed approach to health, safety and quality.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours based on job and project requirements.

Salary

  • £35,000–£40,000 per year plus bonus.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary with bonus opportunity.
  • Varied plumbing and property maintenance work.
  • Opportunity to develop multi-trade experience.
  • Professional working environment.
