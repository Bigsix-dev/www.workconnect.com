After School Club Assistant

Job Description

Primary Schools Chelmsford are committed to providing safe, engaging, and supportive childcare services that help children develop confidence and social skills beyond the classroom. Schools across Chelmsford are seeking a caring and enthusiastic After School Club Assistant to support children after the school day. In this role, you will supervise activities, encourage positive behaviour, and create a welcoming environment where children can relax, learn, and enjoy their time.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise children attending the after school club.
  • Organise fun, educational, and creative activities.
  • Support children's social, emotional, and personal development.
  • Encourage positive behaviour and teamwork among children.
  • Assist with snacks, games, and structured activities.
  • Maintain a safe, clean, and welcoming club environment.
  • Communicate effectively with parents, carers, and school staff.
  • Follow safeguarding procedures and school policies at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous experience working with children in childcare, education, or youth settings is desirable.
  • Friendly, patient, and caring approach towards children.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to supervise children and manage behaviour positively.
  • Understanding of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Ability to create engaging activities suitable for primary-aged children.
  • Reliable and responsible approach to work.
  • Relevant childcare or education qualification is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Part-time position.
  • 20 hours per week.
  • Afternoon shifts after school hours, Monday to Friday.

Salary

  • £13–£15 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • School holiday periods and paid leave entitlement.
  • Training and professional development opportunities.
  • Supportive school team environment.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for progression within childcare and education services.
  • Flexible part-time working hours.
  • Rewarding role supporting children's development.
