Surveying Assistant

Job Description

Hays Construction & Property is seeking a motivated Surveying Assistant to support professional surveyors with property inspections, measurements, documentation, and general surveying activities. The successful candidate will assist with site work, prepare information and reports, maintain accurate records, and develop practical surveying experience.

Responsibilities

  • Assist surveyors with property inspections and site visits.
  • Take measurements and collect accurate property information.
  • Assist with preparing survey reports and technical documentation.
  • Record property condition, defects, and relevant site information.
  • Support surveyors with property assessments and project administration.
  • Maintain accurate project records, drawings, and documentation.
  • Liaise with contractors, clients, and other property professionals where required.
  • Assist with preparing quotations, schedules, and other project information.
  • Use relevant surveying and office software to record and manage information.
  • Support the wider surveying team with day-to-day activities.

Requirements

  • Previous surveying, construction, property, or technical experience is desirable.
  • Interest in developing a career in surveying or property.
  • Good numerical and analytical skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Good written and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Confident using Microsoft Office and relevant computer systems.
  • Willingness to learn and work as part of a professional surveying team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £30,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional construction and property environment.
  • Practical surveying experience and training.
  • Opportunities for professional development.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
