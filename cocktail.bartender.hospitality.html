Cocktail Bartender

Job Description

Cosy Club is hiring an experienced Cocktail Bartender to prepare high-quality beverages and deliver exceptional customer service. You will create signature cocktails, recommend drinks to guests, and maintain a clean and fully stocked bar.

Key Responsibilities

  • Prepare cocktails and beverages to company standards.
  • Provide friendly and efficient customer service.
  • Maintain cleanliness of the bar area.
  • Monitor stock levels and report shortages.
  • Verify customer age where required.
  • Follow food safety and licensing regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous bartending experience preferred.
  • Knowledge of cocktails and drink preparation.
  • Excellent customer service skills.
  • Ability to work under pressure.
  • Flexible working availability.

Working Hours

  • Full-time.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Evening, weekend, and holiday shifts.

Salary & Benefits

  • £13.50–£16.00 per hour.
  • Tips and service charge opportunities.
  • Employee discounts.
  • Career progression.
  • Professional training.
