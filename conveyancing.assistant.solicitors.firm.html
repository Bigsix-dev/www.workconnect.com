Conveyancing Assistant

Job Description

Solicitors' Firm is seeking a reliable Conveyancing Assistant to provide administrative and legal support to a busy property team. The successful candidate will assist with residential property transactions, maintain client files, prepare documentation, communicate with relevant parties, and help ensure matters progress smoothly.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to solicitors and conveyancers.
  • Assist with residential property sales and purchases.
  • Open, organise, and maintain accurate client files.
  • Prepare correspondence and routine conveyancing documentation.
  • Assist with property searches and document checks.
  • Communicate with clients, estate agents, lenders, and other solicitors.
  • Monitor outstanding information and follow up where required.
  • Update case management systems and maintain accurate records.
  • Assist with exchange and completion administration.
  • Provide general administrative support to the property department.

Requirements

  • Previous legal, conveyancing, or administrative experience is desirable.
  • Interest in residential property and conveyancing.
  • Excellent organisational and administrative skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Good written and verbal communication skills.
  • Confident using computers and office systems.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Professional and client-focused approach.
