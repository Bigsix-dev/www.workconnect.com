Fashion Retail Assistant

New Look Retailers Limited is a popular British fashion retailer offering stylish clothing, footwear, and accessories for women, men, and teens. New Look is seeking enthusiastic and customer-focused Fashion Retail Assistants to join its store team. In this role, you will support customers with styling advice, deliver excellent service, maintain attractive store displays, and help ensure the smooth day-to-day running of the shop. This position is ideal for individuals who are passionate about fashion, enjoy working with people, and thrive in a fast-paced retail environment.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide friendly, helpful service.
  • Assist customers in selecting fashion items, sizes, and styles.
  • Operate tills and process sales transactions accurately.
  • Replenish stock and maintain visually appealing displays.
  • Support fitting room operations and customer enquiries.
  • Assist with deliveries, unpacking, and stock organisation.
  • Promote current offers, promotions, and loyalty programmes.
  • Maintain cleanliness and organisation of the sales floor.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Passion for fashion and retail trends.
  • Friendly, approachable, and positive attitude.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced team environment.
  • Good attention to detail and organisational skills.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.
  • Previous retail experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns, including weekends and public holidays.

Salary

  • £12.30–£13.50 per hour.
  • Overtime opportunities may be available.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discount on New Look products.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Opportunities for progression within fashion retail.
