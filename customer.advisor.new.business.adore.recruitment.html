Customer Advisor – New Business

Job Description

Adore Recruitment is seeking a confident and customer-focused Customer Advisor – New Business to support new customer enquiries and help develop business opportunities. You will communicate with prospective customers, understand their requirements, provide suitable information, and help guide them through the next stages of the customer journey. The role is ideal for someone with strong communication skills and a proactive approach to customer service and sales.

Responsibilities

  • Respond to enquiries from prospective customers through telephone, email, and other channels.
  • Understand customer requirements and provide appropriate information about products and services.
  • Identify new business opportunities and support lead generation activities.
  • Build positive relationships with prospective and existing customers.
  • Follow up leads and enquiries to encourage successful conversions.
  • Maintain accurate customer and sales records on internal systems.
  • Work closely with sales and customer service teams to achieve business objectives.
  • Provide a professional and positive customer experience throughout the sales process.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service, sales, telesales, or business development experience is desirable.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Confident telephone manner and strong interpersonal skills.
  • Proactive and commercially aware approach.
  • Good organisation and time management abilities.
  • Strong customer relationship-building skills.
  • Good computer and administrative skills.
  • Ability to work towards individual and team targets.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday.
  • Office-based position.
  • Standard full-time working hours.

Salary

  • £26,000 per year.
  • Monthly commission available.

Benefits

  • Competitive basic salary with commission opportunity.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Customer service and sales training.
  • Supportive office environment.
  • Opportunities to develop sales and business development skills.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
