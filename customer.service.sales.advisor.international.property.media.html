Customer Service & Sales Advisor

Job Description

International Property Media is seeking a motivated Customer Service & Sales Advisor to support customers while identifying and developing new sales opportunities. You will communicate with clients, respond to enquiries, promote products and services, process customer information, and maintain strong business relationships. The role is suited to someone who combines excellent customer service with confidence and enthusiasm for sales.

Responsibilities

  • Respond promptly and professionally to customer and client enquiries.
  • Provide information about products, services, packages, and commercial opportunities.
  • Identify potential sales opportunities through customer conversations and enquiries.
  • Promote relevant products and services to new and existing customers.
  • Build and maintain positive relationships with clients and customers.
  • Follow up leads, enquiries, and prospective customers to support sales activity.
  • Maintain accurate customer and sales records using internal systems.
  • Work closely with sales and other internal teams to achieve customer service and commercial objectives.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service, sales, account management, or commercial experience is desirable.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Confident and persuasive approach when speaking with customers and clients.
  • Strong relationship-building and interpersonal skills.
  • Customer-focused attitude combined with commercial awareness.
  • Strong organisation, time management, and follow-up skills.
  • Good computer and administrative abilities.
  • Motivated, proactive, and able to work effectively towards sales objectives.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Standard business working hours.
  • Working hours may vary according to business requirements.

Salary

  • Commission available.

Benefits

  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Sales and customer service training.
  • Opportunities to develop commercial and account management skills.
  • Professional media and property industry environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
