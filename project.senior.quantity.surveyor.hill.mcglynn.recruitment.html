Project Senior Quantity Surveyor

Job Description

Hill McGlynn Recruitment is seeking a Project Senior Quantity Surveyor to support construction projects across Chelmsford and Essex. The role involves commercial management, cost control and contract administration throughout the project lifecycle.

Responsibilities

  • Manage the commercial aspects of construction projects.
  • Prepare and monitor project budgets, forecasts and cost reports.
  • Manage valuations, variations, payments and final accounts.
  • Monitor project costs and identify commercial risks.
  • Work closely with project managers, contractors and suppliers.
  • Support contract administration and commercial negotiations.

Requirements

  • Strong experience as a Quantity Surveyor within construction.
  • Good knowledge of cost control and commercial management.
  • Understanding of construction contracts and procurement processes.
  • Excellent analytical, negotiation and communication skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple commercial priorities effectively.

Working Hours

  • 37.5 hours per week.

Salary

  • £53,000–£67,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to manage the commercial side of major projects.
  • Competitive salary.
  • Professional development and career progression.
  • Opportunity to work across the Essex construction sector.
