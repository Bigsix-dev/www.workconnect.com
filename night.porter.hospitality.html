Night Porter

Job Description

Channels Hotel is a luxury boutique hotel dedicated to providing exceptional guest experiences through high-quality accommodation and outstanding hospitality services. The hotel is seeking a reliable and professional Night Porter to support overnight operations. In this role, you will assist guests during night hours, maintain hotel security, complete administrative tasks, and ensure the hotel remains clean, safe, and welcoming throughout the night.

Responsibilities

  • Provide excellent customer service and support to overnight guests.
  • Complete guest check-ins and check-outs during night hours.
  • Handle guest enquiries and requests professionally.
  • Monitor hotel security and report any concerns or incidents.
  • Carry out overnight cleaning duties in public areas when required.
  • Prepare reports, complete administrative tasks, and update hotel systems.
  • Assist with setting up breakfast areas and preparing for morning operations.
  • Follow hotel procedures, health and safety guidelines, and emergency protocols.

Requirements

  • Previous hotel, hospitality, or customer service experience is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to work independently during overnight shifts.
  • Strong attention to detail and problem-solving skills.
  • Reliable, professional, and calm approach under pressure.
  • Basic computer skills for handling hotel systems and records.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and public holidays.
  • Commitment to providing excellent guest service.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Night shifts including weekends and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £12.80–£14.20 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Hotel employee discounts.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive working environment.
  • Opportunities for career progression within the hospitality industry.
