Brain Injury Support Worker

Job Description

As a Brain Injury Support Worker at Active Care Group, you will provide person-centred care and rehabilitation support to individuals living with acquired or traumatic brain injuries. You will work closely with clients, their families, and multidisciplinary healthcare professionals to promote independence, enhance quality of life, and help service users achieve their personal goals in a safe and supportive environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Deliver compassionate, high-quality care tailored to each individual’s needs.
  • Assist clients with personal care, mobility, medication prompts, and daily living activities.
  • Support rehabilitation programmes and encourage independence where possible.
  • Monitor and report changes in clients’ physical, emotional, or behavioural wellbeing.
  • Maintain accurate care records and communicate effectively with healthcare teams.
  • Promote dignity, respect, and safeguarding at all times.
  • Build positive relationships with clients and their families while providing emotional support.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Typically 37.5 to 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns, including days, evenings, weekends, and occasional night shifts, depending on service requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in health and social care or supporting individuals with neurological conditions is desirable but not always essential.
  • A compassionate and patient-focused approach with excellent communication skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional in challenging situations.
  • Willingness to complete specialist training related to brain injury care and rehabilitation.
  • NVQ/QCF Level 2 or 3 in Health and Social Care is advantageous but not mandatory.
  • Right to work in the UK and satisfactory employment checks, including an enhanced DBS check.

What Active Care Group Offers

  • Competitive hourly pay with opportunities for overtime.
  • Comprehensive induction and specialist training programmes.
  • Career progression opportunities within one of the UK's leading complex care providers.
  • Company pension scheme and employee assistance programme.
  • Paid annual leave and access to wellbeing resources.
  • Supportive management and opportunities for continuous professional development.
  • Recognition and reward programmes for outstanding performance.
