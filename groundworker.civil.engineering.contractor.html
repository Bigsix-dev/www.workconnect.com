Groundworker

Job Description

Civil Engineering Contractor is recruiting an experienced Groundworker for civil engineering and construction projects in Chelmsford. The position involves carrying out groundwork activities including drainage, foundations, concreting, and general site preparation.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out general groundwork and civil engineering activities.
  • Install drainage systems, pipes, and related infrastructure.
  • Complete concrete works, foundations, and kerbing.
  • Assist with excavation and site preparation.
  • Use hand tools and relevant site equipment safely.
  • Work closely with plant operators and other site trades.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Groundworker.
  • Knowledge of drainage, concreting, and general groundwork.
  • Good understanding of construction site safety.
  • Reliable and physically capable of working on site.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a site team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Based in Chelmsford.

Salary

  • £18–£22 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly rate.
  • Ongoing civil engineering work.
  • Varied site-based projects.
  • Opportunities for further experience and development.
