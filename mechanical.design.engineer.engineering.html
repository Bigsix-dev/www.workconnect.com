Mechanical Design Engineer

Job Description

Product Development Company designs and develops engineered products and mechanical systems for a range of industrial and commercial applications. The company is seeking a Mechanical Design Engineer to create detailed mechanical designs, develop prototypes, and support products throughout the development process. You will work closely with engineering, manufacturing, and product teams to produce practical, reliable, and cost-effective mechanical solutions.

Responsibilities

  • Design and develop mechanical components, assemblies, and products.
  • Create detailed 2D drawings, 3D models, and engineering documentation.
  • Use computer-aided design software to develop and modify mechanical designs.
  • Carry out design calculations, tolerance assessments, and technical evaluations.
  • Develop prototypes and support product testing and validation activities.
  • Work closely with manufacturing teams to ensure designs are practical and cost-effective.
  • Investigate design issues and implement engineering improvements.
  • Maintain design records and ensure products meet relevant technical and quality requirements.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Mechanical Engineering, Product Design, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience in mechanical design or product development is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of mechanical engineering principles and design practices.
  • Proficiency with computer-aided design software.
  • Ability to interpret and produce detailed engineering drawings.
  • Understanding of manufacturing processes, materials, and engineering tolerances.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent attention to detail and communication abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £40,000–£60,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Workplace pension scheme.

  • Opportunities to contribute to new product development.
  • Modern design and engineering environment.
  • Career progression and professional development opportunities.
