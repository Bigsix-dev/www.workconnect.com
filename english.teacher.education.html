English Teacher

Job Description

Chelmsford Secondary Schools are committed to delivering outstanding education and inspiring students to achieve academic excellence. Schools across Chelmsford are seeking a passionate and dedicated English Teacher to deliver engaging lessons that develop students' reading, writing, communication, and critical thinking skills. In this role, you will create an inclusive learning environment, assess student progress, and help prepare learners for future education and employment.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and deliver high-quality English lessons in line with the national curriculum.
  • Teach language, literature, writing, and communication skills to secondary school students.
  • Assess student work and provide constructive feedback to support improvement.
  • Prepare lesson plans, teaching resources, and examination materials.
  • Monitor student progress and maintain accurate academic records.
  • Manage classroom behaviour and create a positive learning environment.
  • Communicate effectively with parents, carers, and school staff regarding student progress.
  • Participate in school meetings, extracurricular activities, and professional development.

Requirements

  • Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) is required.
  • Bachelor's degree in English or a related subject.
  • Previous experience teaching English at secondary school level is desirable.
  • Excellent classroom management and communication skills.
  • Strong knowledge of the secondary English curriculum.
  • Ability to motivate and inspire students of varying abilities.
  • Commitment to safeguarding and promoting student wellbeing.
  • Strong organisational and assessment skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 32.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £34,000–£50,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive teacher salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme.
  • Paid school holidays and generous annual leave.
  • Professional development and continuous training opportunities.
  • Supportive leadership and collaborative teaching environment.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Career progression opportunities within secondary education.
  • Access to school facilities and staff benefits.
