Asbestos Surveyor / Consultant

Job Description

Future Select Recruitment is looking for an Asbestos Surveyor / Consultant to carry out asbestos surveying and consultancy work in Chelmsford. The role involves identifying asbestos-containing materials, producing reports and supporting clients with asbestos management.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out asbestos management, refurbishment and demolition surveys.
  • Identify and assess potential asbestos-containing materials.
  • Collect samples in accordance with required procedures.
  • Prepare accurate survey reports and supporting documentation.
  • Provide professional advice to clients on asbestos management.
  • Maintain high standards of safety and regulatory compliance.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as an Asbestos Surveyor or Consultant.
  • Relevant asbestos surveying knowledge and qualifications.
  • Strong understanding of asbestos regulations and safe working practices.
  • Good report-writing and communication skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and professional approach.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.

Salary

  • £30,000–£40,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to work on varied asbestos surveying projects.
  • Professional development and training.
  • Career progression within asbestos consultancy.
  • Professional working environment.
