Bar Staff

Job Description

J D Wetherspoon PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's largest pub chains, operating hundreds of pubs and hotels across the UK and Ireland. Known for providing quality food, drinks, and excellent customer service at great value, Wetherspoon is seeking enthusiastic and reliable Bar Staff to join its hospitality team. In this role, you will prepare and serve beverages, assist customers, maintain cleanliness, and help create a welcoming atmosphere while ensuring responsible alcohol service and high standards of customer care.

Responsibilities

  • Prepare and serve alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages efficiently.
  • Provide friendly, professional, and attentive customer service.
  • Operate tills and process customer payments accurately.
  • Maintain cleanliness of the bar, seating areas, and service stations.
  • Restock beverages, glassware, and bar supplies throughout shifts.
  • Verify customer identification and comply with responsible alcohol service regulations.
  • Assist with food service when required.
  • Follow company policies, food hygiene standards, and health and safety procedures.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly, energetic, and professional attitude.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a busy bar environment.
  • Good teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Previous bar or hospitality experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 30–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings, weekends, and late-night service.

Salary

  • £12.25–£13.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Discounted food and drinks while on duty.
  • Employee discount programme.
  • Comprehensive training and career progression opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive, diverse, and inclusive working environment.
