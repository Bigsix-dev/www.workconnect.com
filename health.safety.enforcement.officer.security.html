Health & Safety Enforcement Officer

Job Description

Local Authority provides regulatory and public protection services designed to promote safe workplaces and protect employees, businesses, and members of the public. The authority is seeking a professional Health & Safety Enforcement Officer to carry out inspections, investigate workplace safety concerns, provide regulatory guidance, and take appropriate enforcement action where necessary. You will work with businesses, employees, council teams, and partner agencies to promote compliance with health and safety requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct health and safety inspections and assessments at relevant workplaces and premises.
  • Investigate complaints, accidents, incidents, and reported health and safety concerns.
  • Identify workplace hazards and assess compliance with applicable requirements.
  • Provide advice and guidance to businesses and duty holders on health and safety matters.
  • Take appropriate enforcement action in accordance with legislation and established procedures.
  • Prepare detailed inspection reports, investigation records, notices, and case documentation.
  • Work with other regulatory bodies, emergency services, and council departments where required.
  • Maintain accurate case records and contribute to local health and safety improvement initiatives.

Requirements

  • Relevant qualification or professional experience in occupational health and safety, environmental health, enforcement, or a related discipline.
  • Good understanding of workplace health and safety legislation and enforcement principles.
  • Strong inspection, investigation, and analytical skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication abilities.
  • Strong attention to detail and professional judgement.
  • Ability to deal confidently and professionally with businesses and members of the public.
  • Good case management and report writing skills.
  • Ability to work independently and collaborate effectively with partner organisations.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule, with occasional flexibility for inspections and operational requirements.

Salary

  • £32,000–£40,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive local authority salary.
  • Local government pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Health and safety enforcement training.
  • Professional development and specialist qualification opportunities.
  • Stable public-sector working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
