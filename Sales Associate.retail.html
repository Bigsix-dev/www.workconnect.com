Sales Associate

JD Sports Fashion plc (JD Sports) is a leading British sports fashion retailer, offering branded footwear, apparel, and accessories from top global sportswear companies. JD Sports is seeking enthusiastic and customer-focused Sales Associates to join its retail team. In this role, you will assist customers, deliver excellent service, maintain store presentation standards, and support sales targets. This position is ideal for individuals who are passionate about sports fashion, enjoy working in a fast-paced environment, and take pride in delivering great customer experiences.

Responsibilities

  • Provide friendly and professional customer service at all times.
  • Assist customers in selecting footwear, apparel, and accessories.
  • Operate tills accurately and process sales transactions.
  • Replenish stock and maintain visually appealing product displays.
  • Support store merchandising and promotional campaigns.
  • Keep the sales floor, stockroom, and fitting rooms clean and organised.
  • Work towards individual and team sales targets.
  • Follow company policies, procedures, and health and safety guidelines.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Passion for sports, fashion, and retail.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced team environment.
  • Good numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Target-driven and motivated attitude.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and holidays.
  • Previous retail or sales experience is an advantage but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including weekends and public holidays.

Salary

  • £12.25–£13.50 per hour.
  • Additional commission or performance-based incentives may be available.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discount on JD Sports products.
  • Performance-based incentives and bonuses.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Supportive and fast-paced working environment.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
