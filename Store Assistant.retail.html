Store Assistant

Next PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading fashion and homeware retailers, offering stylish clothing, footwear, accessories, and home products. Next is looking for motivated and customer-focused Store Assistants to join its retail team. In this role, you will provide outstanding customer service, maintain high merchandising standards, replenish stock, assist with deliveries, and ensure the store is clean, organised, and welcoming. This position is ideal for individuals who enjoy working in a fast-paced retail environment and are passionate about delivering an exceptional shopping experience.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide friendly, professional assistance.
  • Process purchases, exchanges, and refunds accurately at the till.
  • Replenish stock and maintain attractive product displays.
  • Assist with receiving, unpacking, and organising deliveries.
  • Keep the sales floor, fitting rooms, and stockroom clean and organised.
  • Help customers locate products and answer general enquiries.
  • Support promotional events and seasonal merchandising changes.
  • Follow company policies and health and safety procedures.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Friendly, reliable, and able to work as part of a team.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a busy retail environment.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Good attention to detail and organisational skills.
  • Flexible to work weekdays, evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.
  • Previous retail experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns, including weekends and public holidays.

Salary

  • £12.30–£13.80 per hour.
  • Overtime opportunities may be available.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Generous employee discount on Next products.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
