Property Litigation Lawyer

Job Description

Ellisons is seeking a skilled Property Litigation Lawyer to advise clients on a range of property-related disputes and legal matters. The successful candidate will manage cases from initial instruction through to resolution, provide clear legal advice, prepare documentation, and represent clients' interests professionally.

Responsibilities

  • Advise clients on residential and commercial property disputes.
  • Manage property litigation matters from instruction through to resolution.
  • Assess legal issues and develop appropriate case strategies.
  • Draft correspondence, legal documents, pleadings, and other case materials.
  • Conduct legal research and analyse relevant property law.
  • Negotiate settlements and participate in alternative dispute resolution where appropriate.
  • Prepare cases for court and other formal proceedings.
  • Maintain accurate case files and records.
  • Communicate regularly with clients and provide clear updates on case progress.
  • Work collaboratively with colleagues and other professional advisers.

Requirements

  • Qualified solicitor or lawyer with relevant property litigation experience.
  • Strong knowledge of property law and dispute resolution.
  • Excellent legal research, drafting, and analytical skills.
  • Strong negotiation and advocacy abilities.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong client relationship management skills.
  • Excellent attention to detail and case management abilities.
  • Ability to manage competing deadlines effectively.
