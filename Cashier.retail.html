Cashier

Sainsbury's PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's largest supermarket chains, serving millions of customers through its grocery, clothing, and convenience stores. Sainsbury's is seeking friendly, reliable, and customer-focused Cashiers to join its retail team. As a Cashier, you will play an important role in delivering excellent customer service by processing transactions accurately, assisting customers with enquiries, and ensuring every shopper enjoys a smooth and pleasant checkout experience. This position is ideal for individuals who enjoy interacting with people and working in a fast-paced retail environment.

Responsibilities

  • Operate checkout tills accurately and efficiently.
  • Process cash, card, contactless, and digital payments.
  • Provide friendly and professional customer service.
  • Handle refunds, exchanges, and returns in accordance with company policies.
  • Assist customers with product enquiries and store information.
  • Maintain a clean, tidy, and organised checkout area.
  • Promote loyalty schemes and current store promotions where appropriate.
  • Follow company security procedures and health and safety guidelines.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly, professional, and approachable attitude.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Good attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to work efficiently under pressure.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.
  • Previous cashier or retail experience is an advantage but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 36–39 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns, including weekends and public holidays.

Salary

  • £12.45–£13.50 per hour.
  • Additional overtime opportunities may be available.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discount at Sainsbury's and Argos.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
