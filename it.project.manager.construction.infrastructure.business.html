IT Project Manager

Job Description

A leading construction and infrastructure business in Chelmsford is seeking an experienced IT Project Manager to lead technology projects from planning through to successful delivery. The role involves coordinating teams, managing project schedules and budgets, and ensuring technology initiatives support wider business objectives.

Responsibilities

  • Plan, manage and deliver IT projects from initiation to completion.
  • Develop project plans, timelines, budgets and delivery milestones.
  • Coordinate internal teams, suppliers and external technology partners.
  • Monitor project progress and manage risks, issues and dependencies.
  • Provide regular project updates to senior stakeholders.
  • Ensure projects are delivered within agreed scope, budget and timescales.

Requirements

  • Previous experience managing IT or technology projects.
  • Strong project planning, organisation and communication skills.
  • Experience working with multiple stakeholders and technical teams.
  • Ability to manage competing priorities and project risks.
  • Strong understanding of project delivery methodologies and best practices.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Standard professional working hours, Monday to Friday.

Salary

  • £50,000–£70,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to work on major technology projects.
  • Professional development and career progression opportunities.
  • Collaborative working environment.
  • Opportunity to contribute to business-wide digital transformation.
