Field Service Engineer

Job Description

Engineering Services Provider delivers specialist engineering support, installation services, and technical solutions to clients across various industries. The company is seeking an experienced and customer-focused Field Service Engineer to provide on-site technical support, maintain equipment, and resolve engineering issues. In this role, you will travel to customer locations, carry out inspections, complete repairs, and ensure systems operate safely and efficiently.

Responsibilities

  • Provide on-site engineering support, maintenance, and repair services.
  • Install, commission, and test equipment at customer locations.
  • Diagnose technical faults and implement effective solutions.
  • Carry out preventative maintenance and equipment inspections.
  • Prepare service reports and maintain accurate customer records.
  • Provide technical advice and support to customers.
  • Work closely with internal engineering teams to resolve complex issues.
  • Ensure all work complies with safety procedures and engineering standards.

Requirements

  • Engineering qualification in Mechanical, Electrical, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience as a Field Service Engineer or similar technical role.
  • Strong fault-finding and diagnostic skills.
  • Ability to install, repair, and maintain technical equipment.
  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Ability to work independently while travelling to customer sites.
  • Strong organisational and time management skills.
  • Full driving licence is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with occasional travel requirements.

Salary

  • £32,000–£48,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering salary.
  • Company vehicle or travel allowance where applicable.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Specialist technical training opportunities.
  • Supportive engineering team environment.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
