Teaching Assistant (KS2)

Job Description

Local Primary Academy is a welcoming and inclusive school committed to providing high-quality education and supporting pupils to achieve their full potential. The academy is seeking a dedicated Teaching Assistant (Key Stage 2) to support classroom learning and help pupils develop academically, socially, and emotionally. In this role, you will work alongside teachers to deliver engaging lessons, provide targeted learning support, and create a positive environment where children can succeed.

Responsibilities

  • Support teachers in delivering engaging Key Stage 2 lessons and activities.
  • Provide one-to-one and small-group learning support to pupils.
  • Assist pupils with literacy, numeracy, and classroom assignments.
  • Prepare learning resources and support classroom organisation.
  • Encourage positive behaviour, confidence, and independent learning.
  • Support pupils with additional educational needs where required.
  • Monitor pupil progress and provide feedback to teaching staff.
  • Promote safeguarding, inclusion, and pupil wellbeing.

Requirements

  • Previous experience working with children in a primary school environment is desirable.
  • Understanding of Key Stage 2 curriculum and learning support practices.
  • Patient, caring, and enthusiastic approach to supporting pupils.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with children and staff.
  • Knowledge of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Good organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Relevant Teaching Assistant qualification is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 32.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £22,000–£27,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • School holiday periods and paid leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Supportive school leadership team.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for progression within education.
  • Rewarding role supporting children's learning and development.
  • Inclusive and friendly working environment.
