CCTV Operator

Job Description

Security Services Company provides professional surveillance and security solutions to commercial, public, and private-sector clients. The company is seeking a focused and observant CCTV Operator to monitor surveillance systems and identify potential security threats. You will operate CCTV equipment, monitor activity, report incidents, and maintain accurate records while following security procedures and data protection requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor CCTV cameras and surveillance systems continuously during assigned shifts.
  • Identify suspicious activity, security risks, and potential incidents.
  • Report incidents promptly to security teams, management, or relevant authorities.
  • Monitor access points, public areas, and designated security zones.
  • Maintain accurate CCTV monitoring logs and incident records.
  • Review recorded footage when required for security investigations.
  • Operate surveillance and communication equipment safely and effectively.
  • Maintain confidentiality and comply with relevant security and data protection procedures.

Requirements

  • Valid SIA CCTV Operator licence.
  • Previous CCTV monitoring or control room experience is desirable.
  • Strong observation and concentration skills.
  • Good understanding of CCTV and surveillance systems.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to remain focused during extended monitoring periods.
  • Ability to respond calmly and accurately to incidents.
  • Strong attention to detail and confidentiality.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working schedule may apply.

Salary

  • £27,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive security salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • CCTV and control room training opportunities.
  • Access to professional security development programmes.
  • Modern surveillance and monitoring systems.
  • Career progression opportunities within security operations.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
