Commercial Property Solicitor

Job Description

Recruiter Specialists is seeking an experienced Commercial Property Solicitor to advise clients on a broad range of commercial property matters. The successful candidate will manage transactions, provide clear legal advice, negotiate documentation, and work closely with clients and other professional advisers to ensure matters progress efficiently.

Responsibilities

  • Advise clients on commercial property transactions and legal matters.
  • Handle acquisitions, disposals, leases, and related property transactions.
  • Draft, review, and negotiate commercial property documentation.
  • Conduct legal research and investigate property-related issues.
  • Review title documentation and identify potential risks.
  • Liaise with clients, agents, lenders, surveyors, and other professional advisers.
  • Manage transactions from initial instruction through to completion.
  • Maintain accurate case files and transaction records.
  • Provide clients with timely updates and practical legal advice.
  • Work collaboratively with colleagues to deliver high-quality legal services.

Requirements

  • Qualified solicitor with relevant commercial property experience.
  • Strong knowledge of commercial property law and transactions.
  • Excellent drafting, negotiation, and analytical skills.
  • Strong attention to detail.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong client relationship and business development skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple matters and competing deadlines.
  • Commercially aware and solutions-focused approach.
