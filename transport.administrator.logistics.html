Transport Administrator

Job Description

DHL Supply Chain Limited, part of DHL Group, is one of the world's leading logistics and supply chain providers, delivering transport, warehousing, and distribution solutions across multiple industries. DHL Supply Chain is seeking a highly organised Transport Administrator to support its transport operations. In this role, you will manage transport documentation, maintain operational records, coordinate with drivers and customers, and help ensure deliveries are completed efficiently while complying with company procedures.

Responsibilities

  • Prepare and maintain transport documentation and delivery records.
  • Support transport planners and coordinators with daily administrative tasks.
  • Communicate with drivers, customers, and warehouse teams regarding delivery schedules.
  • Update transport management systems with accurate operational information.
  • Process delivery paperwork, invoices, and proof of delivery documents.
  • Monitor transport activities and report delays or operational issues.
  • Prepare reports relating to transport performance and fleet operations.
  • Ensure compliance with company procedures, transport legislation, and health and safety standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in transport administration, logistics, or office administration.
  • Excellent organisational and administrative skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Good communication and customer service abilities.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel and Outlook.
  • Experience using transport management systems is desirable.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and work under pressure.
  • Professional, reliable, and able to work effectively within a team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with flexibility depending on operational requirements.

Salary

Benefits
