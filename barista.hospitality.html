Barista

Job Description

Starbucks Coffee Company (UK) Limited is one of the world's leading coffeehouse brands, known for its premium coffee, handcrafted beverages, and exceptional customer service. Starbucks is seeking enthusiastic and customer-focused Baristas to join its team. In this role, you will prepare handcrafted beverages, serve food, create meaningful customer connections, and help maintain a clean, welcoming café environment while delivering the Starbucks Experience to every customer.

Responsibilities

  • Prepare handcrafted coffee, tea, and specialty beverages to Starbucks standards.
  • Welcome customers and deliver outstanding customer service.
  • Take customer orders accurately and operate the point-of-sale (POS) system.
  • Serve food items and maintain attractive product displays.
  • Maintain cleanliness of the café, beverage stations, and seating areas.
  • Restock ingredients, cups, and café supplies throughout the shift.
  • Work collaboratively with team members during busy periods.
  • Follow food hygiene regulations, health and safety standards, and company procedures.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Friendly, positive, and energetic attitude.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced café environment.
  • Strong teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Attention to detail when preparing beverages.
  • Flexible availability, including mornings, evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Previous café or hospitality experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Comprehensive barista training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 30–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns including weekends and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £12.40–£13.90 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free drinks during shifts and food discounts.
  • Employee discounts at Starbucks stores.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive, inclusive, and welcoming working environment.
