Sales & Customer Service Advisor

Job Description

Group 1 Automotive is seeking a motivated Sales & Customer Service Advisor to provide an excellent customer experience while supporting vehicle sales and related services. You will build positive relationships with customers, respond to enquiries, arrange appointments, support sales activities, and help customers throughout their purchasing journey. The role combines strong customer service with a proactive and commercially focused approach.

Responsibilities

  • Provide professional customer service throughout the vehicle sales process.
  • Respond to customer enquiries by telephone, email, and in person.
  • Assist customers with vehicle, finance, product, and service-related enquiries.
  • Arrange customer appointments, test drives, and follow-up activities.
  • Support the sales team with customer administration and sales enquiries.
  • Maintain accurate customer records and update internal systems.
  • Identify appropriate sales opportunities and promote relevant products and services.
  • Follow up with prospective and existing customers to maintain strong relationships.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service, sales, retail, or automotive experience is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Confident and professional approach when dealing with customers.
  • Strong commercial awareness and customer-focused mindset.
  • Good organisation and attention to detail.
  • Ability to build rapport and develop lasting customer relationships.
  • Good computer and administrative skills.
  • Motivated, reliable, and able to work effectively as part of a sales team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours may include weekends depending on business requirements.
  • Flexible working schedule may apply according to dealership operations.

Salary

  • £26,436.80 basic salary.
  • £6,515 OTE.

Benefits

  • Competitive basic salary with OTE opportunity.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Automotive sales and customer service training.
  • Opportunities to develop professional sales skills.
  • Professional automotive working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
