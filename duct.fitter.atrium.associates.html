Duct Fitter

Job Description

Atrium Associates is looking for an experienced Duct Fitter to work on construction and mechanical installation projects in Chelmsford. The role involves installing and assembling ductwork systems while working safely and accurately alongside other site trades.

Responsibilities

  • Install and assemble ductwork systems on construction sites.
  • Read and work from drawings, specifications and installation plans.
  • Measure, cut and fit ductwork accurately.
  • Work with other trades to coordinate installation activities.
  • Inspect completed installations and address fitting issues.
  • Maintain safe working practices and a tidy work area.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Duct Fitter or similar role.
  • Good understanding of ductwork installation and construction processes.
  • Ability to read technical drawings and specifications.
  • Good practical skills and attention to detail.
  • Strong commitment to health and safety.

Working Hours

  • 7am–3:30pm.

Salary

  • £26–£27 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly rate.
  • Opportunity to work on construction projects in Chelmsford.
  • Professional site environment.
  • Opportunity to develop specialist ductwork experience.
