Access Control Officer

Job Description

Security Services Company provides access control, guarding, monitoring, and protective security services to commercial and industrial clients. The company is seeking a professional Access Control Officer to manage entry and exit procedures and help protect people, property, and confidential areas. You will verify identification, monitor access systems, maintain visitor records, and respond to security concerns in accordance with site procedures.

Responsibilities

  • Control access to buildings, sites, restricted areas, and designated facilities.
  • Verify identification, access cards, passes, and visitor authorisations.
  • Maintain accurate visitor, contractor, and vehicle access records.
  • Issue and manage temporary access passes where required.
  • Monitor access control systems, gates, doors, and entry points.
  • Identify and report unauthorised access attempts and security concerns.
  • Assist with emergency access and evacuation procedures.
  • Communicate professionally with employees, visitors, contractors, and site management.

Requirements

  • Valid SIA licence where required for the assigned duties.
  • Previous security, access control, reception, or customer service experience is desirable.
  • Good understanding of access control and site security procedures.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and observation skills.
  • Good computer and record-keeping abilities.
  • Ability to remain professional and calm when dealing with difficult situations.
  • Reliable, punctual, and able to work independently.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working may include evenings, nights, weekends, and public holidays.

Salary

  • £27,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive security salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Access control and security systems training.
  • Opportunities for additional security qualifications.
  • Professional working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
