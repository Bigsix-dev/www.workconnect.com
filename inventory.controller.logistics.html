Inventory Controller

Job Description

Culina Group Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading logistics and supply chain providers, delivering specialist warehousing, transport, and distribution services for major national and international brands. Culina Group is seeking an organised and analytical Inventory Controller to oversee inventory accuracy across warehouse operations. In this role, you will monitor stock levels, investigate inventory discrepancies, maintain accurate records, and support efficient supply chain operations while ensuring excellent service standards.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor inventory levels and maintain accurate stock records.
  • Conduct regular stock counts, audits, and inventory reconciliations.
  • Investigate stock discrepancies and implement corrective actions.
  • Update warehouse management systems with accurate inventory information.
  • Prepare inventory reports for warehouse management.
  • Work closely with warehouse, logistics, and transport teams to improve stock accuracy.
  • Support continuous improvement initiatives within warehouse operations.
  • Ensure compliance with company procedures and health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in inventory control, warehouse operations, or stock management.
  • Strong analytical skills and excellent attention to detail.
  • Experience using warehouse management or inventory control systems.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
  • Excellent organisational and problem-solving abilities.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to work accurately under pressure and meet deadlines.
  • Commitment to maintaining high standards of inventory accuracy.

Working Hours

  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with flexibility depending on operational requirements.

Salary

Benefits

  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Culina Group.
