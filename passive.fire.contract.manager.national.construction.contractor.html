Passive Fire Contract Manager

Job Description

National Construction Contractor is seeking an experienced Passive Fire Contract Manager to oversee passive fire protection projects across Chelmsford and Essex. The role involves managing contracts, coordinating teams and subcontractors, and ensuring projects are delivered safely, efficiently, and to the required standards.

Responsibilities

  • Manage passive fire protection contracts from mobilisation through completion.
  • Coordinate subcontractors, site teams, suppliers, and clients.
  • Monitor project progress, budgets, quality, and programme performance.
  • Ensure works comply with relevant fire safety and construction requirements.
  • Prepare project reports, valuations, and commercial documentation.
  • Identify and resolve project issues promptly.

Requirements

  • Previous experience managing passive fire or construction contracts.
  • Strong understanding of passive fire protection systems and processes.
  • Excellent communication and contract management skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and priorities.
  • Strong health and safety awareness.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Based across Chelmsford and Essex.

Salary

  • £55,000–£65,000 per year + package.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and package.
  • Opportunity to manage major construction projects.
  • Professional development opportunities.
  • Supportive working environment.
