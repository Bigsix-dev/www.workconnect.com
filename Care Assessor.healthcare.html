Care Assessor

Job Description

Infinite Healthcare Services Ltd is seeking a dedicated and detail-oriented Care Assessor to evaluate the care needs of clients and develop personalised support plans that promote independence, safety, and wellbeing. The successful candidate will conduct assessments, liaise with healthcare professionals and families, monitor care outcomes, and ensure services comply with relevant regulations and quality standards. This role requires excellent communication skills, sound judgement, and a compassionate approach to delivering person-centred care.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct comprehensive care needs assessments for new and existing clients.
  • Develop, review, and update personalised care plans.
  • Work closely with clients, families, and multidisciplinary teams to coordinate support.
  • Monitor the effectiveness of care services and recommend improvements where needed.
  • Maintain accurate records and ensure compliance with safeguarding and regulatory requirements.
  • Provide guidance and support to care staff regarding client needs and care delivery.
  • Participate in quality assurance activities and contribute to continuous service improvement.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in health and social care, care coordination, or assessment roles.
  • Strong understanding of person-centred care principles and relevant legislation.
  • Excellent organisational, communication, and report-writing skills.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with clients and healthcare professionals.
  • Proficiency in maintaining confidential records and using digital systems.
  • Relevant qualifications in Health and Social Care or a related field are desirable.
  • A valid UK driving licence may be advantageous, depending on service requirements.
