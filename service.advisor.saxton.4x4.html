Service Advisor

Job Description

Saxton 4x4 is seeking a professional Service Advisor to provide excellent customer service and coordinate vehicle servicing and maintenance requirements. You will act as the main point of contact between customers and the service team, explain vehicle work clearly, arrange appointments, and ensure customers receive regular updates throughout the service process.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide professional service throughout their vehicle visit.
  • Book and coordinate vehicle servicing, maintenance, inspections, and repairs.
  • Discuss customer requirements and accurately record vehicle concerns.
  • Provide customers with updates regarding vehicle progress, costs, and completion times.
  • Work closely with technicians and workshop staff to coordinate customer requirements.
  • Prepare and explain service estimates and invoices where required.
  • Maintain accurate customer and vehicle records.
  • Follow up with customers to ensure a positive service experience.

Requirements

  • Previous automotive service advisor, dealership, customer service, or vehicle maintenance experience is desirable.
  • Good understanding of vehicle servicing and maintenance is advantageous.
  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Confident and professional telephone manner.
  • Strong organisational and administrative abilities.
  • Good attention to detail and ability to manage multiple customer requirements.
  • Ability to work effectively with technicians, customers, and management.
  • Commercial awareness and a customer-focused approach.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday, 8:00am–5:00pm.
  • One Saturday in every three weeks.
  • Full-time working schedule.

Salary

  • £30,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive automotive service salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Automotive service and product training.
  • Professional dealership environment.
  • Opportunities to develop automotive customer service skills.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
