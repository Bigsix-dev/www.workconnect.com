Process Engineer

Job Description

Industrial Manufacturer produces engineered products and components for industrial and commercial markets. The company is seeking a Process Engineer to improve manufacturing processes, increase production efficiency, and maintain consistent product quality. You will work closely with production, quality, maintenance, and engineering teams to identify process improvements and implement practical manufacturing solutions.

Responsibilities

  • Develop, improve, and optimise manufacturing processes.
  • Analyse production data to identify inefficiencies and improvement opportunities.
  • Investigate process failures, defects, and production problems.
  • Implement process improvements to increase productivity, quality, and efficiency.
  • Develop and maintain process specifications, procedures, and technical documentation.
  • Work with production teams to improve manufacturing methods and workflows.
  • Support the introduction of new products and manufacturing processes.
  • Participate in continuous improvement and cost-reduction projects.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Process Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience in manufacturing or process engineering is desirable.
  • Good understanding of manufacturing processes and production systems.
  • Knowledge of continuous improvement and process optimisation techniques.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to analyse production data and identify trends.
  • Strong attention to detail and technical accuracy.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £38,000–£55,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Manufacturing and process improvement training.
  • Opportunities to work on efficiency and optimisation projects.
  • Professional engineering environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
