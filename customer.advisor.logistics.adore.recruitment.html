Customer Advisor – Logistics

Job Description

Adore Recruitment is seeking a professional Customer Advisor – Logistics to support customers and coordinate logistics-related enquiries. The successful candidate will communicate with customers, provide accurate delivery information, coordinate with internal teams, and ensure customer requirements are handled efficiently from enquiry through to completion.

Responsibilities

  • Handle customer enquiries relating to deliveries, collections, and logistics services.
  • Provide accurate information regarding orders, delivery schedules, and service requirements.
  • Coordinate with transport, warehouse, and operations teams.
  • Monitor delivery progress and keep customers updated.
  • Record customer requests and update internal systems accurately.
  • Resolve routine delivery and service issues professionally.
  • Escalate complex logistics problems to the appropriate department.
  • Process relevant customer and delivery documentation.
  • Maintain strong customer relationships and service standards.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service, logistics, or administration experience is desirable.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Good attention to detail and accurate administrative skills.
  • Confident using computer systems and Microsoft Office.
  • Ability to manage multiple enquiries and priorities.
  • Strong problem-solving and customer service skills.
  • Ability to work effectively within a busy logistics environment.

Benefits

  • Customer service and logistics experience.

  • Opportunities to develop logistics administration skills.
