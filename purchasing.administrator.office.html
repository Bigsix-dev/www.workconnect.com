Purchasing Administrator

Job Description

Wincanton PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading supply chain and logistics companies, providing warehousing, transport, and supply chain solutions for major businesses across various industries. Wincanton is seeking a highly organised Purchasing Administrator to support procurement activities and supplier management processes. In this role, you will process purchase orders, maintain supplier records, support purchasing teams, and ensure procurement activities are completed accurately and efficiently.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to the purchasing and procurement team.
  • Process purchase orders and maintain accurate procurement records.
  • Manage supplier information, invoices, and purchasing documentation.
  • Monitor order progress and communicate updates to internal teams.
  • Assist with supplier enquiries and resolve administrative issues.
  • Prepare purchasing reports, spreadsheets, and performance data.
  • Maintain accurate filing systems for procurement documents.
  • Ensure purchasing processes comply with company policies and procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in purchasing administration, procurement support, or office administration.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and numerical accuracy.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel, Word, and Outlook.
  • Experience using purchasing or enterprise resource planning systems is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Professional attitude with strong problem-solving abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard business hours.

Salary

  • £26,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts and reward programmes.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance support.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Wincanton.
