Security Control Room Operator

Job Description

Security Services Company provides professional security and monitoring services to commercial, industrial, and public-sector clients. The company is seeking a focused Security Control Room Operator to monitor security systems, respond to alarms, coordinate security teams, and maintain accurate records of incidents and activities. You will play a key role in identifying potential security risks and ensuring appropriate responses are initiated quickly.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor CCTV, access control, alarm, and other security systems.
  • Receive and assess security alerts, alarms, and incident reports.
  • Coordinate mobile security officers and response teams when required.
  • Monitor site activity and identify suspicious or unusual behaviour.
  • Respond to incidents in accordance with established security procedures.
  • Maintain accurate control room logs, incident reports, and records.
  • Communicate effectively with clients, security officers, emergency services, and management.
  • Ensure all control room equipment and communication systems are used correctly.

Requirements

  • Previous control room, CCTV, security, or monitoring experience is desirable.
  • Valid SIA licence where required for the assigned duties.
  • Good understanding of security procedures and incident management.
  • Strong observation and concentration skills.
  • Excellent communication and computer skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and make effective decisions under pressure.
  • Strong attention to detail and accurate record-keeping abilities.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a security operations team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working schedule, including nights, weekends, and public holidays where required.

Salary

  • £27,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive security operations salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • CCTV, control room, and security systems training.
  • Opportunities for additional security qualifications.
  • Modern security monitoring environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
