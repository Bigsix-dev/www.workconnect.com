Multi-Trade Plasterer

Job Description

18 Recruitment Ltd is looking for a skilled Multi-Trade Plasterer to carry out plastering and general property maintenance work in Chelmsford. The successful candidate will combine strong plastering skills with the ability to complete other related trades.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out plastering, patching and finishing work.
  • Complete general property maintenance and refurbishment tasks.
  • Prepare surfaces and ensure high-quality finishes.
  • Identify defects and complete appropriate repairs.
  • Work safely and maintain clean and organised work areas.
  • Coordinate with other trades and site teams when required.

Requirements

  • Proven experience as a plasterer.
  • Additional multi-trade maintenance skills.
  • High standard of practical workmanship and attention to detail.
  • Good understanding of property repair and refurbishment work.
  • Reliable and professional approach to work.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours based on project requirements.

Salary

  • £38,000–£40,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary.
  • Opportunity to work on varied property projects.
  • Use of multiple trade skills across refurbishment work.
  • Potential for long-term career development.
