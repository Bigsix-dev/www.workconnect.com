Early Years Educator

Job Description

Bright Horizons Nursery is a leading childcare provider dedicated to delivering high-quality early years education and care through nurturing, safe, and inspiring environments. Bright Horizons Nursery is seeking a passionate and dedicated Early Years Educator to support young children's development through creative activities, structured learning, and responsive care. In this role, you will encourage children's curiosity, support their individual needs, and help create a positive foundation for lifelong learning.

Responsibilities

  • Provide high-quality early years education and care for children.
  • Plan and deliver age-appropriate learning activities based on children's interests and development needs.
  • Support children's communication, physical, social, and emotional development.
  • Observe, assess, and record children's progress using early years frameworks.
  • Create engaging learning environments that encourage exploration and creativity.
  • Build strong relationships with children, parents, and colleagues.
  • Support daily routines including meals, personal care, and play sessions.
  • Follow safeguarding, health and safety, and childcare policies.

Requirements

  • Previous experience working in an early years, nursery, or childcare setting.
  • Level 3 Early Years Educator qualification or equivalent is desirable.
  • Strong understanding of the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) framework.
  • Caring, patient, and enthusiastic approach to working with children.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to create engaging and stimulating learning experiences.
  • Knowledge of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Commitment to supporting children's growth and development.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with flexible nursery shift patterns.

Salary

  • £25,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Childcare discounts for eligible employees.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
  • Supportive and inclusive nursery environment.
  • Excellent progression opportunities within Bright Horizons.
