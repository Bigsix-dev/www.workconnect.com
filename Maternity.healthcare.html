Maternity Support Worker

Job Overview

A Maternity Support Worker (MSW) plays an essential role in supporting midwives and the wider maternity team to provide safe, compassionate, and high-quality care to pregnant women, new mothers, and their babies. Working across antenatal, labour ward, postnatal, and community settings, you will help ensure patients receive excellent support throughout their maternity journey.

This role is ideal for individuals who are caring, empathetic, organised, and passionate about supporting families during pregnancy and childbirth.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist midwives with the care of mothers and newborn babies.
  • Monitor and record observations such as temperature, pulse, blood pressure, and respiration.
  • Support mothers with infant feeding, including breastfeeding and bottle-feeding guidance.
  • Help with personal care and mobility where required.
  • Prepare equipment and clinical areas for examinations and procedures.
  • Maintain accurate patient records and report concerns to registered staff.
  • Provide emotional reassurance and practical support to patients and their families.
  • Assist with newborn care, including weighing babies and performing routine tasks under supervision.
  • Ensure wards and clinical areas remain clean, safe, and well-stocked.
  • Follow infection prevention, safeguarding, and health and safety procedures at all times.

About You

The ideal candidate will have:

  • A caring and compassionate approach to patient care.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • The ability to remain calm and professional in busy healthcare environments.
  • Good teamwork skills and willingness to work alongside midwives and other healthcare professionals.
  • Basic literacy, numeracy, and IT skills for maintaining records.
  • Previous healthcare or care experience is beneficial but not always essential, as training is often provided.
  • A commitment to maintaining patient dignity, privacy, and confidentiality.
  • Flexibility to work shifts, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays if required.

Benefits

  • Competitive NHS salary with annual pay progression.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement plus bank holidays.
  • Access to the NHS Pension Scheme.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Support to gain additional healthcare qualifications and certifications.
  • Paid maternity, paternity, and family leave benefits.
  • Employee wellbeing programmes and occupational health services.
  • Access to staff discounts through NHS employee schemes.
  • Flexible working opportunities where service needs allow.
  • Opportunities to progress into roles such as Senior Maternity Support Worker, Assistant Practitioner, or Midwifery training pathways.
