Restaurant Team Member

Job Description

Bella Italia Restaurants Limited, trading as Bella Italia, is one of the United Kingdom's leading Italian restaurant chains, renowned for serving authentic Italian-inspired dishes in a warm and welcoming atmosphere. Bella Italia is seeking an enthusiastic and customer-focused Restaurant Team Member to join its team. In this role, you will provide outstanding customer service, serve food and beverages, maintain restaurant cleanliness, and work collaboratively with both front-of-house and kitchen teams to ensure guests enjoy an exceptional dining experience.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome guests warmly and provide excellent customer service throughout their visit.
  • Take food and drink orders accurately using the restaurant's point-of-sale (POS) system.
  • Serve meals and beverages promptly while maintaining high presentation standards.
  • Assist in preparing tables before service and clearing them after guests leave.
  • Maintain cleanliness of dining areas, service stations, and customer facilities.
  • Support kitchen and front-of-house colleagues during busy service periods.
  • Handle customer enquiries and resolve minor issues professionally.
  • Comply with food hygiene regulations, health and safety policies, and company procedures.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Friendly, professional, and positive attitude.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced restaurant environment.
  • Strong teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Good attention to detail and willingness to learn.
  • Previous hospitality or restaurant experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Commitment to delivering outstanding dining experiences.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 30–38 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £12.30–£13.70 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted meals during shifts.
  • Employee discounts across Bella Italia restaurants.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive workplace with opportunities for career progression.
