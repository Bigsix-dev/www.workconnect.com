IT Support Specialist

Job Description

Various Employers in Chelmsford are recruiting IT Support Specialists to provide dependable technical assistance to users and maintain day-to-day IT operations. The role requires strong troubleshooting skills and a customer-focused approach to technology support.

Responsibilities

  • Respond to technical support requests and resolve user issues.
  • Troubleshoot desktop, laptop, software and connectivity problems.
  • Install, configure and maintain IT hardware and applications.
  • Assist users with account, password and access issues.
  • Record incidents and maintain accurate support documentation.
  • Escalate technical problems that require specialist assistance.

Requirements

  • Experience in IT support or a related technical role.
  • Good understanding of common desktop technologies.
  • Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills.
  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Ability to prioritise tasks and work effectively under pressure.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours vary depending on the employer and specific position.

Salary

  • Around £21,000–£31,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunities to develop specialist IT support skills.
  • Technical training and professional development.
  • Potential career progression within IT support and infrastructure.
  • Experience working with a range of business technologies.
