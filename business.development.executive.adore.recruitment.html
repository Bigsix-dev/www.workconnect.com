Business Development Executive

Job Description

Adore Recruitment is seeking a driven Business Development Executive to identify new business opportunities, build client relationships, and contribute to continued company growth. The role requires strong communication, commercial awareness, lead generation skills, and the ability to develop relationships with prospective customers.

Responsibilities

  • Identify and develop new business opportunities.
  • Research prospective customers and generate qualified leads.
  • Contact potential clients by telephone, email, and other channels.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with prospective and existing customers.
  • Understand customer requirements and present appropriate solutions.
  • Arrange and conduct sales discussions and meetings.
  • Prepare quotations, proposals, and relevant sales documentation.
  • Maintain accurate records of leads, opportunities, and customer activity.
  • Follow up prospects and progress opportunities through the sales process.
  • Work towards agreed business development and revenue targets.

Requirements

  • Previous business development, sales, or account management experience is desirable.
  • Strong commercial awareness and sales skills.
  • Excellent communication and presentation abilities.
  • Confident telephone and written communication skills.
  • Strong negotiation and relationship-building abilities.
  • Self-motivated and target-driven approach.
  • Good organisational and time management skills.
  • Confident using CRM systems and standard office software.

Working Hours

  • Office-based position.
  • Monday to Friday, 8:00am–5:00pm.
  • Full-time position.

Salary

  • £26,000 per year.
  • Commission available.

Benefits

  • Competitive base salary with commission opportunity.
  • Professional office-based environment.
  • Sales and business development training.
  • Opportunities to develop commercial skills.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
