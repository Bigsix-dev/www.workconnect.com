Cyber Security Engineer

Job Description

Various Employers in Chelmsford are seeking Cyber Security Engineers to help protect business systems, networks and data from security threats. The role involves monitoring security environments, investigating incidents and supporting the implementation of effective security controls.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor IT systems and networks for potential security threats.
  • Investigate and respond to security incidents.
  • Support the implementation and maintenance of security controls.
  • Identify vulnerabilities and recommend appropriate improvements.
  • Assist with security assessments, audits and compliance activities.
  • Maintain security documentation and incident records.

Requirements

  • Experience in cyber security, information security or a related IT role.
  • Understanding of networks, operating systems and security technologies.
  • Strong analytical and incident investigation skills.
  • Good understanding of cyber security principles and best practices.
  • Excellent problem-solving and communication abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working arrangements and hours may vary depending on the employer.

Salary

  • Around £32,074–£64,349 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to develop specialist cyber security expertise.
  • Professional training and industry development opportunities.
  • Exposure to modern security tools and technologies.
  • Career progression within cyber security and information security.
