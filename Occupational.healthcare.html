Occupational Therapy Assistant

Job Overview

An Occupational Therapy Assistant at Broomfield Hospital works closely with qualified occupational therapists to help patients regain independence and improve their ability to carry out everyday activities. This role involves supporting rehabilitation programmes, assisting with therapeutic exercises, preparing treatment areas, and encouraging patients throughout their recovery journey.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist occupational therapists in delivering personalised rehabilitation plans.
  • Support patients with mobility, daily living skills, and therapeutic activities.
  • Prepare equipment and treatment areas for therapy sessions.
  • Observe and record patient progress, reporting updates to the clinical team.
  • Encourage patients to achieve rehabilitation goals safely and effectively.
  • Maintain accurate records and comply with hospital policies and health and safety standards.
  • Work collaboratively with multidisciplinary healthcare teams to provide high-quality patient care.

Requirements

  • A caring, patient-focused attitude and excellent communication skills.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a multidisciplinary team.
  • Good organisational and record-keeping abilities.
  • Previous experience in healthcare or rehabilitation is beneficial but not always essential, depending on the employer's requirements.
  • Commitment to maintaining confidentiality and delivering compassionate care.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary of £25,000–£31,000.
  • Full-time hours with opportunities for professional development.
  • Experience working in a supportive hospital environment alongside skilled healthcare professionals.
  • Access to training and career progression opportunities within rehabilitation and allied health services.
  • Opportunity to make a meaningful difference in helping patients recover and regain independence.
