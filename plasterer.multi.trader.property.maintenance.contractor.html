Plasterer / Multi-Trader

Requirements

  • Multi-skilled property maintenance background.
  • Good attention to detail and quality workmanship.
  • Ability to work independently and manage daily tasks.
  • Good communication and customer service skills.

  • Based in Chelmsford.
