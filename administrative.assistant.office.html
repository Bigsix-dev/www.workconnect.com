Administrative Assistant

Job Description

Essex County Council is one of the largest local authorities in the United Kingdom, providing essential public services and support to communities across Essex. Essex County Council is seeking a reliable and organised Administrative Assistant to support daily office operations. In this role, you will provide administrative support to teams, manage documents, coordinate communications, and assist with efficient service delivery while maintaining high standards of professionalism and accuracy.

Responsibilities

  • Provide general administrative support to departments and team members.
  • Manage emails, telephone enquiries, and internal communications.
  • Prepare, organise, and maintain documents, records, and reports.
  • Schedule meetings, appointments, and maintain calendars.
  • Process data entry tasks and update administrative systems.
  • Assist with preparing letters, reports, and official correspondence.
  • Support teams with filing, photocopying, and document management.
  • Ensure confidentiality and follow council policies and procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in administration, office support, or customer service is desirable.
  • Strong organisational and time management skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication abilities.
  • Good attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications including Word and Excel.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Professional approach with strong teamwork skills.
  • Commitment to providing excellent administrative support.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £25,000–£29,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Generous annual leave allowance.
  • Local government pension scheme.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Flexible working opportunities where available.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Essex County Council.
