Residential Care Worker

Job Description

Lyons Court Residential Care Home is seeking a compassionate and dependable Residential Care Worker to provide high-quality, person-centred support to residents in a safe and welcoming environment. The successful candidate will assist with daily living activities, promote independence and dignity, and contribute to residents’ physical, emotional, and social wellbeing while working collaboratively with the wider care team.

Key Responsibilities

  • Deliver personalised care and support in line with individual care plans.
  • Assist residents with personal care, mobility, nutrition, and medication where appropriate.
  • Encourage participation in social and recreational activities to enhance quality of life.
  • Monitor residents’ wellbeing and report any concerns or changes promptly.
  • Maintain accurate care records and ensure compliance with safeguarding and health and safety procedures.
  • Build positive relationships with residents, families, and colleagues while promoting a respectful and inclusive environment.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in a residential care, healthcare, or support worker role is desirable but not essential, as training may be provided.
  • A caring, patient, and professional attitude with excellent interpersonal skills.
  • Good written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to work flexible shifts, including weekends and holidays.
  • Understanding of safeguarding principles and a commitment to high-quality care.
  • Eligibility to work in the UK and willingness to undergo an enhanced DBS check.

What We Offer

  • Competitive hourly pay of £12.75–£14.50, depending on experience.
  • Full-time, stable employment with consistent working hours.
  • Paid induction and ongoing training with opportunities for career development.
  • Supportive management and a collaborative working environment.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement and workplace pension scheme.
  • Employee wellbeing initiatives and recognition programmes.
  • Opportunities to gain additional qualifications and progress within the care sector.
