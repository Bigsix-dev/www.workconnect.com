Production Engineer

Job Description

Electronics Manufacturer is a specialist manufacturing company focused on producing reliable and high-quality electronic products through advanced engineering and efficient production processes. The company is seeking a skilled Production Engineer to support manufacturing operations, improve production performance, and ensure products are manufactured to the highest quality standards. In this role, you will work closely with production teams, engineers, and quality specialists to optimise processes and resolve technical challenges.

Responsibilities

  • Support daily manufacturing operations and production engineering activities.
  • Improve production processes to increase efficiency, quality, and reliability.
  • Investigate production issues and implement effective technical solutions.
  • Develop and maintain manufacturing documentation, procedures, and work instructions.
  • Collaborate with production, design, and quality teams to improve products.
  • Support equipment maintenance, testing, and process improvements.
  • Analyse production data and recommend improvements.
  • Ensure manufacturing processes comply with safety and quality standards.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Electronics Engineering, or a related field.
  • Previous experience in production engineering or manufacturing is desirable.
  • Strong understanding of manufacturing processes and engineering principles.
  • Ability to troubleshoot technical and production issues.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Good knowledge of quality control and continuous improvement methods.
  • Strong communication and teamwork abilities.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced manufacturing environment.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £40,000–£55,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Professional engineering training and development opportunities.
  • Access to modern manufacturing technology and equipment.
  • Supportive production and engineering environment.
  • Career progression opportunities within manufacturing engineering.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
