Document Controller

Job Description

Mace Group Limited is a global consultancy and construction company delivering complex infrastructure, property, and programme management projects across multiple industries. Mace Group is seeking a highly organised and detail-oriented Document Controller to manage project documentation and ensure accurate document control throughout the project lifecycle. In this role, you will maintain document management systems, coordinate project documentation, and ensure all records are stored, distributed, and updated in accordance with company procedures and quality standards.

Responsibilities

  • Manage project documentation using electronic document management systems.
  • Receive, register, distribute, and archive project documents.
  • Maintain accurate document control registers and revision histories.
  • Ensure all project documentation complies with company standards and procedures.
  • Coordinate document reviews, approvals, and distribution to project stakeholders.
  • Support project teams with document retrieval and administrative assistance.
  • Prepare document status reports and monitor submission deadlines.
  • Maintain confidentiality and security of sensitive project information.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Document Controller or in project administration is desirable.
  • Excellent organisational and document management skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Experience using document management systems such as Aconex, Viewpoint, Asite, or similar platforms is an advantage.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to prioritise multiple tasks and meet project deadlines.
  • Professional approach with a commitment to maintaining quality standards.
