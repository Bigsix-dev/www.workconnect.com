Retail Assistant

TK Maxx (TJX UK) is one of the United Kingdom's leading off-price retailers, offering designer fashion, footwear, homeware, beauty products, and accessories from well-known brands at exceptional value. TK Maxx is looking for enthusiastic and customer-focused Retail Assistants to join its store team. In this role, you will deliver outstanding customer service, replenish stock, maintain attractive merchandise displays, and help create an enjoyable shopping experience in a fast-paced retail environment.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide excellent customer service.
  • Process purchases, refunds, and exchanges accurately.
  • Replenish stock and maintain attractive merchandise displays.
  • Assist with receiving and organising deliveries.
  • Keep the sales floor and stockroom clean and organised.
  • Support store merchandising and promotional activities.
  • Help prevent stock loss by following security procedures.
  • Follow company policies and health and safety guidelines.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly, reliable, and enthusiastic attitude.
  • Ability to work in a busy retail environment.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Good teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Flexible to work evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.
  • Previous retail experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns including weekends.

Salary

  • £12.35–£13.75 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discount.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
