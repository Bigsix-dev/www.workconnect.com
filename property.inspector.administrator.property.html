Property Inspector Administrator

Job Description

Residential Property Business is seeking an organised Property Inspector Administrator to support property inspection activities and provide efficient administrative assistance. The successful candidate will coordinate inspection appointments, maintain accurate property records, communicate with landlords, tenants, and contractors, and ensure inspection documentation is processed accurately and efficiently.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate property inspection appointments and schedules.
  • Communicate with landlords, tenants, property managers, and contractors.
  • Maintain accurate property and inspection records.
  • Process inspection reports and related documentation.
  • Record maintenance issues and follow up on outstanding actions.
  • Assist property inspectors with administrative requirements.
  • Update property management systems and databases.
  • Monitor inspection schedules and ensure appointments are completed on time.
  • Handle property-related enquiries professionally.
  • Support the wider property management team with administrative duties.

Requirements

  • Previous property administration or office experience is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Excellent attention to detail.
  • Confident using Microsoft Office and property management systems.
  • Good administrative and record-keeping skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple appointments and priorities.
  • Professional approach when dealing with landlords, tenants, and contractors.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Hybrid working arrangement.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • Up to £28,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Hybrid working opportunity.
  • Professional property environment.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Opportunity to develop property management and administration experience.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
