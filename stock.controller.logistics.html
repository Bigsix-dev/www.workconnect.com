Stock Controller

Job Description

B&M Distribution, part of B&M European Value Retail S.A., supports one of the United Kingdom's leading variety retailers by managing the movement and storage of stock across its nationwide distribution network. B&M Distribution is seeking an organised and analytical Stock Controller to oversee inventory accuracy and support efficient warehouse operations. In this role, you will monitor stock levels, investigate inventory discrepancies, maintain accurate records, and work closely with warehouse teams to ensure stock is available to meet operational demands.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor stock levels and maintain accurate inventory records.
  • Carry out regular stock counts, audits, and inventory reconciliations.
  • Investigate stock discrepancies and implement corrective actions.
  • Update warehouse management systems with inventory information.
  • Work closely with warehouse, purchasing, and distribution teams.
  • Prepare inventory reports for supervisors and management.
  • Ensure stock is stored correctly and follows company procedures.
  • Maintain compliance with company policies and health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in stock control, inventory management, or warehouse operations.
  • Strong attention to detail and excellent numerical skills.
  • Good organisational and analytical abilities.
  • Experience using warehouse management or inventory control systems.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
  • Strong communication and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Commitment to maintaining accurate inventory records and operational standards.

Working Hours

  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with flexibility based on operational requirements.
