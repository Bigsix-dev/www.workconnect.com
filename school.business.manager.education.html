School Business Manager

Job Description

Academy Trust is committed to supporting schools through effective leadership, efficient operations, and strong financial management. The trust is seeking an experienced and organised School Business Manager to oversee the administrative, financial, and operational functions of the school. In this role, you will work closely with senior leaders to manage resources, support strategic planning, and ensure the smooth running of school services.

Responsibilities

  • Manage the school's financial administration, budgets, and reporting processes.
  • Support senior leadership with strategic planning and resource management.
  • Oversee administrative, HR, facilities, and operational services.
  • Monitor purchasing, contracts, suppliers, and procurement activities.
  • Ensure compliance with financial regulations, policies, and academy trust procedures.
  • Manage office and support staff while promoting effective teamwork.
  • Support health and safety, premises management, and risk assessments.
  • Prepare reports and provide accurate information to school leaders and governors.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in school business management, finance, administration, or operations.
  • Strong knowledge of financial management and budgeting processes.
  • Excellent organisational, leadership, and problem-solving skills.
  • Experience managing staff and operational services.
  • Strong understanding of education administration and compliance requirements.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Ability to handle confidential information professionally.
  • Relevant qualification in business management, finance, or school administration is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school working hours.

Salary

  • £35,000–£45,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Local Government Pension Scheme.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Professional development and leadership training opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Supportive school leadership environment.
  • Opportunities for career progression within education management.
  • Access to professional networks and staff benefits.
