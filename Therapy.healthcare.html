Therapy Assistant

Job Overview

As a Therapy Assistant, you will work alongside physiotherapists, occupational therapists, and other healthcare professionals to support patients during their rehabilitation and recovery. You will help individuals regain independence, improve mobility, and achieve their treatment goals in a safe and supportive environment.

This role is ideal for someone who enjoys working directly with people, has strong communication skills, and is passionate about making a positive difference in patients' lives.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist therapists in delivering rehabilitation and treatment programmes.
  • Support patients with mobility exercises and daily living activities.
  • Prepare treatment rooms and therapy equipment before and after sessions.
  • Monitor and record patients' progress and report any concerns to qualified therapists.
  • Encourage and motivate patients to participate in therapy sessions.
  • Help maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
  • Ensure equipment is clean, safe, and properly stored.
  • Work collaboratively with multidisciplinary teams to provide high-quality care.
  • Follow health and safety procedures, infection prevention policies, and safeguarding guidelines.
  • Provide compassionate support to patients and their families throughout the recovery process.

About You

The ideal candidate will have:

  • A caring and empathetic approach to patient care.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • The ability to work effectively as part of a team.
  • Good organisational and time management abilities.
  • Basic IT skills for record keeping and communication.
  • A willingness to learn and follow clinical guidance.
  • The ability to remain calm and professional in a busy healthcare environment.
  • Previous experience in healthcare or rehabilitation is beneficial but not always essential, as training may be provided.
