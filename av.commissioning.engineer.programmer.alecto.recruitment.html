AV Commissioning Engineer – Programmer

Job Description

Alecto Recruitment is seeking an experienced AV Commissioning Engineer – Programmer to work on audio-visual technology projects in Chelmsford. The role involves commissioning, programming, testing and supporting sophisticated AV systems while working closely with project teams and clients.

Responsibilities

  • Commission and configure professional audio-visual systems.
  • Program and test AV control systems and associated equipment.
  • Diagnose and resolve technical issues during installation and commissioning.
  • Work closely with engineers, project managers and customers.
  • Carry out system testing and ensure installations meet project requirements.
  • Provide technical support and documentation following project completion.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in AV commissioning, programming or engineering.
  • Good understanding of professional audio-visual systems and technologies.
  • Strong technical troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to read technical documentation and system designs.
  • Good communication skills and a professional approach to customer service.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours may vary depending on project requirements.

Salary

  • £40,000–£45,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to work on varied AV technology projects.
  • Professional development opportunities.
  • Exposure to modern audio-visual systems and technologies.
  • Technical and career progression opportunities.
