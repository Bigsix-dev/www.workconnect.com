Security Guard

Job Description

Private Security Company provides professional guarding and protective security services to commercial, residential, industrial, and public-sector clients. The company is seeking a reliable Security Guard to protect people, property, and premises while maintaining a visible security presence. You will conduct patrols, monitor access points, respond to incidents, and ensure site security procedures are followed.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain a visible and professional security presence at assigned premises.
  • Monitor entrances, exits, restricted areas, and designated security zones.
  • Conduct regular internal and external patrols of the site.
  • Check identification, access passes, and visitor authorisations where required.
  • Monitor CCTV, alarms, and other security systems where applicable.
  • Identify and report suspicious activity, safety hazards, and security breaches.
  • Respond appropriately to incidents and follow emergency procedures.
  • Maintain accurate security logs, incident reports, and daily records.

Requirements

  • Valid SIA licence where required for the assigned duties.
  • Previous security, guarding, customer service, or similar experience is desirable.
  • Good observation and situational awareness skills.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal abilities.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional during incidents.
  • Strong attention to detail and reliability.
  • Ability to follow security procedures and instructions accurately.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a professional security team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working may include nights, weekends, and public holidays.

Salary

  • £25,000–£29,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive private security salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Security and emergency response training.
  • Opportunities for additional security qualifications.
  • Professional working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
