Complex Support Worker

Full Job Description

A Complex Support Worker provides specialist care and support to individuals with complex physical disabilities, learning disabilities, neurological conditions, or long-term health needs. The role focuses on helping clients live as independently and comfortably as possible while delivering high-quality, person-centred care in their own homes or supported living environments.

Key Responsibilities

  • Deliver personal care with dignity and respect.
  • Support individuals with complex healthcare needs, including mobility assistance and medication administration where trained.
  • Follow personalised care plans and risk assessments.
  • Assist with feeding, hydration, and nutrition requirements.
  • Monitor and record changes in health or behaviour and report concerns promptly.
  • Support clients with daily living activities, appointments, education, or community access.
  • Use specialist equipment such as hoists or mobility aids safely (following training).
  • Provide emotional support and build positive relationships with clients and their families.
  • Maintain accurate care records and comply with safeguarding and health and safety policies.
  • Work collaboratively with nurses, therapists, and other healthcare professionals.

About You

The ideal candidate should:

  • Have previous experience in care or support work, preferably with individuals who have complex needs.
  • Be compassionate, patient, and reliable.
  • Have excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Be able to remain calm under pressure and respond appropriately in emergencies.
  • Be willing to work flexible shifts, including evenings, weekends, and holidays if required.
  • Hold or be willing to obtain relevant care qualifications and training.
  • Have the legal right to work in the UK (where applicable).

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay of £15–£18 per hour.
  • Full-time, stable employment with regular shifts.
  • Paid induction and ongoing professional training.
  • Opportunities for career progression into senior support or healthcare roles.
  • Holiday entitlement and workplace pension scheme.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programmes.
  • Uniform and specialist training provided where required.
  • Supportive management team and collaborative working environment.
  • Experience working with advanced care techniques and specialist clinical support.
