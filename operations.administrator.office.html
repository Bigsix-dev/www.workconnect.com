Operations Administrator

Job Description

DHL Supply Chain Limited, part of the DHL Group, is a global leader in logistics and supply chain management, providing warehousing, transportation, and fulfilment services to businesses across multiple industries. DHL Supply Chain is seeking an organised and detail-oriented Operations Administrator to support the daily running of warehouse and logistics operations. In this role, you will maintain operational records, process documentation, coordinate with internal teams, and ensure administrative activities support efficient and compliant supply chain operations.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to warehouse and operations management teams.
  • Maintain accurate operational records, reports, and databases.
  • Process delivery paperwork, transport documentation, and inventory records.
  • Coordinate with warehouse, transport, and customer service teams.
  • Prepare operational reports, spreadsheets, and performance data.
  • Monitor stock movement records and update logistics systems.
  • Respond to internal and customer enquiries regarding operational activities.
  • Ensure compliance with company procedures, health and safety regulations, and data protection requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in operations administration, logistics administration, or office administration.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel, Word, and Outlook.
  • Experience using warehouse management or logistics systems is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced operational environment.
  • Professional attitude with strong problem-solving abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with occasional flexibility depending on operational requirements.

Salary

  • £27,000–£33,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts and wellbeing programmes.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Access to global learning and development resources.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within DHL Supply Chain.
