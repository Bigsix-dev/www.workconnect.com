Senior Software Engineer

Job Description

Various Employers in Chelmsford are seeking experienced Senior Software Engineers to design, develop and maintain high-quality software solutions. The role involves providing technical expertise, supporting development teams and contributing to the delivery of reliable and scalable systems.

Responsibilities

  • Review, improve and maintain existing codebases.

  • Participate in architecture, design and code review discussions.
  • Investigate complex technical issues and implement effective solutions.

  • Strong professional experience in software development.
  • Excellent programming and software engineering skills.
  • Good understanding of software architecture and development practices.
  • Strong problem-solving and analytical abilities.
  • Ability to mentor colleagues and work effectively within development teams.
