IT Project Manager

Job Description

Robertson Sumner is looking for an experienced IT Project Manager to oversee technology projects in Chelmsford. The successful candidate will be responsible for coordinating project teams, managing delivery plans and ensuring technology projects achieve their intended outcomes.

Responsibilities

  • Manage IT projects through all stages of the project lifecycle.
  • Define project objectives, deliverables and implementation plans.
  • Coordinate technical teams and business stakeholders.
  • Track project budgets, schedules, risks and dependencies.
  • Prepare project reports and communicate progress to stakeholders.
  • Support the successful implementation of new technology solutions.

Requirements

  • Proven experience in an IT Project Manager or similar role.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Strong organisational and problem-solving abilities.
  • Experience managing projects involving technical teams.
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and deadlines effectively.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £55,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Career development opportunities.
  • Exposure to varied IT projects and technologies.
  • Professional and collaborative working environment.
