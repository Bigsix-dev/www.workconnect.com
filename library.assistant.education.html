Library Assistant

Job Description

School Library Service is dedicated to supporting schools by providing access to high-quality learning resources, promoting reading, and encouraging a love of learning among students. The service is seeking a friendly and organised Library Assistant to support the daily operation of school libraries. In this role, you will assist students and staff, manage library resources, support reading activities, and help create an engaging learning environment.

Responsibilities

  • Assist students and staff with library enquiries and resource requests.
  • Support the organisation, cataloguing, and maintenance of library materials.
  • Manage book loans, returns, and library records.
  • Help students develop research, reading, and information skills.
  • Support reading programmes, library events, and literacy activities.
  • Maintain a welcoming, safe, and organised library environment.
  • Assist with ordering and processing new books and educational resources.
  • Work with teachers to support classroom learning through library resources.

Requirements

  • Previous experience working in a library, school, education, or customer service environment is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Friendly and approachable attitude when working with students.
  • Strong organisational and administrative abilities.
  • Good attention to detail and record-keeping skills.
  • Ability to encourage reading and support student learning.
  • Basic knowledge of library systems and computer applications.
  • Commitment to safeguarding and supporting student wellbeing.

Working Hours

  • Part-time position.
  • 25 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £20,000–£25,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Local Government Pension Scheme.
  • School holiday periods and paid leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Supportive education environment.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for career progression within library and education services.
  • Rewarding role supporting student learning and literacy.
