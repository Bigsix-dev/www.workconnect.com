Security Officer

Job Description

Mitie Security provides professional security services to commercial, public, and private-sector organisations. The company is seeking a reliable and professional Security Officer to protect people, property, buildings, and valuable assets. You will monitor premises, control access, respond to incidents, conduct security patrols, and maintain a safe and secure environment while providing a professional service to clients and visitors.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor and protect assigned premises, property, equipment, and personnel.
  • Conduct regular internal and external security patrols.
  • Control access to buildings and verify visitors, staff, and contractors where required.
  • Monitor security systems, alarms, and access control equipment.
  • Respond appropriately to security incidents, emergencies, and suspicious activity.
  • Maintain accurate security logs, incident reports, and daily records.
  • Follow site security procedures and company policies at all times.
  • Provide professional assistance and guidance to visitors and members of the public.

Requirements

  • Valid Security Industry Authority (SIA) licence or willingness to obtain the required licence.
  • Previous security experience is desirable but not always essential.
  • Good observation and situational awareness skills.
  • Professional appearance and a responsible approach to security duties.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional during incidents.
  • Good written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to follow procedures and work independently.
  • Good reliability, punctuality, and attention to detail.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working schedule may apply.

Salary

  • £28,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive security salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Security and professional training opportunities.
  • Uniform and required work equipment.
  • Opportunities for additional training and qualifications.
  • Career progression opportunities within the security sector.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
