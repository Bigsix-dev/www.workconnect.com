Security Operations Manager

Job Description

Security Services Company provides professional guarding, monitoring, and protective security services to commercial, industrial, and public-sector clients. The company is seeking an experienced Security Operations Manager to oversee daily security operations, manage security teams, maintain client relationships, and ensure high standards of service delivery. You will be responsible for operational planning, performance management, incident response, compliance, and continuous improvement across assigned contracts or sites.

Responsibilities

  • Manage and oversee day-to-day security operations across assigned sites and contracts.
  • Lead, supervise, and support security supervisors, officers, and operational staff.
  • Develop staffing plans, duty schedules, operational procedures, and deployment arrangements.
  • Monitor security performance and ensure contractual and service standards are maintained.
  • Manage security incidents and coordinate appropriate responses and escalations.
  • Maintain strong working relationships with clients, stakeholders, and partner organisations.
  • Review operational reports, audits, incidents, and performance data to identify improvements.
  • Support recruitment, training, performance management, compliance, and professional development of security personnel.

Requirements

  • Significant experience in security operations, supervision, or management.
  • Valid SIA licence where required for the role.
  • Strong knowledge of security operations, risk management, and incident response.
  • Excellent leadership and people management skills.
  • Strong commercial, organisational, and contract management abilities.
  • Excellent communication and client relationship skills.
  • Strong decision-making, analytical, and problem-solving abilities.
  • Good understanding of security compliance, auditing, and performance management.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule, with occasional out-of-hours availability for operational incidents.

Salary

  • £40,000–£50,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive security management salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Management and leadership training.
  • Opportunities for professional security qualifications.
  • Senior role within a professional security operations environment.
  • Career progression into senior security management positions.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
