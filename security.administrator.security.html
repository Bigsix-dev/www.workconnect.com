Security Administrator

Job Description

Security Services Company provides professional security operations and support services to commercial and industrial clients. The company is seeking an organised Security Administrator to provide administrative support to security management and operational teams. You will maintain records, coordinate schedules, process documentation, support compliance activities, and help ensure the smooth administration of security operations.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to security managers and operational teams.
  • Maintain accurate security records, personnel files, and operational documentation.
  • Assist with staff scheduling, shift allocation, and attendance records.
  • Prepare reports, spreadsheets, correspondence, and security documentation.
  • Maintain records relating to training, licences, certifications, and compliance.
  • Process incident reports and ensure relevant documentation is completed.
  • Communicate with security officers, clients, suppliers, and management.
  • Support audits, compliance checks, meetings, and other security administration activities.

Requirements

  • Previous administrative, security, facilities, or office support experience is desirable.
  • Strong organisational and time management skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication abilities.
  • Good Microsoft Office and computer skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accurate data entry skills.
  • Ability to handle confidential information professionally.
  • Good problem-solving and prioritisation skills.
  • Ability to work independently and support a busy security operations team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £25,000–£29,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive security administration salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Administrative and security operations training.
  • Opportunities to develop specialist security administration skills.
  • Professional office-based working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
