Plumber Multi Trader

Job Description

Emberstone Recruitment is seeking a skilled Plumber Multi Trader to carry out plumbing, maintenance, and general property repair works. The successful candidate will use their plumbing expertise alongside broader multi-trade skills to maintain properties, resolve repairs, and provide a high-quality service to residents and customers.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out plumbing repairs and maintenance across residential properties.
  • Diagnose and resolve plumbing faults and maintenance issues.
  • Complete multi-trade repairs including carpentry, plastering, tiling, and decorating where required.
  • Undertake planned and reactive maintenance works.
  • Inspect properties and identify additional repair requirements.
  • Complete work efficiently and to a high standard.
  • Communicate professionally with residents and customers.
  • Use plumbing tools, equipment, and materials safely.
  • Record completed works and report outstanding issues.
  • Follow health and safety procedures and relevant maintenance standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Plumber Multi Trader or in a similar maintenance role.
  • Strong plumbing knowledge and practical skills.
  • Competence across additional building and maintenance trades.
  • Ability to diagnose and resolve property maintenance problems.
  • Good customer service and communication skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and quality workmanship.
  • Ability to work independently and manage a daily workload.
  • Relevant plumbing or trade qualifications are desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £40,000–£42,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional property maintenance environment.
  • Opportunity to undertake varied plumbing and multi-trade work.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
