Room Attendant

Job Description

Channels Hotel is a well-established boutique hotel committed to providing guests with a clean, comfortable, and welcoming environment. The hotel is seeking a dedicated and detail-oriented Room Attendant to join its housekeeping team. In this role, you will clean and prepare guest rooms to the highest standards, replenish amenities, and ensure every guest enjoys a pleasant and comfortable stay while maintaining the hotel's reputation for excellent hospitality.

Responsibilities

  • Clean guest bedrooms, bathrooms, and public areas to company standards.
  • Make beds, change bed linen, and replace towels as required.
  • Replenish toiletries, refreshments, and other guest amenities.
  • Vacuum carpets, dust furniture, polish surfaces, and empty waste bins.
  • Report maintenance issues, damaged items, or lost property to the housekeeping supervisor.
  • Ensure housekeeping trolleys are fully stocked and organised.
  • Respond politely and professionally to guest requests.
  • Follow health and safety procedures, cleaning protocols, and hotel policies at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous housekeeping or cleaning experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Excellent attention to detail and high cleaning standards.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Good time management and organisational skills.
  • Friendly, reliable, and professional attitude.
  • Physically able to perform cleaning duties throughout the shift.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and public holidays.
  • Commitment to providing excellent guest service.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 30–37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible daytime shifts, including weekends and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £12.30–£13.60 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts on hotel services.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive and friendly working environment.
  • Opportunities for career progression within the hotel.
