Learning Disabilities Support Worker

Full Job Description

As a Learning Disabilities Support Worker, you will provide compassionate, person-centred care and support to adults with learning disabilities, helping them live as independently as possible. You will encourage individuals to achieve their personal goals, participate in their communities, and develop daily living skills while ensuring their safety and wellbeing.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support individuals with daily living activities such as cooking, cleaning, and personal care where required.
  • Promote independence and encourage service users to make their own choices.
  • Assist with medication administration in accordance with training and company policies.
  • Support people to attend appointments, social activities, and community events.
  • Maintain accurate care records and report any concerns to senior staff.
  • Build positive relationships with service users, families, and healthcare professionals.
  • Follow safeguarding procedures and health and safety guidelines at all times.
  • Encourage participation in hobbies, education, and employment opportunities where appropriate.

About You

The ideal candidate will:

  • Be caring, patient, and respectful of others.
  • Have excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Be able to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Remain calm under pressure and adapt to changing situations.
  • Be willing to complete training and professional development.
  • Previous experience in care is beneficial but not always essential, as training may be provided.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay of £12.53 per hour
  • Flexible part-time working hours
  • Paid training and ongoing development opportunities
  • Supportive team environment
  • Opportunities for career progression within the care sector
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing support programmes
  • Holiday entitlement and pension scheme (subject to employer policy)
  • The chance to make a meaningful difference in the lives of people with learning disabilities
