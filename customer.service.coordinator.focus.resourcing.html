Customer Service Coordinator

Job Description

Focus Resourcing is seeking an organised Customer Service Coordinator to manage customer enquiries and coordinate services efficiently. You will act as an important point of contact for customers, coordinate requests with internal teams, maintain accurate records, and ensure services are delivered smoothly. The successful candidate will have excellent communication, organisation, and problem-solving skills.

Responsibilities

  • Act as a key point of contact for customers and clients.
  • Respond to customer enquiries by telephone, email, and other communication channels.
  • Coordinate customer orders, requests, appointments, and service requirements.
  • Work with internal departments to ensure customer requirements are completed efficiently.
  • Monitor outstanding customer requests and provide timely updates.
  • Maintain accurate customer records, schedules, and service documentation.
  • Investigate and resolve customer issues and escalate complex matters where necessary.
  • Support the wider team with administration and customer service activities.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service, coordination, administration, or account support experience.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Excellent attention to detail and accurate record-keeping skills.
  • Good computer skills and confidence using business systems.
  • Strong problem-solving and prioritisation skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple customer requests simultaneously.
  • Professional, reliable, and customer-focused approach.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday.
  • 8:30am–5:30pm.
  • Full-time working schedule.

Salary

  • £31,000–£33,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Customer service and coordination training.
  • Professional office environment.
  • Opportunities to develop administration and customer management skills.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
