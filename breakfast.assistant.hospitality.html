Breakfast Assistant

Job Description

Premier Inn, part of Whitbread PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's largest and most trusted hotel brands, offering comfortable accommodation and high-quality dining experiences. Premier Inn is seeking enthusiastic and customer-focused Breakfast Assistants to join its hospitality team. In this role, you will help prepare and serve breakfast, ensure the dining area remains clean and welcoming, and provide excellent customer service to guests, helping them start their day with an enjoyable dining experience.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome guests warmly and provide friendly, professional customer service.
  • Prepare and replenish the breakfast buffet with fresh food and beverages.
  • Serve hot and cold breakfast items according to company standards.
  • Maintain cleanliness of the dining area, buffet stations, and service equipment.
  • Clear, clean, and reset tables efficiently throughout service.
  • Monitor food quality and report low stock levels to supervisors.
  • Assist the kitchen team with basic food preparation when required.
  • Follow food hygiene regulations, health and safety procedures, and company policies.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Friendly, reliable, and professional attitude.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced hospitality environment.
  • Good teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Excellent attention to cleanliness and food presentation.
  • Flexible availability, including early mornings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Previous hospitality or food service experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 30–37.5 hours per week.
  • Early morning shifts with weekend and bank holiday availability required.

Salary

  • £12.30–£13.60 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Discounted meals during shifts.
  • Employee discounts across Whitbread brands.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
