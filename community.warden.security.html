Community Warden

Job Description

Local Authority works with residents, businesses, and community organisations to maintain safe, clean, and welcoming neighbourhoods. The authority is seeking a Community Warden to provide a visible presence in local communities, identify concerns, support residents, and promote positive behaviour in public spaces. You will work closely with council teams and partner agencies to help address antisocial behaviour, environmental issues, and community safety concerns.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct regular patrols of designated neighbourhoods, public spaces, and community areas.
  • Provide a visible and approachable presence within local communities.
  • Engage with residents, businesses, visitors, and community groups.
  • Identify and report antisocial behaviour, environmental problems, and safety concerns.
  • Provide information and guidance to members of the public.
  • Support local community safety and environmental improvement initiatives.
  • Maintain accurate records of incidents, observations, and community concerns.
  • Work with police, council departments, and other partner organisations where appropriate.

Requirements

  • Previous community, security, enforcement, customer service, or public-sector experience is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with members of the community.
  • Good observation and situational awareness.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional when dealing with challenging situations.
  • Strong organisational and report writing skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a community-focused team.
  • Comfortable working outdoors in different weather conditions.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Working hours may include evenings and weekends depending on community requirements.

Salary

  • £25,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive local authority salary.
  • Local government pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Community safety and engagement training.
  • Uniform and required work equipment.
  • Opportunities to work with local community organisations.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
