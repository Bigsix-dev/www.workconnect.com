Healthcare Assistant (Outpatients)

Job Overview

Phoenix Hospital Chelmsford is seeking a dedicated and compassionate Healthcare Assistant (Outpatients) to join its clinical team on a full-time basis. This role offers an excellent opportunity to support healthcare professionals in delivering high-quality patient care within a busy outpatient environment while ensuring patients receive a positive and comfortable experience throughout their visit.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist nurses and clinicians with the delivery of outpatient services and patient care.
  • Prepare treatment and consultation rooms before and after appointments.
  • Welcome, support, and reassure patients during their hospital visits.
  • Record basic observations such as temperature, pulse, blood pressure, and weight when required.
  • Maintain accurate patient records and assist with administrative tasks.
  • Ensure clinical areas are clean, well-stocked, and compliant with infection prevention standards.
  • Escort patients safely between departments and provide assistance with mobility where necessary.
  • Work collaboratively with the multidisciplinary team to maintain exceptional standards of care.

Skills and Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Healthcare Assistant or in a care-related role is desirable but not always essential.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Compassionate, patient-focused approach with a commitment to delivering outstanding care.
  • Ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a team.
  • Good organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Flexibility to adapt to the demands of a busy outpatient setting.

Salary and Working Pattern

This is a full-time position offering a competitive annual salary ranging from £24,000 to £29,000, depending on experience and qualifications.

Career Opportunities

Working as a Healthcare Assistant (Outpatients) provides valuable clinical experience and can open pathways into senior healthcare assistant roles, specialist support positions, or further professional development within nursing and allied health professions. The role is ideal for individuals looking to build a rewarding long-term career in healthcare while making a meaningful difference in patients’ lives every day.
