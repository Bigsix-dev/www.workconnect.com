Office Coordinator

Job Description

John Lewis & Partners, part of the John Lewis Partnership, is one of the United Kingdom's most respected retail organisations, renowned for delivering outstanding products and exceptional customer service. John Lewis & Partners is seeking a highly organised and proactive Office Coordinator to support daily office operations. In this role, you will coordinate administrative activities, manage office resources, organise meetings, and ensure the workplace operates efficiently while providing excellent support to colleagues and management.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate the day-to-day administrative operations of the office.
  • Manage office supplies, equipment, and facilities.
  • Schedule meetings, appointments, and maintain team calendars.
  • Prepare reports, correspondence, and business documents.
  • Handle telephone calls, emails, and visitor enquiries professionally.
  • Maintain accurate records and filing systems.
  • Support managers and departments with administrative projects.
  • Ensure office procedures comply with company policies and confidentiality standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in office administration or office coordination.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, Outlook, and PowerPoint.
  • Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to prioritise multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Professional and customer-focused attitude.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with flexibility based on business requirements.

Salary

  • £27,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Generous paid annual leave.
  • Partnership pension scheme.
  • Partner discount at John Lewis & Partners and Waitrose.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within the John Lewis Partnership.
