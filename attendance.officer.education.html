Attendance Officer

Job Description

Secondary School is dedicated to creating a positive and supportive environment where students are encouraged to achieve their full potential. The school is seeking a committed and organised Attendance Officer to monitor student attendance, support improvement strategies, and work with students, families, and staff to promote regular school attendance. In this role, you will maintain accurate attendance records, identify concerns, and help remove barriers that affect student participation in education.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor and maintain accurate daily student attendance records.
  • Follow up on student absences and communicate with parents and carers.
  • Identify attendance concerns and support improvement plans.
  • Work closely with pastoral teams, teachers, and senior leaders to improve attendance.
  • Maintain attendance databases and prepare attendance reports.
  • Support meetings with students, families, and external agencies when required.
  • Promote the importance of regular attendance and punctuality.
  • Maintain confidentiality and comply with safeguarding procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in school administration, attendance monitoring, pastoral support, or a similar role.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with students, parents, and staff.
  • Strong organisational and administrative skills.
  • Good attention to detail and accurate record-keeping abilities.
  • Ability to manage sensitive information professionally.
  • Understanding of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a school support team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school working hours.

Salary

  • £24,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Local Government Pension Scheme.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Supportive school environment.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Opportunities for career progression within education support services.
  • Rewarding role supporting student achievement and wellbeing.
