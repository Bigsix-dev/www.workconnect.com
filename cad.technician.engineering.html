CAD Design Engineer

Job Description

Design Engineering Firm provides specialist engineering design and technical development services for a range of projects and industries. The company is seeking a creative and technically skilled CAD Design Engineer to produce accurate engineering designs, develop detailed models, and support projects from concept through to completion. In this role, you will work closely with engineers, designers, and project teams to develop practical and efficient engineering solutions.

Responsibilities

  • Create detailed 2D drawings and 3D models using computer-aided design (CAD) software.
  • Develop engineering designs based on technical specifications and project requirements.
  • Produce manufacturing drawings, layouts, and technical documentation.
  • Modify and improve existing designs to meet engineering and customer requirements.
  • Collaborate with engineers, manufacturers, and project teams throughout the design process.
  • Review designs for accuracy, functionality, and manufacturability.
  • Maintain accurate design files, drawings, and technical records.
  • Support prototype development, testing, and engineering improvement projects.

Requirements

  • Degree, HNC, HND, or equivalent qualification in Engineering or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience in CAD design or engineering design is desirable.
  • Strong experience using computer-aided design (CAD) software.
  • Good understanding of engineering drawings, tolerances, and manufacturing processes.
  • Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Strong technical and problem-solving skills.
  • Good communication and teamwork abilities.
  • Ability to manage multiple design projects and meet deadlines.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £35,000–£50,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Workplace pension scheme.

  • Access to modern design software and technology.
  • Supportive design engineering environment.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
