Store Operative

Dunelm Group plc is a leading UK home furnishings retailer, offering products for home décor, furniture, and accessories. Dunelm is seeking Store Operatives to support warehouse and shop floor operations.

Responsibilities

  • Receive and unpack deliveries.
  • Replenish stock on the shop floor.
  • Assist customers with enquiries.
  • Maintain stockroom organisation.
  • Support merchandising displays.
  • Ensure store cleanliness.
  • Assist with inventory checks.
  • Follow safety procedures.

Requirements

  • Good teamwork skills.
  • Physical ability to handle stock.
  • Attention to detail.
  • Reliable and punctual.
  • Basic communication skills.
  • Flexible availability.
  • No experience required.
  • Full training provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.

Salary

  • £24,000–£28,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Staff discount.
  • Pension scheme.
  • Paid leave.
  • Training opportunities.
  • Career progression.
