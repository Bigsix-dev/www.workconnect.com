Personal Assistant

Job Description

Chelmsford City Council is the local authority responsible for delivering essential public services and supporting communities across Chelmsford. Chelmsford City Council is seeking a highly organised and professional Personal Assistant to provide comprehensive administrative support to senior managers and council teams. In this role, you will manage schedules, coordinate meetings, prepare documentation, and ensure effective administrative support that helps deliver efficient public services.

Responsibilities

  • Provide confidential administrative support to senior managers and leadership teams.
  • Manage diaries, appointments, meetings, and travel arrangements.
  • Prepare reports, presentations, correspondence, and official documents.
  • Organise meetings, prepare agendas, and record accurate minutes.
  • Handle telephone calls, emails, and enquiries professionally.
  • Maintain confidential records and administrative systems.
  • Coordinate communication between internal departments and external stakeholders.
  • Support project administration and senior management activities.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Personal Assistant, Executive Assistant, or senior administrator.
  • Excellent organisational and diary management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • High level of discretion when handling confidential information.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, Outlook, and PowerPoint.
  • Ability to prioritise workloads and manage competing deadlines.
  • Strong attention to detail and administrative accuracy.
  • Professional approach with excellent teamwork skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard council working hours.

Salary

  • £29,000–£34,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive local government salary.
  • Local Government Pension Scheme.
  • Generous annual leave allowance.
  • Flexible and hybrid working opportunities where applicable.
  • Professional learning and development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career development opportunities within Chelmsford City Council.
