IT Sales Specialist

Job Description

A growing technology business in Chelmsford is seeking an ambitious IT Sales Specialist to help develop new business opportunities and strengthen relationships with existing customers. The role is ideal for a commercially focused professional with an interest in technology and solutions.

Responsibilities

  • Identify and develop new IT sales opportunities.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with prospective and existing customers.
  • Understand customer requirements and recommend suitable technology solutions.
  • Prepare proposals, quotations and sales presentations.
  • Manage sales opportunities through the full sales cycle.
  • Achieve agreed sales targets and contribute to business growth.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in IT, technology or solution sales.
  • Strong communication, negotiation and presentation skills.
  • Commercially minded with a proactive approach to business development.
  • Ability to understand and communicate technical solutions clearly.
  • Strong customer relationship management skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £45,000–£50,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to work within a growing technology business.
  • Career progression and development opportunities.
  • Exposure to a range of technology products and solutions.
  • Supportive and commercially focused working environment.
