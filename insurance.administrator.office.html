Insurance Administrator

Job Description

Aviva PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading insurance, wealth, and retirement providers, serving millions of customers across the UK and internationally. Aviva is seeking a highly organised and detail-oriented Insurance Administrator to support insurance operations and customer services. In this role, you will process policy documentation, maintain customer records, assist with insurance enquiries, and provide administrative support while ensuring compliance with industry regulations and company standards.

Responsibilities

  • Process new insurance policies, renewals, amendments, and cancellations.
  • Maintain accurate customer records and policy documentation.
  • Respond to customer and broker enquiries via telephone, email, and written correspondence.
  • Prepare insurance documents, reports, and administrative paperwork.
  • Support underwriting and claims teams with administrative tasks.
  • Verify customer information and ensure records remain accurate and up to date.
  • Handle confidential customer data in accordance with GDPR and company policies.
  • Ensure all administrative activities comply with insurance regulations and internal procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in insurance administration, financial services, or office administration is desirable.
  • Excellent organisational and administrative skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and numerical accuracy.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication abilities.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Strong customer service and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to manage confidential information professionally.
  • Knowledge of insurance products and industry regulations is an advantage.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard business hours.

Salary

  • £26,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Generous paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Private healthcare and wellbeing support.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts on selected Aviva products and services.
  • Flexible and hybrid working opportunities where applicable.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Aviva.
