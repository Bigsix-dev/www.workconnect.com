Autism Support Worker

Job Description

Dimensions UK is seeking compassionate and dedicated Autism Support Workers to provide high-quality, person-centred support for autistic individuals and people with learning disabilities. In this role, you will help people live independently, achieve their personal goals, and participate fully in their local communities while promoting dignity, choice, and inclusion.

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide tailored day-to-day support based on individual care and support plans.
  • Assist with personal care, meal preparation, medication administration, and household tasks where required.
  • Encourage participation in social, recreational, educational, and employment-related activities.
  • Support individuals in developing independent living skills and confidence.
  • Maintain accurate records and communicate effectively with families and healthcare professionals.
  • Promote safeguarding, health and safety, and positive behavioural support practices.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position, typically 37.5 to 40 hours per week.
  • Shifts may include days, evenings, weekends, bank holidays, and occasional sleep-in or overnight duties, depending on service requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in autism support, social care, or a similar role is desirable but not always essential, as training may be provided.
  • A caring, patient, and empathetic approach with excellent communication skills.
  • Ability to work flexibly across a rota, including weekends and holidays.
  • Good teamwork and problem-solving skills.
  • Right to work in the UK and willingness to undergo relevant background checks.
  • A driving licence may be beneficial for some services but is not mandatory for all positions.

What We Offer

  • Competitive hourly pay with opportunities for overtime.
  • Comprehensive induction and ongoing professional training.
  • Career progression opportunities within a nationally recognised social care organisation.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement and pension scheme.
  • Employee assistance programme and wellbeing support.
  • Recognition and reward programmes for outstanding performance.
  • A supportive and inclusive working environment focused on continuous development.
