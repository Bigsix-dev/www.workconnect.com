Reach Truck Driver

Job Description

Culina Group Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading logistics and supply chain companies, providing specialist warehousing, transport, and distribution services for major brands across various industries. Culina Group is seeking an experienced and safety-focused Reach Truck Driver to join its warehouse operations team. In this role, you will operate reach trucks to move, store, and organise stock efficiently while supporting smooth warehouse operations and maintaining high safety standards.

Responsibilities

  • Operate reach trucks safely to move and store goods within the warehouse.
  • Load and unload deliveries using warehouse equipment.
  • Move stock to designated storage locations accurately.
  • Complete stock movements using warehouse management systems and scanners.
  • Carry out daily equipment checks and report any faults or safety issues.
  • Assist with picking, packing, and general warehouse duties when required.
  • Maintain accurate stock control and support inventory checks.
  • Follow company procedures, health and safety regulations, and warehouse standards.

Requirements

  • Valid Reach Truck Forklift licence or accredited training certificate.
  • Previous experience operating reach trucks in a warehouse environment.
  • Good understanding of warehouse safety procedures.
  • Ability to work accurately in a fast-paced environment.
  • Good attention to detail and organisational skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Experience using handheld scanners and warehouse systems is desirable.
  • Flexible availability for shift work when required.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40–45 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, nights, and weekends.

Salary

  • £14.50–£16.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Forklift training and development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts and reward programmes.
  • Health and wellbeing support.
  • Modern warehouse facilities and equipment.
  • Career progression opportunities within Culina Group.
