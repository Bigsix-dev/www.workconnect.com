Security Coordinator

Job Description

Corporate Security Company provides integrated security services to offices, corporate facilities, and commercial organisations. The company is seeking an organised Security Coordinator to support the planning and coordination of daily security operations. You will coordinate security personnel, maintain operational records, communicate with clients, and help ensure that security procedures and service standards are consistently maintained.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate daily security operations and personnel schedules.
  • Assist with the allocation of security officers and site duties.
  • Maintain accurate records of shifts, attendance, incidents, training, and security activities.
  • Communicate with security officers, clients, site management, and security managers.
  • Monitor operational requirements and help resolve staffing or scheduling issues.
  • Prepare reports, documentation, and operational updates.
  • Support audits, compliance checks, meetings, and security reviews.
  • Assist with incident management and ensure relevant information is escalated appropriately.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in security administration, coordination, operations, or a related field is desirable.
  • Strong organisational and time management skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication abilities.
  • Good computer and Microsoft Office skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accurate record-keeping abilities.
  • Ability to prioritise multiple tasks in a busy operational environment.
  • Good problem-solving and decision-making skills.
  • Professional and confident approach when communicating with clients and security personnel.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule, with occasional flexibility for operational requirements.

Salary

  • £30,000–£36,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive corporate security salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Security operations and coordination training.
  • Opportunities for professional security qualifications.
  • Professional corporate working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
