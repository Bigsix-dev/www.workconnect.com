Clinical Administrator

Job Overview

A Clinical Administrator plays a vital role in supporting the smooth day-to-day operation of hospital departments by providing efficient administrative and clerical assistance. Working within a busy healthcare environment, you will help coordinate patient records, manage appointments, maintain confidential documentation, and support clinical teams in delivering high-quality patient care.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage patient appointments, referrals, and clinical correspondence.
  • Maintain accurate and confidential electronic and paper medical records.
  • Respond to telephone and email enquiries from patients and healthcare professionals.
  • Prepare reports, letters, and other administrative documentation.
  • Support doctors, nurses, and allied health professionals with day-to-day administrative tasks.
  • Update hospital databases and ensure information is recorded correctly.
  • Process incoming referrals and monitor waiting lists.
  • Ensure compliance with NHS policies, data protection regulations, and confidentiality standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in an administrative or healthcare setting is desirable.
  • Excellent organisational and time-management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office and general computer systems.
  • Ability to handle sensitive information with discretion and professionalism.
  • Strong attention to detail and the ability to work effectively under pressure.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary of £24,000–£29,000.
  • Full-time, stable employment within a respected healthcare environment.
  • Opportunities for professional development and career progression.
  • Supportive multidisciplinary team and collaborative workplace.
  • Access to training programmes and valuable experience in hospital administration.
