Retail Security Officer

Job Description

Security Services Company provides professional security and loss prevention services to retail businesses and commercial premises. The company is seeking a vigilant Retail Security Officer to help protect customers, employees, stock, and property. You will monitor retail premises, deter theft and antisocial behaviour, respond to incidents, and work closely with store management to maintain a safe and secure shopping environment.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor retail premises to prevent theft, damage, and unauthorised activity.
  • Conduct regular patrols throughout the store and surrounding areas.
  • Monitor customers, visitors, and activity in accordance with site procedures.
  • Respond professionally to suspected theft, security incidents, and disturbances.
  • Monitor CCTV and other security systems where required.
  • Work closely with store management and retail staff to address security concerns.
  • Complete accurate incident reports and daily security records.
  • Provide a visible and professional security presence for customers and staff.

Requirements

  • Valid SIA licence or willingness to obtain the appropriate licence.
  • Previous retail security or customer-facing security experience is desirable.
  • Strong observation and loss prevention awareness.
  • Ability to deal calmly and professionally with difficult situations.
  • Good communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Professional appearance and customer-focused attitude.
  • Ability to follow security procedures and company policies.
  • Reliable, punctual, and able to work independently.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working schedule may include weekends.

Salary

  • £26,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive retail security salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Security and loss prevention training.
  • Uniform and required security equipment.
  • Opportunities for additional security qualifications.
  • Career progression within the security industry.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
