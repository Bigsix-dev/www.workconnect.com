Software Engineer

Job Description

Various Employers in Chelmsford are looking for Software Engineers to design, develop, test and maintain software applications. These opportunities are suitable for developers who enjoy solving technical problems and contributing to reliable, scalable software solutions.

Responsibilities

  • Design, develop and maintain software applications.
  • Write clean, efficient and maintainable code.
  • Work with development teams to deliver new features and improvements.
  • Test software and identify and resolve defects.
  • Participate in code reviews and technical discussions.
  • Contribute to the maintenance and improvement of existing systems.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in software development or engineering.
  • Strong programming and problem-solving skills.
  • Understanding of software development principles and testing.
  • Ability to work effectively with technical and non-technical colleagues.
  • Good attention to detail and a commitment to producing quality software.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working arrangements may vary depending on the employer.

Salary

  • Around £37,370–£63,565 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunities to work with modern software technologies.
  • Professional development and technical training.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Exposure to a range of software development projects.
