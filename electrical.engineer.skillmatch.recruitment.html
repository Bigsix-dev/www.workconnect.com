Electrical Engineer

Job Description

Skillmatch Recruitment is seeking an Electrical Engineer to support the design, installation, testing and maintenance of electrical systems in Chelmsford. The role is suited to an engineer with strong technical knowledge and practical problem-solving skills.

Responsibilities

  • Design and develop electrical systems and installations.
  • Review technical drawings, specifications and electrical documentation.
  • Support installation, testing and commissioning activities.
  • Diagnose electrical faults and recommend effective solutions.
  • Ensure engineering work meets relevant safety and quality requirements.
  • Work closely with project and engineering teams.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in an electrical engineering role.
  • Strong understanding of electrical systems and engineering principles.
  • Ability to read and interpret technical drawings.
  • Good analytical and fault-finding skills.
  • Strong communication and organisational abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.

Salary

  • £38,000–£40,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary.
  • Opportunity to work on varied engineering projects.
  • Professional development opportunities.
  • Technical career progression.
