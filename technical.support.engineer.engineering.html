Technical Support Engineer

Job Description

Technology Solutions Provider delivers innovative technology services and technical solutions to businesses across multiple industries. The company is seeking a skilled and customer-focused Technical Support Engineer to provide expert technical assistance, troubleshoot issues, and ensure reliable operation of technology systems. In this role, you will support customers, diagnose technical problems, and work with internal teams to deliver effective solutions.

Responsibilities

  • Provide technical support and troubleshooting assistance to customers and users.
  • Diagnose and resolve hardware, software, and system-related issues.
  • Respond to technical enquiries through phone, email, and support platforms.
  • Install, configure, and maintain technology systems and equipment.
  • Document technical issues, solutions, and support procedures.
  • Monitor system performance and identify potential problems.
  • Work with engineering and development teams to resolve complex issues.
  • Provide technical guidance and training to users when required.

Requirements

  • Degree or qualification in Engineering, Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field.
  • Previous experience in technical support, engineering support, or a similar role is desirable.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
  • Good understanding of hardware, software, and networking systems.
  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Ability to explain technical information clearly to non-technical users.
  • Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to manage multiple support requests effectively.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £30,000–£45,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive technology sector salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Technical training and certification opportunities.
  • Access to modern technology tools and systems.
  • Supportive technical team environment.
  • Career progression opportunities in engineering and technology.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
