Customer Service Advisor

Job Description

Noble Recruiting is seeking a professional Customer Service Advisor to provide excellent support to customers and ensure enquiries are handled efficiently. The successful candidate will communicate with customers across relevant channels, resolve queries, maintain accurate records, and contribute to a positive customer experience.

Responsibilities

  • Respond to customer enquiries promptly and professionally.
  • Provide accurate information about products, services, and customer requests.
  • Handle customer questions, complaints, and service issues effectively.
  • Identify customer needs and provide appropriate solutions.
  • Maintain accurate customer records and service information.
  • Process customer requests and update internal systems.
  • Communicate with internal departments to resolve customer queries.
  • Follow established customer service procedures and standards.
  • Maintain a professional and helpful approach in all customer interactions.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service experience is desirable.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Strong customer-focused approach.
  • Good organisational and administrative abilities.
  • Confident using computer systems and customer databases.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional when handling difficult situations.
  • Good attention to detail.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a busy team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • Salary not stated.

Benefits

  • Professional working environment.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Opportunities to develop customer service skills.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
