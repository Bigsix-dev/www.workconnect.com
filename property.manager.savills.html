Property Manager

Job Description

Savills is seeking an experienced Property Manager to oversee the effective management and administration of residential properties. The successful candidate will maintain strong relationships with landlords and tenants, coordinate maintenance and inspections, manage property records, and ensure properties are managed professionally and efficiently.

Responsibilities

  • Manage a portfolio of residential properties on behalf of landlords.
  • Act as a key point of contact for landlords, tenants, and contractors.
  • Coordinate property inspections and maintenance requirements.
  • Arrange repairs and liaise with contractors to ensure work is completed appropriately.
  • Monitor property condition and identify maintenance requirements.
  • Maintain accurate property records and documentation.
  • Respond promptly and professionally to landlord and tenant enquiries.
  • Monitor tenancy-related administration and important deadlines.
  • Ensure property management procedures and relevant regulations are followed.
  • Support landlords with property-related issues and provide appropriate updates.

Requirements

  • Previous property management experience is desirable.
  • Good knowledge of residential property management processes.
  • Excellent communication and relationship management skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Good attention to detail and administrative skills.
  • Confident dealing with landlords, tenants, and contractors.
  • Strong problem-solving and decision-making skills.
  • Professional approach with excellent customer service skills.

  • Opportunities to develop property management expertise.
  • Career development opportunities.
