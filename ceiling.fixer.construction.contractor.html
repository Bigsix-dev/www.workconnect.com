Ceiling Fixer

Job Description

Construction Contractor is looking for an experienced Ceiling Fixer to work on construction projects in Chelmsford. The successful candidate will install suspended ceilings and associated systems accurately, safely, and in accordance with project requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Install suspended ceiling systems and ceiling frameworks.
  • Measure, cut, and fit ceiling tiles and boards.
  • Install grid systems and associated components.
  • Work from drawings and project specifications.
  • Coordinate with other trades working within ceiling spaces.
  • Maintain high standards of finish and site safety.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Ceiling Fixer.
  • Knowledge of suspended ceiling and ceiling installation systems.
  • Ability to work accurately from drawings and measurements.
  • Good practical and finishing skills.
  • Strong awareness of construction site safety.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Based in Chelmsford.

Salary

  • £20–£25 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly rate.
  • Ongoing construction projects.
  • Varied commercial and building work.
  • Opportunity for continued professional experience.
