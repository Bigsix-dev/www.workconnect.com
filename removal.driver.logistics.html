Removal Driver

Job Description

Pickfords Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading removals and storage companies, providing professional home, office, and international relocation services. Pickfords is seeking an experienced and reliable Removal Driver to join its moving team. In this role, you will operate company vehicles, assist with the safe transportation of customer belongings, and provide professional moving services while ensuring excellent customer care throughout the relocation process.

Responsibilities

  • Drive company removal vehicles safely and responsibly.
  • Load, transport, and unload customer belongings with care.
  • Assist with packing, wrapping, and protecting furniture and valuable items.
  • Complete home and business removals efficiently and professionally.
  • Communicate with customers to provide a positive moving experience.
  • Complete vehicle checks and maintain accurate delivery documentation.
  • Ensure all goods are handled safely to prevent damage during transportation.
  • Follow company procedures, road safety regulations, and health and safety standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience driving professionally or within the removals industry is desirable.
  • Valid driving licence suitable for operating company vehicles.
  • Good knowledge of road safety and vehicle handling.
  • Physically fit and able to lift and move furniture safely.
  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Reliable, punctual, and professional when representing the company.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a moving team.
  • Flexible availability for longer shifts and occasional weekends.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40–45 hours per week.
  • Flexible working hours depending on customer schedules and relocation requirements.

Salary

  • £13.50–£15.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Full training and professional development opportunities.
  • Employee support and wellbeing programmes.
  • Opportunity to work with a recognised UK removals specialist.
  • Supportive team environment.
  • Career progression opportunities within Pickfords.
