Food & Beverage Assistant

Job Description

Pontlands Park Hotel is a luxury country house hotel in Essex, offering high-quality accommodation, dining, weddings, and event services. The hotel is seeking a friendly, professional, and customer-focused Food & Beverage Assistant to join its hospitality team. In this role, you will support restaurant and event operations, provide excellent table service, assist guests with their needs, and help maintain the high standards expected from a premium hospitality environment.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome guests and provide friendly, professional service.
  • Take food and beverage orders accurately.
  • Serve meals, drinks, and refreshments according to hotel standards.
  • Prepare restaurant and event areas before service.
  • Clear and reset tables efficiently.
  • Assist with weddings, private functions, and special events.
  • Maintain cleanliness of dining areas and service stations.
  • Follow food hygiene, health and safety, and hotel procedures.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Friendly, professional, and welcoming attitude.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a busy hospitality environment.
  • Strong teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Good attention to detail and presentation.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and events.
  • Previous hospitality or food service experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including weekends and public holidays.

Salary

  • £12.50–£14.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Staff meals or refreshments during shifts.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts on hotel services.
  • Supportive and professional working environment.
  • Opportunities to gain experience in luxury hospitality.
