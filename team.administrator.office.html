Team Administrator

Job Description

NHS England leads and supports the National Health Service across England, ensuring high-quality healthcare services are delivered to patients while improving health outcomes nationwide. NHS England is seeking a highly organised and proactive Team Administrator to provide comprehensive administrative support to departmental teams and managers. In this role, you will coordinate meetings, maintain records, prepare documentation, and assist with the smooth day-to-day running of administrative operations while ensuring confidentiality and compliance with NHS policies.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to managers and multidisciplinary teams.
  • Organise meetings, prepare agendas, and record meeting minutes.
  • Maintain accurate electronic and paper-based records.
  • Manage diaries, appointments, and team schedules.
  • Prepare reports, letters, presentations, and other administrative documents.
  • Respond to telephone calls, emails, and enquiries professionally.
  • Coordinate internal communications and support departmental projects.
  • Ensure compliance with NHS policies, GDPR, and information governance standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in administration, office support, or NHS administration is desirable.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, Outlook, and PowerPoint.
  • Ability to manage confidential information professionally.
  • Strong teamwork and customer service skills.
  • Ability to prioritise multiple tasks in a busy healthcare environment.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard NHS working hours.

Salary

  • £25,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive NHS salary package.
  • NHS Pension Scheme.
  • Generous paid annual leave plus public holidays.
  • Access to NHS training and professional development programmes.
  • Employee wellbeing and mental health support services.
  • NHS staff discounts and benefits scheme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within NHS England.
