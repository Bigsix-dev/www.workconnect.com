Staff Nurse

Full Job Description

As a Staff Nurse, you will provide high-quality nursing care to patients while working as part of a multidisciplinary healthcare team. You will assess patients' needs, administer treatments and medications, monitor recovery, and ensure patient safety and comfort throughout their stay. The role requires professionalism, compassion, and the ability to work effectively in a fast-paced clinical environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Deliver safe, effective, and person-centred nursing care.
  • Assess, plan, implement, and evaluate patient care plans.
  • Administer medications and treatments according to hospital policies.
  • Monitor patients' conditions and accurately record observations.
  • Collaborate with doctors, therapists, and other healthcare professionals.
  • Respond promptly to emergencies and changing patient needs.
  • Educate patients and their families about treatments and discharge plans.
  • Maintain accurate clinical documentation and patient records.
  • Follow infection prevention and control procedures.
  • Support junior staff, students, and healthcare assistants when required.
  • Ensure compliance with professional standards and safeguarding policies.
  • Participate in training and continuous professional development.

About You

The ideal candidate should:

  • Be a registered nurse with a valid nursing qualification and professional registration.
  • Have excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Demonstrate compassion, empathy, and respect for patients.
  • Be able to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Have strong clinical assessment and decision-making abilities.
  • Remain calm and organised under pressure.
  • Be committed to maintaining high standards of patient care.
  • Be willing to work flexible shifts, including weekends and holidays if required.

  • Support for continuing professional education.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Sick pay in line with company policy.
  • Uniform provided where applicable.
  • Access to modern healthcare facilities and equipment.
  • Opportunities for promotion into senior nursing or specialist roles.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
