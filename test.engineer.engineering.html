Test Engineer

Job Description

Product Development Company designs and develops products for a range of technical and commercial applications. The company is seeking a Test Engineer to plan, conduct, and document product testing throughout the development process. You will work closely with design, manufacturing, and engineering teams to identify issues, verify product performance, and ensure products meet required specifications and quality standards.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and carry out test plans, procedures, and test schedules.
  • Test products, components, and systems against technical specifications.
  • Record, analyse, and report test results accurately.
  • Identify product defects, failures, and performance issues.
  • Investigate test failures and support root cause analysis.
  • Work with design and engineering teams to resolve technical issues.
  • Maintain test equipment, documentation, and testing records.
  • Support product validation, verification, and continuous improvement activities.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Engineering, Product Development, Manufacturing, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience in engineering testing or product development is desirable.
  • Good understanding of product testing and validation processes.
  • Ability to interpret technical drawings, specifications, and test requirements.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Good attention to detail and accurate record keeping.
  • Ability to use technical testing equipment and measurement tools.
  • Strong communication and teamwork abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £35,000–£50,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Workplace pension scheme.

  • Technical testing and engineering training.
  • Opportunities to contribute to new product development.
  • Modern product development environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
