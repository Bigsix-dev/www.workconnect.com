Warehouse Assistant

Job Description

Wincanton PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading supply chain and logistics providers, delivering warehousing and distribution services across multiple industries. Wincanton is seeking a dependable and motivated Warehouse Assistant to support its warehouse operations. In this role, you will assist with receiving, storing, picking, packing, and dispatching goods while ensuring accuracy, efficiency, and compliance with company procedures and health and safety standards.

Responsibilities

  • Receive and inspect incoming deliveries for accuracy and quality.
  • Pick, pack, and prepare customer orders for dispatch.
  • Load and unload deliveries safely using appropriate equipment.
  • Operate handheld scanners and warehouse management systems accurately.
  • Maintain organised stock locations and assist with inventory checks.
  • Keep the warehouse clean, organised, and free from hazards.
  • Work collaboratively with colleagues to achieve daily operational targets.
  • Follow company policies, warehouse procedures, and health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous warehouse or logistics experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Ability to lift and move goods safely throughout the working day.
  • Good attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Basic computer skills and the ability to use handheld scanners.
  • Strong organisational and time management skills.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a team.
  • Reliable, punctual, and committed to maintaining high standards.
  • Flexible availability to meet operational requirements.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts, including early mornings, evenings, and occasional weekends.

Salary

  • £12.75–£14.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Comprehensive training and career development programmes.
  • Employee discounts and reward schemes.
  • Health and wellbeing support services.
  • Safe, modern warehouse facilities and equipment.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression within Wincanton.
