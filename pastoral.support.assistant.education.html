Pastoral Support Assistant

Job Description

Moulsham High School is a successful secondary school dedicated to providing a safe, supportive, and inclusive environment where students are encouraged to achieve their full potential. Moulsham High School is seeking a caring and committed Pastoral Support Assistant to provide guidance and wellbeing support to students. In this role, you will work closely with students, teachers, and families to promote positive behaviour, improve attendance, and support students' personal and emotional development.

Responsibilities

  • Provide pastoral support and guidance to students across the school.
  • Support students with emotional, social, and behavioural challenges.
  • Monitor attendance, punctuality, and student engagement.
  • Work with teachers and school leaders to support student wellbeing.
  • Build positive relationships with students and encourage personal development.
  • Communicate with parents, carers, and external support services where required.
  • Maintain accurate records of student support activities and progress.
  • Promote safeguarding, inclusion, and a positive school culture.

Requirements

  • Previous experience supporting young people in a school, youth work, or care environment.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Patient, approachable, and supportive attitude.
  • Ability to build trusting relationships with students.
  • Understanding of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Strong organisational and record-keeping abilities.
  • Ability to work effectively with teachers, families, and support teams.
  • Relevant qualification in education, counselling, youth work, or pastoral support is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school working hours.

Salary

  • £25,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Local Government Pension Scheme.
  • School holiday periods and paid leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Supportive school leadership team.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
  • Opportunities for career progression within education support services.
  • Rewarding role supporting student wellbeing and success.
