Office Manager

Job Description

Colchester Institute is one of the largest providers of further and higher education in Essex, offering high-quality education, apprenticeships, and professional training to thousands of students each year. Colchester Institute is seeking an experienced and organised Office Manager to oversee the daily administrative operations of its office. In this role, you will supervise administrative staff, coordinate office procedures, manage resources, and ensure efficient support is provided to academic and operational departments.

Responsibilities

  • Manage the day-to-day operations of the office and administrative team.
  • Supervise, support, and develop administrative staff.
  • Coordinate office procedures to ensure efficient workflow.
  • Manage office budgets, supplies, equipment, and facilities.
  • Prepare reports, presentations, and administrative documentation.
  • Schedule meetings, manage calendars, and coordinate departmental activities.
  • Ensure accurate record keeping and compliance with institutional policies.
  • Support senior management with administrative planning and operational projects.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as an Office Manager or in a senior administrative role.
  • Excellent leadership and people management skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, including Word, Excel, Outlook, and PowerPoint.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines.
  • Strong problem-solving and decision-making skills.
  • Professional, reliable, and committed to maintaining high administrative standards.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard office hours.

Salary

  • £32,000–£38,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Local Government Pension Scheme.
  • Professional training and leadership development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Access to staff discounts and educational benefits.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Colchester Institute.
