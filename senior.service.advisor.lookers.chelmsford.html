Senior Service Advisor

Job Description

Lookers Chelmsford is seeking an experienced Senior Service Advisor to provide a high standard of customer service while coordinating vehicle servicing and repair requirements. You will act as a key point of contact between customers and the workshop, ensuring vehicles progress efficiently and customers remain informed throughout the service process.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and manage vehicle servicing and repair bookings.
  • Discuss customer requirements and accurately record vehicle concerns.
  • Provide clear explanations of recommended servicing and repair work.
  • Prepare estimates and communicate costs and completion times.
  • Coordinate with technicians and workshop colleagues to monitor vehicle progress.
  • Keep customers updated throughout the repair and servicing process.
  • Obtain approval for additional work where required.
  • Maintain accurate job cards, invoices, and service documentation.
  • Build strong customer relationships and support customer satisfaction.
  • Assist with maintaining efficient service department operations.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in an automotive Service Advisor role is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of vehicle servicing, maintenance, and repair procedures.
  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Confident discussing technical information and repair costs with customers.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Good attention to detail and accurate administrative skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in a busy dealership environment.
  • Professional approach with a strong focus on customer satisfaction.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • Salary not stated.

Benefits

  • Professional automotive dealership environment.
  • Opportunities for training and professional development.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Customer service and automotive industry experience.
