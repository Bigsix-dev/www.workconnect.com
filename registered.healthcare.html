Registered Nurse (RGN)

Full Job Description

We are seeking a compassionate and dedicated Registered Nurse (RGN) to join our healthcare team. In this role, you will provide high-quality, patient-centred nursing care while working closely with doctors, allied health professionals, patients, and their families. You will assess patient needs, develop care plans, administer treatments, and ensure that the highest standards of clinical practice and safety are maintained.

This position offers the opportunity to work in a supportive environment where continuous learning, teamwork, and professional development are encouraged.

Key Responsibilities

  • A recognised nursing qualification and current professional registration.
  • Strong clinical assessment and decision-making skills.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal abilities.
  • A caring, empathetic, and professional attitude.
  • The ability to work effectively in a fast-paced healthcare environment.
  • Strong organisational and time management skills.
  • A commitment to maintaining patient confidentiality and dignity.
  • The flexibility to work shifts, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • The ability to work independently as well as collaboratively within a multidisciplinary team.
  • A commitment to continuous professional development and evidence-based practice

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement plus public holidays.
  • NHS or employer pension scheme.
  • Paid sick leave and maternity, paternity, or adoption leave.
  • Ongoing training and professional development opportunities.
  • Career progression pathways and internal promotion opportunities.
  • Access to employee wellbeing and mental health support programmes.
  • Employee assistance programme (confidential counselling and support).
  • Flexible working opportunities where operationally possible.
  • Enhanced pay for nights, weekends, and bank holidays.
  • Staff discounts at selected retailers and services.
  • Access to salary sacrifice schemes such as cycle-to-work or electric vehicle programmes (where available).
  • Occupational health services.
  • Free or subsidised uniforms.
  • Support for continuing professional education and specialist training.
  • Recognition and long-service award programmes.

Working Hours

This is typically a full-time position of around 37.5 hours per week, usually worked on a rotating shift pattern that may include:

  • Day shifts
  • Night shifts
  • Weekends
  • Public holidays
