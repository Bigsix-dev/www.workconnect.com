Banqueting Assistant

Job Description

Pontlands Park Hotel is a luxury country house hotel in Essex, renowned for hosting weddings, banquets, conferences, and private events. The hotel is seeking a professional and enthusiastic Banqueting Assistant to join its hospitality team. In this role, you will assist with setting up function rooms, serving food and beverages, and ensuring guests receive exceptional service throughout weddings, conferences, and special events. You will play a key role in delivering memorable experiences while maintaining the hotel's high standards of hospitality.

Responsibilities

  • Prepare and set up banquet rooms according to event specifications.
  • Serve food and beverages professionally during weddings, conferences, and private functions.
  • Welcome guests and provide outstanding customer service throughout events.
  • Maintain cleanliness and organisation of banquet rooms before, during, and after events.
  • Assist with clearing tables, resetting rooms, and storing equipment after functions.
  • Support colleagues to ensure smooth event operations.
  • Respond promptly to guest requests and resolve minor service issues.
  • Follow food hygiene regulations, health and safety procedures, and hotel policies at all times.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Friendly, professional, and well-presented appearance.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced hospitality environment.
  • Strong teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Good attention to detail and time management.
  • Ability to carry out manual setup and clearing duties when required.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Previous hospitality or banqueting experience is desirable but not essential.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 30–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts based on event schedules, including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £12.50–£13.80 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Complimentary or discounted staff meals during shifts.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts on hotel services.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive team environment with excellent opportunities for career progression.
