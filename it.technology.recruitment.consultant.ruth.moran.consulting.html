IT Technology Recruitment Consultant

Job Description

Ruth Moran Consulting is looking for an IT Technology Recruitment Consultant to join its recruitment team in Chelmsford. This hybrid role involves identifying technology professionals, developing client relationships and managing candidates throughout the recruitment process.

Responsibilities

  • Source and identify qualified IT and technology candidates.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with candidates and clients.
  • Manage vacancies from initial briefing through to successful placement.
  • Conduct candidate screening, interviews and suitability assessments.
  • Develop new business opportunities within the technology recruitment market.
  • Maintain accurate candidate and client records.

Requirements

  • Experience in recruitment, sales or a technology-focused environment.
  • Strong communication and relationship-building skills.
  • Commercially driven and motivated by achieving targets.
  • Good understanding or interest in the IT and technology sector.
  • Excellent organisation and time-management skills.

Working Hours

  • Hybrid working arrangement.
  • Full-time professional working schedule.

Salary

  • £40,000 per year plus commission.

Benefits

  • Hybrid working opportunity.
  • Commission earning potential.
  • Career progression within technology recruitment.
  • Opportunity to build specialist IT recruitment expertise.
  • Professional and performance-focused environment.
