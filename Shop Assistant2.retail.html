Shop Assistant

Greggs PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the UK's most popular bakery and food-on-the-go retailers, serving freshly baked goods, sandwiches, hot drinks, and snacks. Greggs is looking for friendly and energetic Shop Assistants to provide outstanding customer service while preparing food, maintaining store standards, and supporting the smooth running of the shop.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide excellent customer service.
  • Prepare and serve food and beverages.
  • Operate tills and process transactions accurately.
  • Replenish food displays and stock levels.
  • Maintain food hygiene and safety standards.
  • Keep customer and kitchen areas clean and organised.
  • Assist with deliveries and stock rotation.
  • Follow company procedures and health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly and enthusiastic personality.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a busy environment.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Good teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Flexible availability including weekends.
  • Food retail experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Comprehensive training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–39 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including weekends and early mornings.

Salary

  • £12.60–£13.70 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted food and drinks during shifts.
  • Employee discount.
  • Training and career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing programme.
