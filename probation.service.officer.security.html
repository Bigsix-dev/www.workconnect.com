Probation Service Officer

Job Description

HM Prison & Probation Service works to protect the public and support the rehabilitation and resettlement of people under probation supervision. The service is seeking a responsible Probation Service Officer to support probation teams in managing individuals in the community and custodial settings. You will contribute to case management, maintain accurate records, communicate with service users, and support rehabilitation and risk management activities under professional guidance.

Responsibilities

  • Support probation staff in supervising individuals subject to probation requirements.
  • Maintain accurate case records, reports, and administrative documentation.
  • Assist with appointments, assessments, and rehabilitation activities.
  • Communicate professionally with individuals under probation supervision.
  • Monitor compliance with agreed requirements and report concerns appropriately.
  • Support risk management procedures and escalate relevant issues to senior staff.
  • Work with partner organisations and agencies involved in rehabilitation and resettlement.
  • Contribute to activities designed to reduce reoffending and support positive outcomes.

Requirements

  • Relevant qualification or experience in probation, criminal justice, public service, social care, or a related field is desirable.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to build professional relationships while maintaining appropriate boundaries.
  • Strong organisational and administrative skills.
  • Ability to handle confidential information responsibly.
  • Good judgement and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional when dealing with challenging situations.
  • Commitment to equality, professionalism, rehabilitation, and public protection.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule, with flexibility required for operational needs.

Salary

  • £26,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive public-sector salary.
  • Government workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Structured probation and professional training.
  • Opportunities to develop specialist public-service skills.
  • Stable public-sector employment environment.
  • Career progression opportunities within the justice sector.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
