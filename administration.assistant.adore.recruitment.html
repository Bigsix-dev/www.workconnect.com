Administration Assistant

Job Description

Adore Recruitment is seeking an organised Administration Assistant to provide reliable administrative support and help ensure the smooth running of daily office operations. The successful candidate will manage documentation, update records, communicate with customers and colleagues, and assist with a variety of administrative tasks.

Responsibilities

  • Provide general administrative support to the team.
  • Answer telephone calls and respond to emails professionally.
  • Maintain accurate records, files, and databases.
  • Prepare and process documents and correspondence.
  • Complete data-entry and routine administrative tasks.
  • Organise information and ensure records are kept up to date.
  • Liaise with customers, suppliers, and internal colleagues.
  • Assist with scheduling, filing, and other office coordination duties.
  • Monitor outstanding administrative tasks and follow up where necessary.

Requirements

  • Previous administration or office experience is desirable.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Good attention to detail.
  • Confident using Microsoft Office and computer systems.
  • Accurate data-entry and record-keeping skills.
  • Ability to prioritise tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Professional and positive approach to work.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday, 9:00am–5:00pm.
  • Full-time position.

Salary

  • £25,500 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional office environment.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Opportunity to develop administrative and organisational skills.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
