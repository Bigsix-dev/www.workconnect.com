Payroll Administrator

Job Description

Reed Specialist Recruitment Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading recruitment and employment agencies, providing workforce solutions across a wide range of industries. Reed Specialist Recruitment is seeking a detail-oriented Payroll Administrator to support accurate and timely payroll processing. In this role, you will maintain payroll records, process employee payments, ensure compliance with payroll legislation, and provide administrative support to the payroll and finance teams.

Responsibilities

  • Process weekly and monthly payroll accurately and on time.
  • Maintain payroll records, employee information, and payment data.
  • Calculate salaries, overtime, deductions, bonuses, and statutory payments.
  • Respond to payroll enquiries from employees and management.
  • Prepare payroll reports and supporting documentation.
  • Ensure compliance with HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) regulations and company payroll procedures.
  • Support pension administration and employee benefit processing.
  • Maintain confidentiality of payroll and employee information.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in payroll administration or finance administration.
  • Strong numerical and analytical skills.
  • Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
  • Experience using payroll software is desirable.
  • Strong organisational and time management skills.
  • Excellent communication and customer service abilities.
  • Ability to handle confidential information professionally.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard office hours.

Salary

  • £28,000–£34,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional payroll training and development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Flexible working opportunities where applicable.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Reed Specialist Recruitment.
