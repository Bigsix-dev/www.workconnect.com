Delivery Driver

Job Description

Ocado Logistics, part of Ocado Group PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's leading online grocery delivery companies, providing customers with high-quality products and exceptional home delivery services. Ocado Logistics is seeking a reliable and customer-focused Delivery Driver to join its growing logistics team. In this role, you will deliver customer grocery orders safely and on time, maintain excellent customer relationships, and represent Ocado with professionalism while ensuring compliance with company procedures and road safety regulations.

Responsibilities

  • Deliver online grocery orders to customers' homes safely and efficiently.
  • Load, organise, and secure customer orders before beginning each delivery route.
  • Carry out daily vehicle inspections and report any defects promptly.
  • Use handheld delivery devices to manage routes and confirm deliveries.
  • Provide friendly, professional, and courteous customer service.
  • Handle groceries carefully to maintain product quality during transportation.
  • Follow planned delivery schedules while meeting company performance targets.
  • Comply with company policies, road traffic laws, food safety standards, and health and safety procedures.

Requirements

  • Valid UK Category B driving licence.
  • Excellent driving record and commitment to safe driving.
  • Strong customer service and communication skills.
  • Ability to lift and carry grocery deliveries safely.
  • Good organisational and time management abilities.
  • Comfortable using GPS navigation systems and handheld delivery technology.
  • Ability to work independently and efficiently under time constraints.
  • Previous delivery driving or logistics experience is desirable but not essential.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including weekends, evenings, and public holidays.

Salary

  • £13.75–£16.25 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Comprehensive paid training and onboarding programme.
  • Employee discounts and reward schemes.
  • Health and wellbeing support services.
  • Modern delivery vehicles with advanced technology.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression within Ocado Group.
