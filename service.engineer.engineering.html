Service Engineer

Job Description

Engineering Services Company provides installation, maintenance, repair, and technical support services for engineering equipment and systems. The company is seeking a practical and customer-focused Service Engineer to install, inspect, maintain, and repair technical equipment at customer sites. You will be responsible for diagnosing faults, carrying out planned maintenance, and ensuring equipment operates safely and reliably.

Responsibilities

  • Install, commission, maintain, and repair engineering equipment and systems.
  • Attend customer sites to diagnose and resolve technical faults.
  • Carry out planned preventative maintenance and equipment inspections.
  • Identify mechanical, electrical, and technical problems and implement suitable solutions.
  • Complete service reports, maintenance records, and technical documentation.
  • Provide technical guidance and support to customers.
  • Ensure all service work is completed safely and in accordance with company procedures.
  • Work with engineering teams to investigate recurring equipment problems and improve reliability.

Requirements

  • Engineering qualification or equivalent technical experience.
  • Previous experience in field service, maintenance, installation, or engineering is desirable.
  • Good understanding of engineering equipment and technical systems.
  • Strong fault-finding and diagnostic skills.
  • Ability to read and interpret technical drawings and equipment documentation.
  • Strong practical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently and travel to customer locations when required.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £35,000–£50,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Technical service and equipment training.
  • Company vehicle or travel support where applicable.
  • Opportunities to work with a variety of engineering systems.
  • Career progression and professional development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
