Property Inspector

Job Description

LetCheck Inventories is seeking a reliable Property Inspector to carry out professional property inspections and prepare accurate reports for residential properties. The successful candidate will inspect properties, record their condition, identify issues, and provide clear and detailed documentation for landlords, tenants, and property professionals.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out residential property inspections and inventories.
  • Inspect rooms, fixtures, fittings, appliances, and general property condition.
  • Record property condition accurately and objectively.
  • Take appropriate photographs and supporting evidence during inspections.
  • Identify maintenance issues, damage, and areas requiring attention.
  • Prepare clear and detailed inspection reports.
  • Communicate professionally with landlords, tenants, agents, and other property representatives.
  • Complete inspections within agreed timescales.
  • Maintain accurate records and submit reports promptly.

Requirements

  • Previous property inspection, inventory, or property management experience is desirable.
  • Excellent attention to detail.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to produce accurate and objective reports.
  • Good organisational and time management skills.
  • Confident using mobile devices and digital reporting systems.
  • Professional and reliable approach.
  • Ability to work independently when carrying out property inspections.

Working Hours

  • Part-time position.
  • Work is based around individual property inspection assignments.

Salary

  • £35 per job.

Benefits

  • Flexible part-time working opportunity.
  • Opportunity to work independently.
  • Experience within the residential property inspection sector.
  • Opportunity to develop property inspection and reporting skills.
