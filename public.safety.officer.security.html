Public Safety Officer

Job Description

Local Authority works with residents, businesses, emergency services, and community organisations to promote safe and well-managed public spaces. The authority is seeking a professional Public Safety Officer to support local safety initiatives, monitor public areas, respond to concerns, and work with partner agencies to address risks. You will provide a visible presence in the community while promoting public safety and positive engagement.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct patrols and monitoring activities across designated public areas.
  • Identify and report safety hazards, antisocial behaviour, and other community concerns.
  • Provide a visible and approachable public safety presence.
  • Engage professionally with residents, businesses, visitors, and community groups.
  • Support local public safety campaigns and community initiatives.
  • Record incidents, observations, complaints, and other relevant information accurately.
  • Work with police, emergency services, council departments, and partner organisations.
  • Respond appropriately to incidents and escalate serious concerns through established procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in public safety, security, enforcement, community services, or customer service is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong awareness of community safety and public protection issues.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional in challenging situations.
  • Strong observation and situational awareness.
  • Good report writing and record-keeping skills.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively with partner organisations.
  • Reliable, professional, and committed approach to serving the local community.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Working hours may include evenings, weekends, and public holidays depending on operational requirements.

Salary

  • £28,000–£34,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive local authority salary.
  • Local government pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Public safety and community engagement training.
  • Opportunities to develop enforcement and public protection skills.
  • Stable public-sector working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
