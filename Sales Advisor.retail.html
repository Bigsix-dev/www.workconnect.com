Sales Advisor

Job Overview

As a Sales Advisor at H&M, you'll provide excellent customer service, assist shoppers with finding products, operate the tills, replenish stock, maintain attractive store displays, and help keep the store clean and organized. You'll work as part of a team to create a welcoming shopping experience while achieving store sales goals.

H&M stands for Hennes & Mauritz AB. It is a Swedish multinational fashion retailer founded in 1947, known for offering affordable clothing, accessories, footwear, beauty products, and home furnishings. H&M operates thousands of stores worldwide and is one of the largest fashion retail companies globally.

Key Responsibilities

  • Welcome and assist customers in a friendly and professional manner.
  • Provide product recommendations and styling advice.
  • Process purchases, refunds, and exchanges accurately.
  • Replenish stock and maintain merchandise displays.
  • Keep the sales floor and fitting rooms clean and organized.
  • Follow company health and safety procedures.
  • Support colleagues during busy periods.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Friendly, positive, and enthusiastic attitude.
  • Ability to work flexible shifts, including weekends.
  • Retail experience is an advantage but not always required.
  • Legal right to work in the UK.

Benefits

  • Employee discount on H&M products.
  • Paid holiday entitlement.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Comprehensive training and career development.
  • Flexible working schedules.
  • Opportunities for internal promotion.
