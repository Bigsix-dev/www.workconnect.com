Customer Service Administrator

Job Description

Reed is seeking an organised Customer Service Administrator to provide professional customer support while completing a range of administrative duties. The successful candidate will handle customer enquiries, maintain accurate records, process information, and work with colleagues to ensure customer requests are dealt with efficiently.

Responsibilities

  • Respond to customer enquiries by telephone, email, and other communication channels.
  • Provide accurate and professional information to customers.
  • Process customer requests and update internal systems.
  • Maintain accurate customer records and documentation.
  • Handle routine customer issues and escalate complex matters when required.
  • Liaise with internal departments to resolve customer queries.
  • Complete general administrative and data-entry tasks.
  • Monitor outstanding requests and follow up where necessary.
  • Maintain a high standard of customer service at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service or administrative experience is desirable.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Good attention to detail and accurate data-entry skills.
  • Confident using computers and standard office software.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Professional and customer-focused approach.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £13 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly rate.
  • Professional working environment.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Opportunity to develop customer service and administrative skills.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
