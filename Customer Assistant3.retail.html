Customer Assistant

Asda Stores Limited is one of the United Kingdom's largest supermarket retailers, offering groceries, fresh food, clothing, home products, and pharmacy services. Asda is seeking friendly and reliable Customer Assistants to provide outstanding customer service while supporting the day-to-day operation of the store and ensuring shelves remain fully stocked and well presented.

Responsibilities

  • Provide excellent customer service throughout the store.
  • Operate tills and process payments accurately.
  • Replenish shelves and rotate stock.
  • Assist customers with enquiries and product locations.
  • Support deliveries and stock organisation.
  • Maintain store cleanliness and safety standards.
  • Promote current offers and loyalty programmes.
  • Follow food safety and company procedures.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly and dependable attitude.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced retail environment.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Good teamwork and organisational abilities.
  • Flexible availability including weekends.
  • Retail experience is an advantage but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 36–38 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including weekends and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £12.45–£13.60 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discount programme.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive workplace.
