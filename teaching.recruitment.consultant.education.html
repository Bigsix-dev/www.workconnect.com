Teaching Recruitment Consultant

Job Description

GSL Education is a specialist education recruitment agency that connects schools with talented teachers, support staff, and education professionals. The company is seeking an enthusiastic and driven Teaching Recruitment Consultant to manage recruitment activities, develop strong relationships with schools, and support candidates throughout their education careers. In this role, you will identify recruitment needs, source qualified teaching professionals, and provide effective staffing solutions to schools.

Responsibilities

  • Recruit and place teachers, teaching assistants, and education support professionals.
  • Develop and maintain strong relationships with schools, academies, and education providers.
  • Source candidates through advertising, networking, referrals, and recruitment databases.
  • Conduct candidate interviews, screening, and compliance checks.
  • Manage the full recruitment process from registration to placement.
  • Provide guidance and support to candidates throughout their job search.
  • Maintain accurate candidate records and recruitment systems.
  • Work towards recruitment targets while delivering excellent client service.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in recruitment, sales, education, or customer service is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and relationship-building skills.
  • Strong negotiation and influencing abilities.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced, target-driven environment.
  • Good organisational skills with attention to detail.
  • Confident telephone and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to build strong relationships with candidates and schools.
  • Positive attitude with ambition to achieve career success.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday office-based working hours.

Salary

  • £25,000–£40,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Commission and performance-based incentives.
  • Comprehensive recruitment training and career development.
  • Supportive and ambitious team environment.
  • Opportunities for promotion into senior recruitment roles.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Company events and team rewards.
  • Opportunity to build a successful career within education recruitment.
