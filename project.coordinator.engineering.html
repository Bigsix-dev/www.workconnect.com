Project Coordinator (Engineering)

Job Description

Engineering Consultancy provides engineering design, technical consultancy, and project support services to clients across a range of industries. The company is seeking a highly organised Project Coordinator (Engineering) to support the planning, coordination, and administration of engineering projects. You will work closely with engineers, project managers, clients, contractors, and suppliers to help ensure projects remain organised, on schedule, and properly documented.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate day-to-day activities across engineering projects.
  • Maintain project schedules, action logs, records, and technical documentation.
  • Arrange project meetings and prepare agendas, minutes, and follow-up actions.
  • Monitor project progress and communicate updates to project teams.
  • Coordinate information between engineers, clients, contractors, and suppliers.
  • Assist project managers with reporting, resource planning, and project administration.
  • Track project deliverables, deadlines, and outstanding actions.
  • Support the preparation and organisation of project documentation and reports.

Requirements

  • Degree, diploma, or equivalent qualification in Engineering, Project Management, Business Administration, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience in project coordination, engineering administration, or a similar role is desirable.
  • Good understanding of engineering project environments.
  • Strong organisational and time-management skills.
  • Excellent attention to detail and record-keeping abilities.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office and project management tools.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £30,000–£40,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering consultancy salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Project coordination and professional training.
  • Exposure to a variety of engineering projects.
  • Supportive and collaborative consultancy environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
