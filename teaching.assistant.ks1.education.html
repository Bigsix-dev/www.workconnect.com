Teaching Assistant (KS1)

Job Description

Oaklands Infant School is a caring and inclusive primary school committed to providing a supportive learning environment where young children can develop confidence, independence, and essential skills. Oaklands Infant School is seeking a dedicated and enthusiastic Teaching Assistant (Key Stage 1) to support pupils in their early years of education. In this role, you will work closely with teachers to deliver engaging learning activities, provide individual support, and help create a positive classroom environment where children can thrive.

Responsibilities

  • Support teachers in delivering engaging Key Stage 1 learning activities.
  • Provide one-to-one and small-group support for pupils.
  • Assist children with reading, writing, numeracy, and classroom tasks.
  • Help prepare learning materials and maintain an organised classroom environment.
  • Encourage positive behaviour, confidence, and independence among pupils.
  • Support children with additional learning needs where required.
  • Observe and record pupil progress to support teaching plans.
  • Follow safeguarding procedures and promote children's wellbeing.

Requirements

  • Previous experience working with children in a school or childcare setting is desirable.
  • Understanding of Key Stage 1 learning and child development.
  • Caring, patient, and enthusiastic approach to supporting young learners.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with children and staff.
  • Knowledge of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Good organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Relevant Teaching Assistant or childcare qualification is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Part-time position.
  • 25 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £18,000–£22,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • School holiday periods and paid leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Supportive and friendly school environment.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for career growth within education.
  • Rewarding role supporting children's learning and development.
  • Inclusive and collaborative working environment.
