Design Manager

Job Description

A Main Construction Contractor is seeking an experienced Design Manager to oversee design coordination for construction projects across Essex and the Chelmsford area. The role involves coordinating design teams, consultants and contractors to ensure projects are delivered efficiently and in accordance with technical requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Manage and coordinate design activities throughout construction projects.
  • Coordinate architects, engineers, consultants and subcontractors.
  • Review drawings, specifications and technical documentation.
  • Monitor design programmes, deliverables and project requirements.
  • Identify and resolve design issues and technical coordination problems.
  • Ensure design information supports efficient project delivery.

Requirements

  • Proven experience as a Design Manager within the construction industry.
  • Strong understanding of construction design and technical processes.
  • Excellent coordination and project management skills.
  • Strong knowledge of drawings, specifications and construction documentation.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working arrangements based on project requirements.

Salary

  • £65,000–£75,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Senior design management opportunity.
  • Competitive salary.
  • Opportunity to work on major construction projects.
  • Career progression and professional development opportunities.
