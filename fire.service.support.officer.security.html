Fire Service Support Officer

Job Description

Essex County Fire & Rescue Service works to protect communities across Essex through emergency response, fire prevention, education, and community safety activities. The service is seeking a reliable Fire Service Support Officer to provide operational and administrative support to fire service teams. You will help coordinate activities, maintain accurate records, support fire prevention initiatives, and contribute to the effective delivery of fire and rescue services.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative and operational support to fire and rescue service teams.
  • Maintain accurate records, reports, schedules, and service documentation.
  • Assist with the coordination of fire prevention and community safety activities.
  • Support communication between operational teams, managers, and partner organisations.
  • Assist with the organisation of meetings, training activities, and operational events.
  • Process information accurately using relevant computer systems.
  • Support emergency service activities in accordance with assigned responsibilities.
  • Follow fire service health, safety, security, and operational procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous administrative, public service, emergency services, or operational support experience is desirable.
  • Good organisational and time management skills.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.
  • Strong computer and administrative skills.
  • Good attention to detail and accurate record-keeping abilities.
  • Ability to work calmly and effectively in a public service environment.
  • Ability to handle confidential and operational information appropriately.
  • Reliable, professional, and committed approach to public service.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule, with occasional flexibility for operational requirements.

Salary

  • £27,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive fire and rescue service salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Fire service and operational training opportunities.
  • Opportunities to support community safety initiatives.
  • Stable public-sector working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
