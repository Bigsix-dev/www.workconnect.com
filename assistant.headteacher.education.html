Assistant Headteacher

Job Description

Local Academy Trust is committed to delivering exceptional education through strong leadership, innovation, and continuous school improvement. The trust is seeking an inspirational Assistant Headteacher to support the Headteacher and Senior Leadership Team in raising educational standards, improving student outcomes, and fostering an inclusive school culture. In this strategic leadership role, you will contribute to whole-school development, lead curriculum and staff initiatives, and promote excellence in teaching and learning.

Responsibilities

  • Support the Headteacher in the strategic leadership and day-to-day management of the school.
  • Lead and monitor teaching, learning, and curriculum development across designated areas.
  • Promote high standards of behaviour, attendance, and student achievement.
  • Coach, mentor, and support teachers through performance management and professional development.
  • Monitor school improvement plans and implement strategies for continuous improvement.
  • Analyse student performance data to improve educational outcomes.
  • Ensure safeguarding, inclusion, and student wellbeing remain central to school operations.
  • Represent the school professionally with parents, governors, external agencies, and the wider community.

Requirements

  • Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) is required.
  • Substantial teaching experience with proven leadership responsibility.
  • Previous experience in middle or senior leadership within a school.
  • Strong understanding of curriculum development, school improvement, and educational leadership.
  • Excellent communication, leadership, and decision-making skills.
  • Ability to inspire staff and students while promoting a positive school culture.
  • Commitment to safeguarding and promoting student wellbeing.
  • Strong organisational, strategic planning, and people management abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time, with additional leadership responsibilities as required.

Salary

  • £55,000–£70,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive leadership salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme.
  • Generous annual leave and school holiday entitlement.
  • Leadership development and executive training opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Supportive senior leadership and collaborative working environment.
  • Opportunities for progression into Deputy Headteacher and Headteacher roles.
  • Access to modern teaching resources and professional networks.
