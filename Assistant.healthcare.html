Healthcare Assistant

Full Job Description

As a Healthcare Assistant (HCA) at Moulsham Home, you will support residents with their daily living needs while promoting dignity, independence, and well-being. You will work closely with nurses and senior care staff to provide compassionate, person-centred care in a residential setting.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist residents with personal care, including bathing, dressing, grooming, and toileting.
  • Help residents with mobility and transferring safely using appropriate equipment when required.
  • Support individuals with eating and drinking, ensuring nutritional needs are met.
  • Monitor residents' general health and report any changes or concerns to senior staff.
  • Provide companionship and emotional support to improve residents' quality of life.
  • Assist with medication administration where trained and authorised.
  • Maintain accurate care records and documentation.
  • Ensure a clean, safe, and comfortable living environment.
  • Follow infection prevention, health and safety, and safeguarding procedures at all times.
  • Work collaboratively with colleagues, families, and healthcare professionals.

About You

The ideal candidate should:

  • Be caring, patient, and compassionate.
  • Have good communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Be reliable and able to work effectively as part of a team.
  • Be willing to work flexible shifts, including weekends and holidays if required.
  • Have previous care experience (preferred but not always essential).
  • Hold or be willing to work towards a Health and Social Care qualification.
  • Have the legal right to work in the UK and be committed to providing high-quality care.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay (£11.80–£11.90 per hour).
  • Full-time, stable employment.
  • Paid training and ongoing professional development.
  • Opportunities for career progression into senior care roles.
  • Holiday entitlement and workplace pension scheme.
  • Supportive team environment.
  • Uniform provided (where applicable).
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing support.
  • Recognition and reward programmes for outstanding performance.
