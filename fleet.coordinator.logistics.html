Fleet Coordinator

Job Description

Royal Mail Group Limited is one of the United Kingdom's most established postal and logistics organisations, operating one of the largest commercial vehicle fleets in the country. Royal Mail is seeking an organised and proactive Fleet Coordinator to support the management of its vehicle fleet. In this role, you will coordinate vehicle maintenance, monitor fleet performance, maintain accurate records, and ensure vehicles remain roadworthy, compliant, and available to support daily postal and logistics operations.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate the daily operation and availability of the company vehicle fleet.
  • Schedule vehicle servicing, inspections, MOTs, repairs, and maintenance.
  • Maintain accurate fleet records, maintenance logs, and compliance documentation.
  • Monitor vehicle performance and report maintenance or operational issues.
  • Liaise with drivers, workshops, suppliers, and internal departments.
  • Track vehicle downtime and help minimise operational disruptions.
  • Prepare fleet reports and update fleet management systems.
  • Ensure compliance with company policies, DVSA regulations, and health and safety requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in fleet coordination, transport administration, or logistics operations.
  • Strong organisational and administrative skills.
  • Excellent communication and problem-solving abilities.
  • Good attention to detail and record-keeping skills.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
  • Experience using fleet management software is desirable.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Commitment to maintaining high operational and safety standards.

Working Hours

  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with flexibility depending on operational requirements.
