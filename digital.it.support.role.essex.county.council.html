Digital/IT Support Role

Job Description

Essex County Council is looking for a Digital/IT Support professional to help provide reliable technology services to users across the organisation. The role combines technical troubleshooting, user support and digital service delivery in a professional environment.

Responsibilities

  • Provide first-line and second-line technical support to users.
  • Investigate and resolve hardware, software and access issues.
  • Support users with digital systems, applications and workplace technology.
  • Log, manage and update technical support requests.
  • Escalate complex incidents to appropriate technical teams.
  • Assist with maintaining reliable and secure IT services.

Requirements

  • Experience in IT support, digital support or a similar role.
  • Good troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong communication and customer service abilities.
  • Comfortable working with computers, applications and IT systems.
  • Ability to prioritise and manage multiple support requests.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Standard professional working hours.

Salary

  • £30,685–£36,099 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to work within a large public-sector organisation.
  • Professional development and training opportunities.
  • Exposure to a wide range of digital and IT services.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
