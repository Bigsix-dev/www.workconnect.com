Customer Assistant – Operations

Job Description

Marks & Spencer is seeking a reliable Customer Assistant – Operations to support the smooth running of store operations while delivering excellent customer service. The role involves supporting stock and operational activities, maintaining high store standards, and assisting customers when required.

Responsibilities

  • Provide friendly and helpful customer service throughout the store.
  • Support stock delivery, replenishment, and organisation.
  • Receive and process deliveries in accordance with store procedures.
  • Maintain accurate stock levels and report discrepancies where required.
  • Keep stockrooms and operational areas organised and safe.
  • Replenish products efficiently and maintain presentation standards.
  • Assist customers with product enquiries and direct them to appropriate departments.
  • Support colleagues during busy trading periods.
  • Follow health, safety, security, and operational procedures.
  • Help maintain a clean, organised, and welcoming store environment.

Requirements

  • Previous retail or customer service experience is desirable.
  • Friendly and approachable communication style.
  • Good organisational and time management skills.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced retail environment.
  • Good attention to detail.
  • Ability to work as part of a team.
  • Reliable and flexible approach to working hours.
  • Ability to undertake operational and stock-related duties.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Varied shifts depending on store requirements.
  • Flexibility to work different shift patterns may be required.

Salary

  • £13.41 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly rate.
  • Employee retail benefits.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Experience within a major retail environment.
  • Opportunities for career development and progression.
