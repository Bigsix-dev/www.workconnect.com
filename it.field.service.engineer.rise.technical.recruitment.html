IT Field Service Engineer

Job Description

Rise Technical Recruitment is recruiting an IT Field Service Engineer to provide on-site technical support and maintenance for IT systems and equipment. The successful candidate will travel to customer locations, diagnose technical faults, install and configure equipment, and ensure users receive reliable technical support.

Responsibilities

  • Provide on-site technical support at customer locations.
  • Install, configure, maintain, and repair IT hardware and systems.
  • Diagnose and resolve hardware, software, and connectivity problems.
  • Carry out scheduled maintenance and technical inspections.
  • Configure computers, peripherals, networking equipment, and other devices.
  • Record completed service activities and technical findings.
  • Provide clear technical guidance to customers and end users.
  • Escalate complex technical problems to specialist teams when required.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in IT support, field service, or technical engineering.
  • Good understanding of computer hardware and software.
  • Knowledge of networking and connectivity troubleshooting.
  • Strong diagnostic and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent customer service and communication abilities.
  • Ability to travel to different customer locations.
  • Self-motivated and capable of working independently.
  • Relevant IT or technical qualifications are desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours may vary according to customer and service requirements.
  • Regular travel to client sites is required.

Salary

  • £35,000–£40,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary.
  • Field-based technical experience.
  • Training and professional development.
  • Opportunities to work with different technologies and systems.
  • Career progression within technical support and engineering.
  • Varied working environment.
