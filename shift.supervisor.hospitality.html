Shift Supervisor

Job Description

Starbucks Coffee Company (UK) Limited is one of the world's leading coffeehouse brands, recognised for its premium coffee, handcrafted beverages, and exceptional customer service. Starbucks is seeking an experienced and motivated Shift Supervisor to lead the store team during assigned shifts. In this role, you will support the Store Manager, supervise daily operations, ensure excellent customer service, and maintain Starbucks' high standards of quality, cleanliness, and operational excellence.

Responsibilities

  • Lead and supervise the café team during assigned shifts.
  • Deliver outstanding customer service and ensure every customer enjoys the Starbucks Experience.
  • Prepare and serve handcrafted beverages and food products to company standards.
  • Support staff training, coaching, and daily performance.
  • Handle customer enquiries, complaints, and resolve issues professionally.
  • Monitor stock levels and assist with inventory management.
  • Complete opening and closing procedures, including cash handling and store security.
  • Ensure compliance with food hygiene regulations, health and safety standards, and Starbucks policies.

Requirements

  • Previous supervisory experience in a café, retail, or hospitality environment.
  • Excellent leadership and communication skills.
  • Strong customer service and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment.
  • Good organisational and time management skills.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling experience.
  • Flexible availability, including mornings, evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • A positive attitude and commitment to maintaining Starbucks' high service standards.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns, including weekends and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £14.00–£16.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free drinks during shifts and discounts on food.
  • Employee discounts at Starbucks stores.
  • Leadership training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive, inclusive workplace with excellent opportunities for career progression.
