Customer Assistant

Farmfoods Limited is a leading UK frozen food and grocery retailer, offering customers a wide selection of frozen foods, fresh groceries, household essentials, and everyday products at competitive prices. Farmfoods is seeking reliable and customer-focused Customer Assistants to support store operations while delivering excellent customer service and maintaining high retail standards.

Responsibilities

  • Provide friendly and professional customer service.
  • Operate tills and process customer payments accurately.
  • Replenish shelves, freezers, and chilled displays.
  • Assist customers with enquiries and product locations.
  • Receive and organise deliveries.
  • Maintain store cleanliness and food safety standards.
  • Rotate stock and monitor product freshness.
  • Follow company procedures and health and safety guidelines.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly, dependable, and hardworking attitude.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced retail environment.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Good organisational and teamwork abilities.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends.
  • Retail experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns.

Salary

  • £12.25–£13.40 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discount.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance programme.
  • Supportive team environment.
