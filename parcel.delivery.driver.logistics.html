Parcel Delivery Driver

Job Description

Royal Mail Group Limited is one of the United Kingdom's most recognised delivery and logistics organisations, providing reliable postal and parcel services across the country. Royal Mail is seeking a dependable and customer-focused Parcel Delivery Driver to join its delivery team. In this role, you will deliver parcels safely and efficiently to homes and businesses while providing excellent customer service and representing the company professionally.

Responsibilities

  • Deliver parcels and mail items safely to residential and business addresses.
  • Follow allocated delivery routes and complete deliveries within scheduled times.
  • Load, organise, and secure parcels before starting delivery rounds.
  • Carry out vehicle safety checks before and after each shift.
  • Use handheld devices to record deliveries and update delivery information.
  • Provide friendly and professional service to customers during deliveries.
  • Handle parcels carefully to prevent damage or loss.
  • Follow Royal Mail procedures, road safety regulations, and health and safety policies.

Requirements

  • Valid UK Category B driving licence.
  • Good driving record with commitment to safe driving practices.
  • Ability to lift and carry parcels of different sizes and weights.
  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently and manage delivery schedules.
  • Good knowledge of local routes is desirable.
  • Comfortable using handheld delivery technology.
  • Previous delivery or logistics experience is beneficial but not essential.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including weekends and peak delivery periods.

Salary

  • £13.05–£14.75 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts and reward programmes.
  • Comprehensive training and driver support.
  • Health and wellbeing benefits.
  • Opportunities for career development within Royal Mail Group.
  • Stable employment with one of the UK's leading delivery companies.
