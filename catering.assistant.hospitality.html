Catering Assistant

Job Description

Elior UK is one of the United Kingdom's leading contract catering and hospitality providers, delivering high-quality food services across schools, workplaces, healthcare facilities, and leisure venues. Elior UK is seeking a friendly, reliable, and customer-focused Catering Assistant to join its catering team. In this role, you will support the preparation and service of food and beverages, maintain high standards of cleanliness, and help deliver an excellent dining experience while ensuring compliance with food safety and hygiene regulations.

Responsibilities

  • Assist with the preparation and serving of food and beverages.
  • Set up and replenish food counters, serving stations, and dining areas.
  • Welcome customers and provide excellent customer service.
  • Operate tills and process customer transactions accurately.
  • Wash kitchen utensils, crockery, and catering equipment.
  • Maintain cleanliness of kitchen, dining, and food preparation areas.
  • Receive and store deliveries according to food safety procedures.
  • Follow food hygiene regulations, health and safety policies, and company procedures at all times.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Friendly, reliable, and positive attitude.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a busy catering environment.
  • Good teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Flexible availability to work different shifts when required.
  • Previous catering or hospitality experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 30–37.5 hours per week.
  • Shift patterns may include mornings, afternoons, weekends, and public holidays depending on the location.

Salary

  • £12.30–£13.60 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted meals during shifts where applicable.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Employee discounts through company benefit schemes.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment with opportunities for career progression.
