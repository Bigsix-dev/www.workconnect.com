Waiter/Waitress

Job Description

Miller & Carter, part of Mitchells & Butlers PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's leading premium steakhouse restaurant brands, renowned for serving expertly prepared steaks, high-quality food, and exceptional customer service. Miller & Carter is seeking friendly, professional, and enthusiastic Waiters/Waitresses to join its hospitality team. As a Waiter/Waitress, you will provide outstanding table service, ensure guests enjoy an exceptional dining experience, and contribute to maintaining the restaurant's high standards of hospitality.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome guests warmly and escort them to their tables when required.
  • Present menus and explain food, drinks, and daily specials.
  • Take customer food and beverage orders accurately.
  • Serve meals and drinks promptly while maintaining excellent service standards.
  • Check on guests during their meals and respond to requests professionally.
  • Prepare and clear tables before and after service.
  • Process customer payments accurately using the point-of-sale (POS) system.
  • Follow food hygiene, health and safety, and company procedures at all times.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Friendly, confident, and customer-focused attitude.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a busy restaurant environment.
  • Strong teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Professional appearance and positive attitude.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Previous hospitality or restaurant experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Comprehensive training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns, including evenings and weekends.

Salary

  • £12.40–£13.80 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Discounted meals during shifts.
  • Employee discounts across Mitchells & Butlers restaurants.
  • Industry-leading training and career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Supportive, inclusive, and professional working environment.
