Breakfast Club Assistant

Job Description

Primary Schools Chelmsford are committed to providing a safe, friendly, and supportive environment where children can begin their school day positively. Schools across Chelmsford are seeking a caring and enthusiastic Breakfast Club Assistant to support pupils before the start of the school day. In this role, you will help supervise children, organise activities, support breakfast routines, and create a welcoming atmosphere for pupils.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise children attending the school breakfast club.
  • Support pupils during breakfast time and ensure safe food practices.
  • Organise fun, educational, and engaging activities for children.
  • Encourage positive behaviour and promote a friendly environment.
  • Support children's social development and wellbeing.
  • Ensure children are safely prepared for the school day.
  • Maintain a clean, organised, and safe breakfast club area.
  • Follow safeguarding procedures and school policies at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous experience working with children in a school, childcare, or youth setting is desirable.
  • Friendly, patient, and caring approach towards children.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to supervise children and manage behaviour positively.
  • Understanding of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Ability to create engaging activities for primary school pupils.
  • Reliable and punctual approach to work.
  • Relevant childcare or education qualification is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Part-time position.
  • 15 hours per week.
  • Morning shifts before the start of the school day.

Salary

  • £12.50–£14.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • School holiday periods and paid leave entitlement.
  • Training and professional development opportunities.
  • Supportive school team environment.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for progression within education and childcare services.
  • Rewarding role supporting children's early development.
  • Flexible part-time working hours.
