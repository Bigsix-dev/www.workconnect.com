Registered Mental Health Nurse (RMN)

Job Overview

A Registered Mental Health Nurse (RMN) provides specialist nursing care and support to individuals experiencing mental health conditions. The role involves assessing patients, developing care plans, administering treatments, promoting recovery, and working closely with multidisciplinary teams to deliver safe and compassionate care.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct comprehensive mental health assessments.
  • Develop, implement, and review individualized care plans.
  • Administer medications and monitor their effectiveness and side effects.
  • Support patients through therapeutic interventions and recovery-focused care.
  • Assess and manage risks, including safeguarding concerns.
  • Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
  • Collaborate with psychiatrists, psychologists, social workers, and other healthcare professionals.
  • Provide emotional support and education to patients and their families.
  • Respond appropriately to mental health crises and emergencies.
  • Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and professional standards.

Required Skills and Qualifications

  • Valid registration as a Registered Mental Health Nurse.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and make sound decisions under pressure.
  • Knowledge of mental health legislation and safeguarding practices.
  • Excellent observation, assessment, and documentation skills.
  • Compassion, empathy, and a patient-centred approach.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a multidisciplinary team.

Typical Working Hours

  • Full-time position, generally 37.5 hours per week.
  • May include day shifts, night shifts, weekends, bank holidays, and rotational schedules, depending on service requirements.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave and pension scheme.
  • Ongoing professional development and training opportunities.
  • Career progression within mental health services.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
  • Access to NHS or employer-specific staff benefits, where applicable.
