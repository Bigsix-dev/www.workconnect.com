Head of Department – English

Job Description

Chelmsford Secondary School is committed to delivering outstanding education and fostering a culture of academic excellence, creativity, and continuous improvement. The school is seeking an experienced and inspirational Head of Department – English to lead the English faculty, drive high standards of teaching and learning, and ensure outstanding student achievement. In this leadership role, you will manage the department, support teaching staff, oversee curriculum development, and contribute to the school's strategic objectives.

Responsibilities

  • Lead and manage the English Department to achieve excellent academic outcomes.
  • Develop, implement, and review the English curriculum in line with national standards.
  • Monitor teaching quality and support staff through coaching and professional development.
  • Teach English lessons while maintaining a reduced leadership timetable.
  • Analyse student performance data and implement strategies to improve attainment.
  • Manage departmental budgets, resources, and curriculum planning.
  • Promote high standards of behaviour, inclusion, and student wellbeing.
  • Contribute to whole-school leadership, policy development, inspections, and improvement initiatives.

Requirements

  • Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) is required.
  • Bachelor's degree in English or a related subject.
  • Significant experience teaching English at secondary school level.
  • Previous middle leadership or departmental leadership experience is highly desirable.
  • Excellent leadership, communication, and people management skills.
  • Strong knowledge of curriculum planning, assessment, and school improvement.
  • Commitment to safeguarding and promoting student wellbeing.
  • Ability to inspire staff and students while driving continuous improvement.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £45,000–£60,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive leadership salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme.
  • Paid school holidays and generous annual leave.
  • Leadership development and continuous professional training.
  • Supportive senior leadership team and collaborative working environment.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression into senior leadership.
  • Access to modern teaching resources and school facilities.
