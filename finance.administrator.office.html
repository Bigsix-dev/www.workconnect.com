Finance Administrator

Job Description

BDO LLP (Limited Liability Partnership) is one of the United Kingdom's leading accountancy and business advisory firms, providing audit, tax, consulting, and financial services to businesses across a wide range of industries. BDO LLP is seeking an organised and detail-oriented Finance Administrator to support its finance operations. In this role, you will assist with financial administration, maintain accurate financial records, process transactions, and provide administrative support to the finance team while ensuring compliance with company procedures and financial regulations.

Responsibilities

  • Process invoices, purchase orders, and financial transactions accurately.
  • Maintain financial records, spreadsheets, and accounting documentation.
  • Assist with bank reconciliations and expense processing.
  • Support month-end and year-end financial administrative activities.
  • Prepare financial reports and administrative documentation.
  • Communicate with suppliers, clients, and internal departments regarding finance enquiries.
  • Maintain confidentiality of financial and client information.
  • Ensure compliance with company financial procedures and regulatory requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in finance administration, accounting support, or office administration.
  • Excellent numerical skills and attention to detail.
  • Good organisational and time management abilities.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel.
  • Experience using accounting or finance software is desirable.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to manage confidential financial information professionally.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a finance team.

Working Hours

  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

Benefits
