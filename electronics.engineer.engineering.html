Electronics Engineer

Job Description

Electronics Manufacturing Company designs and manufactures electronic products and components for a variety of industrial and commercial applications. The company is seeking an Electronics Engineer to support the design, development, testing, and improvement of electronic systems. You will work alongside engineering and manufacturing teams to develop reliable products and resolve technical issues throughout the production process.

Responsibilities

  • Design and develop electronic circuits, systems, and components.
  • Produce and maintain electronic schematics and technical documentation.
  • Build and test electronic prototypes and assemblies.
  • Carry out testing and troubleshooting of electronic products.
  • Investigate technical faults and implement effective solutions.
  • Work with manufacturing teams to support the production of electronic products.
  • Evaluate components and recommend suitable technical solutions.
  • Support continuous improvement and product development projects.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Electronic Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience in electronics design or manufacturing is desirable.
  • Good understanding of analogue and digital electronic circuits.
  • Ability to read and interpret electronic schematics and technical drawings.
  • Experience with electronic testing and troubleshooting.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Good attention to detail and technical accuracy.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £40,000–£60,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Electronics design and technical training.
  • Opportunities to work on new product development projects.
  • Modern engineering and manufacturing environment.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
