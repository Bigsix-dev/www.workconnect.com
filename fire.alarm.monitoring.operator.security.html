Fire Alarm Monitoring Operator

Job Description

Security Services Company provides professional monitoring and security services for commercial, industrial, and public-sector clients. The company is seeking a vigilant Fire Alarm Monitoring Operator to monitor fire alarm systems, respond to alerts, coordinate appropriate assistance, and maintain accurate records. You will play an important role in ensuring alarm activations are assessed and escalated promptly in accordance with established procedures.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor fire alarm systems and associated monitoring equipment.
  • Receive, assess, and respond to fire alarm activations and system alerts.
  • Follow established procedures for verifying and escalating alarm events.
  • Contact emergency services, clients, engineers, or response teams when required.
  • Monitor system faults and report technical issues to the appropriate teams.
  • Maintain accurate records of alarm activations, responses, and incidents.
  • Communicate effectively with customers, security personnel, engineers, and emergency services.
  • Ensure all monitoring procedures and service standards are consistently followed.

Requirements

  • Previous control room, alarm monitoring, security, or emergency services experience is desirable.
  • Good understanding of fire alarm and monitoring procedures.
  • Excellent concentration, observation, and attention to detail.
  • Strong communication and computer skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and make appropriate decisions under pressure.
  • Good record-keeping and incident reporting abilities.
  • Ability to follow detailed procedures accurately.
  • Reliable and capable of working effectively as part of a monitoring team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working may include nights, weekends, and public holidays.

Salary

  • £26,000–£31,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive monitoring operations salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Fire alarm and control room training.
  • Opportunities for additional security and monitoring qualifications.
  • Professional monitoring environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
