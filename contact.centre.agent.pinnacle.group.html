Contact Centre Agent

Job Description

Pinnacle Group is seeking a professional Contact Centre Agent to provide efficient and customer-focused support through telephone and other communication channels. The successful candidate will handle enquiries, provide accurate information, resolve issues, maintain customer records, and ensure every interaction is handled professionally.

Responsibilities

  • Handle inbound customer enquiries professionally and efficiently.
  • Provide accurate information and appropriate assistance to customers.
  • Listen carefully to customer needs and identify suitable solutions.
  • Resolve customer issues and escalate complex matters when required.
  • Record customer interactions accurately on internal systems.
  • Process customer requests and update relevant information.
  • Follow contact centre procedures and service standards.
  • Maintain confidentiality when handling customer information.
  • Work with colleagues and other departments to resolve customer queries.
  • Meet relevant quality, productivity, and customer service standards.

Requirements

  • Previous contact centre or customer service experience is desirable.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Strong listening and problem-solving abilities.
  • Confident using computers and customer management systems.
  • Good attention to detail and accurate data-entry skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional under pressure.
  • Customer-focused approach with a positive attitude.
  • Ability to work effectively in a busy contact centre environment.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £23,192 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional contact centre environment.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Customer service and communication skills development.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
