Property & Garden Multi Trader

Job Description

Spacio is looking for a practical and reliable Property & Garden Multi Trader to carry out general maintenance, repair, improvement, and garden-related work across residential and property environments. The successful candidate will be comfortable working independently, managing a variety of tasks, and maintaining high standards of workmanship and customer service.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out general property maintenance and repair work.
  • Complete minor plumbing, carpentry, decorating, and repair tasks.
  • Undertake garden maintenance and outdoor improvement work.
  • Identify maintenance issues and recommend appropriate solutions.
  • Use tools and equipment safely and responsibly.
  • Maintain clean, safe, and professional work areas.
  • Communicate effectively with customers, tenants, and property representatives.
  • Complete assigned jobs within agreed timescales.

Requirements

  • Practical experience in property maintenance, gardening, construction, or a related trade.
  • Good range of hands-on repair and maintenance skills.
  • Ability to work independently and manage multiple tasks.
  • Good attention to detail and quality workmanship.
  • Strong reliability and timekeeping.
  • Ability to follow health and safety procedures.
  • Professional communication and customer service skills.
  • Relevant trade qualifications are desirable but not essential where practical experience is strong.

Working Hours

  • Full-time or part-time position.
  • Working hours may vary according to property and maintenance requirements.
  • Flexible scheduling may be available.

Salary

  • Salary not stated; dependent on experience and agreed working arrangement.

Benefits

  • Varied property and outdoor maintenance work.
  • Flexible working opportunities.
  • Opportunities for ongoing practical experience.
  • Professional working environment.
  • Potential for additional work and career development.
