Customer Service Advisor

Halfords Group PLC (Public Limited Company) is the UK's leading retailer of motoring and cycling products, offering vehicle servicing, repairs, bicycles, accessories, and automotive equipment. Halfords is seeking friendly and knowledgeable Customer Service Advisors to deliver exceptional customer experiences while supporting sales and the smooth operation of the store. This role is ideal for individuals who enjoy helping customers and have an interest in motoring or cycling products.

Responsibilities

  • Provide outstanding customer service in person and over the telephone.
  • Assist customers with product recommendations and service bookings.
  • Process sales, returns, exchanges, and payments accurately.
  • Respond to customer enquiries and resolve issues professionally.
  • Promote company products, services, and promotional offers.
  • Maintain attractive product displays and merchandising standards.
  • Support stock replenishment and inventory management.
  • Follow company policies, health and safety regulations, and customer service standards.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong customer service and problem-solving abilities.
  • Basic computer and cash-handling skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced retail environment.
  • Interest in motoring or cycling products is an advantage.
  • Friendly, professional, and reliable attitude.
  • Previous retail or customer service experience is preferred but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including weekends and public holidays.

Salary

  • £24,500–£28,500 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts on products and services.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Opportunities for career progression.
