Customer Assistant

Morrisons Supermarkets PLC is one of the UK’s largest supermarket chains offering fresh food, groceries, and household essentials. Morrisons is looking for Customer Assistants to support store operations and deliver excellent service.

Responsibilities

  • Serve customers at the checkout.
  • Restock shelves and maintain displays.
  • Assist with customer enquiries.
  • Maintain cleanliness of store areas.
  • Support stock rotation and deliveries.
  • Ensure pricing and labels are correct.
  • Follow safety and hygiene standards.
  • Work as part of a team.

Requirements

  • Good communication skills.
  • Friendly and helpful attitude.
  • Ability to work under pressure.
  • Basic numeracy skills.
  • Teamwork ability.
  • Flexible schedule.
  • No experience required.
  • Training provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.

Salary

  • £12.40–£13.60 per hour.

Benefits

  • Staff discounts.
  • Pension scheme.
  • Paid holidays.
  • Training provided.
  • Career progression.
