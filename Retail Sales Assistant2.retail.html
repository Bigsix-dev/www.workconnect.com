Retail Sales Assistant

Sports Direct International plc is one of the United Kingdom's largest sporting goods retailers, offering sportswear, footwear, fitness equipment, and accessories from leading global brands. Sports Direct is seeking energetic Retail Sales Assistants to provide outstanding customer service, achieve sales targets, and support the daily operation of the store.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide expert product advice.
  • Assist customers in selecting sportswear and equipment.
  • Process sales and returns accurately.
  • Maintain stock levels and attractive merchandise displays.
  • Support promotional campaigns and store merchandising.
  • Keep the sales floor clean and organised.
  • Work towards individual and team sales targets.
  • Follow company policies and health and safety procedures.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Passion for sports and retail.
  • Friendly and enthusiastic attitude.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Flexible availability including weekends.
  • Retail or sales experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including weekends and public holidays.

Salary

  • £12.25–£13.40 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Performance-related incentives.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discount.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
