Endoscopy Assistant

Job Overview

Springfield Hospital is seeking a dedicated Endoscopy Assistant to support the smooth operation of its endoscopy unit and help deliver high-quality patient care. This role involves assisting clinical staff before, during, and after endoscopic procedures, preparing equipment and treatment rooms, maintaining hygiene standards, and ensuring patients feel informed and comfortable throughout their visit.

This is an excellent opportunity for individuals with strong organisational skills, attention to detail, and a genuine passion for healthcare. Previous experience in a clinical or healthcare support environment is beneficial but not always essential, as training may be provided.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist nurses and clinicians during endoscopy procedures.
  • Prepare, clean, and restock procedure rooms and medical equipment.
  • Welcome and support patients before and after examinations.
  • Monitor and maintain infection prevention and control standards.
  • Safely handle and decontaminate endoscopy instruments in line with protocols.
  • Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
  • Ensure consumables and supplies are adequately stocked.
  • Work collaboratively with the wider multidisciplinary team to provide efficient patient care.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in a healthcare support or clinical environment is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional in a fast-paced setting.
  • Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Commitment to maintaining patient confidentiality and dignity.
  • Willingness to undertake relevant training and comply with healthcare regulations.
  • Right to work in the UK and satisfactory pre-employment checks.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Full-time, stable employment with opportunities for career progression.
  • Ongoing training and professional development.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Pension scheme and employee benefits package.
  • Opportunity to contribute to outstanding patient care within a respected healthcare team.

How to Apply

Interested candidates should submit an up-to-date CV along with a tailored cover letter highlighting their relevant skills and experience. Applications should include current contact details, employment history, and any applicable healthcare qualifications or certifications. Shortlisted applicants may be invited to attend an interview and complete the necessary pre-employment checks in accordance with UK healthcare recruitment standards.
