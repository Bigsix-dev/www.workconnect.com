Solutions Architect

Job Description

Essex County Council is looking for an experienced Solutions Architect to design and support technology solutions that meet complex organisational and business requirements. The role involves working with technical and business stakeholders to develop scalable, secure, and sustainable IT architectures. The successful candidate will help shape technology strategy while ensuring that solutions integrate effectively with existing systems and services.

Responsibilities

  • Design technology solutions aligned with organisational requirements and objectives.
  • Develop architectural designs, technical specifications, and solution documentation.
  • Evaluate existing systems and recommend appropriate improvements.
  • Work with developers, infrastructure teams, suppliers, and business stakeholders.
  • Ensure proposed solutions meet security, performance, and scalability requirements.
  • Support the implementation and delivery of complex technology projects.
  • Identify technical risks and recommend appropriate mitigation strategies.
  • Contribute to technology standards, architecture principles, and governance.

Requirements

  • Professional experience in solutions architecture or a related IT architecture role.
  • Strong understanding of enterprise technology environments.
  • Knowledge of cloud, infrastructure, applications, integration, and security concepts.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Experience producing technical architecture documentation.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities.
  • Ability to translate business requirements into practical technical solutions.
  • Relevant architecture, cloud, or IT qualifications are desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Standard professional working hours.
  • Hybrid or flexible arrangements may be available depending on organisational requirements.

Salary

  • £51,563–£60,662 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive public-sector salary.
  • Government pension arrangements.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Professional development opportunities.
  • Access to specialist technology training.
  • Opportunities to work on major digital transformation projects.
  • Career progression opportunities.
