Operational Support Grade

Job Description

HM Prison Service provides operational support across prisons and custodial facilities, helping maintain safe, secure, and well-organised environments. The organisation is seeking an Operational Support Grade employee to assist with essential prison operations, security duties, administration, and the safe movement of people and goods. You will work closely with prison officers and other staff to support the effective day-to-day running of the establishment.

Responsibilities

  • Support the safe and secure operation of prison facilities.
  • Assist with the movement and supervision of prisoners in accordance with procedures.
  • Carry out security checks, searches, and access control duties as required.
  • Support the delivery and collection of authorised goods and supplies.
  • Monitor designated areas and report security concerns or unusual activity.
  • Assist with administrative and operational tasks within the prison.
  • Maintain accurate records and complete relevant documentation.
  • Follow prison security, health and safety, and operational procedures at all times.

Requirements

  • No specific degree is required for the role.
  • Previous experience in security, logistics, public service, administration, or customer service is desirable.
  • Ability to follow instructions and established security procedures.
  • Good communication and teamwork skills.
  • Strong reliability, punctuality, and personal responsibility.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional in challenging situations.
  • Good observation and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work effectively in a structured custodial environment.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working arrangements may include weekends and public holidays.

Salary

  • £27,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive public-sector salary.
  • Government workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Structured operational and security training.
  • Opportunities to develop skills within the prison service.
  • Stable public-sector working environment.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
