Technical Project Engineer

Job Description

Engineering Consultancy provides specialist engineering solutions, technical expertise, and project support to clients across various industries. The company is seeking an experienced and organised Technical Project Engineer to manage engineering projects, coordinate technical activities, and support successful project delivery. In this role, you will work with engineering teams, clients, and stakeholders to deliver high-quality solutions while ensuring projects are completed safely, efficiently, and within agreed requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Manage and support engineering projects from initial concept through to completion.
  • Coordinate technical activities between engineering teams, suppliers, and clients.
  • Prepare project plans, technical documentation, and progress reports.
  • Monitor project timelines, budgets, and engineering deliverables.
  • Identify technical challenges and provide effective engineering solutions.
  • Support design reviews, testing, and project validation activities.
  • Communicate project updates with internal teams and external stakeholders.
  • Ensure projects meet quality standards, safety requirements, and customer expectations.

Requirements

  • Degree in Engineering or a related technical discipline.
  • Previous experience in project engineering or technical project management.
  • Strong understanding of engineering processes and project delivery.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple engineering tasks and priorities.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Experience using project management tools and engineering documentation systems is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £45,000–£65,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Professional project management and engineering training.
  • Opportunities to work on diverse engineering projects.
  • Supportive technical and project teams.
  • Career progression opportunities within engineering consultancy.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
