Service Desk Engineer

Job Description

Various Employers across Chelmsford are recruiting Service Desk Engineers to provide technical support and help maintain reliable IT services. This role combines customer support with hands-on troubleshooting across workplace technology and business systems.

Responsibilities

  • Provide first-line and technical service desk support to users.
  • Investigate and resolve hardware, software and connectivity incidents.
  • Configure and support computers, peripherals and business applications.
  • Manage support tickets and maintain accurate incident records.
  • Escalate complex problems to appropriate technical teams.
  • Assist with system upgrades, deployments and other IT activities.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in IT support, service desk or technical support.
  • Good understanding of desktop hardware, software and networking.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent communication and customer service abilities.
  • Ability to work effectively in a busy technical support environment.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours may vary according to the employer and support requirements.

Salary

  • Around £20,934–£27,831 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunities to develop technical support expertise.
  • Professional training and development.
  • Potential progression into infrastructure and systems roles.
  • Exposure to a variety of business technologies.
