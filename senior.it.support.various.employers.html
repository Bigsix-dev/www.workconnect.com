Senior IT Support

Job Description

Various Employers in the Chelmsford area are seeking experienced Senior IT Support professionals to provide advanced technical assistance and help maintain reliable IT operations. These positions are suited to candidates who can handle complex incidents and support users across business environments.

Responsibilities

  • Provide advanced technical support for hardware, software and network issues.
  • Investigate and resolve complex IT incidents.
  • Support junior IT staff and provide technical guidance where required.
  • Manage user accounts, devices and business applications.
  • Monitor recurring technical problems and recommend improvements.
  • Assist with IT projects, system upgrades and technology deployments.

Requirements

  • Strong previous experience in IT support or systems support.
  • Good understanding of desktop systems, networks and business applications.
  • Excellent troubleshooting and problem-solving abilities.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Ability to work independently while supporting wider IT teams.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours may vary depending on the employer.

Salary

  • Around £35,577 per year average.

Benefits

  • Opportunities to work with a variety of IT environments.
  • Professional development and technical training.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Exposure to more advanced IT systems and responsibilities.
