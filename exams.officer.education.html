Exams Officer

Job Description

Secondary School is committed to providing students with a supportive learning environment and ensuring effective academic administration. The school is seeking an organised and detail-focused Exams Officer to manage the administration and coordination of internal and external examinations. In this role, you will oversee examination processes, maintain accurate records, support students and staff, and ensure compliance with examination regulations.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate and manage all aspects of school examination processes.
  • Schedule examinations and prepare examination timetables.
  • Register students for examinations and maintain accurate records.
  • Liaise with examination boards, teachers, students, and parents.
  • Ensure examinations are conducted in line with regulatory requirements.
  • Prepare examination rooms, materials, and supporting documentation.
  • Manage examination results, certificates, and related administration.
  • Maintain confidentiality and security of examination information.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in examinations administration, school administration, or a similar role.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to manage deadlines and work effectively under pressure.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office applications.
  • Strong communication skills when working with students, staff, and external organisations.
  • Understanding of examination regulations and safeguarding procedures is desirable.
  • Ability to handle confidential information professionally.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school working hours, with flexibility during examination periods.

Salary

  • £25,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Local Government Pension Scheme.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Supportive school working environment.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for career progression within education administration.
  • Rewarding role supporting student success.
