Prison Officer

Job Description

HM Prison Service operates prisons across England and Wales and works to maintain safe, secure, and rehabilitative custodial environments. The service is seeking professional and resilient Prison Officers to supervise prisoners, maintain security, and support rehabilitation. You will work as part of a dedicated team to maintain order, enforce prison rules, respond to incidents, and support the wellbeing and rehabilitation of prisoners.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain the safety, security, and good order of the prison environment.
  • Supervise and support prisoners throughout their daily activities.
  • Carry out security checks, searches, and monitoring procedures as required.
  • Escort prisoners safely between authorised areas and appointments.
  • Respond appropriately to incidents, emergencies, and challenging behaviour.
  • Maintain accurate records, reports, and relevant prison documentation.
  • Build professional relationships with prisoners while maintaining appropriate boundaries.
  • Support rehabilitation, education, and resettlement activities where required.

Requirements

  • No specific degree is required; relevant experience and the ability to meet the role's entry requirements are important.
  • Previous experience in security, public service, customer service, or a challenging environment is desirable.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and make sound decisions under pressure.
  • Strong personal integrity, responsibility, and professionalism.
  • Ability to follow security procedures and prison regulations.
  • Good observation and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a diverse team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working schedule, including weekends and public holidays where required.

Salary

  • £35,000–£40,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive public-sector salary.
  • Government workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Comprehensive prison officer training.
  • Opportunities for career development and specialist training.
  • Stable public-sector employment.
  • Opportunities to progress into specialist and leadership roles.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
