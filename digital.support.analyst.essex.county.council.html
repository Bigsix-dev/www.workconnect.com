Digital Support Analyst

Job Description

Essex County Council is seeking a capable and customer-focused Digital Support Analyst to provide technical assistance and support for digital systems and services. The successful candidate will investigate technical issues, assist users, monitor systems, and contribute to the smooth operation of digital technology across the organisation. You will work closely with technical teams and service users to resolve problems efficiently and provide a high standard of IT support.

Responsibilities

  • Provide technical support to users experiencing digital and IT-related issues.
  • Investigate, diagnose, and resolve hardware and software problems.
  • Log, track, and update support requests using appropriate service management systems.
  • Assist users with applications, systems, accounts, and access-related issues.
  • Monitor recurring technical problems and escalate complex incidents when required.
  • Maintain accurate records of technical incidents and resolutions.
  • Work with internal IT teams to improve digital services and user experience.
  • Provide clear guidance and technical information to non-technical users.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in IT support, digital support, or a similar technical environment.
  • Good understanding of computer hardware, software, and operating systems.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving abilities.
  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Ability to explain technical issues clearly to users.
  • Good organisational and time-management skills.
  • Ability to prioritise multiple support requests effectively.
  • Relevant IT qualifications or certifications are desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Standard professional working hours.
  • Working arrangements may vary according to service requirements.

Salary

  • £30,685–£36,099 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive local government salary.
  • Public-sector pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Professional IT training and development.
  • Opportunities for career progression.
  • Supportive professional working environment.
  • Opportunity to contribute to public-sector digital services.
