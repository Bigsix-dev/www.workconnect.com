Residential Property Solicitor / Conveyancer

Job Description

Fisher Jones Greenwood is seeking a Residential Property Solicitor / Conveyancer to manage residential property transactions and provide professional legal advice to clients. The successful candidate will handle transactions from instruction through to completion while maintaining strong client relationships and ensuring matters progress efficiently.

Responsibilities

  • Manage residential property transactions from instruction through to completion.
  • Handle freehold and leasehold sales and purchases.
  • Review contracts, title documents, searches, and property information.
  • Raise and respond to conveyancing enquiries.
  • Draft and review relevant legal documentation.
  • Liaise with clients, estate agents, lenders, and other solicitors.
  • Identify and advise clients on potential legal and property risks.
  • Maintain accurate case files and transaction records.
  • Provide regular updates to clients throughout the transaction.
  • Ensure transactions progress towards exchange and completion efficiently.

Requirements

  • Qualified solicitor, licensed conveyancer, or experienced conveyancing professional.
  • Strong residential property and conveyancing knowledge.
  • Excellent drafting, negotiation, and analytical skills.
  • Strong client care and communication abilities.
  • Excellent attention to detail.
  • Good organisational and time management skills.
  • Ability to manage a varied caseload and meet deadlines.
  • Professional and commercially aware approach.

Benefits

  • Opportunities for professional development.
  • Exposure to a varied residential property caseload.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
