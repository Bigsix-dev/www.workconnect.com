Fire Safety Officer

Job Description

Fire Safety Company provides fire prevention, risk assessment, inspection, and safety services to commercial, residential, and public-sector clients. The company is seeking a professional Fire Safety Officer to help clients identify fire risks, maintain appropriate safety standards, and comply with relevant fire safety requirements. You will conduct assessments, provide guidance, review safety measures, and support clients in maintaining effective fire prevention arrangements.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct fire safety inspections and risk assessments at client premises.
  • Identify fire hazards and recommend appropriate control measures.
  • Review fire detection, alarm, emergency lighting, signage, and evacuation arrangements.
  • Prepare accurate fire safety reports, assessments, and recommendations.
  • Provide professional advice and guidance to clients on fire safety requirements.
  • Support the development and review of emergency evacuation procedures.
  • Monitor compliance with relevant fire safety legislation, standards, and company procedures.
  • Deliver fire safety awareness and training sessions where required.

Requirements

  • Relevant qualification or experience in fire safety, fire risk assessment, health and safety, or a related discipline.
  • Good understanding of fire safety legislation, risk assessment, and prevention principles.
  • Strong inspection, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent communication and report writing abilities.
  • Strong attention to detail and professional judgement.
  • Ability to explain technical safety requirements clearly to clients.
  • Ability to work independently and manage multiple assignments.
  • Professional, organised, and committed approach to health and safety.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule, with occasional flexibility for client visits and inspections.

Salary

  • £30,000–£38,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive fire safety salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Fire safety and professional development training.
  • Opportunities to obtain additional safety qualifications.
  • Professional technical working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
