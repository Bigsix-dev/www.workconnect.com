Home Delivery Driver

Job Description

Tesco PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's largest supermarket retailers, providing groceries, household products, and online shopping services to millions of customers. Tesco is seeking a reliable and customer-focused Home Delivery Driver to join its home shopping team. In this role, you will deliver customer orders safely and on time, provide excellent customer service, and ensure every delivery reflects Tesco's high standards of quality and professionalism.

Responsibilities

  • Deliver online grocery orders safely and efficiently to customers' homes.
  • Load, organise, and secure customer orders before leaving the distribution centre or store.
  • Carry out daily vehicle safety inspections and report any issues immediately.
  • Use handheld devices to manage deliveries and obtain proof of delivery.
  • Provide friendly and professional customer service during every delivery.
  • Handle food products carefully to maintain quality and freshness.
  • Assist customers with deliveries where appropriate.
  • Comply with company policies, road traffic regulations, food safety standards, and health and safety procedures.

Requirements

  • Valid UK Category B driving licence.
  • Excellent driving record and commitment to road safety.
  • Strong customer service and communication skills.
  • Ability to lift and carry grocery orders safely.
  • Good organisational and time management skills.
  • Ability to work independently while meeting delivery schedules.
  • Comfortable using handheld technology and GPS navigation systems.
  • Previous delivery driving experience is desirable but not essential.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 36–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, weekends, and public holidays.

Salary

  • £12.80–£14.30 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts across Tesco stores and services.
  • Comprehensive training and development programmes.
  • Health and wellbeing support.
  • Modern delivery vehicles and equipment.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression within Tesco.
