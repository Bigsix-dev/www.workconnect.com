Scheduling Administrator

Job Description

Serco Group PLC (Public Limited Company) is an international provider of public services, delivering essential services across defence, healthcare, transport, justice, and citizen services. Serco Group is seeking a highly organised Scheduling Administrator to coordinate staff schedules and support operational planning. In this role, you will manage work schedules, maintain accurate records, coordinate with operational teams, and ensure staffing requirements are met efficiently while supporting high-quality service delivery.

Responsibilities

  • Create, maintain, and update staff work schedules and rotas.
  • Coordinate staffing requirements with managers and operational teams.
  • Monitor employee availability, leave requests, and shift changes.
  • Maintain accurate scheduling records and administrative documentation.
  • Communicate schedule updates to employees and supervisors.
  • Prepare reports relating to staffing, attendance, and scheduling performance.
  • Support daily administrative operations and workforce planning activities.
  • Ensure compliance with company procedures, employment regulations, and health and safety requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in scheduling, workforce planning, or office administration.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and administrative accuracy.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel, Word, and Outlook.
  • Experience using scheduling or workforce management software is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to prioritise multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Professional attitude with strong problem-solving and teamwork abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with flexibility depending on operational requirements.

Salary

  • £25,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Employee discounts and reward schemes.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Serco Group.
