Warehouse Packer

Job Description

Lidl Great Britain Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading supermarket retailers, operating a nationwide network of stores and distribution centres. Lidl GB is seeking a reliable and hardworking Warehouse Packer to join its logistics team. In this role, you will prepare products for dispatch, ensure goods are packed accurately, and support efficient warehouse operations while maintaining high standards of safety, quality, and productivity.

Responsibilities

  • Pack products accurately according to warehouse procedures and delivery requirements.
  • Prepare goods for dispatch to Lidl stores and customers.
  • Check products for quality, accuracy, and damage before packing.
  • Label and organise packages correctly for transportation.
  • Assist with loading, unloading, and moving stock when required.
  • Maintain a clean, organised, and safe working environment.
  • Work efficiently to meet daily warehouse targets and deadlines.
  • Follow Lidl's health and safety policies, food safety standards, and operational procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous warehouse, packing, or logistics experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Good attention to detail and ability to work accurately.
  • Ability to stand for long periods and perform physical tasks.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced warehouse environment.
  • Strong teamwork and communication skills.
  • Reliable, punctual, and committed to achieving performance goals.
  • Ability to follow instructions and company procedures.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and overtime when required.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, nights, and weekends.

Salary

  • £13.65–£14.65 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts across Lidl GB stores.
  • Comprehensive training and development opportunities.
  • Health and wellbeing support programmes.
  • Modern distribution centre facilities.
  • Career progression opportunities within Lidl GB.
