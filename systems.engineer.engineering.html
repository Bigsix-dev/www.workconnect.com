Systems Engineer

Job Description

Defence Technology Company specialises in developing advanced engineering systems and technology solutions for complex defence and security applications. The company is seeking a highly skilled Systems Engineer to support the design, integration, testing, and improvement of complex technical systems. In this role, you will work with multidisciplinary engineering teams to deliver reliable, secure, and high-performance solutions that meet customer and industry requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Support the design, development, and integration of complex engineering systems.
  • Analyse system requirements and develop technical solutions.
  • Create system specifications, documentation, and technical reports.
  • Coordinate system testing, verification, and validation activities.
  • Identify technical risks and support engineering problem-solving.
  • Work with software, hardware, and engineering teams to deliver integrated solutions.
  • Support system upgrades, improvements, and lifecycle management.
  • Ensure systems meet performance, quality, and safety standards.

Requirements

  • Degree in Systems Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience working as a Systems Engineer or in a similar technical role.
  • Strong understanding of systems design, integration, and engineering processes.
  • Experience with system modelling, testing, and technical documentation.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong communication and teamwork abilities.
  • Ability to manage complex engineering projects and requirements.
  • Knowledge of defence, aerospace, or safety-critical systems is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £45,000–£70,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Specialist technical training and development opportunities.
  • Access to advanced engineering technologies.
  • Supportive and collaborative engineering environment.
  • Career progression opportunities within defence technology.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
