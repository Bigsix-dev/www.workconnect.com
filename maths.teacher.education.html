Maths Teacher

Job Description

Chelmsford Secondary Schools are dedicated to delivering outstanding education and helping students develop the knowledge and skills needed for future success. Schools across Chelmsford are seeking a motivated and enthusiastic Maths Teacher to deliver engaging mathematics lessons and inspire students to achieve their full potential. In this role, you will support learners of different abilities, monitor academic progress, and foster confidence in mathematical problem-solving.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and deliver high-quality mathematics lessons aligned with the national curriculum.
  • Teach topics including algebra, geometry, statistics, probability, and arithmetic.
  • Assess student work and provide timely, constructive feedback.
  • Prepare lesson plans, teaching resources, and examination materials.
  • Monitor student achievement and maintain accurate academic records.
  • Create a positive and inclusive classroom environment that promotes learning.
  • Communicate with parents, carers, and colleagues regarding student progress.
  • Participate in staff meetings, school events, and ongoing professional development.

Requirements

  • Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) is required.
  • Bachelor's degree in Mathematics or a related subject.
  • Previous experience teaching mathematics at secondary school level is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of the secondary mathematics curriculum.
  • Excellent classroom management and communication skills.
  • Ability to motivate and engage students with different learning abilities.
  • Commitment to safeguarding and promoting student wellbeing.
  • Strong organisational, planning, and assessment skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 32.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £34,000–£50,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive teacher salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme.
  • Paid school holidays and generous annual leave.
  • Professional development and continuous training opportunities.
  • Supportive leadership and collaborative teaching environment.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Career progression opportunities within secondary education.
  • Access to school facilities and staff benefits.
