Police Control Room Operator

Job Description

Police Service relies on professional control room teams to coordinate information, resources, and operational responses to incidents. The service is seeking a focused Police Control Room Operator to support frontline policing by receiving information, managing incident records, communicating with officers, and coordinating appropriate responses. You will work in a fast-paced environment where accuracy, concentration, and effective communication are essential.

Responsibilities

  • Receive and process information relating to police incidents and operational activity.
  • Record incident details accurately using police computer systems.
  • Prioritise incidents according to established police procedures.
  • Communicate relevant information to police officers and operational teams.
  • Monitor ongoing incidents and update records as new information becomes available.
  • Coordinate resources and support officers responding to incidents.
  • Handle sensitive and confidential information appropriately.
  • Respond professionally and efficiently during high-pressure situations.

Requirements

  • Previous control room, emergency services, call handling, or customer service experience is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and listening skills.
  • Strong computer and data entry abilities.
  • Ability to process information accurately and quickly.
  • Excellent concentration and attention to detail.
  • Ability to remain calm and make effective decisions under pressure.
  • Strong confidentiality, integrity, and professional judgement.
  • Ability to work effectively within a busy operational team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working schedule, including nights, weekends, and public holidays.

Salary

  • £27,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive police service salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Structured police control room training.
  • Opportunities for specialist operational training.
  • Modern control room and communications systems.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
