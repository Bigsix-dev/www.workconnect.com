Art Teacher

Job Description

Chelmsford Schools are committed to nurturing creativity, innovation, and academic excellence through an engaging and inclusive curriculum. Schools across Chelmsford are seeking an enthusiastic and creative Art Teacher to inspire students through a wide range of artistic disciplines. In this role, you will encourage students to develop their artistic skills, creativity, and self-expression while preparing them for examinations and future creative opportunities.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and deliver engaging Art lessons in line with the national curriculum.
  • Teach a variety of artistic techniques, including drawing, painting, sculpture, printmaking, and mixed media.
  • Prepare lesson plans, practical demonstrations, and assessment materials.
  • Assess student work and provide constructive feedback to encourage improvement.
  • Maintain a safe, organised, and inspiring art classroom and studio environment.
  • Support students in developing creativity, confidence, and technical skills.
  • Organise art displays, exhibitions, and extracurricular creative activities.
  • Participate in parents' evenings, staff meetings, and professional development programmes.

Requirements

  • Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) is required.
  • Bachelor's degree in Art, Fine Arts, Art & Design, or a related subject.
  • Previous experience teaching Art at secondary school level is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of traditional and contemporary art techniques.
  • Excellent classroom management and communication skills.
  • Ability to motivate and inspire students of all artistic abilities.
  • Commitment to safeguarding and promoting student wellbeing.
  • Strong organisational, planning, and assessment skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 32.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £34,000–£48,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive teacher salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme.
  • Paid school holidays and generous annual leave.
  • Professional development and continuous training opportunities.
  • Access to well-equipped art studios and creative resources.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Career progression opportunities within education.
  • Supportive and collaborative teaching environment.
