Client Service & Sales Advisor

Job Description

International Property Media is seeking a confident Client Service & Sales Advisor to support clients while identifying opportunities to promote relevant products and services. The successful candidate will build strong client relationships, respond to enquiries, provide professional advice, and contribute to sales performance through excellent service and communication.

Responsibilities

  • Build and maintain strong relationships with existing and prospective clients.
  • Respond to client enquiries by telephone, email, and other communication channels.
  • Understand client requirements and recommend appropriate products or services.
  • Identify sales opportunities and generate new business where appropriate.
  • Explain products, services, pricing, and promotional opportunities clearly.
  • Follow up with prospective and existing clients.
  • Maintain accurate client records and sales information.
  • Meet agreed customer service and sales objectives.
  • Work with internal teams to ensure client requirements are delivered effectively.
  • Handle client concerns professionally and identify suitable solutions.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service, sales, or account management experience is desirable.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Confident telephone and relationship-building skills.
  • Strong commercial awareness and sales ability.
  • Good organisational and time management skills.
  • Self-motivated approach with the ability to work towards targets.
  • Good attention to detail and administrative skills.
  • Professional and customer-focused approach.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £24,000 per year.
  • Uncapped commission.

Benefits

  • Competitive base salary with uncapped commission.
  • Sales and customer service training.
  • Professional working environment.
  • Opportunities to develop sales and client management skills.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
