Driver Mate

Job Description

AO Logistics Limited, part of AO World PLC (Public Limited Company), is a leading UK logistics company specialising in the delivery and installation of electrical appliances and household products. AO Logistics is seeking a reliable and customer-focused Driver Mate to support delivery drivers in providing safe, efficient, and professional home delivery services. In this role, you will assist with loading, unloading, delivering, and installing products while ensuring every customer receives an outstanding delivery experience.

Responsibilities

  • Assist the driver with loading and unloading appliances and household products.
  • Deliver products safely and efficiently to customers' homes.
  • Help carry large and heavy items into customers' properties.
  • Support basic installation and positioning of appliances where required.
  • Communicate professionally with customers and provide excellent customer service.
  • Complete delivery paperwork and confirm successful deliveries.
  • Ensure delivery vehicles remain clean and organised.
  • Follow company procedures and health and safety guidelines at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous delivery or warehouse experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Good level of physical fitness and ability to lift heavy items safely.
  • Excellent teamwork and communication skills.
  • Friendly and professional approach to customer service.
  • Reliable, punctual, and hardworking.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced delivery environment.
  • Flexibility to work weekends and overtime when required.
  • Commitment to maintaining high standards of safety and customer satisfaction.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts, including weekends and public holidays as required.

Salary

  • £12.50–£13.80 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts on AO products and services.
  • Comprehensive training and development programmes.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing support.
  • Supportive team environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within AO Logistics.
