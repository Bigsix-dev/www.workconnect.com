Transport Coordinator

Job Description

Wincanton PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading supply chain and logistics companies, providing transport, warehousing, and distribution solutions for major businesses nationwide. Wincanton is seeking an organised and proactive Transport Coordinator to support its transport operations. In this role, you will coordinate vehicle schedules, communicate with drivers and customers, monitor deliveries, and ensure transport activities are completed safely, efficiently, and on time.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate daily transport operations and delivery schedules.
  • Allocate routes and support drivers with delivery requirements.
  • Monitor vehicle movements and provide updates on delivery progress.
  • Communicate with drivers, customers, and internal departments.
  • Prepare and maintain accurate transport documentation and records.
  • Assist with resolving delivery issues and operational challenges.
  • Update transport management systems with relevant information.
  • Ensure compliance with company procedures, transport regulations, and health and safety standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in transport coordination, logistics, or administration.
  • Strong organisational and planning skills.
  • Excellent communication and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks in a busy transport environment.
  • Good knowledge of transport operations and delivery processes.
  • Strong attention to detail when handling records and documentation.
  • Experience using transport management systems is desirable.
  • Ability to work effectively with drivers and operational teams.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible working schedule depending on transport operations.

Salary

  • £29,000–£35,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts and reward schemes.
  • Health and wellbeing support.
  • Supportive and professional working environment.
  • Career progression opportunities within Wincanton.
