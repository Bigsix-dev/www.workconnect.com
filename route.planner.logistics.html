Route Planner

Job Description

Evri Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading parcel delivery companies, delivering millions of parcels every week through an extensive nationwide distribution network. Evri is seeking a highly organised and analytical Route Planner to optimise delivery operations and improve transport efficiency. In this role, you will develop and manage delivery routes, monitor transport performance, and work closely with drivers and operational teams to ensure parcels are delivered safely, efficiently, and on schedule.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and optimise daily delivery routes to maximise operational efficiency.
  • Allocate routes to drivers based on workload, vehicle capacity, and delivery priorities.
  • Monitor route performance and adjust schedules when operational issues arise.
  • Communicate with drivers, depot teams, and transport managers regarding route updates.
  • Analyse delivery data to improve route planning and service performance.
  • Maintain accurate transport records and planning documentation.
  • Support the achievement of delivery targets and customer service standards.
  • Ensure compliance with transport regulations, company procedures, and health and safety requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in route planning, transport planning, or logistics operations.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent organisational and time management abilities.
  • Good communication and teamwork skills.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
  • Experience using route planning or transport management software is desirable.
  • Ability to work under pressure in a fast-paced logistics environment.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to operational excellence.
