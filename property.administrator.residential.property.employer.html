Property Administrator

Job Description

A Residential Property Employer is seeking an organised and detail-focused Property Administrator to provide administrative support across residential property operations. The role involves maintaining property records, coordinating appointments, communicating with tenants and contractors, processing documentation, and ensuring day-to-day property administration is completed accurately and efficiently.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support for residential property management activities.
  • Maintain accurate property, tenant, contractor, and maintenance records.
  • Respond to enquiries from tenants, landlords, contractors, and other stakeholders.
  • Coordinate property inspections, maintenance visits, and appointments.
  • Process property-related documents and correspondence.
  • Assist with maintenance requests and contractor communications.
  • Update databases and property management systems.
  • Prepare reports, schedules, and other administrative documents as required.

Requirements

  • Previous administrative or property-sector experience is desirable.
  • Excellent organisation and time-management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication.
  • Good attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Confident using Microsoft Office and computer-based systems.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and competing deadlines.
  • Professional and helpful approach to customer service.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a property management team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Standard office-based working hours.
  • Some flexibility may be required depending on property management requirements.

Salary

  • Salary not stated; dependent on experience and employer requirements.

Benefits

  • Stable residential property employment.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Opportunities to develop property management expertise.
  • Supportive office environment.
  • Potential career progression within the property sector.
  • Exposure to a wide range of residential property operations.
