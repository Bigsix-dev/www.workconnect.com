Chartered Town Planner

Job Description

Penguin Recruitment is seeking a qualified Chartered Town Planner to provide professional planning advice and support on a range of development and planning projects. The successful candidate will work with clients, local authorities, and other stakeholders to assess proposals, prepare planning applications, and support projects through the planning process.

Responsibilities

  • Provide professional town planning advice to clients and project teams.
  • Prepare and submit planning applications and supporting documentation.
  • Assess development proposals against relevant planning policies.
  • Conduct planning research and prepare written reports.
  • Liaise with local authorities, clients, consultants, and other stakeholders.
  • Monitor planning applications and provide updates on progress.
  • Attend meetings and contribute to development and planning discussions.
  • Assist with planning appeals and other planning matters where required.
  • Maintain accurate project records and documentation.
  • Keep up to date with changes in planning legislation and policy.

Requirements

  • Chartered Town Planner with relevant professional experience.
  • Strong knowledge of UK planning policy and development processes.
  • Excellent report writing and analytical skills.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management abilities.
  • Good understanding of planning applications and local authority procedures.
  • Strong organisational and project management skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and deadlines.
  • Commercial awareness and professional client-service approach.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £35,000–£45,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional planning environment.
  • Opportunities for professional development.
  • Exposure to a varied range of planning projects.
  • Career progression opportunities.
