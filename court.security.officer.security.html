Court Security Officer

Job Description

HM Courts & Tribunals Service supports the administration of justice by operating courts and tribunals across England and Wales. The organisation is seeking a professional Court Security Officer to help maintain a safe, secure, and orderly environment for judges, court staff, legal professionals, defendants, witnesses, and members of the public. You will monitor access, carry out security checks, respond to incidents, and ensure court security procedures are followed.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain a safe and secure environment within court buildings and surrounding areas.
  • Control access to court facilities and verify authorised visitors where required.
  • Carry out security screening and searches in accordance with established procedures.
  • Monitor court premises for potential security risks and suspicious activity.
  • Respond appropriately to security incidents, emergencies, and disturbances.
  • Support the safe movement of individuals within court facilities when required.
  • Maintain accurate security logs, incident reports, and relevant documentation.
  • Work closely with court staff, police, legal professionals, and other authorised personnel.

Requirements

  • Valid SIA licence where required for the position.
  • Previous security, public service, law enforcement, or customer-facing experience is desirable.
  • Strong observation and situational awareness skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional in challenging situations.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong integrity and ability to maintain confidentiality.
  • Ability to follow detailed security procedures and instructions.
  • Reliable, punctual, and professional approach to work.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £27,000–£31,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive public-sector salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Structured court security training.
  • Opportunities for professional development.
  • Stable public-sector working environment.
  • Career progression opportunities within the justice sector.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
