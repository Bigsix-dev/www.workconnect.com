Event Security Officer

Job Description

Event Security Company provides professional security services for concerts, sporting events, festivals, exhibitions, conferences, and other public gatherings. The company is seeking reliable Event Security Officers to help maintain a safe and secure environment for visitors, staff, performers, and event organisers. You will monitor entrances, assist with crowd management, conduct security checks, and respond appropriately to incidents.

Responsibilities

  • Provide a visible and professional security presence at events.
  • Check tickets, passes, identification, and access permissions where required.
  • Monitor entrances, exits, restricted areas, and designated event zones.
  • Assist with crowd management and maintain safe movement of visitors.
  • Carry out searches in accordance with event security procedures.
  • Identify and report suspicious behaviour, safety concerns, and security incidents.
  • Respond professionally to incidents and follow emergency procedures.
  • Work closely with event organisers, venue staff, police, medical teams, and other security personnel.

Requirements

  • Valid SIA licence where required for the role.
  • Previous event, security, customer service, or hospitality experience is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Professional, approachable, and confident manner.
  • Ability to remain calm when dealing with challenging situations.
  • Strong observation and situational awareness.
  • Ability to stand and walk for extended periods.
  • Reliable, punctual, and able to work effectively as part of a team.

Working Hours

  • Part-time position.
  • 16–30 hours per week.
  • Evening, weekend, and event-based working may be required.

Salary

  • £12.00–£15.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly security rate.
  • Flexible event-based working opportunities.
  • Security and event safety training.
  • Opportunities to work at a variety of events and venues.
  • Opportunities for additional security qualifications.
  • Supportive event security teams.
  • Opportunities for regular event assignments.
  • Career progression into senior security roles.
