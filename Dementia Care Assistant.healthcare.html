Dementia Care Assistant

Job Overview

A Dementia Care Assistant provides compassionate, person-centred support to residents living with dementia. The role focuses on promoting dignity, independence, and quality of life while assisting with daily activities and ensuring residents feel safe, respected, and valued.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist residents with personal care, including washing, dressing, grooming, and toileting.
  • Support individuals with eating, drinking, and mobility needs.
  • Build trusting relationships with residents and provide emotional reassurance.
  • Follow personalised care plans and report changes in residents' physical or mental wellbeing.
  • Encourage participation in activities that stimulate memory and social interaction.
  • Administer or assist with medication where authorised and trained.
  • Maintain accurate care records and communicate effectively with colleagues and families.
  • Ensure a clean, safe, and comfortable living environment.
  • Uphold safeguarding procedures and maintain residents' dignity and confidentiality.

Essential Skills and Qualifications

  • Compassionate and patient approach to caring for older adults.
  • Good communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to remain calm in challenging situations.
  • Teamwork and reliability.
  • Basic literacy and numeracy skills.
  • Previous experience in health or social care is beneficial but not always required.
  • Relevant care qualifications (such as Level 2 or Level 3 Health and Social Care) are advantageous but often optional.

Typical Working Hours

Full-time roles commonly involve 37.5–40 hours per week, including a mixture of day shifts, night shifts, weekends, and public holidays on a rota basis.

Visa Sponsorship (for International Applicants)

Some UK care providers offer visa sponsorship for eligible overseas candidates, but availability varies by employer and vacancy. Applicants should always verify sponsorship status before applying.
