Senior Home Care Worker

Job Description

Helping Hands Home Care is seeking a compassionate and experienced Senior Home Care Worker to lead and support care staff while delivering exceptional person-centred care to clients in their own homes. The successful candidate will assist with care planning, mentor team members, monitor service quality, and ensure clients receive safe, dignified, and reliable support that promotes independence and wellbeing.

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide high-quality personal care and practical support to clients in their homes.
  • Supervise, guide, and mentor Home Care Workers during daily operations.
  • Assist with creating and reviewing individual care plans.
  • Administer or support medication in line with company policies and training.
  • Carry out risk assessments and maintain accurate care documentation.
  • Liaise with families, healthcare professionals, and management to coordinate care.
  • Report safeguarding concerns or changes in clients’ conditions promptly.
  • Promote dignity, respect, and independence in every aspect of care delivery.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in domiciliary or community care, ideally in a senior or supervisory role.
  • NVQ/QCF Level 2 or Level 3 in Health and Social Care (or willingness to work toward one).
  • Strong communication and leadership skills.
  • Good understanding of safeguarding, medication procedures, and person-centred care.
  • Ability to work flexibly, including weekends and evenings when required.
  • A valid UK driving licence and access to a vehicle may be desirable depending on the service area.
  • Right to work in the UK and a satisfactory enhanced DBS check.

What We Offer

  • Competitive annual salary of £28,000–£34,000.
  • Paid induction and ongoing professional development.
  • Opportunities for career progression into management roles.
  • Pension scheme and holiday entitlement.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing support programmes.
  • Supportive team environment with regular supervision and training.
  • Recognition programmes that reward dedication and outstanding performance.
