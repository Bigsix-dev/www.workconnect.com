Dryliner

Job Description

Construction Contractor is seeking an experienced Dryliner to work on construction projects in Chelmsford. The role involves installing drylining systems, partitions, ceilings, and associated components while maintaining accurate measurements and high-quality finishes.

Responsibilities

  • Install plasterboard, partitions, and drylining systems.
  • Construct metal and timber framing where required.
  • Measure, cut, and fix boards accurately.
  • Complete finishing and jointing work to a high standard.
  • Read drawings and follow project specifications.
  • Maintain safe and organised work areas.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Dryliner.
  • Strong understanding of drylining installation techniques.
  • Ability to work accurately from drawings and measurements.
  • Good attention to detail.
  • Reliable and safety-conscious approach.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Based in Chelmsford.

Salary

  • £20–£25 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly rate.
  • Regular construction projects.
  • Opportunity to work on different building developments.
  • Professional site environment.
