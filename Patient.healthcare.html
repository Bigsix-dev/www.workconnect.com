Patient Support Worker

Job Overview

As a Patient Support Worker, you will play a vital role in delivering compassionate, person-centred care to patients in hospitals, clinics, or community healthcare settings. You will work alongside nurses and other healthcare professionals to ensure patients are safe, comfortable, and treated with dignity while supporting their physical and emotional wellbeing.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist patients with personal care, including washing, dressing, and mobility.
  • Support patients during meals and encourage adequate nutrition and hydration.
  • Monitor and record basic observations such as temperature, pulse, and blood pressure where trained.
  • Help maintain a clean, safe, and comfortable environment for patients.
  • Provide emotional support and reassurance to patients and their families.
  • Escort patients to appointments, treatments, or diagnostic procedures.
  • Report any concerns or changes in a patient's condition to senior healthcare staff.
  • Follow infection prevention, safeguarding, and health and safety procedures at all times.
  • Maintain accurate records and contribute to high-quality patient care.

About You

The ideal candidate should:

  • Have a caring, empathetic, and respectful approach to supporting others.
  • Communicate effectively with patients, families, and colleagues.
  • Be able to work independently and as part of a multidisciplinary team.
  • Remain calm under pressure and demonstrate good problem-solving skills.
  • Be reliable, flexible, and committed to delivering excellent care.
  • Previous experience in healthcare or care settings is beneficial but may not always be required, as training is often provided.
