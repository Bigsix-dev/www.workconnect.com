IT Systems Engineer

Job Description

Principal IT is seeking an IT Systems Engineer to support, maintain, and improve business technology infrastructure. The successful candidate will be responsible for maintaining systems, resolving technical problems, monitoring infrastructure, and supporting users across the organisation. This is an opportunity for a technically minded professional to work within a structured IT environment.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain and support servers, networks, workstations, and IT infrastructure.
  • Diagnose and resolve hardware, software, and system issues.
  • Monitor system performance and identify potential technical problems.
  • Configure and maintain user accounts, devices, and access permissions.
  • Support backup, security, and system maintenance procedures.
  • Document technical issues, configurations, and solutions.
  • Assist with infrastructure upgrades and technology projects.
  • Provide technical support to internal users when required.

Requirements

  • Experience working in an IT systems or infrastructure support role.
  • Good knowledge of Windows systems and common business applications.
  • Understanding of networking, servers, and system administration.
  • Strong troubleshooting and analytical skills.
  • Good understanding of IT security and access management.
  • Ability to work independently and manage technical tasks.
  • Strong communication and documentation skills.
  • Relevant IT qualifications or certifications are advantageous.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday.
  • 9:00am–6:00pm.
  • Full-time position.

Salary

  • Up to £35,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive IT-sector salary.
  • Professional technology training.
  • Opportunities to develop systems engineering skills.
  • Career progression within IT infrastructure.
  • Exposure to a variety of business technology environments.
  • Supportive technical team environment.
