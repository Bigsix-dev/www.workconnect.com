Property Manager

Job Description

Kings Permanent Recruitment is seeking an organised Property Manager to oversee the day-to-day management of residential properties. The successful candidate will manage landlord and tenant relationships, coordinate maintenance and inspections, handle tenancy administration, and ensure properties are managed efficiently and professionally.

Responsibilities

  • Manage a portfolio of residential properties.
  • Act as the main point of contact for landlords and tenants.
  • Coordinate property maintenance, repairs, and inspections.
  • Liaise with contractors and monitor completion of maintenance works.
  • Respond to landlord and tenant enquiries promptly and professionally.
  • Maintain accurate property and tenancy records.
  • Monitor tenancy dates, renewals, and other important deadlines.
  • Arrange and monitor property inspections.
  • Assist with tenancy documentation and related administration.
  • Ensure property management procedures and relevant regulations are followed.

Requirements

  • Previous residential property management experience is desirable.
  • Good knowledge of lettings and property management processes.
  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Excellent attention to detail and administrative skills.
  • Confident dealing with landlords, tenants, and contractors.
  • Strong problem-solving abilities.
  • Professional, proactive, and commercially aware approach.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional estate and property management environment.
  • Training and professional development opportunities.
  • Opportunity to manage a varied property portfolio.
  • Career progression opportunities.
