Prison Security Escort

Job Description

Ministry of Justice is responsible for supporting the justice system and maintaining safe, secure, and effective custodial environments. The organisation is seeking a responsible Prison Security Escort to support the safe movement and supervision of authorised visitors, contractors, and other individuals within prison facilities. You will follow strict security procedures, maintain professional standards, and help ensure that prison operations remain safe and orderly.

Responsibilities

  • Escort authorised visitors, contractors, and other individuals within designated prison areas.
  • Follow prison security procedures and instructions at all times.
  • Maintain awareness of security risks and report concerns promptly.
  • Carry out appropriate identification and access checks.
  • Ensure escorted individuals remain within authorised areas.
  • Maintain accurate records and complete required security documentation.
  • Communicate effectively with prison staff, contractors, visitors, and other authorised personnel.
  • Respond appropriately to incidents and follow emergency procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous security, custodial, military, customer service, or public-sector experience is desirable.
  • Strong awareness of security procedures and professional boundaries.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to follow detailed instructions and established procedures.
  • Strong observation and situational awareness.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional in challenging environments.
  • Good reliability, punctuality, and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a professional security team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working arrangements may apply.

Salary

  • £27,000–£31,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive public-sector salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Structured security and custodial training.
  • Opportunities for professional development.
  • Stable public-sector employment environment.
  • Career progression opportunities within the justice sector.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
