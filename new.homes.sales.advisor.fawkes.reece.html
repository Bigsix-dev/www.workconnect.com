New Homes Sales Advisor

Job Description

Fawkes & Reece is seeking a motivated New Homes Sales Advisor to promote and sell new-build residential properties. The successful candidate will guide prospective buyers through the sales process, conduct viewings, manage enquiries, build strong customer relationships, and work towards achieving sales targets.

Responsibilities

  • Promote and sell new-build residential properties.
  • Meet prospective buyers and understand their property requirements.
  • Conduct property viewings and provide detailed information about developments.
  • Respond to sales enquiries by telephone, email, and in person.
  • Follow up prospective buyers and manage sales leads.
  • Explain property specifications, pricing, availability, and purchase processes.
  • Negotiate offers and support buyers through the sales progression process.
  • Maintain accurate records of customers, viewings, and sales activity.
  • Liaise with solicitors, mortgage advisers, developers, and other stakeholders.
  • Work towards individual and development sales targets.

Requirements

  • Previous property sales, new homes, estate agency, or customer-facing sales experience is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong sales and negotiation abilities.
  • Confident conducting property viewings and presentations.
  • Target-driven and commercially aware approach.
  • Strong customer service and relationship-building skills.
  • Good organisational and administrative abilities.
  • Professional, enthusiastic, and self-motivated approach.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £30,000–£31,000 per year.
  • Commission available.

Benefits

  • Competitive base salary with commission opportunity.
  • Professional new homes sales environment.
  • Sales and property training opportunities.
  • Opportunity to work on new-build residential developments.
  • Career progression opportunities.
