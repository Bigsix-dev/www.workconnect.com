IT Support

Job Description

Various Employers across the Chelmsford area are seeking IT Support professionals to assist users with day-to-day technology issues. These roles involve troubleshooting technical problems, maintaining IT equipment and delivering a reliable support service to employees and customers.

Responsibilities

  • Provide technical support to users by phone, email or in person.
  • Diagnose and resolve hardware, software and connectivity problems.
  • Install and configure computers, applications and peripheral devices.
  • Manage support tickets and ensure incidents are resolved efficiently.
  • Assist with user account and access-related issues.
  • Escalate complex technical problems when required.

Requirements

  • Previous IT support or technical customer service experience is beneficial.
  • Good knowledge of common computer hardware and software.
  • Strong troubleshooting and analytical skills.
  • Good communication and customer service abilities.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a technical support team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours may vary depending on the employer and role.

Salary

  • Around £25,420–£39,765 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunities across a range of IT environments.
  • Technical training and professional development.
  • Potential for career progression within IT.
  • Experience with different technologies and support environments.
