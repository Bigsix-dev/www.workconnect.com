SEN Support Worker

Job Description

Columbus School & College is a specialist education provider dedicated to supporting children and young people with special educational needs and disabilities. The school provides a safe, inclusive, and nurturing environment where students are supported to develop independence, confidence, and essential life skills. Columbus School & College is seeking a caring and committed SEN Support Worker to provide practical, emotional, and educational support to students with additional needs while helping them achieve their personal goals.

Responsibilities

  • Provide daily support to students with special educational needs and disabilities.
  • Assist students with learning activities, communication, and social development.
  • Support individual care plans and personalised development goals.
  • Encourage independence, confidence, and positive behaviour.
  • Assist with personal care, mobility, and wellbeing needs where required.
  • Work alongside teachers, therapists, and support teams to meet student needs.
  • Monitor student progress and report concerns to appropriate staff.
  • Promote safeguarding, equality, inclusion, and student wellbeing.

Requirements

  • Previous experience working with children or young people with additional needs is desirable.
  • Understanding of special educational needs, autism, or learning disabilities is beneficial.
  • Caring, patient, and supportive approach.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to build positive and trusting relationships with students.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a multidisciplinary team.
  • Knowledge of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Relevant care, support work, or education qualification is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £25,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Professional training and specialist SEN development opportunities.
  • Paid annual leave entitlement.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
  • Supportive and experienced education team.
  • Opportunities for career progression in special education and care.
  • Rewarding role making a positive difference in students' lives.
  • Inclusive and supportive working environment.
