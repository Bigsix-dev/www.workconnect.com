Customer Team Member

Co-op (The Co-operative Group Limited) is one of the United Kingdom's largest consumer co-operatives, operating food stores, funeral care, insurance, and legal services. Co-op is looking for friendly, reliable, and customer-focused Customer Team Members to join its retail team. In this role, you will provide exceptional customer service, operate tills, replenish stock, prepare products for sale, and help maintain a clean, safe, and welcoming store environment. You will work closely with your colleagues to ensure customers enjoy a positive shopping experience every time they visit.

Responsibilities

  • Deliver friendly and efficient customer service at all times.
  • Operate checkout tills and process transactions accurately.
  • Replenish shelves and ensure products are well-stocked and neatly displayed.
  • Assist with stock deliveries, unpacking, and inventory organisation.
  • Prepare bakery, chilled, or fresh food products where required.
  • Maintain high standards of cleanliness and food safety throughout the store.
  • Support promotional activities and product merchandising.
  • Follow company policies, security procedures, and health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly, dependable, and able to work well within a team.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced retail environment.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Good organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.
  • Previous retail or customer service experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns, including weekends, evenings, and public holidays.

Salary

  • £12.30–£13.50 per hour.
  • Additional overtime opportunities may be available.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discount on Co-op products and services.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Life assurance and colleague support benefits.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
