Mechanical Technician

Job Description

Energy Services Provider delivers specialist engineering services and technical support within the energy sector. The company is seeking an experienced and reliable Mechanical Technician to maintain, inspect, and repair mechanical systems and equipment. In this role, you will support engineering operations, carry out maintenance activities, and ensure mechanical assets operate safely, efficiently, and reliably.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out mechanical maintenance, repairs, and servicing of industrial equipment.
  • Inspect mechanical systems and identify faults or performance issues.
  • Perform preventative maintenance to improve equipment reliability.
  • Assist engineers with installation, testing, and commissioning activities.
  • Diagnose mechanical problems and implement effective solutions.
  • Complete maintenance records, reports, and technical documentation.
  • Support shutdown, refurbishment, and improvement projects.
  • Ensure all work is completed safely and in line with industry standards.

Requirements

  • Mechanical engineering qualification or recognised technical apprenticeship.
  • Previous experience working as a Mechanical Technician or similar role.
  • Strong knowledge of mechanical systems, machinery, and maintenance practices.
  • Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings and technical documents.
  • Strong fault-finding and problem-solving skills.
  • Good understanding of health, safety, and environmental procedures.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of an engineering team.
  • Experience within the energy or industrial sector is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £44,000–£52,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Specialist energy sector training opportunities.
  • Access to advanced engineering tools and equipment.
  • Supportive technical team environment.
  • Career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
