Commis Chef

Job Description

The County Hotel is a well-established hospitality business committed to providing exceptional accommodation, dining, and guest experiences. The hotel is seeking a passionate and motivated Commis Chef to join its kitchen team. In this role, you will support senior chefs with food preparation, cooking, and kitchen operations while developing your culinary skills in a professional environment. You will help ensure every dish is prepared to the hotel's quality standards while maintaining excellent food hygiene and safety practices.

Responsibilities

  • Assist senior chefs with preparing and cooking a variety of dishes.
  • Prepare ingredients, including washing, peeling, chopping, and portioning food.
  • Maintain cleanliness and organisation of kitchen workstations.
  • Ensure food is prepared according to recipes and presentation standards.
  • Monitor stock levels and assist with stock rotation and storage.
  • Operate kitchen equipment safely and correctly.
  • Support the kitchen team during busy service periods.
  • Comply with food hygiene regulations, HACCP procedures, and health and safety policies.

Requirements

  • Previous kitchen experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Passion for cooking and developing culinary skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced kitchen environment.
  • Good teamwork and communication skills.
  • Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Willingness to learn from experienced chefs.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Food Safety Level 2 certification is desirable but not essential.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 38–45 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £13.50–£15.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Complimentary or discounted meals during shifts.
  • Professional chef training and development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression within the hotel.
