Salesperson – Logistics

Job Description

Adore Recruitment is seeking a motivated Salesperson – Logistics to develop customer relationships and generate new business within the logistics sector. The successful candidate will identify sales opportunities, understand client requirements, promote suitable logistics solutions, and contribute to business growth.

Responsibilities

  • Develop new business opportunities within the logistics market.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with new and existing customers.
  • Identify potential clients and generate sales leads.
  • Contact prospective customers by telephone, email, and other channels.
  • Understand customer logistics requirements and recommend appropriate solutions.
  • Prepare quotations and sales proposals where required.
  • Follow up leads and maintain accurate sales records.
  • Negotiate pricing and service requirements within agreed guidelines.
  • Work with operations and logistics teams to support customer requirements.
  • Meet agreed sales and business development objectives.

Requirements

  • Previous logistics, transport, or sales experience is desirable.
  • Strong commercial awareness and sales ability.
  • Excellent communication and negotiation skills.
  • Confident telephone and face-to-face communication style.
  • Strong relationship-building abilities.
  • Self-motivated and target-driven approach.
  • Good organisational and administrative skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced logistics environment.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday, 8:30am–5:00pm.
  • Full-time position.

Salary

  • £32,000–£34,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional logistics and sales environment.
  • Sales and industry training opportunities.
  • Opportunities to develop commercial and business development skills.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
