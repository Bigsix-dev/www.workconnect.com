Office Administrator

Job Description

Anglian Water Services Limited is one of the United Kingdom's largest water companies, providing essential water and water recycling services to millions of customers across the East of England. Anglian Water is seeking a highly organised and dependable Office Administrator to support business operations. In this role, you will manage administrative tasks, maintain records, coordinate communications, and provide efficient support to teams to ensure smooth daily office operations.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to departments and operational teams.
  • Manage emails, phone calls, and general office enquiries professionally.
  • Maintain accurate records, databases, and digital filing systems.
  • Prepare reports, documents, and correspondence as required.
  • Schedule meetings, organise appointments, and update calendars.
  • Process invoices, purchase orders, and administrative paperwork.
  • Support data entry and document management activities.
  • Ensure company procedures, confidentiality requirements, and quality standards are followed.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in office administration or a similar administrative role.
  • Strong organisational and time management skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Good attention to detail and ability to maintain accurate records.
  • Confident using Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Ability to prioritise tasks and work independently.
  • Strong teamwork and problem-solving abilities.
  • Professional approach when dealing with colleagues and customers.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £26,000–£31,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programmes.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Flexible working options where applicable.
  • Supportive and inclusive workplace culture.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Anglian Water.
