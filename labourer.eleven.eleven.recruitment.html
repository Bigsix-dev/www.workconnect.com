Labourer

Job Description

Eleven Eleven Recruitment is seeking a reliable Labourer to support construction and site operations in Chelmsford. The role involves general labouring duties, assisting tradespeople and maintaining a safe and organised working environment.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out general labouring duties on construction sites.
  • Assist tradespeople with materials, tools and site preparation.
  • Load, unload and move materials safely around the site.
  • Maintain clean and organised work areas.
  • Follow site health and safety procedures at all times.
  • Support the wider site team with daily construction activities.

Requirements

  • Previous construction or labouring experience is beneficial.
  • Good physical fitness and ability to carry out manual work.
  • Reliable and punctual approach to work.
  • Good understanding of construction site safety.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a team.

Working Hours

  • 9 hours paid per day.

Salary

  • £14.25 PAYE / £16.56 Umbrella.

Benefits

  • Competitive daily earning opportunity.
  • Paid working hours based on the site schedule.
  • Opportunity to gain further construction experience.
  • Practical, hands-on working environment.
