Oncology Staff Nurse

Full Job Description

An Oncology Staff Nurse is a registered nurse who provides specialized care to patients diagnosed with cancer. They work closely with doctors and other healthcare professionals to administer treatments, monitor patients, manage symptoms, and provide emotional support to patients and their families.

Typical Responsibilities

  • Administer chemotherapy, immunotherapy, and other cancer treatments as prescribed.
  • Monitor patients for side effects and adverse reactions.
  • Assess patients' physical and emotional health.
  • Manage pain and other symptoms associated with cancer and its treatment.
  • Educate patients and families about treatment plans, medications, and self-care.
  • Maintain accurate medical records and care documentation.
  • Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams to develop and implement care plans.
  • Support patients through diagnosis, treatment, recovery, or end-of-life care.

Common Requirements

  • Registered Nurse (RN) qualification and valid nursing registration.
  • Strong clinical assessment and communication skills.
  • Compassion, empathy, and the ability to support patients facing serious illness.
  • Knowledge of oncology treatments and medication safety.
  • Experience in oncology or acute care is often preferred, though some employers offer training for newly qualified nurses.

Typical Work Settings

  • Hospital oncology wards
  • Chemotherapy day units
  • Cancer treatment centers
  • Outpatient clinics
  • Hospices
  • Community healthcare services

Work Pattern

  • Usually available as full-time, part-time, or bank/flexible positions.
  • Shifts may include days, nights, weekends, and public holidays.

Salary (UK)

Typical salaries vary by employer and experience:

  • NHS Band 5: approximately £31,000–£38,000 per year
  • NHS Band 6 (experienced/specialist roles): approximately £38,000–£46,000 per year
  • Private healthcare providers may offer different pay scales and additional benefits.

Key Skills

  • Clinical nursing expertise
  • Attention to detail
  • Medication administration
  • Compassionate patient care
  • Teamwork and collaboration
  • Critical thinking and problem-solving
  • Time management
  • Excellent communication and patient education skills
