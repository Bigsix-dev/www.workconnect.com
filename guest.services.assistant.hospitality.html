Guest Services Assistant

Job Description

The County Hotel is looking for a friendly and professional Guest Services Assistant to provide exceptional customer service to hotel guests. You will be responsible for welcoming visitors, assisting with check-ins and check-outs, answering guest enquiries, and ensuring every guest enjoys a comfortable and memorable stay.

Key Responsibilities

  • Welcome guests with a warm and professional attitude.
  • Assist with check-in and check-out procedures.
  • Respond to guest enquiries and resolve concerns promptly.
  • Handle reservations and update guest information.
  • Provide information about hotel facilities and local attractions.
  • Work closely with housekeeping and other hotel departments.
  • Maintain a clean and organized reception area.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service or hospitality experience is preferred.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Friendly, reliable, and well-presented.
  • Basic computer skills.
  • Ability to work flexible shifts, including weekends.

Working Hours

  • Full-time.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Weekend and evening shifts may be required.

Salary & Benefits

  • £12.60–£14.00 per hour.
  • Paid holidays.
  • Staff discounts on hotel services.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Supportive working environment.
