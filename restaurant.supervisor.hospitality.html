Restaurant Supervisor

Job Description

Miller & Carter, part of Mitchells & Butlers PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's leading premium steakhouse restaurant brands, recognised for delivering exceptional food and outstanding customer service. Miller & Carter is seeking an experienced and motivated Restaurant Supervisor to oversee the daily operation of the restaurant, lead the front-of-house team, and ensure every guest enjoys an exceptional dining experience while maintaining the company's high standards of service and hospitality.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise the day-to-day operations of the restaurant and front-of-house team.
  • Lead, motivate, and support team members to deliver outstanding customer service.
  • Welcome guests and ensure a high-quality dining experience throughout their visit.
  • Assist with staff training, coaching, and performance monitoring.
  • Handle customer enquiries, complaints, and feedback professionally.
  • Monitor food and beverage service to ensure company standards are maintained.
  • Assist with staff scheduling, stock control, and daily opening and closing procedures.
  • Ensure compliance with food hygiene, licensing regulations, health and safety, and company policies.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in a restaurant supervisory or hospitality leadership role.
  • Excellent leadership, communication, and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong organisational and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to lead a team in a fast-paced restaurant environment.
  • Outstanding customer service skills and attention to detail.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Good knowledge of food hygiene and health and safety procedures.
  • A positive attitude with a passion for hospitality.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £14.50–£16.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Discounted meals during shifts.
  • Employee discounts across Mitchells & Butlers restaurants.
  • Management training and career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Supportive and professional working environment.
