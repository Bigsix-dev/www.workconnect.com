Customer Service Administrator

Job Description

Adecco is seeking an organised Customer Service Administrator to support customers while completing a range of administrative duties. The role involves responding to enquiries, maintaining accurate records, processing customer information, and working with colleagues to ensure customer requests are handled efficiently.

Responsibilities

  • Handle customer enquiries and provide professional assistance.
  • Process customer requests and update relevant systems.
  • Maintain accurate customer records and documentation.
  • Respond to telephone and email enquiries promptly.
  • Provide accurate information and resolve routine customer issues.
  • Liaise with internal teams to ensure customer requirements are met.
  • Complete general administrative and data-entry duties.
  • Monitor outstanding customer requests and follow up when necessary.
  • Ensure all customer interactions are recorded accurately.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in customer service or administration is desirable.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Excellent attention to detail.
  • Good organisational and time management skills.
  • Confident using computer systems and Microsoft Office.
  • Ability to manage a varied workload and meet deadlines.
  • Professional and customer-focused attitude.
  • Ability to work effectively within a team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £25,000–£29,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional working environment.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Opportunities for career progression.
  • Experience within a professional customer service environment.
