Project Engineer – Water / Commissioning

Job Description

Ernest Gordon Recruitment is seeking a Project Engineer specialising in Water and Commissioning to support engineering projects in Chelmsford. The role combines technical project coordination with commissioning activities and offers a hybrid working arrangement.

Responsibilities

  • Support the delivery of water and commissioning projects.
  • Coordinate engineering activities throughout project delivery.
  • Assist with commissioning plans, testing and system handover.
  • Review technical documentation, drawings and project specifications.
  • Work with engineers, contractors and project stakeholders.
  • Identify technical issues and support effective project solutions.

Requirements

  • Experience in project engineering, water engineering or commissioning.
  • Strong technical understanding of engineering project delivery.
  • Good analytical, organisational and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to interpret technical drawings and documentation.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time / hybrid position.

Salary

  • £70,000–£80,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Hybrid working opportunity.
  • Competitive salary.
  • Opportunity to work on specialist water and commissioning projects.
  • Technical and professional development opportunities.
