Warehouse Picker

Job Description

Aldi Distribution Centre, operated by Aldi Stores Limited, plays a vital role in supplying Aldi supermarkets across the United Kingdom with high-quality products. Aldi is seeking a hardworking and detail-oriented Warehouse Picker to join its distribution team. In this role, you will accurately pick customer and store orders, prepare goods for dispatch, and help maintain efficient warehouse operations while adhering to company standards and health and safety regulations.

Responsibilities

  • Pick products accurately using handheld scanners and picking lists.
  • Prepare and organise orders for dispatch to Aldi stores.
  • Load and unload goods safely and efficiently.
  • Inspect products for quality and report damaged or missing items.
  • Maintain accurate stock records and assist with inventory counts.
  • Keep warehouse aisles, storage areas, and workstations clean and organised.
  • Meet daily productivity and accuracy targets.
  • Follow company procedures, food safety standards, and health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous warehouse or order picking experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Ability to lift, carry, and move goods safely.
  • Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced warehouse environment.
  • Basic computer skills and experience using handheld scanners is an advantage.
  • Strong teamwork and communication skills.
  • Reliable, punctual, and committed to achieving performance targets.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and overtime when required.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including early mornings, evenings, nights, and weekends.

Salary

  • £13.50–£15.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Comprehensive training and ongoing development.
  • Employee discounts and reward programmes.
  • Health and wellbeing support services.
  • Modern warehouse facilities and equipment.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression within Aldi.
