Customer Service Consultant

Job Description

TSB Bank is seeking a professional Customer Service Consultant to provide excellent support to customers and help them with their banking needs. The successful candidate will build trusted customer relationships, handle enquiries, explain suitable products and services, and ensure customers receive an efficient and professional service.

Responsibilities

  • Provide friendly and professional customer service.
  • Assist customers with banking enquiries and service requests.
  • Listen to customer needs and identify appropriate solutions.
  • Explain banking products and services clearly and accurately.
  • Process customer requests and maintain accurate records.
  • Handle customer concerns and resolve issues where possible.
  • Follow banking procedures and security requirements.
  • Protect customer information and maintain confidentiality.
  • Work with colleagues to deliver a consistent customer experience.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service experience is desirable.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Strong listening and interpersonal skills.
  • Good numerical and administrative abilities.
  • Confident using computer systems and digital banking tools.
  • Strong attention to detail.
  • Professional approach to handling sensitive customer information.
  • Ability to work effectively within a busy customer-focused environment.

Working Hours

  • 35 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working between 8:00am and 8:00pm, Monday to Saturday.
  • Full-time position.

Salary

  • £26,160 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional banking environment.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Opportunities to develop financial services experience.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
