Internal Sales & Warehouse Assistant

Job Description

New D Zambelis Ltd is seeking a reliable Internal Sales & Warehouse Assistant to support customers, internal sales activities, and day-to-day warehouse operations. The successful candidate will combine strong customer service and sales skills with practical warehouse support to ensure orders are processed accurately and efficiently.

Responsibilities

  • Respond to customer enquiries and provide product information.
  • Process internal sales enquiries, orders, and quotations.
  • Build positive relationships with customers and assist with their requirements.
  • Process orders accurately and coordinate with warehouse colleagues.
  • Receive, check, organise, and store incoming stock.
  • Pick and prepare customer orders for dispatch.
  • Maintain accurate stock records and report discrepancies.
  • Assist with loading, unloading, and general warehouse duties where required.
  • Maintain a clean, safe, and organised warehouse environment.
  • Support colleagues across sales and warehouse operations during busy periods.

Requirements

  • Previous sales, customer service, warehouse, or administration experience is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Good organisational and time management abilities.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Confident using computers and order-processing systems.
  • Commercial awareness and a customer-focused approach.
  • Ability to undertake practical warehouse duties.
  • Reliable, flexible, and willing to work as part of a team.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to develop sales and warehouse experience.
