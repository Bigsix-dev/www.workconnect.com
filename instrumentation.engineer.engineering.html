Instrumentation Engineer

Job Description

Industrial Engineering Company provides engineering design, installation, and technical services for industrial facilities and process systems. The company is seeking an Instrumentation Engineer to design, maintain, test, and improve instrumentation and control systems used in industrial operations. You will work with multidisciplinary engineering teams to ensure measurement and control systems operate accurately, safely, and reliably.

Responsibilities

  • Design and develop instrumentation and control systems for industrial applications.
  • Prepare instrumentation specifications, drawings, schedules, and technical documentation.
  • Select and specify suitable instrumentation and measurement equipment.
  • Support the installation, testing, commissioning, and maintenance of instrumentation systems.
  • Investigate instrumentation faults and develop effective technical solutions.
  • Work with electrical, control, mechanical, and process engineering teams.
  • Carry out inspections and performance checks on instrumentation equipment.
  • Ensure instrumentation systems comply with relevant technical, safety, and industry standards.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Instrumentation Engineering, Control Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience with industrial instrumentation and control systems is desirable.
  • Good understanding of measurement systems, sensors, transmitters, and control equipment.
  • Ability to read and interpret instrumentation and engineering drawings.
  • Experience with testing, calibration, commissioning, and fault diagnosis.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent attention to detail and technical accuracy.
  • Good communication and multidisciplinary teamwork abilities.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday working schedule.
