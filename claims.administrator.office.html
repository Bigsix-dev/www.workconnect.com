Claims Administrator

Job Description

Direct Line Group PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading insurance companies, providing a wide range of personal and commercial insurance products to millions of customers. Direct Line Group is seeking a highly organised and customer-focused Claims Administrator to support the claims management process. In this role, you will manage claims documentation, maintain accurate records, communicate with customers, and provide administrative support to ensure claims are handled efficiently and professionally.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to claims handlers and insurance teams.
  • Process new claims information and maintain accurate customer records.
  • Review, update, and manage claims documentation and correspondence.
  • Communicate with customers, suppliers, and internal teams regarding claim progress.
  • Update claims management systems with accurate information.
  • Prepare reports, letters, and administrative documents.
  • Assist with resolving customer queries and escalate issues when required.
  • Ensure all claims activities comply with company procedures, regulations, and data protection requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in claims administration, insurance, customer service, or office administration.
  • Excellent organisational skills with strong attention to detail.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • Ability to manage confidential customer information professionally.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Strong problem-solving and decision-making skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Customer-focused approach with excellent interpersonal skills.

Working Hours

  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard business hours.

Salary

  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Direct Line Group.
