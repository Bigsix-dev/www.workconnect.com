Customer Service Administrator

Job Description

Office Angels is seeking a Customer Service Administrator to provide efficient customer support and administrative assistance. The successful candidate will manage customer enquiries, maintain accurate records, process documentation, and work closely with colleagues to ensure a high standard of service is maintained.

Responsibilities

  • Respond to customer enquiries by telephone and email.
  • Provide professional and helpful customer support.
  • Process customer orders, requests, and relevant documentation.
  • Maintain accurate customer records and internal databases.
  • Handle routine customer issues and escalate complex matters when necessary.
  • Liaise with internal departments to resolve customer queries.
  • Complete general administrative and data-entry duties.
  • Monitor outstanding enquiries and follow up with customers or colleagues.
  • Ensure customer information is handled accurately and professionally.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service or administrative experience is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong organisational and administrative abilities.
  • Good attention to detail.
  • Confident using Microsoft Office and computer systems.
  • Ability to prioritise tasks and work to deadlines.
  • Positive, professional, and customer-focused attitude.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £24,500 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional office environment.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Opportunities to develop administrative and customer service experience.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
