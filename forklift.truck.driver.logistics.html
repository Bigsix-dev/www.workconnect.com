Forklift Truck Driver

Job Description

Travis Perkins PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading builders' merchants, supplying construction materials, tools, and products to trade professionals and customers nationwide. Travis Perkins is seeking a reliable and safety-focused Forklift Truck Driver to support its warehouse and yard operations. In this role, you will operate forklift equipment, manage stock movements, and ensure goods are handled safely and efficiently while maintaining excellent service standards.

Responsibilities

  • Operate forklift trucks safely to move and organise materials within the yard and warehouse.
  • Load and unload customer and supplier deliveries efficiently.
  • Move, stack, and store building materials correctly.
  • Assist with stock checks and maintain accurate inventory records.
  • Carry out daily forklift safety inspections and report any defects.
  • Prepare customer orders accurately for collection and delivery.
  • Maintain a clean, safe, and organised working environment.
  • Provide helpful and professional support to customers and colleagues.

Requirements

  • Valid forklift truck licence or recognised operator certification.
  • Previous forklift operating experience in a warehouse, yard, or distribution environment.
  • Good understanding of workplace health and safety procedures.
  • Ability to safely handle heavy materials and products.
  • Strong attention to detail and organisational skills.
  • Good communication and teamwork abilities.
  • Ability to work outdoors in different weather conditions when required.
  • Flexible availability to meet operational needs.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, and occasional weekends.

Salary

  • £13.80–£15.80 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts across Travis Perkins Group brands.
  • Forklift training and professional development opportunities.
  • Health and wellbeing support services.
  • Supportive team environment.
  • Career progression opportunities within Travis Perkins.
