Hotel Receptionist

Job Description

The County Hotel is a well-established hospitality business dedicated to providing comfortable accommodation and outstanding customer service for leisure and business travellers. The hotel is seeking a professional, friendly, and organised Hotel Receptionist to join its front office team. In this role, you will be the first point of contact for guests, managing check-ins and check-outs, handling reservations, responding to enquiries, and ensuring every guest receives a warm welcome and an excellent stay.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome guests and provide a friendly, professional first impression.
  • Complete guest check-ins and check-outs efficiently.
  • Manage room reservations, cancellations, and booking enquiries.
  • Answer telephone calls, emails, and respond to guest enquiries promptly.
  • Process payments, invoices, and maintain accurate guest records.
  • Coordinate with housekeeping and maintenance teams to ensure room availability.
  • Handle guest concerns professionally and resolve issues where possible.
  • Follow hotel procedures, data protection requirements, and health and safety policies.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Professional appearance and positive attitude.
  • Good computer skills and ability to use hotel booking systems.
  • Strong organisational and multitasking abilities.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional under pressure.
  • Good numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Previous hotel reception or customer service experience is preferred but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts, including mornings, evenings, weekends, and public holidays.

Salary

  • £12.50–£13.90 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee meals or refreshments during shifts where applicable.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive and professional working environment.
  • Opportunities for career progression within the hotel.
