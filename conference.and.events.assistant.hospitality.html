Conference & Events Assistant

Job Description

Pontlands Park Hotel is a luxury country house hotel in Essex, renowned for hosting conferences, corporate meetings, weddings, banquets, and private events. The hotel is seeking a professional, organised, and customer-focused Conference & Events Assistant to support the planning and delivery of successful events. In this role, you will assist with event preparation, room setup, guest services, and event operations while ensuring every client and guest receives an exceptional hospitality experience.

Responsibilities

  • Assist with setting up conference rooms, meeting spaces, and event venues.
  • Welcome guests and provide excellent customer service throughout events.
  • Prepare and serve refreshments, meals, and beverages during conferences and functions.
  • Support weddings, corporate events, banquets, and private functions.
  • Ensure event rooms remain clean, organised, and fully equipped.
  • Assist with moving furniture and arranging equipment according to event plans.
  • Respond promptly to guest requests and resolve minor issues professionally.
  • Follow health and safety regulations, food hygiene standards, and hotel procedures.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Friendly, professional, and well-presented appearance.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced hospitality environment.
  • Strong teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Good attention to detail and time management.
  • Ability to perform manual setup duties when required.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Previous hospitality or events experience is desirable but not essential.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts based on conference and event schedules.

Salary

  • £12.60–£14.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Complimentary or discounted staff meals during shifts.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts on hotel services.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive working environment with opportunities for career progression.
