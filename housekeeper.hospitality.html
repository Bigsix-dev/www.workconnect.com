Housekeeper

Job Description

Premier Inn, part of Whitbread PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's leading hotel brands, providing comfortable accommodation and exceptional guest service. Premier Inn is looking for reliable, hardworking, and detail-oriented Housekeepers to join its housekeeping team. In this role, you will ensure guest rooms and public areas are cleaned and maintained to the highest standards, creating a clean, safe, and welcoming environment for every guest.

Responsibilities

  • Clean and prepare guest rooms to company standards.
  • Change bed linens, replace towels, and replenish guest amenities.
  • Vacuum, dust, mop, and sanitise guest rooms, bathrooms, and public areas.
  • Report maintenance issues or damaged equipment to supervisors.
  • Restock housekeeping trolleys with cleaning supplies and toiletries.
  • Handle guest requests politely and professionally.
  • Maintain high standards of cleanliness throughout the hotel.
  • Follow health and safety, infection control, and company housekeeping procedures.

Requirements

  • Excellent attention to detail and cleanliness.
  • Good communication and customer service skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Physically fit and able to perform manual cleaning tasks.
  • Good time management and organisational skills.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and public holidays.
  • Previous housekeeping or cleaning experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 30–37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts, including weekends and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £12.25–£13.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts across Whitbread brands.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Opportunities for career progression within the company.
