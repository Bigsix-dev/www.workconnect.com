Security Supervisor

Job Description

Security Services Company provides professional guarding, monitoring, and protective security services to a range of commercial and industrial clients. The company is seeking an experienced Security Supervisor to lead security officers, maintain site standards, coordinate daily operations, and ensure security procedures are followed. You will act as a key point of contact between security teams, clients, site management, and emergency services.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise and coordinate security officers across assigned sites or shifts.
  • Allocate duties, patrols, access control responsibilities, and other security tasks.
  • Monitor staff performance and ensure security procedures are followed.
  • Conduct regular site inspections and security checks.
  • Respond to incidents, emergencies, and security concerns appropriately.
  • Complete and review security logs, incident reports, and operational records.
  • Communicate regularly with clients, site management, and security management.
  • Provide guidance, support, and training to security officers where required.

Requirements

  • Valid SIA licence appropriate to the role.
  • Previous security officer and supervisory experience is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of security procedures, incident management, and site operations.
  • Good leadership and team management skills.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal abilities.
  • Strong decision-making and problem-solving skills.
  • Good report writing and administrative abilities.
  • Professional, reliable, and confident approach to managing security teams.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working may include nights, weekends, and public holidays.

Salary

  • £30,000–£36,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive security supervisory salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Leadership and security management training.
  • Opportunities for additional professional security qualifications.
  • Supportive security operations environment.
  • Career progression into security management roles.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
