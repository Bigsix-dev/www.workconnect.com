Loss Prevention Officer

Job Description

Retail Security Company provides loss prevention and security services to retail stores, shopping centres, and commercial premises. The company is seeking a professional Loss Prevention Officer to help reduce theft, fraud, stock loss, and other security risks. You will monitor retail environments, identify suspicious activity, investigate incidents, and work with store teams to improve security and protect company assets.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor retail premises to identify theft, fraud, and other forms of loss.
  • Conduct visible and discreet security patrols throughout designated areas.
  • Monitor CCTV and other security systems where required.
  • Identify suspicious behaviour and investigate incidents in accordance with company procedures.
  • Prepare accurate incident, theft, and loss prevention reports.
  • Work closely with store management and retail employees to reduce stock loss.
  • Support investigations into internal and external theft.
  • Respond appropriately to security incidents while following legal and company procedures.

Requirements

  • Valid SIA licence where required for the position.
  • Previous retail security, loss prevention, or security experience is desirable.
  • Strong observation and investigation skills.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal abilities.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional during incidents.
  • Good understanding of confidentiality and professional boundaries.
  • Strong attention to detail and report writing skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a retail security team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Working hours may include evenings, weekends, and public holidays.

Salary

  • £26,000–£31,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive retail security salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Loss prevention and investigation training.
  • Opportunities for additional security qualifications.
  • Professional retail security environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
