Sales Administrator

Job Description

Travis Perkins PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading suppliers of building materials and home improvement products, serving trade professionals and customers nationwide. Travis Perkins is seeking a highly organised and customer-focused Sales Administrator to support the sales team and ensure the smooth processing of customer orders. In this role, you will manage sales documentation, process orders, maintain customer records, and provide excellent administrative support while helping deliver outstanding customer service.

Responsibilities

  • Process customer orders accurately and efficiently.
  • Prepare quotations, invoices, sales reports, and other sales documentation.
  • Maintain accurate customer records and update sales databases.
  • Respond to customer enquiries via telephone and email.
  • Coordinate with warehouse and logistics teams to ensure timely order fulfilment.
  • Support the sales team with administrative and operational tasks.
  • Monitor order progress and resolve customer issues promptly.
  • Ensure compliance with company procedures and data protection requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in sales administration, customer service, or office administration.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Experience using CRM or sales management systems is desirable.
  • Ability to work effectively in a busy team environment.
  • Professional attitude with a strong commitment to customer satisfaction.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with occasional flexibility depending on business needs.

Salary

  • £25,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts on Travis Perkins products.
  • Professional training and career development programmes.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing support.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Travis Perkins.
