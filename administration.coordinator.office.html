Administration Coordinator

Job Description

Veolia Environmental Services Limited is a leading environmental services company providing waste management, recycling, water, and energy solutions across the United Kingdom. Veolia is seeking an organised and proactive Administration Coordinator to support operational teams and ensure efficient administrative processes. In this role, you will coordinate office activities, maintain records, support reporting processes, and assist teams in delivering reliable environmental services.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative coordination support to operational managers and teams.
  • Maintain accurate records, databases, and filing systems.
  • Prepare reports, spreadsheets, correspondence, and operational documents.
  • Coordinate meetings, schedules, and team communications.
  • Process invoices, purchase orders, and administrative paperwork.
  • Support data entry and update internal business systems.
  • Respond to internal and external enquiries professionally.
  • Ensure administrative procedures comply with company policies and health and safety standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in administration, coordination, or business support roles.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accurate record keeping abilities.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a busy operational environment.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Experience using administrative or business management systems is desirable.
  • Professional attitude with strong teamwork and problem-solving skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard working hours.

Salary

  • £27,000–£33,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Employee discounts and reward schemes.
  • Flexible working opportunities where available.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Veolia.
