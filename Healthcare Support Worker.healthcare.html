Healthcare Support Worker

Job Description

HCRG Care Group is looking for a dedicated Healthcare Support Worker to join its multidisciplinary team and help deliver outstanding community and clinical care services. You will work closely with nurses, therapists, and other healthcare professionals to support patients with their physical, emotional, and day-to-day care needs while promoting independence and improving health outcomes. This role is ideal for individuals who are compassionate, adaptable, and committed to providing high-quality patient care.

Working Hours

  • 37.5 hours per week
  • Flexible shift patterns, including early, late, weekend, and occasional bank holiday shifts
  • Opportunities for additional hours based on service requirements

Requirements

  • Experience in a healthcare, care home, or community support setting is beneficial
  • A caring and professional attitude with strong communication skills
  • Ability to work collaboratively within a multidisciplinary team
  • Basic literacy and IT skills for maintaining care records
  • Willingness to complete mandatory training and ongoing professional development
  • Relevant Health and Social Care qualifications are desirable but not essential

What We Offer

  • Competitive annual salary of £25,000–£29,000
  • Structured induction and continuous learning opportunities
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment
  • Career development pathways across healthcare services
  • Pension scheme and annual leave entitlement
  • Employee wellbeing initiatives and assistance programmes
  • Access to training that helps you build skills and progress within the organisation
  • Opportunities to make a meaningful impact in local communities through person-centred care
