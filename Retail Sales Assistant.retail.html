Retail Sales Assistant

Job Description

Primark is seeking a motivated and customer-focused Retail Sales Assistant to join its dynamic store team. In this role, you will play an important part in delivering an outstanding shopping experience by assisting customers, maintaining attractive product displays, replenishing stock, and ensuring the sales floor remains clean, organised, and welcoming. You will work collaboratively with colleagues to achieve store targets while upholding the company's high standards of service and operational excellence.

Key Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide friendly, knowledgeable assistance throughout their shopping experience.
  • Process purchases, returns, and exchanges accurately using the point-of-sale system.
  • Replenish shelves and ensure merchandise is displayed neatly and in line with company standards.
  • Maintain a clean, safe, and organised sales environment.
  • Assist with stock deliveries, inventory checks, and stockroom organisation.
  • Promote current offers, seasonal campaigns, and loyalty initiatives where applicable.
  • Handle customer enquiries and resolve issues professionally and efficiently.
  • Support the team in achieving daily sales and customer service objectives.
  • Follow company policies relating to health and safety, security, and loss prevention.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in retail or customer service is desirable but not essential, as training may be provided.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Friendly, approachable, and committed to delivering excellent customer service.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced retail environment.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Reliable, punctual, and flexible with working hours, including weekends and holidays when required.
  • Ability to work both independently and as part of a team.

What Primark Offers

  • Competitive hourly pay of £12.50–£14.00, depending on experience and location.
  • Full-time schedule of 37.5 hours per week with opportunities for overtime.
  • Comprehensive training and ongoing professional development.
  • Employee discounts on in-store purchases.
  • Career progression opportunities within one of the UK's leading fashion retailers.
  • Supportive team environment with opportunities to develop valuable retail and customer service skills.
  • Access to company benefits, including holiday entitlement and workplace wellbeing initiatives, subject to eligibility.
