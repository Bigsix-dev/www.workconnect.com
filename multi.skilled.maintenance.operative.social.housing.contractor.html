Multi-Skilled Maintenance Operative

Job Description

Social Housing Contractor is seeking a Multi-Skilled Maintenance Operative to carry out repairs and maintenance across residential properties in Chelmsford. The successful candidate will handle a variety of building maintenance tasks while delivering a professional service to residents.

Responsibilities

  • Complete planned and reactive maintenance works in social housing properties.
  • Carry out general repairs across multiple building trades.
  • Diagnose faults and determine appropriate repair solutions.
  • Maintain high standards of workmanship and customer service.
  • Complete job records and report additional works where required.
  • Follow health, safety, and company procedures.

Requirements

  • Experience working in property maintenance or social housing.
  • Multi-skilled ability across several construction trades.
  • Good problem-solving and practical repair skills.
  • Professional approach when working in occupied properties.
  • Strong commitment to health and safety.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Based in Chelmsford.

Salary

  • £35,000–£40,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary.
  • Stable work within the social housing sector.
  • Opportunities for ongoing development.
  • Varied maintenance work.
