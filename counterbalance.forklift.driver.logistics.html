Counterbalance Forklift Driver

Job Description

Uniserve Group Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading logistics and supply chain providers, delivering warehousing, transport, and global supply chain solutions for businesses across multiple sectors. Uniserve Group is seeking an experienced and safety-conscious Counterbalance Forklift Driver to support its warehouse operations. In this role, you will operate counterbalance forklift trucks, handle stock efficiently, and ensure goods are moved safely while maintaining high standards of accuracy and workplace safety.

Responsibilities

  • Operate counterbalance forklift trucks safely within the warehouse environment.
  • Load and unload goods from vehicles and storage areas.
  • Move, stack, and store pallets accurately in designated locations.
  • Assist with goods-in, goods-out, and stock control operations.
  • Complete equipment checks before operating forklift machinery.
  • Use handheld scanners and warehouse systems to update stock movements.
  • Maintain a clean, organised, and safe working environment.
  • Follow company procedures, warehouse rules, and health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Valid Counterbalance Forklift Truck licence or recognised certification.
  • Previous counterbalance forklift experience in a warehouse or logistics environment.
  • Good knowledge of warehouse safety procedures.
  • Ability to handle stock accurately and efficiently.
  • Strong attention to detail and organisational skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Experience using warehouse management systems is desirable.
  • Flexible availability to support operational requirements.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including early mornings, evenings, and weekends when required.

Salary

  • £14.00–£16.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Forklift training and skills development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts and reward schemes.
  • Health and wellbeing support programmes.
  • Modern warehouse facilities and equipment.
  • Career progression opportunities within Uniserve Group.
