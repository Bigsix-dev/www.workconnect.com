Courier Driver

Job Description

DPD UK Limited, part of Geopost, is one of the United Kingdom's leading parcel delivery and logistics companies, renowned for its reliable next-day delivery service and commitment to customer satisfaction. DPD UK is seeking a dependable and customer-focused Courier Driver to join its delivery team. In this role, you will safely deliver and collect parcels, manage your daily route efficiently, and provide exceptional customer service while complying with company procedures and UK road transport regulations.

Responsibilities

  • Deliver and collect parcels from residential and commercial customers.
  • Plan and complete daily delivery routes efficiently and safely.
  • Load, organise, and secure parcels before beginning each route.
  • Operate handheld delivery devices to record deliveries and obtain proof of delivery.
  • Carry out daily vehicle inspections and report any defects promptly.
  • Provide professional, friendly, and efficient customer service at every delivery location.
  • Ensure parcels are handled carefully to minimise damage and loss.
  • Comply with company policies, road traffic legislation, and health and safety procedures.

Requirements

  • Valid UK Category B driving licence.
  • Excellent driving record and commitment to road safety.
  • Ability to lift and carry parcels of varying sizes and weights.
  • Strong communication and customer service skills.
  • Good organisational and time management abilities.
  • Experience using GPS navigation systems and handheld delivery devices.
  • Ability to work independently and meet delivery targets.
  • Previous courier or delivery driving experience is desirable but not essential.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts, including weekends and peak seasonal periods when required.

Salary

  • £13.50–£15.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Comprehensive driver training and ongoing professional development.
  • Employee discounts and reward programmes.
  • Health and wellbeing support services.
  • Modern fleet and delivery technology.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression within DPD UK.
