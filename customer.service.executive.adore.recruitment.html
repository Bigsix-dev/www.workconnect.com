Customer Service Executive

Job Description

Adore Recruitment is looking for a professional Customer Service Executive to deliver high-quality support to customers and ensure enquiries are handled efficiently. You will manage customer communications, resolve issues, maintain accurate records, and work with internal teams to provide effective solutions. The successful candidate will be organised, approachable, and confident when dealing with a wide range of customers.

Responsibilities

  • Handle customer enquiries through telephone, email, and other communication channels.
  • Provide accurate information about products, services, orders, and customer accounts.
  • Investigate and resolve customer complaints and service issues.
  • Process customer requests and update relevant systems accurately.
  • Follow up outstanding enquiries to ensure customers receive timely responses.
  • Work with internal departments to resolve complex customer issues.
  • Maintain accurate records of customer interactions and actions taken.
  • Contribute to customer satisfaction and continuous service improvements.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service, administration, retail, or contact centre experience is desirable.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong customer-focused attitude and professional manner.
  • Good problem-solving and decision-making skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and organisational abilities.
  • Good computer and data entry skills.
  • Ability to handle challenging customer situations calmly and professionally.
  • Ability to work effectively within a busy customer service team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Two-week rota system.
  • Working hours may vary according to the assigned rota.

Salary

  • Up to £27,000 per year.
  • Performance-related bonus available.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary with bonus opportunity.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Customer service training and development.
  • Supportive professional working environment.
  • Opportunities to develop specialist customer service skills.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
