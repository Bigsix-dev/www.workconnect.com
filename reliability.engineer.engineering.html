Reliability Engineer

Job Description

Manufacturing Company operates production facilities that manufacture engineered products and components for industrial customers. The company is seeking a Reliability Engineer to improve the reliability, availability, and performance of production equipment and manufacturing systems. You will analyse equipment performance, identify recurring failures, and work with maintenance and engineering teams to implement long-term improvements.

Responsibilities

  • Analyse equipment reliability and identify recurring failures and performance issues.
  • Develop and implement reliability improvement strategies.
  • Carry out root cause analysis on equipment failures and production problems.
  • Work with maintenance teams to improve preventive and predictive maintenance programmes.
  • Monitor equipment performance and reliability metrics.
  • Recommend engineering modifications to improve equipment availability and efficiency.
  • Maintain reliability reports, technical documentation, and equipment records.
  • Support continuous improvement and maintenance optimisation projects.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Reliability Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience in manufacturing, maintenance, or reliability engineering is desirable.
  • Strong understanding of engineering equipment and manufacturing processes.
  • Experience with root cause analysis and failure investigation.
  • Good knowledge of preventive and predictive maintenance principles.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Excellent attention to detail and technical judgement.
  • Good communication and teamwork skills.
