Sous Chef

Job Description

Miller & Carter, part of Mitchells & Butlers PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's leading premium steakhouse restaurant brands, renowned for serving expertly prepared steaks and delivering exceptional dining experiences. Miller & Carter is seeking an experienced and dedicated Sous Chef to support the Head Chef in managing kitchen operations, maintaining high food quality standards, and leading the kitchen team. This role is ideal for a passionate chef with strong leadership skills and a commitment to culinary excellence.

Responsibilities

  • Assist the Head Chef in overseeing daily kitchen operations.
  • Prepare, cook, and present high-quality dishes according to company standards.
  • Supervise and support junior chefs and kitchen staff.
  • Ensure food quality, consistency, and portion control are maintained.
  • Assist with menu planning, stock ordering, and inventory management.
  • Monitor food costs and minimise waste.
  • Ensure all kitchen equipment is used and maintained safely.
  • Comply with food hygiene regulations, HACCP procedures, health and safety standards, and company policies.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Sous Chef or Senior Chef in a busy kitchen.
  • Excellent culinary knowledge and food preparation skills.
  • Strong leadership, communication, and organisational abilities.
  • Ability to perform well under pressure in a fast-paced environment.
  • Knowledge of food safety and hygiene regulations.
  • Passion for producing high-quality food.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Professional culinary qualifications are desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 45 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £17.00–£20.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted meals during shifts.
  • Employee discounts across Mitchells & Butlers restaurants.
  • Advanced chef training and leadership development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression into senior kitchen management.
