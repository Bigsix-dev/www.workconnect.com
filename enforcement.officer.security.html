Enforcement Officer

Job Description

Local Authority provides regulatory and enforcement services to protect residents, businesses, and the wider community. The authority is seeking a professional Enforcement Officer to monitor compliance with relevant local regulations, investigate reported concerns, and take appropriate enforcement action when required. You will work with members of the public, businesses, council departments, and partner agencies to support fair and effective enforcement.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out inspections, visits, patrols, and compliance checks within designated areas.
  • Investigate complaints, reports, and potential breaches of relevant regulations.
  • Gather and record information to support enforcement decisions.
  • Issue notices or take appropriate enforcement action in accordance with established procedures.
  • Maintain accurate case records, reports, and enforcement documentation.
  • Provide information and guidance to residents, businesses, and other stakeholders.
  • Work with council departments and external agencies on enforcement matters.
  • Attend meetings, inspections, and hearings where required.

Requirements

  • Relevant enforcement, regulatory, security, public service, or investigative experience is desirable.
  • Good understanding of regulatory procedures and professional standards.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong investigation and analytical abilities.
  • Good report writing and case management skills.
  • Ability to deal confidently and professionally with members of the public.
  • Strong attention to detail and accurate record-keeping skills.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively with other agencies.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule, with occasional flexibility for operational requirements.

Salary

  • £27,000–£33,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive local authority salary.
  • Local government pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Regulatory and enforcement training.
  • Opportunities for professional qualifications and development.
  • Stable public-sector working environment.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
