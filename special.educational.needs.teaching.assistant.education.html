Special Educational Needs Teaching Assistant

Job Description

Columbus School & College is a specialist education provider dedicated to supporting children and young people with special educational needs and disabilities. The school provides a caring, inclusive, and supportive environment where students are encouraged to develop independence, confidence, and essential life skills. Columbus School & College is seeking a compassionate and dedicated Special Educational Needs Teaching Assistant to support pupils with their learning, communication, and personal development.

Responsibilities

  • Provide one-to-one and small-group support for pupils with special educational needs.
  • Assist teachers in delivering personalised learning activities.
  • Support students with communication, social skills, and emotional development.
  • Help implement individual education plans and support strategies.
  • Encourage independence and confidence through structured activities.
  • Assist with classroom organisation and preparation of learning resources.
  • Support students with personal care and additional needs where required.
  • Promote safeguarding, inclusion, and student wellbeing at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous experience supporting children or young people with special educational needs.
  • Understanding of autism, learning difficulties, or complex needs is desirable.
  • Patient, caring, and supportive approach.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a multidisciplinary team.
  • Knowledge of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with students.
  • Relevant Teaching Assistant or Special Educational Needs qualification is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £24,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • School holiday periods and paid leave entitlement.
  • Specialist training and professional development opportunities.
  • Supportive and experienced education team.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for career progression in special education.
  • Rewarding role supporting young people's development.
  • Inclusive and supportive working environment.
