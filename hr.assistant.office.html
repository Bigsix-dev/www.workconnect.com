HR Assistant

Job Description

NHS Property Services Limited provides essential property and facilities management services that support NHS organisations across England, helping create safe, efficient, and high-quality healthcare environments. NHS Property Services is seeking a professional and organised HR Assistant to support the Human Resources team with employee administration and HR processes. In this role, you will maintain employee records, assist with recruitment activities, support HR documentation, and provide administrative assistance to ensure effective people management across the organisation.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to the Human Resources team.
  • Maintain accurate employee records and HR databases.
  • Support recruitment processes including job postings, interviews, and onboarding activities.
  • Prepare HR documents, contracts, letters, and employee correspondence.
  • Assist with payroll information, absence records, and employee updates.
  • Respond to employee enquiries regarding HR processes and procedures.
  • Support training administration and staff development activities.
  • Ensure compliance with NHS policies, GDPR, and employment regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in HR administration, office administration, or a similar role.
  • Good understanding of HR processes and employee administration.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to handle confidential employee information professionally.
  • Strong teamwork and problem-solving abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard business hours.

Salary

  • £26,000–£31,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • NHS Pension Scheme.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Professional HR training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
  • Flexible working opportunities where applicable.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within NHS Property Services.
