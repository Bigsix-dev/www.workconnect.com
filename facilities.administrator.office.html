Facilities Administrator

Job Description

Mitie Group PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading facilities management and professional services companies, providing cleaning, security, engineering, and workplace management solutions across multiple sectors. Mitie Group is seeking a highly organised and proactive Facilities Administrator to support the efficient operation of facilities management services. In this role, you will coordinate maintenance requests, manage administrative records, support facilities teams, and ensure the workplace operates safely and efficiently.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to the facilities management team.
  • Coordinate maintenance requests and monitor work orders.
  • Maintain accurate records of facilities, contractors, and service schedules.
  • Prepare reports, invoices, purchase orders, and administrative documentation.
  • Schedule maintenance visits, inspections, and contractor appointments.
  • Monitor facilities budgets and assist with procurement administration.
  • Respond to internal facilities enquiries and service requests promptly.
  • Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations, company policies, and facilities management procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in facilities administration, office administration, or property management support.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and administrative accuracy.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Good communication and customer service skills.
  • Ability to coordinate multiple tasks and prioritise workloads effectively.
  • Experience using facilities management software is desirable.
  • Professional attitude with strong teamwork and problem-solving abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with flexibility depending on operational requirements.

Salary

  • £26,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts and reward programmes.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Mitie Group.
