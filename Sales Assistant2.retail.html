Sales Assistant

Superdrug Stores PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading health and beauty retailers, offering cosmetics, skincare, fragrances, toiletries, healthcare products, and pharmacy services. Superdrug is looking for enthusiastic Sales Assistants to provide exceptional customer service while supporting the smooth day-to-day operation of the store.

Responsibilities

  • Provide friendly and professional customer service.
  • Help customers find suitable health and beauty products.
  • Operate tills and process payments accurately.
  • Replenish shelves and maintain attractive product displays.
  • Promote store offers and loyalty programmes.
  • Assist with deliveries and stock organisation.
  • Maintain a clean and organised shopping environment.
  • Follow company procedures and health and safety policies.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly and approachable personality.
  • Interest in beauty and healthcare products.
  • Ability to work effectively in a team.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Flexible availability including weekends.
  • Retail experience is preferred but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns.

Salary

  • £12.30–£13.60 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discount.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing support.
  • Supportive working environment.
