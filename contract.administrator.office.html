Contract Administrator

Job Description

Morgan Sindall Group PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading construction, infrastructure, and regeneration companies, delivering major projects across commercial, residential, education, and public sectors. Morgan Sindall is seeking a highly organised Contract Administrator to support the management of contracts and project documentation. In this role, you will maintain contract records, coordinate documentation, assist project teams, and ensure contractual processes are completed accurately and in compliance with company policies.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support for construction and project contracts.
  • Prepare, maintain, and update contract documentation and records.
  • Monitor contract deadlines, renewals, and key project milestones.
  • Coordinate communication between clients, contractors, suppliers, and project teams.
  • Prepare reports, correspondence, and project documentation.
  • Maintain accurate filing systems for contractual and project records.
  • Assist with procurement documentation and invoice administration.
  • Ensure compliance with contractual requirements, company procedures, and relevant legislation.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in contract administration, project administration, or office administration.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and document management abilities.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Experience using document management or contract management software is desirable.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines.
  • Professional attitude with strong teamwork and problem-solving skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard business hours.

Salary

  • £30,000–£37,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Private healthcare and wellbeing support.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance programme.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Morgan Sindall Group.
