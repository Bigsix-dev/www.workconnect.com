Distribution Coordinator

Job Description

DHL Supply Chain Limited, part of DHL Group, is one of the world's leading logistics and supply chain providers, delivering warehousing, transport, and distribution solutions for businesses worldwide. DHL Supply Chain is seeking an organised and experienced Distribution Coordinator to support its distribution operations. In this role, you will coordinate daily distribution activities, monitor deliveries, manage operational information, and work closely with warehouse and transport teams to ensure efficient service delivery.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate daily distribution activities and operational schedules.
  • Monitor deliveries and ensure orders are completed on time.
  • Communicate with drivers, warehouse teams, customers, and internal departments.
  • Prepare and maintain accurate distribution records and documentation.
  • Track shipments and resolve delivery issues efficiently.
  • Update logistics and transport management systems with operational data.
  • Support improvements to distribution processes and service performance.
  • Ensure compliance with company procedures, transport regulations, and health and safety standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in logistics, distribution, or transport coordination.
  • Strong organisational and administrative skills.
  • Excellent communication and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a busy logistics environment.
  • Good understanding of distribution operations and supply chain processes.
  • Strong attention to detail when handling reports and documentation.
  • Experience using logistics management systems is desirable.
  • Ability to work effectively with operational teams and customers.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday schedule with flexibility based on operational requirements.

Salary

  • £30,000–£36,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary package.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee benefits and reward programmes.
  • Health and wellbeing support services.
  • Opportunities to work with a global logistics leader.
  • Career progression opportunities within DHL Supply Chain.
