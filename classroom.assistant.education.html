Classroom Assistant

Job Description

Chelmsford Primary Schools provide nurturing and inclusive learning environments where children are supported to develop academically, socially, and emotionally. Local primary schools in Chelmsford are seeking enthusiastic and caring Classroom Assistants to support teachers and pupils throughout the school day. In this role, you will assist with classroom activities, help pupils with their learning, prepare resources, and contribute to creating a positive and engaging educational environment.

Responsibilities

  • Support teachers with classroom activities and daily lesson preparation.
  • Assist pupils with reading, writing, numeracy, and other learning activities.
  • Provide additional support to individuals and small groups of pupils.
  • Help prepare classroom resources and maintain an organised learning space.
  • Encourage positive behaviour, confidence, and independence among pupils.
  • Support children with additional educational needs where required.
  • Monitor pupil engagement and provide feedback to teaching staff.
  • Promote safeguarding, inclusion, and pupil wellbeing at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous experience working with children in a school, childcare, or community setting is desirable.
  • Caring, patient, and enthusiastic approach to supporting pupils.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with children and staff.
  • Understanding of child development and classroom support practices.
  • Knowledge of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Good organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Relevant Teaching Assistant, childcare, or education qualification is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 32.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £21,000–£26,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • School holiday periods and paid leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Supportive and friendly school environment.
  • Employee wellbeing support.
  • Opportunities to progress within education.
  • Rewarding role supporting children's learning and development.
  • Inclusive and collaborative working environment.
