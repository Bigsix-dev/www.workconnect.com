Project Manager – MOJ Project

Job Description

Randstad is recruiting a Project Manager to lead an MOJ project in Chelmsford and Essex. The successful candidate will be responsible for coordinating project delivery, managing stakeholders and ensuring construction activities are completed safely, efficiently and within agreed budgets and timescales.

Responsibilities

  • Lead the delivery of the assigned construction project.
  • Manage project programmes, budgets, resources and milestones.
  • Coordinate contractors, subcontractors and project teams.
  • Monitor progress and manage project risks and issues.
  • Ensure compliance with health, safety and quality requirements.
  • Provide regular project updates to stakeholders and senior management.

Requirements

  • Proven experience as a Construction Project Manager.
  • Strong knowledge of project delivery and construction processes.
  • Excellent leadership and stakeholder management skills.
  • Strong commercial, organisational and problem-solving abilities.
  • Experience managing complex construction projects is advantageous.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours based on project and site requirements.

Salary

  • £80,000–£85,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Senior project management opportunity.
  • Opportunity to work on a significant MOJ project.
  • Competitive salary.
  • Professional and career development opportunities.
