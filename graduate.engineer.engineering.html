Graduate Engineer

Job Description

Engineering Consultancy provides professional engineering services and innovative technical solutions across a variety of industries. The company is seeking an enthusiastic Graduate Engineer to join its engineering team and develop practical skills while supporting experienced engineers on real-world projects. This role provides an excellent opportunity for a recent engineering graduate to gain industry experience, contribute to technical projects, and build a successful engineering career.

Responsibilities

  • Support senior engineers with design, analysis, and technical project activities.
  • Assist with preparing engineering drawings, reports, and documentation.
  • Carry out technical research, calculations, and project assessments.
  • Support testing, inspections, and engineering improvement activities.
  • Work collaboratively with engineering teams across different projects.
  • Learn and apply engineering standards, processes, and best practices.
  • Assist with solving technical challenges and developing engineering solutions.
  • Maintain accurate project records and technical documentation.

Requirements

  • Recently completed degree in Engineering or a related technical discipline.
  • Strong interest in developing a career within engineering.
  • Good understanding of engineering principles and problem-solving methods.
  • Strong analytical and mathematical skills.
  • Good communication and teamwork abilities.
  • Willingness to learn new engineering tools and technologies.
  • Good organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of an engineering team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £28,000–£35,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive graduate salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Structured graduate training programme.
  • Mentoring from experienced engineers.
  • Professional development opportunities.
  • Supportive engineering environment.
  • Career progression opportunities.
