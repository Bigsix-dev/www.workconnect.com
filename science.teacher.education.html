Science Teacher

Job Description

Chelmsford Secondary Schools are committed to delivering high-quality education that inspires curiosity, innovation, and academic excellence. Schools across Chelmsford are seeking a dedicated and enthusiastic Science Teacher to deliver engaging lessons across Biology, Chemistry, and Physics. In this role, you will encourage students to develop scientific knowledge, practical laboratory skills, and critical thinking while preparing them for examinations and future careers in science-related fields.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and deliver engaging science lessons in accordance with the national curriculum.
  • Teach Biology, Chemistry, and Physics across Key Stages as required.
  • Prepare practical laboratory activities while ensuring health and safety compliance.
  • Assess student work and provide constructive feedback to support academic progress.
  • Develop lesson plans, teaching resources, and assessment materials.
  • Monitor student performance and maintain accurate academic records.
  • Create a safe, inclusive, and stimulating classroom environment.
  • Participate in parents' evenings, staff meetings, extracurricular activities, and professional development.

Requirements

  • Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) is required.
  • Bachelor's degree in Science or a related subject.
  • Previous experience teaching science at secondary school level is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of Biology, Chemistry, and Physics curriculum requirements.
  • Excellent classroom management and communication skills.
  • Ability to inspire students through practical and theoretical learning.
  • Commitment to safeguarding and promoting student wellbeing.
  • Strong organisational, planning, and assessment skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 32.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £34,000–£52,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive teacher salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme.
  • Paid school holidays and generous annual leave.
  • Professional development and continuous training opportunities.
  • Access to modern science laboratories and teaching resources.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Career progression opportunities within secondary education.
  • Supportive and collaborative teaching environment.
