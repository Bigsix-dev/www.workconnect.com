Clinical Scheduler

Role Overview

Superior Healthcare is seeking a highly organised and detail-oriented Clinical Scheduler to join our Essex team. The successful candidate will be responsible for coordinating clinical staff rotas, managing scheduling systems, and ensuring smooth daily deployment of healthcare professionals across services.

Key Responsibilities

  • Plan and manage clinical staff schedules in line with service needs
  • Allocate shifts efficiently to ensure adequate staffing levels at all times
  • Respond to rota changes, absences, and urgent staffing requirements
  • Maintain accurate scheduling records and update internal systems
  • Liaise with healthcare staff and management to ensure effective communication
  • Support compliance with staffing policies and operational standards

Requirements

  • Previous experience in scheduling, coordination, or healthcare administration
  • Strong organisational and time-management skills
  • Excellent communication and problem-solving abilities
  • Ability to work under pressure in a fast-paced environment
  • Proficiency with scheduling systems or workforce management tools (preferred)

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package (£26,000–£32,000)
  • Opportunity to work within a supportive healthcare environment
  • Career development opportunities within the organisation
