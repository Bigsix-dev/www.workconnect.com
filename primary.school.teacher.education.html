Primary School Teacher

Job Description

The Sir Geoff Hurst Academy is a welcoming and inclusive primary school committed to providing high-quality education and creating a positive learning environment where children can achieve their full potential. The academy is seeking an enthusiastic and dedicated Primary School Teacher to inspire young learners and deliver engaging lessons that support academic, social, and emotional development. In this role, you will plan and deliver effective teaching, assess pupil progress, and work closely with colleagues and families to provide outstanding educational experiences.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and deliver engaging lessons that meet the needs of primary-aged pupils.
  • Create a safe, supportive, and inclusive classroom environment.
  • Assess and monitor pupil progress, providing feedback to support improvement.
  • Develop lesson plans, teaching resources, and learning activities.
  • Support pupils with different learning abilities and educational needs.
  • Maintain positive relationships with pupils, parents, and school staff.
  • Participate in school meetings, training sessions, and professional development activities.
  • Follow safeguarding procedures and promote pupil wellbeing.

Requirements

  • Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) is required.
  • Previous experience teaching in a primary school environment.
  • Strong classroom management and communication skills.
  • Excellent organisational and planning abilities.
  • Ability to create engaging and creative learning experiences.
  • Commitment to safeguarding and child protection practices.
  • Strong teamwork skills and ability to collaborate with colleagues.
  • Passion for supporting children's learning and development.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 32.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £34,000–£52,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive teacher salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme.
  • Paid annual leave and school holidays.
  • Professional development and training opportunities.
  • Supportive school leadership team.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for career progression within education.
  • Positive and inclusive working environment.
