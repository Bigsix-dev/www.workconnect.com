Customer Assistant

Job Overview

Lidl Great Britain Limited is seeking a dedicated and customer-focused Customer Assistant to join its retail team. The role is essential in delivering excellent in-store service, maintaining high operational standards, and ensuring customers have a smooth and positive shopping experience.

Key Responsibilities

  • Providing excellent customer service and assisting shoppers with queries
  • Operating tills accurately and handling transactions efficiently
  • Stocking shelves and ensuring products are well presented
  • Maintaining cleanliness and organisation across the store
  • Supporting deliveries and stock rotation
  • Working collaboratively with team members to meet store targets

Requirements

  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced retail environment
  • Flexibility to work shifts, including evenings and weekends
  • A positive, proactive, and team-oriented attitude
  • Previous retail experience is beneficial but not essential

What We Offer

  • Competitive hourly pay within £13.45–£14.45 range
  • Staff discount and company benefits
  • Full training and development opportunities
  • Career progression within Lidl GB
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment
