Transport Planner

Job Description

Gregory Distribution Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading transport and logistics providers, delivering specialist distribution services for customers across food, retail, and industrial sectors. Gregory Distribution is seeking an organised and analytical Transport Planner to join its operations team. In this role, you will plan delivery routes, coordinate transport schedules, support drivers, and ensure deliveries are completed efficiently while meeting customer expectations and operational targets.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and organise daily delivery routes to maximise efficiency.
  • Schedule vehicle movements and allocate work to drivers.
  • Monitor transport operations and make adjustments when required.
  • Communicate with drivers, customers, and internal teams regarding delivery updates.
  • Ensure transport plans meet customer requirements and deadlines.
  • Maintain accurate transport records, schedules, and operational reports.
  • Monitor vehicle availability and support efficient fleet utilisation.
  • Ensure compliance with transport legislation, company policies, and health and safety standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in transport planning, logistics, or distribution operations.
  • Strong planning and organisational skills.
  • Excellent problem-solving and decision-making abilities.
  • Ability to work under pressure in a fast-paced transport environment.
  • Good knowledge of UK transport operations and delivery processes.
  • Strong communication skills with drivers, customers, and colleagues.
  • Experience using transport management systems is desirable.
  • Good attention to detail and ability to manage multiple priorities.

Working Hours

  • Flexible working hours depending on operational requirements.
