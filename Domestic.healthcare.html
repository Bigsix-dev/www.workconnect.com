Domestic Assistant

Job Description

As a Domestic Assistant at Madelayne Court Care Home, you will help maintain a clean, safe, and comfortable environment for residents, staff, and visitors. Your work plays an important role in supporting the wellbeing of elderly residents by ensuring high standards of cleanliness and hygiene throughout the care home.

Key Responsibilities

  • Clean residents' bedrooms, bathrooms, lounges, dining areas, and communal spaces.
  • Dust, vacuum, mop floors, and polish surfaces.
  • Change bed linen and replenish room supplies when required.
  • Follow infection prevention and control procedures at all times.
  • Safely use and store cleaning equipment and chemicals.
  • Dispose of waste in accordance with health and safety guidelines.
  • Report maintenance issues or hazards to the appropriate team.
  • Work respectfully around residents, preserving their privacy and dignity.
  • Assist with laundry duties when needed.
  • Help maintain a welcoming and pleasant environment for residents and visitors.

About You

  • Previous cleaning or housekeeping experience is helpful but not essential.
  • Reliable, punctual, and able to work independently.
  • Good attention to detail and commitment to cleanliness.
  • Friendly and respectful attitude towards older people and colleagues.
  • Ability to follow health and safety procedures and instructions.
  • Physically able to carry out routine cleaning tasks.
  • Willingness to work as part of a team in a busy care home environment.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay of £12.71 per hour.
  • Full-time, stable working hours.
  • Paid training and induction.
  • Uniform provided.
  • Pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Opportunities for career development within the care sector.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes (where available).
  • Chance to make a meaningful difference in residents' daily lives.
