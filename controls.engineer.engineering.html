Controls Engineer

Job Description

Automation Solutions Provider delivers industrial automation and control solutions for manufacturing and engineering environments. The company is seeking a skilled Controls Engineer to design, develop, test, and maintain control systems. In this role, you will work with engineering and production teams to improve automated processes, troubleshoot control systems, and ensure reliable and efficient operation of industrial equipment.

Responsibilities

  • Design and develop industrial control systems for automated equipment.
  • Program and configure programmable logic controllers and human-machine interfaces.
  • Develop control system specifications, drawings, and technical documentation.
  • Test, commission, and troubleshoot automation and control systems.
  • Identify and resolve electrical, control, and automation faults.
  • Work closely with mechanical, electrical, and software engineering teams.
  • Support the installation and commissioning of automated machinery.
  • Identify opportunities to improve system performance, reliability, and efficiency.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Control Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Automation, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience with industrial automation and control systems is desirable.
  • Knowledge of programmable logic controllers and industrial control technologies.
  • Good understanding of electrical control systems and automation principles.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to read and interpret electrical and control system drawings.
  • Strong attention to detail and technical accuracy.
  • Good communication and teamwork skills.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday working schedule.
