Emergency Planning Officer

Job Description

Local Authority is responsible for preparing communities and essential services to respond effectively to emergencies and major incidents. The authority is seeking an organised Emergency Planning Officer to support emergency preparedness, business continuity, risk assessment, and incident response planning. You will work with council departments, emergency services, partner organisations, and other stakeholders to ensure effective plans are developed, maintained, tested, and improved.

Responsibilities

  • Develop, review, and maintain emergency response and business continuity plans.
  • Support risk assessments and identify potential threats to local services and communities.
  • Coordinate emergency planning exercises, training sessions, and simulations.
  • Work with police, fire and rescue services, health organisations, and other partner agencies.
  • Support the local authority response to major incidents and emergencies when required.
  • Maintain accurate emergency planning documentation, contact information, and records.
  • Review lessons learned from exercises and incidents and recommend improvements.
  • Provide advice and guidance to council departments on emergency preparedness and resilience.

Requirements

  • Relevant qualification or experience in emergency planning, resilience, risk management, public services, or a related field.
  • Good understanding of emergency management and business continuity principles.
  • Strong planning, organisational, and project management skills.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and make effective decisions during incidents.
  • Good report writing and presentation skills.
  • Ability to work effectively with multiple agencies and professional teams.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule, with occasional out-of-hours emergency response requirements.

Salary

  • £32,000–£40,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive local authority salary.
  • Local government pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Emergency planning and resilience training.
  • Opportunities to work with emergency services and partner agencies.
  • Professional development and specialist training opportunities.
  • Career progression within emergency planning and public services.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
