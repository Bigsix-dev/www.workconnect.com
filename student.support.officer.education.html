Student Support Officer

Job Description

Chelmsford College is a leading further education provider committed to supporting students through high-quality teaching, guidance, and personal development services. Chelmsford College is seeking a dedicated and approachable Student Support Officer to provide guidance and assistance to students throughout their college journey. In this role, you will support student wellbeing, provide advice and information, coordinate support services, and help create an inclusive environment where students can succeed academically and personally.

Responsibilities

  • Provide advice, guidance, and support to students throughout their college experience.
  • Assist students with personal, academic, and wellbeing-related concerns.
  • Coordinate support services and refer students to appropriate resources when required.
  • Maintain accurate student records and support documentation processes.
  • Work closely with teaching staff and support teams to improve student outcomes.
  • Promote equality, inclusion, and student engagement across the college.
  • Support student events, induction activities, and college initiatives.
  • Handle confidential student information in line with data protection requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in student support, education, youth work, or a similar role.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with students and colleagues.
  • Strong organisational and administrative skills.
  • Understanding of safeguarding, equality, and student welfare practices.
  • Ability to provide professional and confidential support.
  • Strong problem-solving and decision-making abilities.
  • Commitment to supporting student achievement and wellbeing.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during college working hours.

Salary

  • £27,000–£34,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme or workplace pension.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Professional development and training opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
  • Supportive and inclusive college environment.
  • Opportunities for career progression in education support services.
  • Access to staff benefits and college facilities.
