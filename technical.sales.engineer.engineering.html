Technical Sales Engineer

Job Description

Engineering Solutions Provider supplies technical products, engineering services, and specialist solutions to customers across industrial and commercial sectors. The company is seeking a Technical Sales Engineer to combine engineering knowledge with customer-focused sales activities. You will work with customers to understand their technical requirements, recommend suitable solutions, prepare quotations, and support projects from initial enquiry through to delivery.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain strong relationships with existing and prospective customers.
  • Understand customer requirements and recommend appropriate engineering solutions.
  • Prepare technical quotations, proposals, and product presentations.
  • Provide technical information and guidance to customers throughout the sales process.
  • Work with engineering and technical teams to develop suitable solutions.
  • Identify new business opportunities within target markets.
  • Attend customer meetings, site visits, exhibitions, and industry events when required.
  • Monitor sales opportunities and maintain accurate customer and project records.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Engineering or a related technical discipline.
  • Previous experience in technical sales, engineering, or a customer-facing technical role is desirable.
  • Strong understanding of engineering products, systems, or technical solutions.
  • Excellent communication, presentation, and negotiation skills.
  • Strong commercial awareness and customer-focused approach.
  • Ability to understand complex technical requirements and explain them clearly.
  • Strong organisational and time-management skills.
  • Ability to work effectively with sales, engineering, and technical teams.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £40,000–£50,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering and sales salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Technical and commercial sales training.
  • Opportunities to develop strong industry and customer relationships.
  • Performance and career development opportunities.
  • Supportive engineering and commercial environment.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
