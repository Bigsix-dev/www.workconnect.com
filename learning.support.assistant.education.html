Learning Support Assistant

Job Description

Oaklands Infant School is a caring and inclusive primary school dedicated to providing a nurturing learning environment where young children can develop confidence, independence, and essential skills. Oaklands Infant School is seeking a compassionate and supportive Learning Support Assistant to work alongside teachers in helping pupils achieve their educational goals. In this role, you will provide classroom assistance, support individual learning needs, and encourage children's social, emotional, and academic development.

Responsibilities

  • Support teachers with classroom activities and daily learning tasks.
  • Provide one-to-one and small-group support for pupils who require additional help.
  • Assist children with literacy, numeracy, and early learning activities.
  • Encourage pupils' confidence, independence, and positive behaviour.
  • Support children with special educational needs and individual learning plans.
  • Prepare learning resources and maintain an organised classroom environment.
  • Observe and record pupil progress to support teaching decisions.
  • Promote safeguarding, inclusion, and pupil wellbeing at all times.

Requirements

  • Experience working with children in a school, nursery, or childcare environment is desirable.
  • Patient, caring, and enthusiastic approach to supporting young learners.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with children and staff.
  • Understanding of child development and learning support practices.
  • Knowledge of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Good organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Relevant Teaching Assistant or childcare qualification is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Part-time position.
  • 25 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £17,700–£18,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • School holiday periods and paid leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Supportive and welcoming school environment.
  • Employee wellbeing support.
  • Opportunities to gain education experience and career progression.
  • Rewarding role supporting children's early development.
  • Inclusive and collaborative working environment.
