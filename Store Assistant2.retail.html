Store Assistant

Poundstretcher Limited is a well-established UK discount retailer offering affordable groceries, household goods, homeware, gardening products, clothing, and seasonal items. Poundstretcher is looking for enthusiastic and dependable Store Assistants to support daily store operations, provide excellent customer service, and help maintain a clean, organised, and fully stocked shopping environment.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide friendly, professional assistance.
  • Operate tills and process payments accurately.
  • Replenish shelves and rotate stock as required.
  • Receive, unpack, and organise deliveries.
  • Maintain clean, safe, and well-presented sales floor areas.
  • Assist customers with locating products and answering enquiries.
  • Support promotional displays and merchandising activities.
  • Follow company policies and health and safety procedures.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly, reliable, and positive attitude.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a busy retail environment.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Good teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Flexible to work weekends and bank holidays.
  • Previous retail experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns including weekends.

Salary

  • £12.20–£13.30 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discount on selected products.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive workplace.
  • Opportunities for career progression.
