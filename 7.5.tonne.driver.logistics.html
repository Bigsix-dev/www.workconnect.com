7.5 Tonne Driver

Job Description

Driver Hire Chelmsford is a specialist recruitment company providing professional transport and logistics staffing solutions across the United Kingdom. Driver Hire Chelmsford is seeking an experienced and reliable 7.5 Tonne Driver to support its clients' delivery operations. In this role, you will operate a 7.5 tonne vehicle, complete scheduled deliveries, handle goods safely, and provide excellent customer service while maintaining high standards of professionalism and road safety.

Responsibilities

  • Operate a 7.5 tonne vehicle safely and efficiently.
  • Complete scheduled deliveries and collections according to assigned routes.
  • Carry out daily vehicle inspections and report any faults or defects.
  • Load, unload, and secure goods safely during transportation.
  • Complete delivery paperwork and electronic delivery records accurately.
  • Provide professional customer service during deliveries and collections.
  • Ensure goods are handled carefully to prevent damage.
  • Follow UK road transport regulations, company policies, and health and safety procedures.

Requirements

  • Valid UK Category C1 driving licence.
  • Valid Driver Certificate of Professional Competence (Driver CPC).
  • Digital Tachograph Driver Card.
  • Good understanding of drivers' hours regulations and road safety requirements.
  • Strong communication and customer service skills.
  • Ability to work independently and complete delivery schedules efficiently.
  • Physically fit and able to assist with loading and unloading duties.
  • Previous 7.5 tonne driving experience is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40–45 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts depending on client requirements.

Salary

  • £15.00–£17.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Access to a wide range of transport and logistics opportunities.
  • Driver training and professional development support.
  • Dedicated recruitment support from Driver Hire Chelmsford.
  • Opportunities for permanent employment with leading companies.
  • Flexible working options across different logistics contracts.
