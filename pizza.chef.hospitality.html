Pizza Chef

Job Description

PizzaExpress Restaurants Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading casual dining restaurant chains, celebrated for its handcrafted pizzas, fresh ingredients, and authentic Italian-inspired cuisine. PizzaExpress is seeking a skilled and enthusiastic Pizza Chef to join its kitchen team. In this role, you will prepare and cook handcrafted pizzas, maintain exceptional food quality, and ensure every order meets the company's high standards while working in a fast-paced and collaborative kitchen environment.

Responsibilities

  • Prepare pizza dough, sauces, toppings, and other ingredients according to company recipes.
  • Cook handcrafted pizzas using pizza ovens while maintaining quality and consistency.
  • Ensure all dishes are presented to company standards before serving.
  • Monitor ingredient freshness and assist with stock rotation.
  • Maintain cleanliness and organisation of the pizza preparation area.
  • Support the wider kitchen team during busy service periods.
  • Minimise food waste and follow portion control guidelines.
  • Comply with food hygiene regulations, HACCP procedures, and health and safety standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Pizza Chef, Line Cook, or similar kitchen role is preferred.
  • Passion for preparing authentic, high-quality pizzas.
  • Good understanding of food hygiene and kitchen safety procedures.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced restaurant environment.
  • Excellent teamwork and communication skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and food presentation.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Professional culinary qualifications are desirable but not essential.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £13.80–£16.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted meals during shifts.
  • Employee discounts at PizzaExpress restaurants.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment with opportunities for career progression.
