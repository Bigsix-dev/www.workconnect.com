Retail Sales Associate

The Body Shop International Limited is a globally recognised British cosmetics, skincare, and beauty retailer committed to producing ethically sourced, cruelty-free, and sustainable products. The Body Shop is seeking enthusiastic and customer-focused Retail Sales Associates to deliver exceptional customer service, provide expert product recommendations, and help customers find products that meet their skincare and beauty needs while maintaining the company's high retail standards.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and deliver exceptional customer service.
  • Recommend skincare, cosmetics, body care, and fragrance products.
  • Operate tills and process sales transactions accurately.
  • Maintain attractive product displays and visual merchandising standards.
  • Replenish stock and organise shelves throughout the store.
  • Promote current offers, loyalty programmes, and new product launches.
  • Assist with stock deliveries and inventory checks.
  • Follow company policies, health and safety procedures, and ethical retail standards.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Passion for skincare, beauty products, and ethical retailing.
  • Friendly, approachable, and professional attitude.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced retail environment.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and public holidays.
  • Previous retail experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including weekends and seasonal trading periods.

Salary

  • £12.30–£13.75 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Generous employee discount on The Body Shop products.
  • Training and professional development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Supportive, diverse, and inclusive working environment.
  • Career progression opportunities within the business.
