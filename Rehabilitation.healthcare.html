Rehabilitation Assistant

Job Description

As a Rehabilitation Assistant, you will work alongside physiotherapists, occupational therapists, nurses, and other healthcare professionals to help patients regain their independence after illness, injury, or surgery. You will provide practical support, encourage patients throughout their recovery journey, and assist with rehabilitation programmes designed to improve mobility, strength, and daily living skills.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist patients with rehabilitation exercises and treatment plans under professional supervision.
  • Support individuals with mobility training and safe use of walking aids or equipment.
  • Monitor patient progress and accurately record observations.
  • Encourage patients to achieve personal recovery goals and maintain motivation.
  • Assist with personal care and activities of daily living where required.
  • Prepare therapy areas and ensure equipment is clean and ready for use.
  • Work closely with multidisciplinary teams to deliver high-quality patient care.
  • Follow infection prevention, health and safety, and safeguarding procedures at all times.
  • Communicate effectively with patients, families, and healthcare colleagues.
  • Maintain confidentiality and uphold professional standards.

About You

The ideal candidate will:

  • Have a caring, compassionate, and patient-focused approach.
  • Enjoy working as part of a multidisciplinary healthcare team.
  • Possess excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Be physically capable of supporting patients with mobility and rehabilitation activities.`
  • Demonstrate reliability, flexibility, and strong organisational skills.
  • Have previous healthcare or care experience (preferred but not always essential).
  • Be willing to undertake training and professional development.
  • Be committed to delivering safe and high-quality patient care.

  • Chance to make a meaningful difference in patients' recovery and quality of life.
