Health Visitor Assistant

Job Description

A Health Visitor Assistant supports Health Visitors in delivering community healthcare services for babies, young children, and families. The role involves assisting with developmental checks, promoting healthy lifestyles, providing practical support to parents, maintaining accurate records, and helping coordinate clinics and home visits. You may also signpost families to local health and social care services and contribute to safeguarding and wellbeing initiatives.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Assist Health Visitors during home visits and child health clinics.
  • Support parents and carers with advice on child development and healthy living.
  • Record observations and maintain confidential patient records.
  • Help organise appointments and community outreach activities.
  • Promote immunisation, nutrition, and public health initiatives.
  • Work collaboratively with healthcare professionals and local services.

Requirements:

  • Good communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Experience in healthcare, childcare, or community support is advantageous but not always essential.
  • Ability to work compassionately with children and families.
  • Basic IT and record-keeping skills.
  • Willingness to undergo relevant training and background checks.
