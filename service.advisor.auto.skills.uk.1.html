Service Advisor

Job Description

Auto Skills UK is seeking an experienced Service Advisor to manage customer vehicle servicing and repair requirements. You will provide professional customer support, coordinate workshop activities, explain recommended work, and ensure customers remain informed throughout the service process. The role requires strong automotive knowledge, communication skills, and excellent organisation.

Responsibilities

  • Receive customers and manage vehicle service and repair bookings.
  • Record customer concerns and accurately communicate requirements to technicians.
  • Prepare estimates and explain service and repair recommendations.
  • Coordinate workshop activities and monitor vehicle progress.
  • Keep customers updated regarding repairs, costs, and completion times.
  • Obtain customer approval for additional work where required.
  • Process job cards, invoices, and other service documentation.
  • Maintain strong customer relationships and support high satisfaction levels.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as an Automotive Service Advisor is desirable.
  • Good knowledge of vehicle servicing, maintenance, and repair processes.
  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Confident discussing technical information and repair costs.
  • Good attention to detail and administrative skills.
  • Commercial awareness and ability to identify appropriate service opportunities.
  • Ability to work effectively in a busy automotive workshop environment.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday, 8:00am–5:00pm.
  • One Saturday in every three weeks.
  • Full-time position.

Salary

  • £30,000+ per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Automotive technical and customer service training.
  • Professional workshop environment.
  • Opportunities for professional development.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
