Senior to Director-level Surveyor

Job Description

Property Consultancy is seeking an experienced Senior to Director-level Surveyor to provide specialist property advice and lead client assignments. The successful candidate will manage complex surveying instructions, develop client relationships, provide strategic property advice, and contribute to business development and team leadership.

Responsibilities

  • Provide professional property surveying and consultancy advice to clients.
  • Manage a portfolio of property instructions and client assignments.
  • Undertake property inspections, assessments, and valuations where required.
  • Prepare detailed reports, recommendations, and professional advice.
  • Manage complex property projects and transactions.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with clients and professional advisers.
  • Identify new business opportunities and contribute to business development.
  • Lead and support junior surveyors and other members of the team.
  • Attend client meetings, negotiations, and property-related discussions.
  • Maintain professional standards and ensure work is delivered accurately and on time.

Requirements

  • Significant experience in property surveying or consultancy.
  • Relevant professional surveying qualification is desirable.
  • Strong technical knowledge and property market understanding.
  • Excellent analytical, reporting, and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong client relationship and business development abilities.
  • Excellent communication and negotiation skills.
  • Leadership and team management experience at senior level.
  • Strong commercial awareness and strategic approach.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £50,000–£80,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive senior-level salary package.
  • Professional property consultancy environment.
  • Leadership and professional development opportunities.
  • Exposure to complex and varied property assignments.
  • Career progression opportunities.
