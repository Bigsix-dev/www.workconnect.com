Hotel Duty Supervisor

Job Description

Channels Hotel is a well-established boutique hotel renowned for delivering exceptional accommodation, dining, and guest services. Channels Hotel is seeking an experienced and proactive Hotel Duty Supervisor to oversee the day-to-day operation of the hotel and support departmental teams in delivering outstanding customer service. In this role, you will supervise daily hotel activities, ensure operational standards are maintained, resolve guest concerns, and assist management in creating a welcoming and efficient environment for both guests and employees.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise the daily operation of the hotel across reception, housekeeping, and food and beverage departments.
  • Lead and support hotel team members to deliver exceptional guest service.
  • Handle guest enquiries, complaints, and special requests professionally and efficiently.
  • Monitor hotel standards, cleanliness, security, and operational performance.
  • Assist with staff scheduling, shift handovers, and daily operational reporting.
  • Support check-in and check-out procedures during busy periods.
  • Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations, hotel policies, and company procedures.
  • Respond appropriately to emergencies and ensure guest safety at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous supervisory experience within a hotel or hospitality environment.
  • Excellent leadership and customer service skills.
  • Strong communication, organisational, and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to remain calm and make decisions under pressure.
  • Good knowledge of hotel operations and guest service standards.
  • Basic computer skills and experience using hotel management systems are desirable.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Professional appearance and commitment to delivering exceptional guest experiences.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible rotating shifts, including mornings, evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £15.50–£18.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts on hotel accommodation and dining.
  • Leadership training and professional development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression within the hospitality industry.
