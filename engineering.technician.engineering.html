Engineering Technician

Job Description

Manufacturing Company specialises in producing high-quality products through advanced engineering and efficient manufacturing processes. The company is seeking a skilled and practical Engineering Technician to support production, testing, maintenance, and engineering improvement activities. In this role, you will work alongside engineers and production teams to maintain equipment performance, assist with technical projects, and ensure manufacturing operations run smoothly.

Responsibilities

  • Support engineers with manufacturing, testing, and technical activities.
  • Carry out inspections, equipment checks, and technical assessments.
  • Assist with the installation, maintenance, and repair of engineering equipment.
  • Perform testing and troubleshooting of mechanical and electrical systems.
  • Prepare technical reports, records, and engineering documentation.
  • Support production teams with technical problem-solving.
  • Maintain tools, equipment, and workshop areas to required standards.
  • Follow health and safety procedures during all engineering activities.

Requirements

  • Engineering qualification such as HNC, HND, apprenticeship, or equivalent experience.
  • Previous experience as an Engineering Technician or similar technical role is desirable.
  • Practical knowledge of engineering systems and manufacturing processes.
  • Ability to read technical drawings and engineering documents.
  • Strong fault-finding and problem-solving skills.
  • Good attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to work effectively within an engineering team.
  • Understanding of workplace health and safety requirements.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £28,000–£38,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive technical salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Engineering training and development opportunities.
  • Access to modern manufacturing equipment.
  • Supportive technical team environment.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
