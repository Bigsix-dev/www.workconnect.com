Site Manager

Job Description

Romax Site Services is looking for an experienced Site Manager to oversee construction activities in Chelmsford. The role involves coordinating trades, managing site operations and ensuring projects are delivered safely, efficiently and to the required standards.

Responsibilities

  • Manage daily construction site activities and operations.
  • Coordinate subcontractors, trades and site personnel.
  • Monitor project progress and ensure work meets required standards.
  • Maintain site health and safety procedures.
  • Coordinate deliveries, materials and site resources.
  • Report progress, issues and project requirements to management.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Site Manager in the construction industry.
  • Strong knowledge of construction processes and site management.
  • Excellent organisational and communication skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple trades and competing priorities.
  • Strong commitment to health, safety and quality standards.

Working Hours

  • Full-time contract position.
  • Working hours based on site requirements.

Salary

  • £280–£320 per day.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to manage construction projects in Chelmsford.
  • Competitive daily rate.
  • Professional construction environment.
  • Potential for further contract opportunities.
