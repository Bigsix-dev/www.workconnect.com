Electrician – Commercial Fit-Out

Job Description

Camelot Build Ltd is seeking an Electrician to work on commercial fit-out projects in the Chelmsford CM area. The role involves electrical installation, testing and associated works within commercial construction environments.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out electrical installation work on commercial fit-out projects.
  • Install lighting, power and associated electrical systems.
  • Read and work from electrical drawings and specifications.
  • Carry out testing and fault finding where required.
  • Maintain safe working practices throughout each project.
  • Coordinate effectively with other trades on site.

Requirements

  • Qualified and experienced electrician.
  • Previous commercial fit-out experience is beneficial.
  • Good understanding of electrical installation standards and practices.
  • Ability to work from drawings and technical specifications.
  • Strong commitment to site safety and quality workmanship.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Thursday: 7am–6pm.
  • Friday: 7am–2pm.

Salary

  • Salary not stated.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to work on commercial fit-out projects.
  • Structured weekday working schedule.
  • Professional construction environment.
  • Opportunity to work alongside experienced tradespeople.
