Multi Trader – Social Housing

Job Description

Emberstone Recruitment is seeking an experienced Multi Trader to carry out maintenance and repair works across social housing properties. The successful candidate will complete a range of multi-trade repairs, ensure work is completed to a high standard, and provide professional service to residents while maintaining safe working practices.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out general repairs and maintenance across social housing properties.
  • Complete multi-trade works including carpentry, plumbing, plastering, tiling, and decorating.
  • Respond to planned and reactive maintenance requirements.
  • Inspect properties and identify repair and maintenance issues.
  • Complete repairs efficiently and to agreed quality standards.
  • Communicate professionally with residents and provide updates where required.
  • Use tools, equipment, and materials safely and appropriately.
  • Record completed works and report any additional requirements.
  • Maintain a clean and safe working environment.
  • Follow health and safety procedures and relevant housing maintenance standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Multi Trader or in social housing maintenance.
  • Strong practical skills across multiple building trades.
  • Good knowledge of property repairs and maintenance.
  • Ability to diagnose and resolve a variety of maintenance issues.
  • Good communication and customer service skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and quality workmanship.
  • Ability to work independently and manage daily workloads.
  • Relevant trade qualifications or experience are desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £38,000–£42,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Stable social housing maintenance environment.
  • Opportunity to work across varied properties.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
