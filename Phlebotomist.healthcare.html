Phlebotomist

Job Description

A Phlebotomist is responsible for collecting blood samples from patients for laboratory testing, transfusions, research, or blood donations. The role requires excellent communication skills, attention to detail, and the ability to reassure patients who may be anxious about blood draws.

Typical Responsibilities

  • Drawing blood safely and accurately from patients.
  • Correctly labeling and processing blood specimens.
  • Maintaining sterile equipment and following infection control procedures.
  • Recording patient information and updating medical records.
  • Transporting samples to the laboratory promptly.
  • Ensuring patient comfort and explaining procedures before collection.

Common Requirements

  • Good interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Ability to work calmly under pressure.
  • Knowledge of infection prevention and health and safety practices.
  • Previous healthcare or phlebotomy experience is often preferred, though some employers provide training.
  • Relevant certification or training in phlebotomy may be advantageous.
