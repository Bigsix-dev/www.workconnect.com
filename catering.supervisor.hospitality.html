Catering Supervisor

Job Description

Elior UK is one of the United Kingdom's leading contract catering and hospitality providers, delivering high-quality food services across workplaces, schools, healthcare facilities, and leisure venues. Elior UK is seeking an experienced and motivated Catering Supervisor to oversee the daily operation of its catering service. In this role, you will supervise catering staff, ensure excellent customer service, maintain high food quality standards, and support the Catering Manager in delivering efficient and professional food service operations.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise the daily operation of the catering department.
  • Lead, motivate, and support catering team members.
  • Ensure food and beverages are prepared and served to company standards.
  • Monitor food quality, presentation, and portion control.
  • Assist with stock ordering, inventory management, and stock rotation.
  • Handle customer enquiries and resolve service issues professionally.
  • Support staff training, scheduling, and daily operational planning.
  • Ensure compliance with food hygiene regulations, health and safety legislation, and company policies.

Requirements

  • Previous supervisory experience in catering, hospitality, or food service.
  • Excellent leadership and communication skills.
  • Strong customer service and organisational abilities.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced catering environment.
  • Knowledge of food hygiene and food safety regulations.
  • Good problem-solving and decision-making skills.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and public holidays where required.
  • Food Safety Level 2 or Level 3 qualification is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts depending on operational requirements.

Salary

  • £14.20–£16.20 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted meals during shifts where applicable.
  • Leadership training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Employee discounts through company benefit schemes.
  • Supportive working environment with excellent opportunities for career progression.
