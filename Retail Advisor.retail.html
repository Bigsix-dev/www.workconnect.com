Retail Advisor

EE (Everything Everywhere Limited) is one of the United Kingdom's leading mobile network and broadband providers, offering mobile phones, broadband, tablets, accessories, and digital services. EE is looking for enthusiastic and customer-focused Retail Advisors to join its store team. In this role, you will help customers choose the right products and services, provide expert advice on mobile technology, process sales, and deliver an outstanding customer experience. This is an excellent opportunity for individuals who enjoy technology, sales, and building strong customer relationships in a fast-paced retail environment.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide friendly, professional service.
  • Recommend suitable mobile phones, broadband packages, and accessories based on customer needs.
  • Process sales, upgrades, contract renewals, and payments accurately.
  • Explain product features, pricing plans, and promotions clearly.
  • Achieve individual and team sales targets.
  • Handle customer enquiries and resolve issues professionally.
  • Maintain attractive product displays and a clean, organised store.
  • Follow company policies, security procedures, and health and safety guidelines.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Confident in selling products and building customer relationships.
  • Interest in mobile technology and digital services.
  • Ability to work in a target-driven retail environment.
  • Good teamwork and problem-solving skills.
  • Basic computer and cash-handling skills.
  • Flexible to work evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.
  • Previous retail or sales experience is beneficial but not essential, as full training is provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts, including weekends and public holidays.

Salary

  • £25,000–£31,000 per year.
  • Performance-related commission and bonus opportunities may be available.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Performance-based bonus and commission scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts on EE products and BT Group services.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
