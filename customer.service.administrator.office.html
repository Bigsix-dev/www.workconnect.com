Customer Service Administrator

Job Description

Royal Mail Group Limited is one of the United Kingdom's most established postal and logistics organisations, delivering letters and parcels to millions of homes and businesses nationwide. Royal Mail is seeking a professional and customer-focused Customer Service Administrator to support customer enquiries and administrative operations. In this role, you will manage customer communications, maintain accurate records, process service requests, and help ensure an excellent customer experience while supporting daily business operations.

Responsibilities

  • Respond to customer enquiries via telephone, email, and online channels.
  • Process customer requests, complaints, and service updates accurately.
  • Maintain customer records and update internal databases.
  • Prepare administrative documents, reports, and correspondence.
  • Work closely with operational teams to resolve customer issues promptly.
  • Monitor service requests and ensure timely follow-up.
  • Support general office administration and document management.
  • Ensure compliance with company procedures, data protection regulations, and confidentiality requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in customer service, administration, or office support.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional when resolving customer concerns.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Accurate data entry and record management skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in a busy team environment.
  • Commitment to delivering outstanding customer service.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with occasional flexibility based on operational needs.

Salary

  • £25,000–£29,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts and reward programmes.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Health and wellbeing support services.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Royal Mail Group.
