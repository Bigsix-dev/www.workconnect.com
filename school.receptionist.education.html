School Receptionist

Job Description

Chelmsford Schools Partnership is a network of schools committed to providing high-quality education and creating supportive environments where pupils can achieve their full potential. Chelmsford Schools Partnership is seeking a friendly and professional School Receptionist to provide a welcoming first point of contact for students, parents, staff, and visitors. In this role, you will manage reception duties, support school administration, and help ensure the smooth daily operation of the school office.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome students, parents, visitors, and staff in a professional manner.
  • Manage incoming telephone calls, emails, and general enquiries.
  • Maintain accurate visitor records and follow safeguarding procedures.
  • Provide administrative support to the school office team.
  • Manage appointments, messages, and daily reception activities.
  • Assist with attendance records and student information updates.
  • Prepare letters, documents, and school communications.
  • Maintain confidentiality of student, parent, and staff information.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in reception, administration, customer service, or school office work.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Friendly, professional, and approachable attitude.
  • Strong organisational skills with attention to detail.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks in a busy environment.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office applications.
  • Understanding of safeguarding and confidentiality requirements.
  • Ability to work effectively with students, parents, and staff.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school hours.

Salary

  • £22,000–£26,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Local Government Pension Scheme.
  • Paid annual leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Supportive school working environment.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for career progression in education administration.
  • Rewarding role supporting the school community.
