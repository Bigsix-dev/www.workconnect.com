Grill Chef

Job Description

Harvester, part of Mitchells & Butlers PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's leading family restaurant brands, renowned for its expertly grilled dishes, fresh ingredients, and welcoming dining experience. Harvester is seeking skilled and passionate Grill Chefs to join its kitchen team. As a Grill Chef, you will be responsible for preparing and cooking high-quality meals, ensuring food is presented to company standards, and maintaining a safe, clean, and efficient kitchen environment while delivering an outstanding dining experience for every guest.

Responsibilities

  • Prepare, season, and cook grilled meals according to company recipes and standards.
  • Ensure all food is cooked safely and served at the correct temperature.
  • Monitor food quality, portion sizes, and presentation.
  • Maintain cleanliness of grills, cooking equipment, and kitchen workstations.
  • Assist with stock rotation, storage, and inventory management.
  • Work closely with kitchen and front-of-house teams during busy service periods.
  • Minimise food waste while maintaining high-quality standards.
  • Comply with food hygiene regulations, health and safety procedures, and company policies.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Grill Chef, Line Cook, or similar kitchen role is preferred.
  • Knowledge of food safety and hygiene regulations.
  • Excellent time management and organisational skills.
  • Ability to work efficiently under pressure.
  • Strong attention to detail and food presentation.
  • Excellent teamwork and communication skills.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Food Safety Level 2 certification is desirable but not essential.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 38–45 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £13.50–£15.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted meals during shifts.
  • Employee discounts across Mitchells & Butlers restaurants.
  • Comprehensive chef training and career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive workplace with opportunities for advancement.
