Civil Enforcement Officer

Job Description

Local Authority is responsible for delivering essential public services and maintaining safe, accessible, and well-managed local communities. The authority is seeking a professional Civil Enforcement Officer to support the enforcement of parking and traffic regulations. You will patrol designated areas, monitor compliance, issue notices where appropriate, and provide helpful information to members of the public while maintaining a professional approach.

Responsibilities

  • Patrol designated streets, car parks, and public areas in accordance with local regulations.
  • Monitor vehicles for compliance with parking restrictions and enforcement requirements.
  • Issue penalty notices where appropriate and in accordance with relevant regulations.
  • Record observations and maintain accurate enforcement documentation.
  • Respond professionally to questions and enquiries from members of the public.
  • Report damaged signs, road markings, parking equipment, and other local issues.
  • Use handheld enforcement equipment and systems accurately.
  • Work with colleagues and local authority teams to support effective traffic management.

Requirements

  • No specific degree is required; relevant enforcement, customer service, or public-facing experience is desirable.
  • Good understanding of the importance of following regulations and procedures.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to deal calmly and professionally with members of the public.
  • Strong observation and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work independently while following established procedures.
  • Good organisational and record-keeping skills.
  • Reliable, punctual, and comfortable working outdoors in different weather conditions.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Working hours may include weekends depending on local enforcement requirements.

Salary

  • £26,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive local authority salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Enforcement and public service training.
  • Uniform and required work equipment.
  • Stable local government working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
