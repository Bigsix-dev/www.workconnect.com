Supply Chain Coordinator

Job Description

Britvic PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading soft drinks manufacturers, producing and distributing well-known beverage brands across the UK and international markets. Britvic is seeking an organised and proactive Supply Chain Coordinator to support its end-to-end supply chain operations. In this role, you will coordinate inventory, production, warehousing, and transportation activities to ensure products are delivered efficiently while maintaining excellent customer service and operational performance.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate daily supply chain activities across production, warehousing, and distribution.
  • Monitor inventory levels and support stock planning and replenishment.
  • Work closely with suppliers, transport providers, warehouses, and internal departments.
  • Track orders and deliveries to ensure customer requirements are met.
  • Maintain accurate supply chain records and prepare operational reports.
  • Identify and resolve supply chain issues to minimise operational disruptions.
  • Support continuous improvement initiatives to enhance supply chain efficiency.
  • Ensure compliance with company policies, food safety standards, and health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in supply chain, logistics, or operations coordination.
  • Strong organisational and time management skills.
  • Excellent communication and problem-solving abilities.
  • Good analytical skills and attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
  • Experience using ERP or supply chain management systems is desirable.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment.
  • Commitment to delivering high standards of operational performance.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with flexibility based on business requirements.

Salary

  • £31,000–£37,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts on selected Britvic products.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Health and wellbeing support programmes.
  • Performance-related bonus opportunities where applicable.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Britvic.
