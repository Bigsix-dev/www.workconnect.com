Café Supervisor

Job Description

Costa Limited, trading as Costa Coffee, is one of the United Kingdom's leading coffeehouse chains, recognised for its handcrafted coffee, quality food, and outstanding customer service. Costa Coffee is seeking an experienced and motivated Café Supervisor to oversee the daily operation of the café, support the Store Manager, and lead the team in delivering an exceptional customer experience. You will ensure smooth daily operations while maintaining Costa's high standards of service, cleanliness, and food safety.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise the daily operation of the café and support the Store Manager.
  • Lead, motivate, and coach team members to deliver excellent customer service.
  • Prepare and serve coffee, beverages, and food to Costa Coffee standards.
  • Monitor stock levels and assist with ordering and inventory management.
  • Handle customer enquiries and resolve complaints professionally.
  • Assist with staff training, scheduling, opening, and closing procedures.
  • Ensure the café remains clean, organised, and fully stocked.
  • Maintain compliance with food hygiene regulations, health and safety policies, and company procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in a café, retail, or hospitality supervisory role.
  • Excellent leadership and communication skills.
  • Strong customer service and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment.
  • Good organisational and time management skills.
  • Knowledge of food safety and hygiene standards.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and public holidays.
  • A positive attitude with a passion for delivering exceptional customer experiences.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £13.80–£15.80 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted drinks and food during shifts.
  • Employee discounts at Costa Coffee stores.
  • Leadership training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment with opportunities for career progression.
