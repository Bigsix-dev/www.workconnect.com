Recruitment Coordinator

Job Description

Manpower UK Limited is a leading recruitment and workforce solutions provider, helping organisations find skilled professionals and supporting candidates throughout their employment journey. Manpower UK is seeking an organised and proactive Recruitment Coordinator to support recruitment operations and ensure an efficient hiring process. In this role, you will coordinate interviews, manage candidate information, support recruitment campaigns, and provide administrative assistance to recruitment consultants and clients.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate recruitment activities from candidate application through onboarding.
  • Schedule interviews and communicate with candidates and hiring managers.
  • Maintain accurate candidate records and recruitment databases.
  • Prepare employment documents, contracts, and onboarding paperwork.
  • Support recruitment consultants with administrative tasks.
  • Track candidate progress and provide regular recruitment updates.
  • Respond to candidate and client enquiries professionally.
  • Ensure recruitment processes comply with company policies and employment regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in recruitment administration, HR support, or coordination roles is desirable.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal abilities.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Ability to manage multiple vacancies and priorities effectively.
  • Strong customer service skills with a professional approach.
  • Ability to maintain confidentiality of candidate information.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard business hours.

Salary

  • £27,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional recruitment training and development programmes.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance support.
  • Performance rewards and career development opportunities.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Excellent progression opportunities within Manpower UK.
