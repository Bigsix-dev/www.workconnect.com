Logistics Assistant

Job Description

Kuehne + Nagel Limited is one of the world's leading logistics and supply chain companies, providing global freight forwarding, warehousing, and distribution services across multiple industries. Kuehne + Nagel is seeking a dependable and organised Logistics Assistant to support its warehouse and transport operations. In this role, you will assist with daily logistics activities, coordinate stock movements, process orders, and help ensure goods are handled efficiently and delivered according to customer requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Support daily warehouse and logistics operations.
  • Assist with receiving, checking, and processing incoming goods.
  • Prepare orders and documentation for dispatch and delivery.
  • Update stock records and maintain accurate inventory information.
  • Coordinate with warehouse and transport teams to support smooth operations.
  • Operate warehouse systems, scanners, and computer-based logistics tools.
  • Assist with loading, unloading, and movement of goods when required.
  • Maintain a safe, clean, and organised working environment.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in logistics, warehousing, or supply chain operations is desirable.
  • Good organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work accurately in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Basic computer skills and ability to use warehouse systems.
  • Ability to handle physical warehouse tasks when required.
  • Reliable, punctual, and committed to maintaining high standards.
  • Flexible availability to support operational requirements.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, and occasional weekends.

Salary

  • £12.80–£14.80 per hour.

Benefits

  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts and reward programmes.
  • Health and wellbeing support services.
  • Supportive and professional working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Kuehne + Nagel.
