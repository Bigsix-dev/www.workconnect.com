PE Teacher

Job Description

Chelmsford Schools are committed to promoting healthy lifestyles, physical wellbeing, and academic excellence through a balanced and engaging curriculum. Schools across Chelmsford are seeking an enthusiastic and energetic PE Teacher to inspire students to participate in physical education and sports. In this role, you will deliver high-quality practical and theoretical lessons, encourage teamwork and sportsmanship, and help students develop lifelong fitness habits.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and deliver engaging Physical Education lessons in line with the national curriculum.
  • Teach a variety of sports, fitness activities, and health education topics.
  • Promote teamwork, discipline, resilience, and healthy lifestyles among students.
  • Organise sports fixtures, competitions, tournaments, and extracurricular clubs.
  • Assess student performance and provide constructive feedback.
  • Ensure the safe use of sports facilities and equipment.
  • Maintain accurate records of student progress and achievement.
  • Participate in school events, parents' evenings, and professional development activities.

Requirements

  • Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) is required.
  • Bachelor's degree in Physical Education, Sports Science, or a related subject.
  • Previous experience teaching Physical Education at secondary school level is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of physical education, fitness, and sports coaching.
  • Excellent classroom and sports activity management skills.
  • Ability to motivate and inspire students of all sporting abilities.
  • Commitment to safeguarding and promoting student wellbeing.
  • Strong organisational, planning, and assessment skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 32.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £34,000–£50,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive teacher salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme.
  • Paid school holidays and generous annual leave.
  • Professional development and continuous training opportunities.
  • Access to modern sports facilities and equipment.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Career progression opportunities within education.
  • Supportive and collaborative teaching environment.
