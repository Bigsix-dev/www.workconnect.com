Front of House Team Member

Job Description

Nando's UK Limited is one of the United Kingdom's most popular casual dining restaurant chains, famous for its flame-grilled PERi-PERi chicken and exceptional customer service. Nando's is looking for enthusiastic, friendly, and customer-focused Front of House Team Members to create memorable dining experiences for every guest. In this role, you will welcome customers, take orders, serve food and drinks, maintain restaurant cleanliness, and work closely with both the front-of-house and kitchen teams to deliver outstanding hospitality.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome guests warmly and provide exceptional customer service.
  • Take food and beverage orders accurately using the point-of-sale (POS) system.
  • Serve meals and drinks promptly while maintaining high service standards.
  • Prepare dining areas before, during, and after service.
  • Clear and reset tables efficiently.
  • Respond professionally to customer enquiries and complaints.
  • Maintain cleanliness throughout the restaurant.
  • Follow food hygiene, health and safety, and company procedures at all times.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly, energetic, and positive attitude.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced restaurant environment.
  • Strong teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Professional appearance and good attention to detail.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Previous hospitality experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 30–38 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £12.40–£13.80 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted meals during shifts.
  • Employee discount programme.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Supportive, inclusive, and friendly working environment.
