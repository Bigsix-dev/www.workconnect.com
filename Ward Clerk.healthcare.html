Ward Clerk

Job Overview

A Ward Clerk plays a vital administrative role in supporting the day-to-day operations of a hospital ward. They act as a central point of communication between patients, nurses, doctors, and other healthcare professionals while ensuring that records and documentation are kept accurate and up to date.

Key Responsibilities

  • Greet patients, visitors, and healthcare staff professionally.
  • Manage patient admissions, discharges, and transfers.
  • Maintain accurate medical records and ward documentation.
  • Answer telephone calls and direct enquiries appropriately.
  • Schedule appointments and coordinate with clinical teams.
  • Prepare and process paperwork, forms, and correspondence.
  • Update hospital computer systems with patient information.
  • Assist nurses and doctors with administrative tasks.
  • Ensure confidential information is handled securely.
  • Monitor office supplies and maintain an organised ward environment.

Requirements

  • Good GCSEs (or equivalent), including English and Maths, are often preferred.
  • Previous administrative, receptionist, or healthcare experience is beneficial.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Excellent organisational and time-management abilities.
  • Basic computer literacy and familiarity with Microsoft Office.
  • Ability to work calmly in a busy hospital environment.
  • High attention to detail and respect for patient confidentiality.
  • A friendly, professional, and team-oriented attitude.

Typical Working Hours

  • Full-time position, generally 37.5 hours per week.
  • Shifts may include mornings, evenings, weekends, or bank holidays depending on ward requirements.
