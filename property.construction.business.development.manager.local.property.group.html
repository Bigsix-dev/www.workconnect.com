Property & Construction Business Development Manager

Job Description

A Local Property Group is seeking an ambitious Business Development Manager to identify new commercial opportunities across the property and construction sectors. The successful candidate will develop client relationships, generate new business, promote property and construction services, and contribute to revenue growth. This role is suited to a commercially minded professional with strong networking, negotiation, and sales skills.

Responsibilities

  • Identify and develop new business opportunities within property and construction.
  • Build and maintain relationships with property owners, developers, contractors, and professional partners.
  • Generate leads through networking, research, referrals, and business development activities.
  • Arrange meetings and presentations with prospective clients.
  • Understand client requirements and recommend appropriate services.
  • Prepare proposals, quotations, and commercial presentations.
  • Negotiate opportunities and support the conversion of leads into contracts.
  • Maintain accurate records of prospects, opportunities, and business activity.

Requirements

  • Experience in business development, sales, property, construction, or a related commercial environment.
  • Strong networking and relationship-building skills.
  • Confident negotiation and presentation abilities.
  • Commercial awareness and understanding of sales processes.
  • Strong written and verbal communication.
  • Self-motivated and capable of working independently.
  • Ability to manage a pipeline of opportunities.
  • Professional and target-driven approach.

Working Hours

  • Full-time or part-time position.
  • Hybrid working arrangement may be available.
  • Flexible scheduling depending on business requirements.

Salary

  • £30,000–£45,000 OTE.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and earning potential.
  • Hybrid and flexible working opportunities.
  • Performance-based earning opportunities.
  • Professional networking opportunities.
  • Career development within the property and construction sector.
  • Opportunity to contribute directly to business growth.
