Information Technology Field Technician

Job Description

Brabner Fox is seeking an Information Technology Field Technician to provide hands-on technical support and IT services in Chelmsford. The successful candidate will assist with hardware, software, networking and end-user technology issues while maintaining a high standard of customer service.

Responsibilities

  • Provide on-site technical support to users and business teams.
  • Install, configure and maintain computers and IT equipment.
  • Troubleshoot hardware, software and connectivity issues.
  • Set up user accounts, devices and workplace technology.
  • Respond to support requests and resolve technical incidents efficiently.
  • Maintain accurate records of equipment, incidents and completed work.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in IT support, field engineering or a similar technical role.
  • Good knowledge of computer hardware, software and networking.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently and manage technical support requests effectively.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Standard Monday to Friday working hours.

Salary

  • £30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to develop practical IT engineering experience.
  • Professional development opportunities.
  • Varied technical support responsibilities.
  • Supportive working environment.
