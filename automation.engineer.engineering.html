Automation Engineer

Job Description

Manufacturing Technology Company develops advanced automation solutions that improve efficiency, productivity, and reliability within modern manufacturing environments. The company is seeking an experienced and innovative Automation Engineer to design, program, and maintain automated systems. In this role, you will support automation projects, optimise production processes, and develop technical solutions that enhance manufacturing performance.

Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and maintain automated control systems.
  • Program and configure programmable logic controllers (PLCs) and automation equipment.
  • Support the installation, testing, and commissioning of automated machinery.
  • Troubleshoot automation faults and implement effective solutions.
  • Improve production efficiency through automation and process optimisation.
  • Collaborate with engineering, manufacturing, and maintenance teams.
  • Create technical documentation, reports, and system specifications.
  • Ensure automation systems comply with safety and industry standards.

Requirements

  • Degree or qualification in Automation Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience working as an Automation Engineer or Controls Engineer.
  • Strong knowledge of PLC programming and industrial automation systems.
  • Experience with robotics, control systems, and manufacturing technology.
  • Excellent fault-finding and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong understanding of electrical systems and industrial processes.
  • Ability to work effectively within multidisciplinary engineering teams.
  • Good communication and technical documentation skills.

Working Hours

  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £45,000–£65,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Career progression opportunities in automation engineering.
