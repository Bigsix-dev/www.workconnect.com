Retail Team Member

WHSmith PLC (William Henry Smith) is a leading UK retail company operating in travel, high street, and workplace locations, offering books, stationery, newspapers, magazines, technology, and convenience items. WHSmith is seeking motivated and customer-focused Retail Team Members to join its store team. In this role, you will provide excellent customer service, assist with sales, maintain stock levels, and ensure the store remains clean, organised, and welcoming. This position is ideal for individuals who enjoy working in a busy retail environment and interacting with customers daily.

Responsibilities

  • Provide friendly and efficient customer service at all times.
  • Operate tills and process transactions accurately.
  • Assist customers with product enquiries and recommendations.
  • Replenish stock and maintain well-presented shelves.
  • Unpack deliveries and organise stock in the store and stockroom.
  • Maintain cleanliness and organisation of the sales floor.
  • Support promotional displays and merchandising tasks.
  • Follow company policies, procedures, and health and safety guidelines.

Requirements

  • Strong communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly, reliable, and team-oriented attitude.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment.
  • Good attention to detail and organisational ability.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and public holidays.
  • Previous retail experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns depending on store location and demand.

Salary

  • £24,000–£28,000 per year.
  • Overtime opportunities may be available.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discount on WHSmith products.
  • Training and career development opportunities.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Opportunities for progression within retail.
