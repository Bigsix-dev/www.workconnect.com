Office Assistant

Job Description

G4S Limited is a global security and facilities management company providing essential services to businesses, government organisations, and communities worldwide. G4S is seeking a reliable and organised Office Assistant to provide administrative support and ensure the smooth running of daily office operations. In this role, you will manage documents, support team members, handle communications, and assist with general administrative duties while maintaining high standards of professionalism and confidentiality.

Responsibilities

  • Provide general administrative support to office teams and managers.
  • Manage incoming emails, telephone calls, and office enquiries.
  • Prepare documents, reports, spreadsheets, and correspondence.
  • Maintain accurate filing systems and office records.
  • Assist with scheduling meetings, appointments, and team activities.
  • Process administrative paperwork and update internal systems.
  • Support daily office operations and coordinate with different departments.
  • Maintain confidentiality and comply with company policies and procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in office administration, reception, or business support is desirable.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks effectively.
  • Strong customer service and teamwork skills.
  • Professional and positive approach to administrative responsibilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard office hours.

Salary

  • £24,000–£28,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts and reward schemes.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Career progression opportunities within G4S.
