Multi-Trade Operative

Job Description

Invision Group is seeking a skilled Multi-Trade Operative to carry out a range of maintenance, repair, and construction duties across property and building projects. The successful candidate will be able to work across multiple trades, complete tasks to a high standard, and maintain safe and professional working practices.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out a range of general maintenance and construction tasks.
  • Complete repairs and refurbishment work across properties and sites.
  • Undertake basic carpentry, plumbing, decorating, plastering, and other relevant trade duties.
  • Inspect work areas and identify maintenance or repair requirements.
  • Complete work to agreed specifications and quality standards.
  • Use hand tools, power tools, and equipment safely and appropriately.
  • Work alongside other trades and site teams to complete projects efficiently.
  • Maintain a clean, organised, and safe working environment.
  • Report defects, additional requirements, and health and safety concerns.
  • Follow site procedures, risk assessments, and relevant health and safety requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Multi-Trade Operative or in a similar construction role.
  • Practical skills across multiple building or maintenance trades.
  • Good understanding of construction and property maintenance processes.
  • Ability to use tools and equipment safely.
  • Strong attention to detail and quality workmanship.
  • Good communication and teamwork skills.
  • Reliable and able to work independently when required.
  • Relevant trade qualifications or experience are desirable.

Working Hours

  • Contract / full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £160–£180 per day.

Benefits

  • Competitive daily rate.
  • Opportunity to work on varied property and construction projects.
  • Professional site environment.
  • Opportunity to develop and use multiple trade skills.
  • Potential for ongoing work and career development.
