Manufacturing Engineer

Job Description

Advanced Manufacturing Company specialises in modern manufacturing technologies and engineering solutions designed to improve production efficiency, quality, and performance. The company is seeking an experienced Manufacturing Engineer to develop, optimise, and improve manufacturing processes. In this role, you will work closely with production, design, and quality teams to introduce efficient processes, resolve technical problems, and support continuous improvement across manufacturing operations.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and improve manufacturing processes to increase efficiency and productivity.
  • Analyse production processes and identify opportunities for improvement.
  • Support the introduction of new products and manufacturing methods.
  • Develop manufacturing instructions, process documentation, and technical specifications.
  • Investigate production problems and implement effective engineering solutions.
  • Work with production, design, quality, and maintenance teams.
  • Monitor manufacturing performance and analyse production data.
  • Ensure manufacturing processes comply with quality, safety, and industry standards.

Requirements

  • Degree in Manufacturing Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Production Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience in manufacturing or production engineering.
  • Strong understanding of manufacturing processes and engineering principles.
  • Experience with continuous improvement and process optimisation techniques.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Good understanding of quality control and production standards.
  • Strong communication and teamwork abilities.
  • Ability to manage multiple engineering projects and deadlines.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £40,000–£55,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Professional engineering training and development.
  • Access to advanced manufacturing technologies.
  • Opportunities to work on innovative manufacturing projects.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
