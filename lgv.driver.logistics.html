LGV Driver

Job Description

Booker Wholesale Limited, part of Tesco PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's leading food and wholesale suppliers, serving thousands of businesses with quality products and reliable delivery services. Booker Wholesale is seeking a professional and safety-focused LGV Driver to join its transport team. In this role, you will deliver wholesale goods to customers efficiently, maintain excellent service standards, and ensure all deliveries are completed safely and on schedule.

Responsibilities

  • Operate LGV vehicles safely and responsibly in accordance with UK transport regulations.
  • Complete scheduled deliveries to customers and business locations.
  • Load, unload, and check goods to ensure accurate deliveries.
  • Carry out daily vehicle safety checks and report any defects promptly.
  • Complete delivery paperwork and electronic delivery records accurately.
  • Provide professional and friendly customer service during deliveries.
  • Ensure goods are handled carefully and stored correctly during transportation.
  • Follow company procedures, food safety requirements, and health and safety policies.

Requirements

  • Valid UK LGV/HGV driving licence.
  • Valid Driver Certificate of Professional Competence (Driver CPC).
  • Digital Tachograph Driver Card.
  • Good knowledge of UK road transport regulations.
  • Strong commitment to safe driving practices.
  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Ability to handle physical duties including loading and unloading goods.
  • Previous LGV or wholesale delivery experience is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 45 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including early mornings, weekends, and bank holidays when required.

Salary

  • £16.00–£18.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts across Booker Wholesale and Tesco brands.
  • Driver training and professional development opportunities.
  • Health and wellbeing support programmes.
  • Modern fleet with maintained vehicles.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within the logistics industry.
