Public Protection Officer

Job Description

Local Authority provides a range of regulatory and public protection services designed to safeguard residents, businesses, and the wider community. The authority is seeking a professional Public Protection Officer to support the enforcement of relevant legislation, investigate concerns, and help ensure that local businesses and individuals comply with applicable requirements. You will work with colleagues, residents, businesses, and partner agencies to identify risks and support a safe and well-regulated community.

Responsibilities

  • Support the delivery of local authority public protection and regulatory services.
  • Investigate complaints, reports, and potential breaches of relevant regulations.
  • Carry out inspections, visits, and compliance checks as required.
  • Maintain accurate case records, reports, and enforcement documentation.
  • Provide information and guidance to residents, businesses, and other stakeholders.
  • Identify potential risks and escalate serious concerns through appropriate procedures.
  • Work with internal departments and external agencies on public protection matters.
  • Support enforcement action in accordance with relevant legislation and local authority procedures.

Requirements

  • Relevant qualification or experience in public protection, enforcement, regulatory services, environmental health, or a related field is desirable.
  • Good understanding of regulatory procedures and professional standards.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong investigation, analytical, and problem-solving abilities.
  • Good report writing and case management skills.
  • Ability to deal confidently and professionally with members of the public and businesses.
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to maintain accurate records.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively with partner agencies.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule, with occasional flexibility for inspections or operational requirements.

Salary

  • £30,000–£36,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive local authority salary.
  • Local government pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Regulatory and public protection training.
  • Opportunities for professional qualifications and development.
  • Stable public-sector working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
