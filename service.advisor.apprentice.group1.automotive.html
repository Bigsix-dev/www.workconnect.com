Service Advisor Apprentice

Job Description

Group 1 Automotive is seeking a motivated Service Advisor Apprentice to develop professional customer service and automotive service skills within a dealership environment. The apprentice will learn how to manage customer vehicle requirements, communicate with technicians, support workshop operations, and provide an excellent customer experience while completing their training.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and assist with vehicle servicing and repair enquiries.
  • Learn how to record customer concerns and vehicle requirements accurately.
  • Support experienced Service Advisors with bookings and job cards.
  • Learn how to explain servicing and repair requirements to customers.
  • Assist with preparing estimates and communicating costs.
  • Monitor vehicle progress and liaise with workshop technicians.
  • Keep customers informed about vehicle progress and completion times.
  • Assist with obtaining approval for additional work.
  • Maintain accurate service documentation and customer records.
  • Complete required apprenticeship training and development activities.

Requirements

  • Strong interest in the automotive industry.
  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Willingness to learn automotive servicing and repair processes.
  • Good organisational and time management abilities.
  • Positive and professional attitude.
  • Good attention to detail.
  • Ability to communicate effectively with customers and colleagues.
  • Ability to work as part of a busy dealership team.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday, 8:30am–5:30pm.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Full-time apprenticeship position.

Salary

  • £16,640 during the first year.

Benefits

  • Structured automotive apprenticeship training.
  • Practical experience within a professional dealership.
  • Opportunity to develop customer service and automotive knowledge.
  • Support and guidance from experienced colleagues.
  • Potential career progression within the automotive industry.
