Operations Support Administrator

Job Description

Tesco Business Services provides operational and administrative support services that help Tesco teams across the United Kingdom deliver efficient and reliable business operations. Tesco Business Services is seeking an organised and detail-focused Operations Support Administrator to assist with daily operational activities. In this role, you will maintain records, coordinate administrative processes, support operational teams, and ensure business activities run smoothly while meeting company standards.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to operational managers and business teams.
  • Maintain accurate operational records, reports, and databases.
  • Process documentation, spreadsheets, and operational paperwork.
  • Support team communications, meetings, and administrative activities.
  • Monitor operational tasks and assist with workflow coordination.
  • Prepare reports and performance updates for management teams.
  • Respond to internal enquiries and provide administrative assistance when required.
  • Ensure compliance with company procedures, data protection policies, and operational standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in operations support, administration, or business services.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to maintain accurate records.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel, Word, and Outlook.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Experience using business systems or databases is desirable.
  • Professional attitude with strong problem-solving abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard business hours.

Salary

  • £26,000–£31,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Tesco colleague discount scheme.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
  • Flexible working opportunities where applicable.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Tesco Business Services.
