Multi-Trade Operative

Job Description

Fortus Recruitment Group is seeking a skilled Multi-Trade Operative to carry out maintenance, repair and refurbishment work across properties in Chelmsford and Essex. The successful candidate should be comfortable working across several construction trades and delivering high-quality workmanship.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out general maintenance and refurbishment works.
  • Complete repairs across multiple construction trades.
  • Perform carpentry, plastering, decorating and other associated tasks.
  • Diagnose problems and complete repairs efficiently.
  • Maintain high standards of workmanship and site safety.
  • Work professionally in occupied and unoccupied properties.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Multi-Trade Operative or similar role.
  • Good practical skills across several construction trades.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to quality.
  • Ability to work independently and manage assigned tasks.
  • Good communication and customer service skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time contract position.
  • Working hours based on project and site requirements.

Salary

  • £160–£180 per day.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to work on varied property maintenance projects.
  • Regular contract work across Essex.
  • Opportunity to develop and use a broad range of trade skills.
  • Professional working environment.
