Retail Assistant

B&M Retail Limited is a UK variety retailer offering household, grocery, and general merchandise at discounted prices. B&M is seeking Retail Assistants to support store operations and deliver great customer service.

Responsibilities

  • Serve customers at the till.
  • Restock shelves and aisles.
  • Assist customers with enquiries.
  • Maintain store cleanliness.
  • Support deliveries and stock handling.
  • Ensure products are correctly priced.
  • Follow health and safety procedures.
  • Support store promotions.

Requirements

  • Good communication skills.
  • Friendly attitude.
  • Ability to work in a busy environment.
  • Basic numeracy skills.
  • Team player.
  • Flexible availability.
  • No experience required.
  • Training provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.

Salary

  • £12.30–£13.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Staff discounts.
  • Pension scheme.
  • Paid holidays.
  • Training and support.
  • Career progression opportunities.
