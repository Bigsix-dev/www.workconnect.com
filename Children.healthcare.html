Children's Support Worker

Full Job Description

As a Children's Support Worker, you will provide compassionate care and practical assistance to children and young people who require additional support due to disabilities, complex needs, or challenging circumstances. The role focuses on promoting safety, independence, emotional well-being, and personal development while working closely with families and healthcare professionals.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support children with daily living activities, including personal care and hygiene where required.
  • Build positive, trusting relationships with children and their families.
  • Assist with educational, recreational, and social activities.
  • Follow individual care plans and encourage independence.
  • Monitor children's physical and emotional well-being and report concerns promptly.
  • Maintain accurate records of care and observations.
  • Work alongside healthcare professionals, teachers, and social workers to provide consistent support.
  • Ensure safeguarding policies and health and safety procedures are always followed.
  • Administer medication or assist with healthcare tasks when appropriately trained.
  • Provide emotional support and encourage positive behaviour.

About You

The ideal candidate should:

  • Have a caring, patient, and empathetic nature.
  • Be able to communicate effectively with children, families, and colleagues.
  • Be reliable and able to work independently or as part of a team.
  • Have previous experience in care, support work, childcare, or a related field (desirable but not always essential).
  • Be willing to complete relevant training and undergo background checks.
  • Hold the right to work in the UK and possess a flexible approach to shifts.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay (£13–£15 per hour).
  • Full-time, stable employment with regular hours.
  • Paid induction and ongoing professional training.
  • Opportunities for career progression within the care sector.
  • Supportive management and team environment.
  • Holiday entitlement and workplace pension scheme.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programmes.
  • Flexible scheduling opportunities depending on service needs.
  • Reward and recognition initiatives for outstanding performance.
