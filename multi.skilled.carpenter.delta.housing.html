Multi-Skilled Carpenter

Job Description

Delta Housing is seeking a Multi-Skilled Carpenter to carry out carpentry, maintenance and repair work in Chelmsford. The role is suited to an experienced tradesperson who can complete a broad range of property maintenance tasks to a high standard.

  • Carry out carpentry repairs and installations.
  • Complete general property maintenance and refurbishment work.
  • Repair doors, frames, flooring, fixtures and other property components.
  • Diagnose defects and complete appropriate remedial work.
  • Work safely within residential and property environments.
  • Maintain high standards of workmanship and customer service.

Requirements

  • Proven experience as a carpenter or multi-skilled tradesperson.
  • Strong carpentry and general maintenance skills.
  • Good fault-finding and practical problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to work independently and complete jobs efficiently.
  • Strong focus on quality, safety and professionalism.
