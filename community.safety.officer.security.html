Community Safety Officer

Job Description

Chelmsford City Council works with residents, partner organisations, and local services to promote safe, welcoming, and well-managed communities. The council is seeking a Community Safety Officer to support local initiatives designed to reduce antisocial behaviour, improve community safety, and respond to concerns raised by residents and businesses. You will work with council teams and external partners to identify issues, coordinate appropriate responses, and promote positive community engagement.

Responsibilities

  • Support initiatives designed to improve community safety and reduce antisocial behaviour.
  • Monitor and respond to community safety concerns raised by residents and businesses.
  • Work with local partners, community groups, police, and other public services.
  • Collect and analyse information relating to community safety issues.
  • Assist with community safety projects, campaigns, and awareness activities.
  • Maintain accurate records, reports, and case information.
  • Provide information and guidance to residents and local organisations.
  • Identify emerging community concerns and escalate serious issues through appropriate channels.

Requirements

  • Relevant qualification or experience in community safety, public services, enforcement, social care, or a related field is desirable.
  • Strong communication and community engagement skills.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with residents and partner organisations.
  • Good understanding of confidentiality and professional information handling.
  • Strong organisational and case management skills.
  • Good analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to remain professional when dealing with challenging situations.
  • Commitment to supporting safe, inclusive, and positive communities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule, with occasional evening or weekend work where required.

Salary

  • £29,000–£35,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive local government salary.
  • Local government pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Community safety and professional development training.
  • Opportunities to work with local community organisations and public services.
  • Stable public-sector working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
