Customer Support Administrator

Job Description

British Gas Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading energy providers, delivering heating, energy, and home services to millions of customers. British Gas is seeking a professional and customer-focused Customer Support Administrator to support customer service operations and ensure efficient handling of customer requests. In this role, you will manage customer records, process enquiries, coordinate service information, and provide administrative support to help deliver excellent customer experiences.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to customer service teams.
  • Handle customer enquiries through telephone, email, and online channels.
  • Maintain accurate customer records and update internal systems.
  • Process customer requests, service information, and documentation.
  • Support scheduling and coordination of customer appointments.
  • Prepare reports, correspondence, and administrative documents.
  • Resolve customer issues efficiently or escalate where necessary.
  • Ensure compliance with company policies, data protection regulations, and customer service standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in customer service, administration, or contact centre support.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong organisational skills with attention to detail.
  • Ability to handle customer information professionally and confidentially.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and ability to manage customer concerns.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment.
  • Positive attitude with a commitment to delivering excellent customer service.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with flexibility depending on customer service requirements.

Salary

  • £26,000–£31,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee energy discounts.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
  • Flexible working options where available.
  • Career progression opportunities within British Gas.
