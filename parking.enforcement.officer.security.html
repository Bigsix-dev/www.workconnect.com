Parking Enforcement Officer

Job Description

Local Authority manages parking, traffic, and public-space services to support safe and accessible communities. The authority is seeking a professional Parking Enforcement Officer to monitor parking restrictions, promote compliance, and ensure local parking regulations are followed. You will patrol designated areas, issue enforcement notices where appropriate, provide information to the public, and report parking-related issues.

Responsibilities

  • Patrol designated streets, car parks, and public areas to monitor parking compliance.
  • Check vehicles against applicable parking restrictions and regulations.
  • Issue penalty notices where appropriate and in accordance with established procedures.
  • Record observations and enforcement activity accurately using approved systems.
  • Respond professionally to questions and concerns from members of the public.
  • Report damaged signs, road markings, parking machines, and other local issues.
  • Identify potential parking and traffic management problems and report them appropriately.
  • Work with council colleagues and other agencies to support effective parking management.

Requirements

  • Previous enforcement, security, customer service, or public-sector experience is desirable.
  • Ability to understand and follow parking regulations and enforcement procedures.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong observation and attention to detail.
  • Ability to deal calmly and professionally with members of the public.
  • Good organisational and record-keeping skills.
  • Ability to work independently while following established procedures.
  • Comfortable working outdoors in different weather conditions.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Working hours may include weekends depending on local parking requirements.

Salary

  • £25,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive local authority salary.
  • Local government pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Parking enforcement and public service training.
  • Uniform and required enforcement equipment.
  • Stable public-sector working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
