Service Advisor

Job Description

Auto Skills UK is seeking an experienced Service Advisor to provide high-quality customer service and coordinate vehicle servicing and repair operations. You will manage customer bookings, communicate repair requirements, coordinate with technicians, and ensure customers receive a professional and efficient service from vehicle check-in through to collection.

Responsibilities

  • Manage customer bookings for servicing, repairs, maintenance, and inspections.
  • Welcome customers and establish their vehicle service requirements.
  • Record customer concerns accurately and communicate them to workshop technicians.
  • Prepare and explain estimates, service recommendations, and repair costs.
  • Monitor workshop progress and provide customers with regular updates.
  • Arrange approval for additional work identified during inspections.
  • Process job cards, invoices, and other service documentation accurately.
  • Build strong customer relationships and contribute to customer retention and satisfaction.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Service Advisor within an automotive environment is desirable.
  • Good understanding of vehicle servicing, maintenance, and repair processes.
  • Excellent communication and customer relationship skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Confident explaining technical information and costs to customers.
  • Strong attention to detail and accurate administrative skills.
  • Good computer literacy and ability to use workshop management systems.
  • Commercially aware, proactive, and committed to delivering excellent customer service.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Standard full-time working schedule.
  • Working hours may vary according to operational requirements.

Salary

  • Up to £35,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive automotive salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Automotive technical and customer service training.
  • Professional workshop environment.
  • Opportunities for additional qualifications and development.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
