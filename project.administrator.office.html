Project Administrator

Job Description

Turner & Townsend Limited is a leading global professional services company specialising in project management, cost management, programme management, and consultancy services across construction, infrastructure, and real estate sectors. Turner & Townsend is seeking a highly organised Project Administrator to provide administrative support to project managers and project teams. In this role, you will coordinate project documentation, organise meetings, maintain project records, and help ensure projects are delivered efficiently and in accordance with company standards.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to project managers and project teams.
  • Maintain accurate project documentation, records, and filing systems.
  • Schedule project meetings, prepare agendas, and record meeting minutes.
  • Monitor project correspondence and distribute documents to relevant stakeholders.
  • Prepare reports, spreadsheets, presentations, and project documentation.
  • Track project deadlines and assist with project planning activities.
  • Coordinate communication between clients, contractors, and internal teams.
  • Ensure project documentation complies with company procedures and quality standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in project administration, office administration, or project support.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, Outlook, and PowerPoint.
  • Experience using project management software is desirable.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Professional attitude with strong teamwork and problem-solving skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard business hours.

Salary

  • £28,000–£34,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Flexible working opportunities where available.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Turner & Townsend.
