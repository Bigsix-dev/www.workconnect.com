Engineering Planner

Job Description

Manufacturing Company operates engineering and production facilities that manufacture products and components for industrial customers. The company is seeking an Engineering Planner to coordinate maintenance, engineering activities, resources, and work schedules. You will work closely with engineering, maintenance, production, and procurement teams to ensure planned work is completed efficiently and production requirements are supported.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and schedule engineering and maintenance activities.
  • Coordinate planned preventative maintenance programmes and engineering work orders.
  • Work with engineering and production teams to prioritise maintenance and improvement activities.
  • Allocate resources, materials, equipment, and labour required for planned work.
  • Monitor work schedules and identify potential delays or resource constraints.
  • Maintain accurate engineering, maintenance, and planning records.
  • Coordinate with procurement teams to ensure required parts and materials are available.
  • Analyse planning information and support improvements to engineering and maintenance processes.

Requirements

  • Degree, diploma, or equivalent qualification in Engineering, Maintenance, Manufacturing, Planning, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience in engineering planning, maintenance planning, or manufacturing is desirable.
  • Good understanding of engineering maintenance and production environments.
  • Strong scheduling, coordination, and organisational skills.
  • Ability to manage competing priorities and meet deadlines.
  • Good understanding of maintenance management systems and planning processes.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills.

Working Hours

  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

Benefits

  • Supportive manufacturing environment.
