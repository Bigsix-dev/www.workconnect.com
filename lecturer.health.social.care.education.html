Lecturer – Health & Social Care

Job Description

Chelmsford College is a leading further education provider dedicated to delivering excellent teaching, skills development, and career opportunities for students. Chelmsford College is seeking a passionate and experienced Lecturer in Health & Social Care to inspire students and prepare them for careers within the healthcare and social care sectors. In this role, you will deliver high-quality lessons, support student development, assess academic progress, and contribute to the success of Health & Social Care programmes.

Responsibilities

  • Deliver engaging Health & Social Care lessons to students across different levels.
  • Plan and develop lesson materials, assessments, and learning activities.
  • Assess student work and provide effective feedback to support improvement.
  • Support students with academic progress and career development.
  • Maintain accurate records of student achievement and attendance.
  • Use innovative teaching methods to support different learning needs.
  • Contribute to curriculum development and quality improvement activities.
  • Work closely with colleagues to create a supportive learning environment.

Requirements

  • Relevant degree or professional qualification in Health & Social Care or related field.
  • Teaching qualification such as PGCE, Certificate in Education, or equivalent is desirable.
  • Experience teaching Health & Social Care in further education or a similar setting.
  • Strong knowledge of healthcare and social care practices.
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills.
  • Ability to motivate, support, and inspire students.
  • Strong organisational and assessment abilities.
  • Commitment to safeguarding and student welfare.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during college term time.

Salary

  • £32,000–£45,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive lecturer salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
  • Supportive and inclusive college environment.
  • Career progression opportunities within further education.
  • Access to college facilities and staff benefits.
