Dispatch Supervisor

Job Description

GXO Logistics UK Limited is one of the world's leading contract logistics providers, delivering advanced warehousing, transportation, and supply chain solutions for businesses across multiple industries. GXO Logistics is seeking an experienced and motivated Dispatch Supervisor to lead its dispatch operations. In this role, you will oversee dispatch teams, coordinate outbound shipments, ensure operational efficiency, and maintain high standards of safety, accuracy, and customer service.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise the daily dispatch operations and ensure all outbound deliveries are completed on schedule.
  • Lead, motivate, and support dispatch operatives to achieve operational targets.
  • Coordinate with warehouse, transport, and customer service teams to ensure efficient order fulfilment.
  • Monitor shipment accuracy and resolve dispatch issues promptly.
  • Prepare and maintain dispatch reports, records, and operational documentation.
  • Ensure delivery vehicles are loaded safely and according to company procedures.
  • Monitor staff performance and provide coaching and training where required.
  • Ensure compliance with company policies, transport regulations, and health and safety standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in a dispatch, warehouse, or logistics supervisory role.
  • Strong leadership and team management skills.
  • Excellent organisational and problem-solving abilities.
  • Good understanding of warehouse and distribution operations.
  • Experience using warehouse or transport management systems.
  • Strong communication skills and the ability to work under pressure.
  • Good attention to detail and commitment to operational excellence.
  • Flexible availability to support shift operations and business requirements.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40–45 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, weekends, and overtime when required.

Salary

  • £34,000–£40,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Leadership training and professional development programmes.
  • Employee discounts and reward schemes.
  • Health and wellbeing support services.
  • Modern logistics facilities and advanced warehouse technology.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within GXO Logistics.
