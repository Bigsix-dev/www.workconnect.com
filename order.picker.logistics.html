Order Picker

Job Description

Bidfood UK Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading foodservice wholesalers, supplying high-quality food products and catering solutions to restaurants, schools, healthcare providers, and businesses nationwide. Bidfood UK is seeking a reliable and detail-focused Order Picker to join its warehouse team. In this role, you will accurately pick customer orders, prepare products for delivery, and support efficient warehouse operations while maintaining excellent standards of safety, accuracy, and productivity.

Responsibilities

  • Pick and prepare customer orders accurately using warehouse systems and picking instructions.
  • Select food products from storage areas while maintaining order accuracy.
  • Check products for quality, quantity, and condition before dispatch.
  • Pack and organise orders ready for delivery to customers.
  • Operate handheld scanners and warehouse equipment safely.
  • Assist with loading and unloading deliveries when required.
  • Maintain clean, organised, and safe warehouse areas.
  • Follow food safety procedures, company policies, and health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous warehouse, picking, or logistics experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Good attention to detail and ability to work accurately.
  • Ability to lift and move products safely throughout the shift.
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced warehouse environment.
  • Good teamwork and communication skills.
  • Ability to use handheld scanners and warehouse systems.
  • Reliable, punctual, and committed to meeting performance targets.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and overtime when required.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including early mornings, evenings, and weekend work.

Salary

  • £13.20–£15.20 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts and reward programmes.
  • Comprehensive warehouse training and development.
  • Health and wellbeing support services.
  • Modern warehouse facilities and equipment.
  • Career progression opportunities within Bidfood UK.
