Multi Trader – Social Housing Repairs

Job Description

Regen Solutions is seeking an experienced Multi Trader to carry out repairs and maintenance across social housing properties. The successful candidate will complete a range of responsive and planned repairs, use multiple trade skills, and provide a professional service to residents while maintaining high standards of workmanship and safety.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out responsive and planned repairs within social housing properties.
  • Complete multi-trade works including carpentry, plumbing, plastering, tiling, and decorating.
  • Diagnose property maintenance issues and identify appropriate repair solutions.
  • Complete repairs within agreed timescales and quality standards.
  • Communicate professionally with residents and provide updates where required.
  • Inspect completed work and ensure repairs meet required standards.
  • Maintain accurate records of completed works.
  • Report additional repair requirements and potential property issues.
  • Maintain tools, equipment, and work areas in a safe condition.
  • Follow health and safety requirements and relevant social housing procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in social housing repairs or property maintenance.
  • Strong practical skills across multiple building trades.
  • Good understanding of responsive repairs and maintenance processes.
  • Ability to work independently and manage a busy schedule.
  • Good communication and resident-facing customer service skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and quality workmanship.
  • Reliable and professional approach to work.
  • Relevant trade qualifications are desirable.

Working Hours

  • Contract position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £200–£220 per day.

Benefits

  • Competitive daily rate.
  • Opportunity for contract work within social housing.
  • Varied repair and maintenance assignments.
  • Opportunity to develop multi-trade experience.
  • Potential for ongoing contract opportunities.
