Accounts Administrator

Job Description

Page Personnel Limited is a leading recruitment consultancy specialising in professional roles across finance, accounting, administration, and business support sectors. Page Personnel is seeking a detail-oriented and organised Accounts Administrator to support finance operations and ensure accurate financial administration. In this role, you will process invoices, maintain financial records, support accounts teams, and assist with day-to-day accounting activities while ensuring accuracy and compliance with company procedures.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to the finance and accounts team.
  • Process invoices, payments, and financial documentation accurately.
  • Maintain accurate accounting records and update financial systems.
  • Assist with accounts payable and accounts receivable activities.
  • Perform data entry and reconcile financial information.
  • Prepare financial reports, spreadsheets, and administrative documents.
  • Respond to supplier and internal finance enquiries professionally.
  • Ensure compliance with financial procedures, company policies, and data protection requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in accounts administration, finance administration, or a similar role.
  • Good understanding of basic accounting processes.
  • Strong numerical skills and attention to detail.
  • Excellent organisational and time management abilities.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, especially Excel and Outlook.
  • Experience using accounting software is desirable.
  • Strong communication and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to manage confidential financial information professionally.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard business hours.

Salary

  • £25,000–£31,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional accounting training and development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Flexible working opportunities where applicable.
  • Supportive and professional working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Page Personnel.
