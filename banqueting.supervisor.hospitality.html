Banqueting Supervisor

Job Description

Pontlands Park Hotel is a prestigious country house hotel in Essex, renowned for hosting weddings, conferences, banquets, and private events. The hotel is seeking an experienced and motivated Banqueting Supervisor to oversee banquet operations and lead the banqueting team during functions. In this role, you will ensure exceptional customer service, coordinate event service, supervise staff, and maintain the highest standards of hospitality to create memorable experiences for every guest.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise the banqueting team during weddings, conferences, and private events.
  • Coordinate the setup and presentation of function rooms according to event plans.
  • Ensure food and beverage service is delivered efficiently and professionally.
  • Welcome clients and guests, ensuring excellent customer satisfaction throughout events.
  • Allocate duties to team members and monitor staff performance during service.
  • Resolve guest enquiries and service issues promptly and professionally.
  • Support event planning by working closely with kitchen and hotel management teams.
  • Ensure compliance with food hygiene regulations, health and safety standards, and hotel policies.

Requirements

  • Previous supervisory experience in banqueting, hospitality, or food and beverage service.
  • Excellent leadership and communication skills.
  • Outstanding customer service and organisational abilities.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced event environment.
  • Strong attention to detail and problem-solving skills.
  • Knowledge of food hygiene and health and safety procedures.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • A professional appearance and a positive, team-focused attitude.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 38–42 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts based on event schedules, including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £14.50–£16.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Complimentary or discounted staff meals during shifts.
  • Leadership training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts on hotel services.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive working environment with excellent opportunities for career progression.
