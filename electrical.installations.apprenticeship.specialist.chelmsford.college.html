Electrical Installations Apprenticeship Specialist

Job Description

Chelmsford College is looking for an Electrical Installations Apprenticeship Specialist to support the delivery of high-quality electrical installation training. The position involves teaching practical and theoretical skills while helping apprentices develop the knowledge required for successful careers in the electrical industry.

Responsibilities

  • Deliver electrical installation training to apprentices and learners.
  • Provide practical demonstrations and classroom-based instruction.
  • Assess learner performance and maintain accurate training records.
  • Support apprentices with their development and progression.
  • Maintain safe and effective workshop environments.
  • Keep teaching materials and technical knowledge up to date.

Requirements

  • Relevant electrical installation experience and qualifications.
  • Strong practical knowledge of electrical systems and installations.
  • Ability to communicate technical information clearly.
  • Good organisational and assessment skills.
  • Commitment to learner development and safety.

Working Hours

  • 37 hours per week.
  • Based in Chelmsford.

Salary

  • £46,642 per year + supplements/bonus.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary with additional supplements/bonus.
  • Opportunity to support the next generation of electrical professionals.
  • Professional development opportunities.
  • Stable college-based working environment.
