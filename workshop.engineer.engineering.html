Workshop Engineer

Job Description

Engineering Workshop provides engineering fabrication, repair, maintenance, assembly, and technical services for industrial and commercial customers. The company is seeking a practical Workshop Engineer to support the manufacture, assembly, repair, testing, and maintenance of engineering equipment and components. You will work with workshop and engineering teams to complete technical work safely, accurately, and to required specifications.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out engineering assembly, repair, modification, and maintenance work.
  • Read and interpret engineering drawings, specifications, and technical instructions.
  • Use workshop machinery, tools, and measurement equipment safely and effectively.
  • Inspect completed work to ensure it meets required quality and technical standards.
  • Diagnose mechanical and engineering faults and recommend appropriate repairs.
  • Support the manufacture and refurbishment of engineering components and equipment.
  • Maintain accurate workshop records, inspection reports, and job documentation.
  • Follow health and safety procedures and maintain a clean and organised workshop environment.

Requirements

  • Engineering qualification, apprenticeship, or equivalent practical engineering experience.
  • Previous workshop, mechanical, manufacturing, or maintenance experience is desirable.
  • Good understanding of engineering tools, machinery, and workshop practices.
  • Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings.
  • Strong practical skills and mechanical aptitude.
  • Good fault-finding and problem-solving abilities.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to quality.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of an engineering workshop team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £30,000–£45,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering workshop salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Practical engineering and technical training.
  • Access to modern workshop equipment.
  • Opportunities to develop a broad range of engineering skills.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
