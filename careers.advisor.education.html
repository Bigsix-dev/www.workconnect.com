Careers Advisor

Job Description

Chelmsford College is committed to helping students achieve their ambitions by providing high-quality education, guidance, and career support. Chelmsford College is seeking a knowledgeable and enthusiastic Careers Advisor to provide professional careers guidance and support students in making informed decisions about their future. In this role, you will offer career advice, support progression planning, develop employability skills, and help students prepare for further education, training, and employment opportunities.

Responsibilities

  • Provide careers guidance and advice to students across the college.
  • Support students with career planning, applications, and progression pathways.
  • Deliver careers workshops, employability sessions, and information events.
  • Assist students with CV writing, interview preparation, and job searching skills.
  • Maintain accurate records of student guidance activities and outcomes.
  • Build relationships with employers, universities, and training providers.
  • Keep up to date with labour market information and career opportunities.
  • Promote equality, inclusion, and access to career support services.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in careers advice, education support, recruitment, or guidance services.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to support students from diverse backgrounds.
  • Strong knowledge of career pathways, employment, and education options.
  • Good organisational and administrative skills.
  • Ability to provide confidential and professional advice.
  • Strong presentation and workshop delivery skills.
  • Relevant careers guidance qualification is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during college working hours.

Salary

  • £30,000–£38,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme or workplace pension.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Professional development and careers guidance training opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
  • Supportive college environment.
  • Opportunities for career progression within education and student services.
  • Access to college facilities and staff benefits.
