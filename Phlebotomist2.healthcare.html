Phlebotomist

Job Overview

Phoenix Hospital Chelmsford is seeking a skilled and patient-focused Phlebotomist to join our clinical team. You will play a key role in supporting accurate diagnosis and patient care by collecting high-quality blood samples in a safe, efficient, and reassuring manner.

Key Responsibilities

  • Perform venepuncture procedures in accordance with approved clinical guidelines and hospital protocols
  • Ensure accurate patient identification and correct labelling of all specimens
  • Prepare, handle, and dispatch samples for laboratory analysis in a timely manner
  • Maintain strict adherence to infection prevention and control standards
  • Provide professional reassurance and support to patients throughout the procedure
  • Ensure all documentation and electronic records are completed accurately and promptly
  • Maintain and monitor phlebotomy equipment, ensuring readiness for clinical use
  • Work collaboratively with nursing, medical, and laboratory teams to support efficient patient care delivery

Person Specification

  • Proven experience in phlebotomy within a healthcare or clinical environment
  • Competent in venous blood collection techniques with a strong focus on patient safety
  • Excellent interpersonal and communication skills
  • High level of accuracy, organisation, and attention to detail
  • Understanding of clinical governance, safeguarding, and infection control standards
  • Ability to work effectively under pressure within a multidisciplinary team
  • Relevant phlebotomy qualification or healthcare training (desirable)

Working Hours

  • Full-time position (approximately 37.5 hours per week)
  • Rotational shifts may apply depending on departmental requirements

Benefits

  • Competitive salary aligned with experience
  • Structured clinical environment within a reputable private hospital
  • Opportunities for continuous professional development and training
  • Supportive multidisciplinary team culture
  • Access to employee wellbeing initiatives and staff benefits
