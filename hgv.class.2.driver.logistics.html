HGV Class 2 Driver

Job Description

GXO Logistics UK Limited is one of the world's leading providers of logistics, warehousing, and transportation solutions, serving a wide range of retail, manufacturing, and consumer goods customers. GXO Logistics is seeking a reliable and safety-focused HGV Class 2 Driver to join its transport team. In this role, you will deliver goods safely and efficiently to customers while maintaining excellent customer service and complying with all UK transport legislation and company standards.

Responsibilities

  • Operate HGV Class 2 (Category C) vehicles safely and responsibly.
  • Complete scheduled deliveries and collections within allocated timeframes.
  • Carry out daily vehicle inspections and report any defects promptly.
  • Load and unload goods safely while ensuring cargo is properly secured.
  • Complete delivery documentation and electronic proof of delivery accurately.
  • Comply with Drivers' Hours Regulations, Driver CPC requirements, and road safety legislation.
  • Provide professional customer service during deliveries and collections.
  • Maintain vehicle cleanliness and ensure company equipment is used correctly.

Requirements

  • Valid UK HGV Class 2 (Category C) driving licence.
  • Valid Driver Certificate of Professional Competence (Driver CPC).
  • Digital Tachograph Driver Card.
  • Good understanding of UK road transport regulations.
  • Strong commitment to health, safety, and customer service.
  • Good communication and organisational skills.
  • Ability to work flexible hours, including weekends when required.
  • Previous HGV Class 2 driving experience is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40–45 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including early mornings, evenings, and occasional weekends.

Salary

  • £16.50–£19.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Paid Driver CPC training and ongoing professional development.
  • Employee discounts and reward programmes.
  • Life assurance and employee wellbeing support.
  • Modern fleet with well-maintained vehicles.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within GXO Logistics.
