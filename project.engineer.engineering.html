Project Engineer

Job Description

Engineering Consultancy provides engineering design, technical consultancy, and project delivery services to clients across a range of industries. The company is seeking an experienced Project Engineer to support the planning, coordination, and delivery of engineering projects. You will work with clients, engineers, contractors, and project teams to ensure projects are delivered safely, efficiently, and in accordance with technical and commercial requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate engineering projects from initial planning through to completion.
  • Develop project plans, schedules, technical documentation, and progress reports.
  • Work with engineering teams to define project requirements and deliverables.
  • Monitor project progress, budgets, resources, and technical performance.
  • Coordinate with clients, contractors, suppliers, and other project stakeholders.
  • Identify project risks and support the development of appropriate mitigation measures.
  • Ensure engineering work complies with relevant standards, specifications, and regulations.
  • Support project reviews, technical meetings, and continuous improvement activities.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Engineering or a related technical discipline.
  • Previous experience in engineering project delivery or project engineering is desirable.
  • Good understanding of engineering design and project management principles.
  • Strong organisational and project coordination skills.
  • Excellent problem-solving and decision-making abilities.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks, deadlines, and project priorities.
  • Strong technical documentation and reporting skills.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £50,000–£70,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering consultancy salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Project management and professional engineering training.
  • Opportunities to work on varied engineering projects.
  • Collaborative consultancy environment.
  • Career progression and professional development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
