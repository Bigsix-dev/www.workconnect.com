Conveyancing Paralegal

Job Description

LJ Recruitment is seeking an organised Conveyancing Paralegal to provide legal and administrative support within a residential property team. The successful candidate will assist with property transactions, prepare documentation, communicate with clients and third parties, and help ensure conveyancing matters progress efficiently.

Responsibilities

  • Assist solicitors and conveyancers with residential property transactions.
  • Open and maintain accurate client and matter files.
  • Review property documentation and title information.
  • Assist with searches, enquiries, and conveyancing administration.
  • Prepare and process legal documents and correspondence.
  • Liaise with clients, estate agents, lenders, and other legal professionals.
  • Monitor transaction deadlines and outstanding requirements.
  • Maintain accurate records on case management systems.
  • Provide clients with updates and respond to routine enquiries.
  • Support the team with exchange and completion administration.

Requirements

  • Previous conveyancing or legal administration experience is desirable.
  • Good understanding of residential property transactions.
  • Excellent organisational and administrative skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Good written and verbal communication skills.
  • Confident using computer and case management systems.
  • Ability to manage multiple matters and deadlines.
  • Professional and client-focused approach.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday, 9:00am–5:30pm.
  • Full-time position.

Salary

  • Salary not stated.

Benefits

  • Professional legal working environment.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Opportunity to develop conveyancing expertise.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
  • Experience supporting a residential property caseload.
