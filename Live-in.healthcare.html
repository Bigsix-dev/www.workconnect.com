Live-in Care Assistant

Job Description

As a Live-in Care Assistant, you will reside in the client's home and provide dedicated one-to-one support to help them maintain their independence, dignity, and quality of life. This rewarding role involves assisting with daily living activities, offering companionship, and ensuring clients receive compassionate, person-centred care in the comfort of their own homes.

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide personal care, including bathing, dressing, and grooming.
  • Provide personal care, including bathing, dressing, and grooming.
  • Prepare nutritious meals and support with feeding when required.
  • Administer or prompt medication in accordance with care plans.
  • Carry out light housekeeping duties, including cleaning and laundry.
  • Offer companionship and emotional support.
  • Accompany clients to appointments or social activities.
  • Monitor and report changes in clients' health or wellbeing.
  • Maintain accurate care records and communicate effectively with the care team.
  • Promote independence while respecting each client's preferences and dignity.
