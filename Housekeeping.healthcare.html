Housekeeping Supervisor (Healthcare)

Job Overview

Manor Lodge Care Home is seeking a dedicated and experienced Housekeeping Supervisor (Healthcare) to lead its housekeeping team in maintaining a clean, safe, and welcoming environment for residents, visitors, and staff. This role combines hands-on housekeeping responsibilities with team leadership, ensuring high standards of hygiene and cleanliness are consistently met throughout the care home.

The successful candidate will play an important part in supporting infection prevention measures, supervising daily cleaning operations, managing staff schedules, and ensuring compliance with health and safety regulations.

Key Responsibilities

  • Supervise and support the housekeeping team to deliver exceptional cleaning standards.
  • Plan and allocate daily cleaning duties across the care home.
  • Monitor quality control and carry out regular inspections of resident rooms and communal areas.
  • Ensure compliance with infection prevention, health and safety, and hygiene policies.
  • Train, mentor, and motivate housekeeping staff to maintain excellent performance.
  • Manage cleaning supplies and stock levels, reporting any shortages as needed.
  • Respond promptly to housekeeping requests from residents and management.
  • Collaborate with other departments to provide a comfortable and pleasant living environment.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in housekeeping, ideally within a healthcare, care home, or hospitality setting.
  • Experience supervising or leading a team is highly desirable.
  • Strong organisational and communication skills.
  • Good understanding of infection control procedures and health and safety practices.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment while maintaining attention to detail.
  • A positive attitude and commitment to delivering high-quality service.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary of £25,000–£30,000.
  • Full-time, stable employment with opportunities for career progression.
  • Ongoing training and professional development.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Opportunity to make a meaningful difference in the daily lives of residents.
  • Employee wellbeing initiatives and company benefits package.
