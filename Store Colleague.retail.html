Store Colleague

Job Description

Aldi is seeking enthusiastic and hardworking Store Colleagues to join its retail team. In this role, you will help deliver an excellent shopping experience by providing outstanding customer service, maintaining well-stocked shelves, operating tills, and ensuring the store remains clean, safe, and organised. You will work as part of a supportive team in a fast-paced environment where every colleague contributes to the smooth running of the store.

Responsibilities

  • Serve customers politely and efficiently at the checkout.
  • Replenish shelves and ensure products are displayed correctly.
  • Receive, unpack, and organise stock deliveries.
  • Maintain high standards of cleanliness throughout the store.
  • Monitor product quality and rotate stock where necessary.
  • Assist customers with enquiries and product locations.
  • Follow all company health and safety procedures.
  • Support colleagues with daily store operations.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced retail environment.
  • Reliable, punctual, and hardworking.
  • Ability to lift and move stock safely.
  • Flexible to work weekdays, evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.
  • Previous retail experience is an advantage but not essential.
  • Full training is provided for successful applicants.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 30–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns, including mornings, evenings, weekends, and public holidays.

Salary

  • £13.65–£14.35 per hour.
  • Overtime opportunities may be available where applicable.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Comprehensive training and development.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee discounts and wellbeing support.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
