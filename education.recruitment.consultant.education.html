Education Recruitment Consultant

Job Description

4myschools is a specialist education recruitment agency dedicated to connecting talented education professionals with schools and learning providers. The company is seeking an ambitious and motivated Education Recruitment Consultant to manage recruitment processes, build strong relationships with schools and candidates, and support the delivery of high-quality staffing solutions. In this role, you will source education professionals, develop client relationships, and help schools find suitable teaching and support staff.

Responsibilities

  • Source, interview, and place qualified education professionals into school roles.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with schools, academies, and education providers.
  • Manage candidate registrations, compliance checks, and recruitment processes.
  • Advertise vacancies and attract suitable teaching and support staff.
  • Provide career guidance and support to education candidates.
  • Develop new business opportunities and maintain existing client accounts.
  • Maintain accurate recruitment records and databases.
  • Work towards recruitment targets while delivering excellent customer service.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in recruitment, sales, education, or customer-focused roles is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and relationship-building skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Ability to work in a target-driven recruitment environment.
  • Confident telephone and negotiation skills.
  • Strong attention to detail when managing candidate information.
  • Ability to build trust with schools and education professionals.
  • Positive, proactive, and results-focused approach.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday office-based working hours.

Salary

  • £25,000–£35,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Commission and performance-related incentives.
  • Professional recruitment training and development.
  • Supportive and collaborative team environment.
  • Career progression opportunities within recruitment.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Company events and team activities.
  • Opportunities to build a successful career in education recruitment.
