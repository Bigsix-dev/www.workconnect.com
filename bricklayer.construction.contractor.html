Bricklayer

Job Description

Construction Contractor is seeking a skilled Bricklayer for construction projects in Chelmsford. The successful candidate will be responsible for producing accurate brick and blockwork while maintaining high standards of workmanship, safety, and productivity.

Responsibilities

  • Complete brickwork and blockwork to project specifications.
  • Set out walls and structures accurately.
  • Mix and apply mortar correctly.
  • Read drawings and follow construction measurements.
  • Maintain consistent quality and neat finishes.
  • Keep tools and work areas clean and safe.

Requirements

  • Proven experience as a Bricklayer.
  • Strong knowledge of bricklaying techniques.
  • Ability to work from drawings and specifications.
  • Good attention to detail.
  • Commitment to site safety and quality.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Based in Chelmsford.

Salary

  • £180–£220 per day.

Benefits

  • Competitive daily rate.
  • Regular construction work.
  • Opportunity to work on varied projects.
  • Professional site environment.
