Cover Supervisor

Job Description

Moulsham High School is a successful secondary school committed to providing a high-quality education and a supportive learning environment where students are encouraged to achieve their full potential. Moulsham High School is seeking a confident and organised Cover Supervisor to support classroom learning by supervising lessons during teacher absences. In this role, you will ensure students remain engaged with their work, maintain positive behaviour, and provide effective classroom support in line with school expectations.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise classes during teacher absences and ensure learning activities are completed.
  • Deliver prepared lesson materials and support students with classroom tasks.
  • Maintain a positive and respectful learning environment.
  • Manage classroom behaviour in accordance with school policies.
  • Monitor student progress and report any concerns to teaching staff.
  • Support students with questions and encourage independent learning.
  • Assist teachers and departments with general classroom support when required.
  • Follow safeguarding procedures and promote student wellbeing.

Requirements

  • Previous experience working with young people in an educational setting is desirable.
  • Strong classroom management and communication skills.
  • Confident ability to supervise groups of students independently.
  • Patient, approachable, and supportive attitude.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with students and staff.
  • Good organisational skills and ability to follow lesson plans.
  • Understanding of safeguarding and child protection responsibilities.
  • Experience in teaching, tutoring, youth work, or education support is advantageous.

Working Hours

  • Part-time position.
  • 25–30 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £23,000–£25,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • School holiday periods and paid leave entitlement.
  • Professional development and training opportunities.
  • Supportive school leadership team.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for career progression in education.
  • Positive and inclusive working environment.
  • Rewarding role supporting student achievement.
