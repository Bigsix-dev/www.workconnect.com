Customer Service Advisor

Job Description

Adecco is seeking a friendly and professional Customer Service Advisor to provide excellent support to customers and ensure their enquiries are handled efficiently. You will be responsible for responding to customer questions, resolving issues, maintaining accurate records, and providing a consistently positive customer experience. The role requires strong communication skills, attention to detail, and the ability to manage a range of customer enquiries professionally.

Responsibilities

  • Respond to customer enquiries by telephone, email, and other communication channels.
  • Provide accurate information about products, services, orders, and processes.
  • Resolve customer complaints and issues in a professional and timely manner.
  • Record customer interactions and update relevant systems accurately.
  • Process customer requests, orders, and service enquiries where required.
  • Work with internal departments to resolve customer issues and follow up on outstanding matters.
  • Maintain a high standard of customer service throughout every interaction.
  • Identify recurring customer issues and provide feedback to management to support service improvements.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service, administration, retail, or contact centre experience is desirable.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Friendly, professional, and customer-focused approach.
  • Strong listening and problem-solving abilities.
  • Good computer and data entry skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accurate record-keeping abilities.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional when handling complaints or difficult situations.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a customer service team.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday, 8:00am–4:00pm.
  • Alternate Saturdays are required.
  • Full-time working schedule.

Salary

  • £12.80 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly rate.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Customer service and professional development training.
  • Supportive team environment.
  • Opportunities to develop customer service and administration skills.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
