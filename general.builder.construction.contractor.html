General Builder

Job Description

Construction Contractor is looking for an experienced General Builder to support construction and refurbishment projects in Chelmsford. The role involves completing a broad range of building tasks and working alongside other trades to deliver quality projects.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out general building and refurbishment works.
  • Assist with brickwork, blockwork, plastering, and repairs.
  • Prepare materials and work areas for construction activities.
  • Work alongside specialist trades and site management.
  • Complete tasks to project specifications and quality standards.
  • Follow site health and safety requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a General Builder.
  • Broad knowledge of construction and building practices.
  • Good practical and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Strong commitment to quality and safety.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Based in Chelmsford.

Salary

  • £35,000–£45,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary.
  • Varied construction projects.
  • Opportunities for long-term work.
  • Professional development opportunities.
