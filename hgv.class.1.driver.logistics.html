HGV Class 1 Driver

Job Description

DHL Supply Chain Limited, a division of DHL Group, is one of the world's leading logistics and supply chain management companies. DHL Supply Chain is seeking a professional and safety-conscious HGV Class 1 Driver to join its transport team. In this role, you will transport goods efficiently between distribution centres and customer locations, ensuring deliveries are completed safely, on time, and in accordance with company policies and UK road transport regulations.

Responsibilities

  • Operate HGV Class 1 vehicles safely and efficiently.
  • Complete scheduled deliveries and collections on time.
  • Carry out daily vehicle safety inspections before and after each shift.
  • Load and unload goods where required, ensuring cargo is secured correctly.
  • Complete delivery paperwork and electronic proof of delivery accurately.
  • Comply with Drivers' Hours Regulations, Working Time Directive, and road transport legislation.
  • Report vehicle defects, accidents, or delivery issues promptly.
  • Maintain high standards of customer service during deliveries and collections.

Requirements

  • Valid UK HGV Class 1 (Category C+E) driving licence.
  • Valid Driver Certificate of Professional Competence (Driver CPC).
  • Digital Tachograph Driver Card.
  • Good knowledge of UK road transport regulations.
  • Excellent driving record and commitment to safety.
  • Strong communication and organisational skills.
  • Ability to work flexible shifts, including weekends when required.
  • Previous HGV driving experience is preferred.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 45–50 hours per week.
  • Day, night, and weekend shifts may be required depending on operational needs.

Salary

  • £18.50–£22.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Paid Driver CPC training and professional development.
  • Employee discounts and reward programmes.
  • Life assurance and wellbeing support.
  • Modern fleet with well-maintained vehicles.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression within DHL Supply Chain.
