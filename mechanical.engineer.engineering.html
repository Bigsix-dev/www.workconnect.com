Mechanical Engineer

Job Description

Engineering Consultancy Chelmsford is a professional engineering organisation delivering innovative design, technical solutions, and engineering support across a range of industries. The company is seeking an experienced and motivated Mechanical Engineer to join its engineering team. In this role, you will be responsible for designing mechanical systems, developing engineering solutions, supporting projects, and ensuring designs meet quality, safety, and performance standards.

Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and improve mechanical systems and components.
  • Create detailed engineering drawings, specifications, and technical documentation.
  • Use computer-aided design (CAD) software to produce accurate designs.
  • Perform calculations, simulations, and technical analysis to support projects.
  • Collaborate with engineers, project managers, and clients to deliver solutions.
  • Support product development, testing, and continuous improvement activities.
  • Identify engineering issues and provide effective technical solutions.
  • Ensure all designs comply with industry standards, regulations, and safety requirements.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience working as a Mechanical Engineer is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of mechanical design principles and engineering processes.
  • Experience using CAD software and engineering design tools.
  • Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Strong communication and teamwork abilities.
  • Ability to manage multiple engineering projects and deadlines.
  • Knowledge of industry standards and health and safety requirements.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £35,000–£55,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Access to modern engineering tools and technology.
  • Supportive technical team environment.
  • Career progression opportunities within engineering.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
