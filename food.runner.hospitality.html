Food Runner

Job Description

Turtle Bay is looking for energetic Food Runners to ensure meals are delivered quickly and accurately from the kitchen to guests. You will support the restaurant team by providing efficient service and helping create an outstanding dining experience.

Key Responsibilities

  • Deliver food orders promptly to guests.
  • Ensure every order is accurate before serving.
  • Assist servers with clearing and resetting tables.
  • Maintain cleanliness throughout the dining area.
  • Support kitchen and front-of-house staff during busy periods.

Requirements

  • Friendly and energetic personality.
  • Ability to work quickly in a busy environment.
  • Strong teamwork skills.
  • Excellent communication skills.
  • Previous hospitality experience is an advantage.

Working Hours

  • Full-time.
  • 30–38 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings and weekends.

Salary & Benefits

  • £12.30–£13.60 per hour.
  • Staff meals.
  • Employee discounts.
  • Training opportunities.
  • Supportive team environment.
