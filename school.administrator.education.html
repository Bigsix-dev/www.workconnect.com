School Administrator

Job Description

Bridge Academy Trust is a forward-thinking education organisation dedicated to supporting schools in providing excellent learning experiences for children and young people. Bridge Academy Trust is seeking an organised and professional School Administrator to provide essential administrative support and ensure the smooth running of school operations. In this role, you will manage school records, support staff and parents, coordinate daily administrative activities, and contribute to an efficient school environment.

Responsibilities

  • Provide general administrative support to school leaders, teachers, and staff.
  • Manage school records, databases, and confidential information.
  • Handle enquiries from parents, students, visitors, and external organisations.
  • Maintain attendance records and update school management systems.
  • Prepare letters, reports, spreadsheets, and school documentation.
  • Support school events, meetings, and administrative arrangements.
  • Process invoices, orders, and general office paperwork.
  • Ensure compliance with school policies, safeguarding procedures, and data protection regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in school administration, office administration, or a similar role.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong communication and customer service abilities.
  • Ability to handle confidential information professionally.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Experience using school management systems is desirable.
  • Strong attention to detail and accurate record keeping skills.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a busy school team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school working hours.

Salary

  • £24,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Local Government Pension Scheme.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Supportive and friendly school environment.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for career progression within education administration.
  • Inclusive and collaborative working environment.
