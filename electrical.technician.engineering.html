Electrical Technician

Job Description

Engineering Contractor provides specialist electrical engineering, installation, maintenance, and technical services to commercial and industrial clients. The company is seeking a skilled and reliable Electrical Technician to support electrical installation, testing, maintenance, and fault-finding activities. In this role, you will work alongside engineers and technical teams to ensure electrical systems and equipment operate safely, efficiently, and reliably.

Responsibilities

  • Install, maintain, inspect, and repair electrical systems and equipment.
  • Carry out electrical testing and fault-finding activities.
  • Assist engineers with installation, commissioning, and maintenance projects.
  • Read and interpret electrical drawings, schematics, and technical documentation.
  • Identify electrical faults and implement appropriate corrective solutions.
  • Complete maintenance records, inspection reports, and technical documentation.
  • Carry out preventative maintenance to improve equipment reliability.
  • Ensure all electrical work complies with relevant safety regulations and company procedures.

Requirements

  • Electrical engineering qualification, technical apprenticeship, or equivalent experience.
  • Previous experience as an Electrical Technician or similar technical role.
  • Strong knowledge of electrical systems, wiring, testing, and maintenance.
  • Ability to read and understand electrical drawings and schematics.
  • Strong fault-finding and problem-solving skills.
  • Good understanding of electrical health and safety requirements.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of an engineering team.
  • Excellent attention to detail and practical technical skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £32,000–£45,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive technical salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Electrical training and professional development opportunities.
  • Access to modern electrical tools and equipment.
  • Supportive engineering team environment.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
