Customer Service Assistant

Job Description

East of England Co-op is seeking a friendly and reliable Customer Service Assistant to provide excellent service to customers while supporting the smooth operation of the store. The successful candidate will assist customers with enquiries, maintain high presentation standards, process transactions, and work as part of a supportive team.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide friendly, professional assistance.
  • Handle customer enquiries and provide accurate product information.
  • Process customer purchases and transactions accurately.
  • Replenish stock and maintain attractive product displays.
  • Keep the store clean, organised, and welcoming.
  • Check stock levels and report shortages where required.
  • Assist with deliveries and other store operational activities.
  • Handle customer concerns professionally and escalate issues when necessary.
  • Follow company, health and safety, and security procedures.
  • Support colleagues during busy trading periods.

Requirements

  • Previous retail or customer service experience is desirable.
  • Friendly and approachable personality.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Good attention to detail.
  • Reliable and responsible approach to work.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a team.
  • Customer-focused attitude.
  • Flexibility to work within the required part-time hours.

Working Hours

  • 24 hours per week.
  • Part-time position.

Salary

  • £12.73 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly rate.
  • Employee benefits associated with the organisation.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Supportive team environment.
  • Opportunities to develop retail and customer service experience.
