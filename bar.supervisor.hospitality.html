Bar Supervisor

Job Description

Brewhouse & Kitchen Limited is a leading British pub and microbrewery chain, offering freshly brewed craft beers, quality food, and outstanding hospitality. Brewhouse & Kitchen is seeking an experienced and enthusiastic Bar Supervisor to oversee daily bar operations, lead the bar team, and ensure guests receive exceptional service in a welcoming and vibrant environment. This role combines leadership, customer service, and operational responsibilities to maintain the highest standards of hospitality.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise the daily operation of the bar and support the management team.
  • Lead, motivate, and train bar staff to deliver exceptional customer service.
  • Prepare and serve alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages to company standards.
  • Monitor stock levels, place orders, and assist with inventory control.
  • Handle customer enquiries, complaints, and feedback professionally.
  • Ensure the bar area remains clean, organised, and fully stocked.
  • Assist with staff scheduling and opening and closing procedures.
  • Ensure compliance with licensing laws, food hygiene regulations, health and safety policies, and company procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in a bar supervisory or hospitality leadership role.
  • Excellent leadership and communication skills.
  • Strong customer service and problem-solving abilities.
  • Knowledge of bar operations, beverages, and responsible alcohol service.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced hospitality environment.
  • Good organisational and time management skills.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Personal Licence or licensing knowledge is desirable but not essential.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 38–42 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £14.50–£16.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted meals during shifts.
  • Employee discounts on food and beverages.
  • Management training and career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Friendly, supportive, and inclusive working environment.
