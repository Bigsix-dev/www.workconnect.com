Host/Hostess

Job Description

Turtle Bay is seeking a welcoming and organized Host/Hostess to greet guests, manage reservations, and ensure customers receive a positive first impression. You will coordinate seating arrangements and provide excellent customer service from arrival to departure.

Key Responsibilities

  • Welcome guests with a friendly and professional attitude.
  • Manage reservations and waiting lists.
  • Escort guests to their tables.
  • Answer telephone and customer enquiries.
  • Coordinate with restaurant staff to maintain smooth service.
  • Keep the reception area clean and organized.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Professional appearance and positive attitude.
  • Strong organizational skills.
  • Ability to work in a busy restaurant environment.
  • Previous hospitality experience is preferred but not essential.

Working Hours

  • Full-time.
  • 30–38 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings, weekends, and holidays.

Salary & Benefits

  • £12.40–£13.70 per hour.
  • Employee discounts.
  • Paid holidays.
  • Career development opportunities.
  • Friendly and supportive workplace.
