Deputy Headteacher

Job Description

Chelmsford Schools are committed to providing outstanding education through strong leadership, innovation, and a focus on student success. Schools across Chelmsford are seeking an experienced and inspiring Deputy Headteacher to support the Headteacher in leading school improvement, maintaining high academic standards, and developing a positive learning environment. In this senior leadership role, you will help shape school strategy, support staff development, and ensure excellent outcomes for all students.

Responsibilities

  • Support the Headteacher in the strategic leadership and management of the school.
  • Lead key areas of school improvement, curriculum development, and quality assurance.
  • Monitor teaching standards and support staff development initiatives.
  • Promote high expectations for student achievement, behaviour, and attendance.
  • Analyse performance data and implement strategies to improve outcomes.
  • Support the development and implementation of school policies and procedures.
  • Lead and motivate teaching and support staff across the school.
  • Work with parents, governors, and external partners to strengthen school relationships.

Requirements

  • Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) is required.
  • Significant experience in teaching and senior leadership within a school environment.
  • Previous experience as an Assistant Headteacher or senior leader is desirable.
  • Strong understanding of school improvement, curriculum planning, and educational standards.
  • Excellent leadership, communication, and decision-making skills.
  • Ability to inspire and develop staff teams.
  • Commitment to safeguarding and promoting student wellbeing.
  • Strong strategic planning and organisational abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time, with additional leadership duties when required.

Salary

  • £65,000–£80,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive senior leadership salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme.
  • Generous annual leave and school holiday entitlement.
  • Leadership training and professional development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Supportive senior leadership environment.
  • Opportunities for progression to Headteacher and executive leadership roles.
  • Access to professional education networks and resources.
