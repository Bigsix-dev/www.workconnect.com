School Caretaker

Job Description

Parkwood Academy is committed to providing a safe, welcoming, and well-maintained environment where students and staff can thrive. The academy is seeking a reliable and skilled School Caretaker to oversee the maintenance, security, and day-to-day operation of school premises. In this role, you will ensure buildings, facilities, and grounds are maintained to a high standard while supporting the smooth running of school activities.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out general maintenance, repairs, and upkeep of school buildings and facilities.
  • Ensure the school site is safe, secure, and well-maintained.
  • Perform regular health and safety checks and report any issues.
  • Support opening and closing of school premises when required.
  • Maintain outdoor areas, grounds, and facilities.
  • Monitor contractors and assist with site maintenance projects.
  • Manage basic repairs including plumbing, electrical, and building maintenance tasks where appropriate.
  • Ensure compliance with school policies, security procedures, and health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in caretaking, facilities management, maintenance, or a similar role.
  • Good practical skills and ability to carry out basic repairs.
  • Strong understanding of health and safety procedures.
  • Ability to work independently and manage daily tasks effectively.
  • Good organisational and problem-solving skills.
  • Reliable, flexible, and committed approach to work.
  • Ability to communicate effectively with staff, contractors, and visitors.
  • Relevant maintenance or facilities qualification is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with flexibility for occasional evening or weekend duties.

Salary

  • £25,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Local Government Pension Scheme.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Supportive school working environment.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Opportunities for career progression in facilities management.
  • Rewarding role supporting a safe learning environment.
