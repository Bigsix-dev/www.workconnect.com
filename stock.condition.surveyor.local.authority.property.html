Stock Condition Surveyor

Job Description

A Local Authority / Property organisation is seeking a Stock Condition Surveyor to assess the condition, performance, and maintenance requirements of residential and other property assets. The role involves carrying out inspections, recording building information, identifying defects, and producing accurate condition data to support planned maintenance and investment decisions.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out detailed stock condition surveys of residential and property assets.
  • Inspect internal and external building components.
  • Identify defects, deterioration, and required maintenance works.
  • Record accurate property condition information using appropriate systems.
  • Assess remaining life of building components and installations.
  • Prepare reports and recommendations for planned maintenance programmes.
  • Identify potential health, safety, compliance, and property risks.
  • Work with property and maintenance teams to support asset management decisions.

Requirements

  • Experience in property surveying, stock condition surveying, or building inspection.
  • Good knowledge of building construction and property defects.
  • Ability to accurately assess and record property condition.
  • Strong attention to detail.
  • Good report-writing and communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently across multiple properties.
  • Good understanding of health and safety requirements.
  • Relevant surveying or construction qualification is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours may include regular travel between properties.
  • Site-based inspection work forms a significant part of the position.

Salary

  • £32 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly rate.
  • Professional property surveying experience.
  • Opportunities to work across a diverse property portfolio.
  • Potential for ongoing professional development.
  • Experience supporting planned maintenance and asset management programmes.
