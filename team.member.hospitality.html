Team Member

Job Description

Greggs PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading bakery and food-on-the-go retailers, serving freshly prepared sandwiches, baked goods, hot drinks, and snacks to millions of customers every week. Greggs is seeking enthusiastic, friendly, and reliable Team Members to join its shop team. In this role, you will provide outstanding customer service, prepare and serve food and beverages, maintain high standards of cleanliness, and help ensure the smooth day-to-day operation of the shop.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers with a friendly and professional attitude.
  • Prepare and serve freshly made food, hot drinks, and bakery products.
  • Operate tills and process customer transactions accurately.
  • Replenish food displays and ensure products are presented attractively.
  • Maintain cleanliness of the shop, seating areas, and food preparation stations.
  • Monitor product quality and follow food safety procedures.
  • Assist with receiving deliveries and restocking inventory.
  • Follow company policies, food hygiene regulations, and health and safety procedures at all times.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills.
  • Friendly, reliable, and enthusiastic attitude.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment.
  • Good teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling abilities.
  • Flexible availability, including early mornings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Previous retail or hospitality experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 30–39 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £12.60–£13.70 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted food and drinks during shifts.
  • Employee discounts on Greggs products.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive, friendly, and inclusive working environment.
