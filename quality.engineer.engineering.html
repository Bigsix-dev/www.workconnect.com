Quality Engineer

Job Description

Engineering Manufacturer produces engineered products and components for a range of industrial and commercial applications. The company is seeking a detail-oriented Quality Engineer to maintain high product and manufacturing standards, investigate quality issues, and support continuous improvement. In this role, you will work closely with engineering, production, and supply chain teams to identify defects, improve processes, and ensure products meet customer and regulatory requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Monitor and maintain quality standards across manufacturing processes.
  • Investigate product defects, non-conformities, and customer complaints.
  • Carry out root cause analysis and implement corrective actions.
  • Develop and maintain quality procedures, inspection plans, and technical documentation.
  • Work closely with production and engineering teams to improve manufacturing processes.
  • Conduct inspections, audits, and quality checks on products and processes.
  • Analyse quality data and identify opportunities for continuous improvement.
  • Ensure products and processes comply with relevant industry standards and regulations.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Engineering, Quality Engineering, Manufacturing, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience in quality engineering or manufacturing is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of quality control and quality assurance processes.
  • Experience with root cause analysis and corrective action techniques.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Good understanding of engineering and manufacturing processes.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.
