Café Assistant

Job Description

Costa Limited, trading as Costa Coffee, is one of the United Kingdom's leading coffeehouse chains, renowned for serving handcrafted coffee, premium beverages, and freshly prepared food in a welcoming environment. Costa Coffee is seeking friendly, enthusiastic, and customer-focused Café Assistants to join its team. In this role, you will prepare drinks, serve food, deliver exceptional customer service, and help maintain a clean, organised, and inviting café while ensuring every customer enjoys an outstanding experience.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers warmly and provide excellent customer service.
  • Prepare handcrafted coffee, tea, and other beverages to company standards.
  • Serve sandwiches, pastries, cakes, and other food items.
  • Operate tills and process customer transactions accurately.
  • Maintain cleanliness of the café, seating areas, and preparation stations.
  • Replenish stock and ensure product displays remain attractive.
  • Assist with opening, closing, and daily cleaning duties.
  • Follow food hygiene regulations, health and safety standards, and company policies.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly, positive, and professional attitude.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced café environment.
  • Good teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and public holidays.
  • Previous café, retail, or hospitality experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full barista and customer service training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 30–38 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £12.40–£13.80 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted drinks and food during shifts.
  • Employee discounts at Costa Coffee stores.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive, inclusive, and friendly working environment.
