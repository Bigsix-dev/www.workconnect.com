Nursery Deputy Manager

Job Description

Early Years Provider is committed to delivering outstanding childcare and early education that supports children's growth, learning, and development. The provider is seeking an experienced and dedicated Nursery Deputy Manager to support the Nursery Manager in leading daily operations, maintaining high care standards, and ensuring children receive exceptional early years experiences. In this leadership role, you will support staff teams, oversee quality standards, and help create a safe and inspiring nursery environment.

Responsibilities

  • Support the Nursery Manager with the daily management of nursery operations.
  • Lead, motivate, and support nursery practitioners to deliver high-quality childcare.
  • Ensure compliance with the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) framework and Ofsted requirements.
  • Monitor children's learning, development, and progress records.
  • Support staff training, mentoring, and performance development.
  • Build strong relationships with children, parents, families, and colleagues.
  • Maintain excellent safeguarding, health, safety, and hygiene standards.
  • Assist with planning, inspections, audits, and continuous improvement activities.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in a nursery leadership or senior childcare role.
  • Level 3 Early Years qualification or equivalent is required.
  • Strong knowledge of the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) framework.
  • Experience supporting and managing childcare teams.
  • Excellent leadership, communication, and organisational skills.
  • Understanding of Ofsted requirements and safeguarding procedures.
  • Ability to create a positive and nurturing environment for children.
  • Strong problem-solving and decision-making abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday nursery shifts.

Salary

  • £30,000–£38,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Professional leadership training and development opportunities.
  • Supportive early years management team.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Career progression opportunities into Nursery Manager roles.
  • Staff childcare benefits and discounts where applicable.
