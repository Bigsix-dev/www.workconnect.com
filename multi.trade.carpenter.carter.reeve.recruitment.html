Multi-Trade Carpenter

Job Description

Carter & Reeve Recruitment is recruiting a Multi-Trade Carpenter to undertake carpentry and general property maintenance work in Chelmsford and Essex. The role requires a skilled tradesperson who can deliver quality carpentry while supporting wider maintenance requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Complete carpentry repairs, installations and maintenance.
  • Install doors, frames, skirting, flooring and other timber components.
  • Carry out general multi-trade property repairs.
  • Complete refurbishment and maintenance tasks to a high standard.
  • Identify defects and provide effective repair solutions.
  • Maintain safe and professional working practices.

Requirements

  • Experienced carpenter with multi-trade abilities.
  • Strong knowledge of carpentry and property maintenance.
  • Excellent practical skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work independently and manage a varied workload.
  • Reliable and professional approach.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours based on project requirements.

Salary

  • £30,000–£40,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to work across a variety of property projects.
  • Competitive salary range.
  • Opportunity to use and develop multiple trade skills.
  • Professional working environment.
