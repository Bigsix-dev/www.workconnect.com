Electrical Engineer

Job Description

Local Engineering Firm is a specialist engineering company delivering reliable electrical solutions, technical design services, and innovative engineering projects. The firm is seeking a skilled and detail-oriented Electrical Engineer to join its team. In this role, you will design, develop, test, and maintain electrical systems while supporting engineering projects and ensuring all work meets industry standards, safety requirements, and client expectations.

Responsibilities

  • Design and develop electrical systems, circuits, and components.
  • Prepare electrical drawings, technical reports, and project documentation.
  • Carry out electrical testing, fault finding, and system improvements.
  • Support installation, commissioning, and maintenance activities.
  • Work closely with mechanical engineers, project teams, and clients.
  • Analyse electrical performance and recommend technical solutions.
  • Ensure designs comply with electrical regulations and safety standards.
  • Support continuous improvement and engineering development projects.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience working as an Electrical Engineer is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of electrical systems, circuits, and engineering principles.
  • Experience with electrical design software and technical documentation.
  • Excellent problem-solving and analytical abilities.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to manage technical projects and meet deadlines.
  • Knowledge of electrical safety regulations and industry standards.

Working Hours

  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.
