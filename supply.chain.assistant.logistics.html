Supply Chain Assistant

Job Description

Nestlé UK Limited, part of Nestlé S.A., is one of the world's largest food and beverage companies, supplying trusted brands to millions of customers across the United Kingdom. Nestlé UK is seeking a proactive and organised Supply Chain Assistant to support its supply chain operations. In this role, you will assist with inventory management, order processing, logistics coordination, and administrative activities to ensure products move efficiently through the supply chain while maintaining high standards of accuracy and customer service.

Responsibilities

  • Assist with daily supply chain and logistics operations.
  • Process purchase orders, stock movements, and delivery documentation.
  • Monitor inventory levels and support stock replenishment activities.
  • Coordinate with suppliers, warehouses, transport providers, and internal departments.
  • Maintain accurate supply chain records and update company systems.
  • Prepare reports relating to inventory, deliveries, and operational performance.
  • Support the resolution of supply chain and delivery issues.
  • Ensure compliance with company procedures, food safety standards, and health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in supply chain, logistics, administration, or warehouse operations is desirable.
  • Strong organisational and administrative skills.
  • Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Good communication and problem-solving abilities.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
  • Experience using ERP or supply chain management systems is an advantage.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a team.
  • Commitment to delivering high-quality work and meeting deadlines.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with flexibility based on operational requirements.

Salary

  • £27,000–£31,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts on selected Nestlé products.
  • Professional training and career development programmes.
  • Health and wellbeing support services.
  • Flexible working policies where applicable.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Nestlé UK.
