Distribution Assistant

Job Description

Tesco Distribution Limited, part of Tesco PLC (Public Limited Company), operates a nationwide network of distribution centres that supply Tesco stores across the United Kingdom. Tesco Distribution is seeking a reliable and hardworking Distribution Assistant to support warehouse and delivery operations. In this role, you will help manage stock movement, prepare orders, and ensure products are processed accurately and efficiently while maintaining high standards of safety and quality.

Responsibilities

  • Assist with receiving, sorting, and processing incoming stock.
  • Pick and prepare products for delivery to Tesco stores.
  • Load and unload goods safely and efficiently.
  • Operate warehouse equipment and handheld scanning devices.
  • Complete stock checks and maintain accurate inventory records.
  • Ensure products are handled carefully and prepared correctly for dispatch.
  • Maintain a clean, organised, and safe distribution centre environment.
  • Follow Tesco procedures, food safety standards, and health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous warehouse or distribution experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment.
  • Good attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to lift and move products safely.
  • Good teamwork and communication skills.
  • Ability to use handheld scanners and warehouse systems.
  • Reliable, punctual, and committed to achieving operational targets.
  • Flexible availability including weekends and overtime when required.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, nights, and weekends.

Salary

  • £12.90–£14.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts across Tesco stores and services.
  • Comprehensive training and development programmes.
  • Health and wellbeing support.
  • Modern distribution centre facilities.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Tesco Distribution.
