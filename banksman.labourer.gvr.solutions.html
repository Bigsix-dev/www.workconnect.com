Banksman / Labourer

Job Description

GVR Solutions is seeking a reliable Banksman / Labourer to support construction activities in Chelmsford. The role involves assisting with site operations, safely guiding vehicles and plant, and carrying out general labouring duties.

Responsibilities

  • Act as a banksman to safely guide vehicles and plant on site.
  • Assist with deliveries, loading and unloading materials.
  • Carry out general construction labouring duties.
  • Maintain clean, safe and organised work areas.
  • Support tradespeople and site teams when required.
  • Follow site health and safety procedures at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous construction site or labouring experience is beneficial.
  • Experience or training as a banksman is advantageous.
  • Good awareness of site safety procedures.
  • Reliable, punctual and physically capable of carrying out manual work.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a construction team.

Working Hours

  • 8am–4pm.
  • Full working schedule based on site requirements.

Salary

  • £18 per hour.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to gain further construction site experience.
  • Regular daytime working hours.
  • Practical, hands-on role within a construction environment.
  • Opportunity to work alongside experienced site professionals.
