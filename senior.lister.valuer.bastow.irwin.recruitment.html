Senior Lister / Valuer

Job Description

Bastow Irwin Recruitment is seeking an experienced Senior Lister / Valuer to provide professional property valuation and sales services. The successful candidate will develop new instructions, conduct property valuations, advise vendors on market conditions, and work towards achieving strong sales performance while delivering excellent customer service.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct accurate residential property valuations.
  • Meet with prospective vendors and provide professional market advice.
  • Generate new property listings and secure instructions.
  • Develop and maintain strong relationships with vendors and buyers.
  • Prepare property particulars and advise on appropriate marketing strategies.
  • Promote properties through suitable sales and marketing channels.
  • Conduct and support property viewings where required.
  • Manage offers and negotiate sales between buyers and vendors.
  • Monitor sales progression and maintain regular communication with clients.
  • Identify new business opportunities and contribute to branch performance.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a property valuer, lister, or senior estate agent.
  • Strong knowledge of the residential property market.
  • Excellent valuation, negotiation, and sales skills.
  • Strong business development and lead-generation abilities.
  • Excellent communication and relationship-building skills.
  • Confident advising clients on property pricing and market conditions.
  • Target-driven and commercially aware approach.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £65,000–£75,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional estate agency environment.
  • Opportunities for professional development.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Opportunity to develop senior property sales and valuation expertise.
