Infrastructure Engineer

Job Description

Technology Services Company provides technology infrastructure, systems, and support services to businesses and organisations. The company is seeking an Infrastructure Engineer to maintain and improve reliable information technology infrastructure across its operations. You will work with technical teams to manage servers, networks, cloud services, and other infrastructure while ensuring systems remain secure, available, and efficient.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain and support server, network, and information technology infrastructure.
  • Monitor infrastructure performance, availability, and capacity.
  • Install, configure, and maintain servers and infrastructure systems.
  • Support cloud-based and on-premises infrastructure environments.
  • Investigate and resolve infrastructure faults and technical incidents.
  • Implement system updates, patches, backups, and maintenance activities.
  • Support infrastructure security and access management requirements.
  • Contribute to infrastructure upgrades, migrations, and improvement projects.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Information Technology, Computer Science, Infrastructure Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience supporting information technology infrastructure is desirable.
  • Good understanding of servers, networks, operating systems, and cloud technologies.
  • Experience with infrastructure monitoring and troubleshooting.
  • Knowledge of backup, security, patching, and system maintenance practices.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to manage technical tasks and prioritise incidents effectively.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £40,000–£60,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive technology infrastructure salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Infrastructure and cloud technology training.
  • Professional certification and development opportunities.
  • Access to modern technology platforms and systems.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
