Executive Assistant

Job Description

Essex Police is one of the largest police forces in England, responsible for protecting communities, preventing crime, and delivering professional public services across Essex. Essex Police is seeking an organised and highly professional Executive Assistant to provide dedicated administrative support to senior leaders and management teams. In this role, you will manage complex schedules, coordinate meetings, handle confidential information, and support effective decision-making through efficient administrative services.

Responsibilities

  • Provide high-level administrative support to senior officers and leadership teams.
  • Manage diaries, appointments, meetings, and important schedules.
  • Prepare reports, presentations, meeting documents, and correspondence.
  • Organise meetings, record minutes, and track follow-up actions.
  • Handle confidential and sensitive information professionally.
  • Coordinate communications with internal departments and external partners.
  • Support administrative projects and organisational priorities.
  • Maintain accurate records while following police policies and data protection requirements.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as an Executive Assistant, Personal Assistant, or senior administrator.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • Ability to manage confidential information with professionalism.
  • Advanced knowledge of Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to work independently and manage competing priorities.
  • Professional approach with excellent stakeholder management skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £30,000–£35,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive public sector salary.
  • Local government pension scheme.
  • Generous annual leave allowance.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support services.
  • Flexible working opportunities where available.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Opportunities for career progression within Essex Police.
