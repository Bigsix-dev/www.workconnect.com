Maintenance Engineer

Job Description

Industrial Services Company provides specialist engineering solutions, maintenance services, and technical support to industrial clients. The company is seeking a skilled and reliable Maintenance Engineer to ensure the smooth operation of machinery, equipment, and production systems. In this role, you will carry out preventative maintenance, diagnose technical faults, and support continuous improvement across industrial operations.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out planned and reactive maintenance on industrial equipment and machinery.
  • Diagnose mechanical and electrical faults and implement effective repairs.
  • Perform preventative maintenance activities to minimise equipment downtime.
  • Inspect machinery and identify potential performance issues.
  • Maintain accurate maintenance records and service reports.
  • Support installation, upgrades, and improvement projects.
  • Work with production teams to resolve technical problems quickly.
  • Ensure all maintenance activities comply with health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Engineering qualification in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
  • Previous experience as a Maintenance Engineer or similar role.
  • Strong knowledge of industrial machinery and maintenance processes.
  • Ability to troubleshoot mechanical and electrical faults.
  • Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Good understanding of health and safety procedures.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of an engineering team.
  • Strong attention to detail and technical skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

Benefits

  • Supportive industrial engineering environment.
