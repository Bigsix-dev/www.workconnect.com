Hospitality Team Member

Job Description

Cosy Club is seeking enthusiastic Hospitality Team Members to deliver outstanding food and drink service while creating a welcoming atmosphere for guests. This role involves supporting restaurant operations, maintaining high service standards, and working effectively within a team.

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide excellent customer service throughout every shift.
  • Take food and drink orders accurately.
  • Serve meals and beverages promptly.
  • Prepare dining areas before and after service.
  • Maintain cleanliness and food safety standards.
  • Support colleagues during busy periods.

Requirements

  • Positive attitude and willingness to learn.
  • Excellent communication skills.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment.
  • Previous hospitality experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Flexible availability.

Working Hours

  • Full-time.
  • 30–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings and weekends.

Salary & Benefits

  • £12.40–£13.90 per hour.
  • Staff meals during shifts.
  • Employee discounts.
  • Training and promotion opportunities.
  • Pension scheme.
