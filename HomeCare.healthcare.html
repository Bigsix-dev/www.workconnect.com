Home Care Assistant

Job Description

Brisca Healthcare is looking for compassionate and dependable Home Care Assistants to provide high-quality care and support to individuals in their own homes. The role focuses on helping clients maintain their independence while ensuring their safety, comfort, and wellbeing.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist clients with personal care, including washing, dressing, and grooming.
  • Support with mobility and transfers where required.
  • Prepare meals and assist with eating and drinking if needed.
  • Administer or prompt medication according to care plans.
  • Carry out light household duties such as cleaning, laundry, and shopping.
  • Provide companionship and emotional support to reduce loneliness.
  • Accompany clients to appointments or community activities when necessary.
  • Monitor clients' wellbeing and report any concerns to supervisors.
  • Maintain accurate care records and follow safeguarding procedures.
  • Deliver care with dignity, respect, and professionalism at all times.

About You

The ideal candidate should:

  • Have a caring and patient nature with excellent communication skills.
  • Be reliable, trustworthy, and able to work independently.
  • Be willing to work flexible shifts, including weekends if required.
  • Have the right to work in the UK.
  • Hold previous care experience (preferred but often not essential, as training may be provided).
  • Be committed to promoting independence and person-centred care.
  • Possess a valid driving licence and access to a vehicle if travelling between clients is required.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay of £12.50–£15.00.
  • Full-time, stable employment.
  • Paid training and ongoing professional development.
  • Opportunities for career progression within the care sector.
  • Flexible scheduling and supportive management.
  • Holiday entitlement and workplace pension (subject to eligibility).
  • Uniform and personal protective equipment (PPE) provided.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing support programmes where available.
  • The opportunity to make a meaningful difference in people's daily lives.
