Multi-Drop Delivery Driver

Job Description

Evri Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading parcel delivery companies, delivering millions of parcels to homes and businesses every year. Evri is seeking a reliable and motivated Multi-Drop Delivery Driver to join its logistics team. In this role, you will complete multiple parcel deliveries and collections on a designated route while providing excellent customer service and ensuring deliveries are completed safely, accurately, and on schedule.

Responsibilities

  • Deliver and collect parcels from residential and commercial customers.
  • Plan and complete daily multi-drop delivery routes efficiently.
  • Load, organise, and secure parcels before leaving the depot.
  • Use handheld delivery devices to record deliveries and obtain proof of delivery.
  • Carry out daily vehicle safety inspections and report any defects.
  • Provide friendly and professional customer service during every delivery.
  • Ensure parcels are handled carefully to prevent damage.
  • Comply with company procedures, UK road traffic laws, and health and safety regulations.

Requirements

  • Valid UK Category B driving licence.
  • Good driving record with a strong commitment to road safety.
  • Ability to lift and carry parcels of various sizes and weights.
  • Excellent time management and route planning skills.
  • Strong communication and customer service abilities.
  • Comfortable using GPS navigation systems and handheld delivery devices.
  • Ability to work independently in a fast-paced environment.
  • Previous parcel delivery or courier experience is desirable but not essential.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 38–45 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including weekends and peak seasonal periods.

Salary

  • £13.00–£15.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Comprehensive training and ongoing support.
  • Employee discounts and reward programmes.
  • Health and wellbeing support.
  • Modern delivery technology and equipment.
  • Career progression opportunities within Evri.
