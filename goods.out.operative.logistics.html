Goods Out Operative

Job Description

Screwfix Direct Limited, part of Kingfisher PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's leading retailers of trade tools, hardware, and home improvement products. Screwfix is seeking a dependable and organised Goods Out Operative to join its warehouse team. In this role, you will prepare customer orders for dispatch, ensure outgoing shipments are accurate, and support efficient warehouse operations while maintaining high standards of safety and quality.

Responsibilities

  • Pick, pack, and prepare customer orders for dispatch.
  • Verify products, quantities, and shipping documentation before goods leave the warehouse.
  • Load delivery vehicles safely and efficiently.
  • Operate handheld scanners and warehouse management systems.
  • Inspect outgoing goods to ensure quality and accuracy.
  • Report damaged or incorrect products to supervisors promptly.
  • Maintain a clean, organised, and safe goods-out area.
  • Follow company procedures and health and safety regulations at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous warehouse or dispatch experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Good attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to lift and move goods safely.
  • Experience using handheld scanners or warehouse systems is an advantage.
  • Strong teamwork and communication skills.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced warehouse environment.
  • Reliable, punctual, and committed to achieving daily targets.
  • Flexible availability, including overtime when required.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, and occasional weekends.

Salary

  • £12.70–£14.20 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts across Screwfix and Kingfisher brands.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Health and wellbeing support programmes.
  • Modern warehouse facilities and equipment.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression within Screwfix.
