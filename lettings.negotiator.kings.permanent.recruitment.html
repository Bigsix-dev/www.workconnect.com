Lettings Negotiator

Job Description

Kings Permanent Recruitment is seeking a motivated Lettings Negotiator to support landlords and tenants throughout the property letting process. The successful candidate will generate and manage lettings opportunities, conduct property viewings, communicate with prospective tenants, and help ensure properties are successfully let while delivering excellent customer service.

Responsibilities

  • Respond to enquiries from prospective tenants and landlords.
  • Arrange and conduct property viewings.
  • Promote available properties through appropriate channels.
  • Qualify prospective tenants and understand their property requirements.
  • Maintain regular communication with landlords and applicants.
  • Negotiate offers and support the progression of successful applications.
  • Maintain accurate property and applicant records.
  • Assist with tenancy administration and documentation.
  • Build strong relationships with landlords, tenants, and other stakeholders.
  • Work towards agreed lettings and business development targets.

Requirements

  • Previous lettings, estate agency, or sales experience is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and negotiation skills.
  • Strong customer service and relationship-building abilities.
  • Confident conducting property viewings and dealing with prospective tenants.
  • Commercially aware and target-driven.
  • Strong organisational and administrative skills.
  • Professional and confident telephone manner.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced property environment.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £26,000–£35,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional estate agency environment.
  • Training and development opportunities.
  • Opportunities to develop property sales and lettings expertise.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
