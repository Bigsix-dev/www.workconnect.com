School Administrator

Job Description

Essex County Council supports schools across Essex by providing high-quality educational services and administrative support that help create positive learning environments for pupils. Essex County Council is seeking a professional and organised School Administrator to support the daily administrative operations of a school. In this role, you will manage student records, communicate with parents and staff, coordinate office activities, and ensure the smooth running of the school's administrative functions.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to the Headteacher, teaching staff, and school leadership team.
  • Manage student records, attendance registers, and admissions documentation.
  • Handle telephone calls, emails, and enquiries from parents, visitors, and external organisations.
  • Prepare letters, reports, newsletters, and other school correspondence.
  • Schedule meetings, appointments, and school events.
  • Maintain accurate filing systems and confidential records.
  • Order office supplies and assist with general office management duties.
  • Ensure compliance with school policies, safeguarding procedures, GDPR, and Essex County Council guidelines.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in school administration, office administration, or a similar administrative role is desirable.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Experience using school management systems such as SIMS or Arbor is an advantage.
  • Ability to maintain confidentiality and handle sensitive information professionally.
  • Friendly, approachable, and able to work effectively with pupils, parents, and staff.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during normal school operating hours.

Salary

  • £24,000–£29,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Local Government Pension Scheme.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Opportunities to contribute to children's education and development.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Essex County Council.
