Dispatch Operative

Job Description

Brakes, part of Sysco GB, is one of the United Kingdom's leading foodservice wholesalers, supplying high-quality food products to restaurants, hotels, schools, healthcare providers, and catering businesses nationwide. Brakes is seeking a dependable and organised Dispatch Operative to join its warehouse team. In this role, you will prepare customer orders for dispatch, ensure deliveries are loaded accurately, and help maintain efficient warehouse operations while meeting strict food safety and quality standards.

Responsibilities

  • Prepare and dispatch customer orders accurately and on time.
  • Load delivery vehicles safely according to dispatch schedules.
  • Verify products, quantities, and documentation before dispatch.
  • Operate handheld scanners and warehouse management systems.
  • Assist with stock movement and inventory control when required.
  • Report damaged, missing, or incorrect products promptly.
  • Maintain a clean, organised, and safe dispatch area.
  • Follow company procedures, food safety standards, and health and safety regulations at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous warehouse or dispatch experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Good attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to lift and move goods safely throughout the working day.
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced warehouse environment.
  • Basic computer skills and experience using handheld scanners is an advantage.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Reliable, punctual, and committed to meeting daily performance targets.
  • Flexible availability for shift work, including weekends when required.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including early mornings, evenings, and occasional weekends.

Salary

  • £13.00–£14.75 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Comprehensive training and development opportunities.
  • Employee discounts and reward programmes.
  • Health and wellbeing support services.
  • Modern warehouse facilities and equipment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Brakes and Sysco GB.
