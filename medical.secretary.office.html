Medical Secretary

Job Description

Mid and South Essex NHS Foundation Trust is one of the largest NHS healthcare providers in England, delivering high-quality hospital and community healthcare services across Essex. The Trust is seeking a highly organised and professional Medical Secretary to provide comprehensive administrative and secretarial support to consultants and clinical teams. In this role, you will prepare medical correspondence, manage patient records, coordinate appointments, and ensure the smooth operation of clinical administration while maintaining strict patient confidentiality.

Responsibilities

  • Provide secretarial and administrative support to consultants and clinical staff.
  • Prepare, type, and proofread medical letters, reports, and clinical documents.
  • Manage consultant diaries, clinics, meetings, and patient appointments.
  • Maintain accurate patient records using NHS electronic systems.
  • Handle telephone enquiries from patients, healthcare professionals, and external organisations.
  • Process referrals, test results, and clinical correspondence efficiently.
  • Organise medical records and ensure documentation is complete and up to date.
  • Maintain confidentiality while complying with NHS policies, GDPR, and information governance standards.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Medical Secretary, NHS Administrator, or healthcare administrator is desirable.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office applications, particularly Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Experience using NHS systems such as PAS, EPR, or similar healthcare software is an advantage.
  • Ability to manage confidential patient information professionally.
  • Strong teamwork skills with a compassionate and patient-focused approach.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with standard NHS working hours.

Salary

  • £24,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive NHS salary package.
  • NHS Pension Scheme.
  • Generous paid annual leave plus public holidays.
  • Access to NHS training and career development programmes.
  • Employee wellbeing and mental health support services.
  • NHS staff discounts and benefits scheme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Mid and South Essex NHS Foundation Trust.
