Education Administrator

Job Description

Chelmsford College is a leading further education provider committed to delivering high-quality learning opportunities and supporting students to achieve their academic and career goals. Chelmsford College is seeking an organised and efficient Education Administrator to provide administrative support across academic departments. In this role, you will manage student records, coordinate educational activities, support staff, and ensure effective administration of college services.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to academic teams and college departments.
  • Maintain accurate student records, databases, and documentation.
  • Support enrolment processes, course administration, and student services.
  • Prepare reports, spreadsheets, letters, and educational documents.
  • Coordinate meetings, appointments, and departmental activities.
  • Respond to student, staff, and external enquiries professionally.
  • Update internal systems and maintain accurate information records.
  • Ensure compliance with college policies, data protection, and administrative procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in education administration, office administration, or a similar role.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • Ability to manage confidential student information.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Strong attention to detail and accurate record-keeping skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Experience working within an educational environment is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard college working hours.

Salary

  • £26,000–£32,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme or workplace pension options.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
  • Supportive and inclusive college environment.
  • Opportunities for career progression within education administration.
  • Access to staff benefits and college facilities.
