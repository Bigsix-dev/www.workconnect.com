Senior Systems Engineer

Job Description

Defence Technology Company develops advanced engineering and technology systems for specialist applications. The company is seeking an experienced Senior Systems Engineer to lead the development, integration, testing, and improvement of complex technical systems. In this role, you will work with multidisciplinary engineering teams to define system requirements, manage technical interfaces, solve complex engineering problems, and ensure systems meet demanding performance and reliability requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Lead the engineering development and integration of complex technical systems.
  • Define, analyse, and manage system requirements throughout the project lifecycle.
  • Develop system architectures, specifications, and technical documentation.
  • Coordinate technical activities across multidisciplinary engineering teams.
  • Support system integration, verification, validation, and testing activities.
  • Identify technical risks and develop appropriate mitigation strategies.
  • Investigate complex system issues and recommend effective engineering solutions.
  • Provide technical leadership and guidance to engineers and project teams.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Systems Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Significant experience in systems engineering or complex engineering projects.
  • Strong understanding of systems engineering principles and lifecycle processes.
  • Experience with requirements management, system integration, and verification.
  • Strong analytical and technical problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to manage complex technical requirements and interfaces.
  • Excellent technical documentation and communication skills.
  • Strong leadership and multidisciplinary teamwork abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £55,000–£75,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive senior engineering salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Advanced systems engineering training.
  • Opportunities to work on complex technology projects.
  • Professional engineering development opportunities.
  • Career progression into senior technical and leadership roles.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
