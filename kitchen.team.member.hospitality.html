Kitchen Team Member

Job Description

Nando's Chickenland Limited, trading as Nando's, is one of the United Kingdom's most popular restaurant chains, renowned for its flame-grilled PERi-PERi chicken and exceptional customer service. Nando's is seeking an enthusiastic and hardworking Kitchen Team Member to join its restaurant team. In this role, you will prepare fresh ingredients, cook menu items to company standards, maintain excellent food hygiene, and work closely with colleagues to ensure every customer enjoys a high-quality dining experience.

Responsibilities

  • Prepare fresh ingredients and menu items following Nando's recipes and standards.
  • Cook food safely while maintaining consistent quality and presentation.
  • Maintain cleanliness of kitchen equipment, workstations, and food preparation areas.
  • Monitor food stock levels and assist with stock rotation and storage.
  • Work efficiently with the front-of-house team to ensure prompt service.
  • Follow food hygiene, health and safety, and company procedures at all times.
  • Support opening and closing kitchen duties.
  • Contribute to a positive, safe, and team-oriented working environment.

Requirements

  • Previous kitchen or hospitality experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced kitchen environment.
  • Strong teamwork and communication skills.
  • Excellent attention to detail and commitment to food quality.
  • Willingness to learn and follow company procedures.
  • Good organisational and time management skills.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • A positive attitude and passion for delivering excellent food.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £12.40–£13.80 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free meals during shifts and generous employee discounts.
  • Comprehensive training and career development programmes.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive, diverse, and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression within Nando's.
