Service Advisor

Job Description

Auto Skills UK is seeking a professional Service Advisor to deliver excellent customer service while coordinating vehicle servicing and repair activities. You will act as the link between customers and the workshop, ensuring customer requirements are understood, work is scheduled correctly, and customers receive clear updates throughout the service process.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and manage vehicle service and repair bookings.
  • Discuss customer concerns and accurately record required work.
  • Prepare service estimates and explain recommended repairs and maintenance.
  • Coordinate with technicians and workshop staff to manage vehicle work.
  • Keep customers informed about progress, costs, and completion times.
  • Obtain approval for additional work where necessary.
  • Process job cards, invoices, and other relevant documentation.
  • Maintain positive customer relationships and support high service standards.

Requirements

  • Previous automotive service advisor or customer service experience is desirable.
  • Good knowledge of vehicle servicing and repair procedures.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong organisational and administrative abilities.
  • Good attention to detail and ability to manage competing priorities.
  • Confident explaining technical information and costs to customers.
  • Good computer skills and accurate record-keeping abilities.
  • Professional, reliable, and customer-focused approach.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • No weekend working required.
  • Standard weekday working schedule.

Salary

  • £28,600 per year.
  • Bonus opportunity available.

Benefits

  • Competitive basic salary with bonus opportunity.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Automotive service and customer care training.
  • Monday-to-Friday working environment.
  • Professional development opportunities.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
