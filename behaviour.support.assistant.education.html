Behaviour Support Assistant

Job Description

Local Academy Trust is committed to providing inclusive, high-quality education where every student is supported to achieve academic success and personal growth. The trust is seeking a compassionate and proactive Behaviour Support Assistant to help students develop positive behaviour, improve engagement in learning, and overcome barriers to education. In this role, you will work closely with teaching staff, pastoral teams, and families to create a safe and supportive learning environment.

Responsibilities

  • Provide one-to-one and small-group behaviour support for students.
  • Implement behaviour management strategies in line with school policies.
  • Support students in developing positive social, emotional, and behavioural skills.
  • Work with teachers and pastoral staff to improve student engagement and attendance.
  • Monitor student behaviour and maintain accurate support records.
  • Build positive relationships with students, parents, and carers.
  • Assist in resolving conflicts and promoting positive behaviour throughout the school.
  • Promote safeguarding, inclusion, and student wellbeing at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous experience supporting children or young people in an educational, youth work, or care setting.
  • Strong understanding of behaviour management techniques.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Patient, resilient, and supportive approach.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with students from diverse backgrounds.
  • Knowledge of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Good organisational and record-keeping skills.
  • Relevant qualification in education, youth work, or behaviour support is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £24,000–£29,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Local Government Pension Scheme.
  • School holiday periods and paid leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
  • Supportive and collaborative school environment.
  • Opportunities for career progression within education.
  • Rewarding role helping students achieve positive outcomes.
