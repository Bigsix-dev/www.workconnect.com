Building Surveyor

Job Description

Braintree District Council is seeking a professional Building Surveyor to provide technical surveying expertise across its property portfolio. The role involves inspecting buildings, identifying defects, managing maintenance and improvement works, preparing reports, and supporting the delivery of high-quality property services. The successful candidate will work with internal teams, contractors, and stakeholders to ensure buildings remain safe, compliant, and well maintained.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out detailed building inspections and condition surveys.
  • Identify defects, maintenance requirements, and potential building risks.
  • Prepare technical reports, specifications, and recommendations.
  • Manage building repair, refurbishment, and improvement projects.
  • Monitor contractors and assess the quality of completed works.
  • Provide professional building surveying advice to colleagues and stakeholders.
  • Support compliance with relevant building regulations, standards, and health and safety requirements.
  • Maintain accurate property records and project documentation.

Requirements

  • Relevant qualification or professional experience in building surveying.
  • Strong knowledge of building construction, defects, and maintenance.
  • Experience preparing technical reports and specifications.
  • Good understanding of property legislation and building standards.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to manage projects, contractors, and competing priorities.
  • Professional, organised, and detail-focused approach.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Standard professional working hours.
  • Site visits and inspections will form part of the role.

Salary

  • £48,945–£53,082 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive local government salary.
  • Public-sector pension arrangements.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Professional development opportunities.
  • Opportunities for specialist training.
  • Stable public-sector employment.
  • Supportive professional working environment.
