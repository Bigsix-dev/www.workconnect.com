Property Administrator

Job Description

Savills PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the world's leading property and real estate services providers, offering expertise in residential, commercial, rural, and investment property. Savills is seeking a highly organised and proactive Property Administrator to support its property management team. In this role, you will coordinate administrative activities, maintain property records, assist with tenancy documentation, and ensure the smooth day-to-day operation of property management services while delivering excellent customer support.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative support to property managers and estate teams.
  • Maintain accurate property, tenancy, and client records.
  • Prepare tenancy agreements, reports, correspondence, and property documentation.
  • Coordinate property inspections, maintenance appointments, and contractor visits.
  • Respond to enquiries from landlords, tenants, contractors, and clients.
  • Process invoices, purchase orders, and property-related documentation.
  • Maintain electronic filing systems and update property management databases.
  • Ensure compliance with company procedures, property legislation, and data protection regulations.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in property administration, real estate administration, or office administration.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Experience using property management software is desirable.
  • Strong customer service and problem-solving skills.
  • Professional attitude with the ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during standard business hours.

Salary

  • £27,000–£34,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Generous paid annual leave.

  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Flexible working opportunities where applicable.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Savills.
