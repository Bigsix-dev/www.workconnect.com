Duty Manager

Job Description

Premier Inn, part of Whitbread PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's largest and most trusted hotel brands, providing comfortable accommodation and excellent guest experiences nationwide. Premier Inn is seeking an experienced and motivated Duty Manager to support the daily operation of the hotel. In this role, you will lead hotel teams, support guest services, manage operational standards, and ensure every guest receives a welcoming and high-quality stay.

Responsibilities

  • Support the General Manager in managing daily hotel operations.
  • Lead, motivate, and support hotel team members across different departments.
  • Ensure guests receive excellent customer service throughout their stay.
  • Handle guest enquiries, feedback, and complaints professionally.
  • Monitor hotel standards including cleanliness, safety, and service quality.
  • Support reception, housekeeping, and food service operations when required.
  • Complete operational checks, reports, and administrative tasks.
  • Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations and company procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in a hotel, hospitality, or customer service management role.
  • Strong leadership and team management skills.
  • Excellent communication and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong organisational and decision-making skills.
  • Commitment to delivering outstanding guest experiences.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Experience with hotel systems and operations is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £16.00–£19.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee discounts on Premier Inn stays and Whitbread brands.
  • Comprehensive leadership training and development programmes.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Excellent opportunities for career progression within the hospitality industry.
