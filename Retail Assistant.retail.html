Retail Assistant

Job Description

Marks & Spencer (M&S) is one of the United Kingdom's leading retailers, renowned for its high-quality clothing, food, home products, and exceptional customer service. As a Retail Assistant, you will play a key role in delivering an outstanding shopping experience by assisting customers, maintaining attractive product displays, replenishing stock, and supporting daily store operations. This position is ideal for individuals who are friendly, reliable, and enjoy working in a fast-paced retail environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide friendly, professional assistance.
  • Process purchases, refunds, and exchanges accurately using the till system.
  • Replenish shelves and ensure products are displayed neatly.
  • Monitor stock levels and report shortages to supervisors.
  • Keep the sales floor, fitting rooms, and stock areas clean and organised.
  • Assist with pricing updates, promotions, and seasonal merchandising.
  • Handle customer enquiries and resolve issues professionally.
  • Follow company policies on health, safety, and loss prevention.
  • Work collaboratively with colleagues to achieve store sales targets.

Requirements

  • No previous retail experience is required; training is provided.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Friendly, approachable, and customer-focused attitude.
  • Ability to work flexible shifts, including weekends and public holidays.
  • Physically able to stand for extended periods and lift light to moderate stock.
  • Right to work in the UK (visa sponsorship availability depends on the specific vacancy).

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay with opportunities for annual pay reviews.
  • Employee discount on selected M&S products.
  • Paid holiday entitlement.
  • Pension scheme.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Performance-related recognition and reward schemes.
  • Flexible shift patterns in selected stores.
