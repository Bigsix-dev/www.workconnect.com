Hospitality Assistant

Job Description

Compass Group UK is one of the United Kingdom's leading food service and hospitality providers, delivering exceptional catering and hospitality services across corporate offices, healthcare facilities, education, sports venues, and major events. Compass Group UK is seeking a professional, enthusiastic, and customer-focused Hospitality Assistant to join its team. In this role, you will provide outstanding food and beverage service, assist with hospitality events, and ensure guests receive a first-class experience while maintaining the highest standards of cleanliness and customer care.

Responsibilities

  • Provide excellent customer service to guests and clients.
  • Prepare, serve, and replenish food and beverages.
  • Set up meeting rooms, hospitality suites, and event areas.
  • Maintain cleanliness of dining areas, service stations, and hospitality facilities.
  • Operate tills and process customer transactions accurately where required.
  • Assist with conferences, corporate functions, and special events.
  • Receive deliveries and restock food, beverages, and hospitality supplies.
  • Follow food hygiene regulations, health and safety standards, and company procedures at all times.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and customer service skills.
  • Friendly, professional, and positive attitude.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced hospitality environment.
  • Strong teamwork and organisational skills.
  • Good attention to detail and presentation.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays where required.
  • Previous hospitality or catering experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts depending on business and event requirements.

Salary

  • £12.40–£13.80 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted meals during shifts where applicable.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Employee discounts through company benefit schemes.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment with opportunities for career progression.
