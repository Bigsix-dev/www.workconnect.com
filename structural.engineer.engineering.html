Structural Engineer

Job Description

Structural Engineering Consultancy provides specialist structural design, analysis, and engineering solutions for construction and infrastructure projects. The consultancy is seeking a skilled and detail-oriented Structural Engineer to support the design, assessment, and delivery of safe and efficient structures. In this role, you will carry out structural calculations, prepare technical documentation, and work with project teams to deliver high-quality engineering solutions.

Responsibilities

  • Design and analyse structural systems for buildings and infrastructure projects.
  • Prepare structural calculations, drawings, reports, and technical specifications.
  • Carry out structural assessments, inspections, and surveys.
  • Use engineering software to model and analyse structural designs.
  • Collaborate with architects, civil engineers, contractors, and project teams.
  • Ensure designs comply with building regulations and engineering standards.
  • Provide technical advice and support throughout project delivery.
  • Identify structural issues and develop effective engineering solutions.

Requirements

  • Degree in Structural Engineering, Civil Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience working as a Structural Engineer is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of structural design principles and construction methods.
  • Experience using structural analysis and design software.
  • Excellent technical, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to manage multiple engineering projects and deadlines.
  • Good communication and teamwork abilities.

Working Hours

  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

Benefits

  • Opportunity to work on challenging structural projects.
  • Supportive consultancy environment.
