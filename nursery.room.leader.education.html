Nursery Room Leader

Job Description

Local Nursery Provider is dedicated to creating a safe, caring, and stimulating environment where young children can learn, develop, and build confidence. The nursery is seeking an experienced and passionate Nursery Room Leader to manage a childcare room, lead a team of practitioners, and provide high-quality early years education. In this role, you will support children's development, ensure outstanding care standards, and create engaging learning experiences based on the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) framework.

Responsibilities

  • Lead the daily operation of a nursery room and supervise childcare practitioners.
  • Plan and deliver engaging activities that support children's learning and development.
  • Ensure care and education practices follow the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) framework.
  • Monitor children's progress and maintain accurate development records.
  • Build positive relationships with children, parents, and families.
  • Support staff training, mentoring, and professional development.
  • Maintain high standards of safeguarding, health, safety, and hygiene.
  • Manage resources, routines, and the daily organisation of the nursery room.

Requirements

  • Previous experience working as a Nursery Room Leader, Senior Nursery Practitioner, or similar role.
  • Level 3 Early Years qualification or equivalent is required.
  • Strong knowledge of the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) framework.
  • Excellent leadership and communication skills.
  • Ability to support and motivate a team of childcare professionals.
  • Strong understanding of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Ability to create engaging learning activities for young children.
  • Excellent organisational and problem-solving skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday nursery shifts.

Salary

  • £28,000–£34,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Professional childcare training and development opportunities.
  • Supportive nursery team environment.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Career progression opportunities within early years education.
  • Staff discounts and childcare benefits where applicable.
