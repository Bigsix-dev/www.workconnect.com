Care Team Leader

Job Overview

Admirals Reach Care Home is seeking a compassionate and experienced Care Team Leader to oversee the delivery of high-quality care and provide day-to-day leadership to care staff. This full-time role is ideal for an individual with strong organisational skills, a commitment to person-centred care, and the ability to motivate and support a team in a residential care environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead and supervise care staff during daily shifts, ensuring excellent standards of care.
  • Support residents with their personal care needs while promoting dignity, independence, and wellbeing.
  • Develop and maintain personalised care plans in collaboration with residents, families, and healthcare professionals.
  • Administer or oversee medication in accordance with company policies and regulatory requirements.
  • Monitor residents’ health and promptly report any changes or concerns.
  • Mentor, train, and support new and existing team members to maintain high-quality care standards.
  • Ensure accurate record-keeping and compliance with safeguarding, health and safety, and care regulations.
  • Liaise effectively with management, medical professionals, and residents’ families to ensure coordinated care.
  • Foster a positive, respectful, and supportive environment for both residents and staff.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in a senior care or team leader role within a care home or healthcare setting.
  • Strong leadership, communication, and interpersonal skills.
  • A good understanding of safeguarding procedures and person-centred care practices.
  • NVQ Level 3 in Health and Social Care (or equivalent) is typically preferred.
  • Ability to work collaboratively and make informed decisions under pressure.
  • Flexibility to work shifts, including weekends and holidays when required.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary of £28,000–£35,000.
  • Full-time, stable employment with opportunities for career progression.
  • Ongoing professional training and development.
  • Supportive management team and collaborative working environment.
  • Paid annual leave and workplace pension scheme.
  • Employee wellbeing initiatives and recognition programmes.

Why Join Admirals Reach Care Home?

Joining Admirals Reach Care Home means becoming part of a dedicated team focused on delivering compassionate, high-quality care. As a Care Team Leader, you will have the opportunity to make a meaningful impact on residents’ lives while developing your leadership skills in a rewarding and supportive environment.
