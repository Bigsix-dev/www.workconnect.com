Design Engineer

Job Description

Manufacturing Company Chelmsford is a leading manufacturer focused on delivering high-quality products through innovative engineering and advanced manufacturing processes. The company is seeking a creative and technically skilled Design Engineer to develop product designs, improve manufacturing solutions, and support engineering projects from concept through to production. In this role, you will work with engineering teams to create efficient designs that meet customer requirements and industry standards.

Responsibilities

  • Create detailed engineering designs, drawings, and technical specifications.
  • Develop and improve products through the design and testing process.
  • Use computer-aided design (CAD) software to produce accurate models and drawings.
  • Collaborate with manufacturing, production, and engineering teams.
  • Review designs to ensure quality, cost-effectiveness, and manufacturability.
  • Support prototype development, testing, and product improvements.
  • Identify design issues and provide practical engineering solutions.
  • Maintain accurate design documentation and engineering records.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Product Design, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience as a Design Engineer or similar engineering role is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of mechanical design and manufacturing processes.
  • Experience using CAD software and engineering design tools.
  • Excellent attention to detail and technical problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to interpret engineering drawings and specifications.
  • Strong communication and teamwork abilities.
  • Ability to manage multiple design projects and deadlines.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £35,000–£50,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive engineering salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Professional engineering training and development opportunities.
  • Access to advanced design and manufacturing technology.
  • Supportive engineering and production teams.
  • Career progression opportunities within manufacturing.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
