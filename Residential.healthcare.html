Residential Care Assistant

Job Description

As a Residential Care Assistant, you will provide compassionate, person-centred care to residents in a care home setting. The role focuses on supporting older adults or vulnerable individuals with their daily needs while promoting dignity, independence, and overall wellbeing. You will work closely with nurses, senior carers, and other healthcare professionals to ensure residents receive high-quality care in a safe and comfortable environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist residents with personal care, including washing, dressing, grooming, and toileting.
  • Support individuals with mobility and the safe use of mobility aids.
  • Help residents during mealtimes and encourage proper nutrition and hydration.
  • Administer or assist with medication where trained and authorised.
  • Monitor residents' health and wellbeing and report any concerns to senior staff.
  • Build positive relationships with residents, families, and colleagues.
  • Maintain accurate care records and complete daily documentation.
  • Encourage participation in social and recreational activities.
  • Ensure residents live in a clean, safe, and welcoming environment.
  • Follow safeguarding procedures, infection prevention guidelines, and health and safety policies.

About You

The ideal candidate will:

  • Have a caring, patient, and respectful attitude.
  • Possess excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Be reliable, trustworthy, and able to work as part of a team.
  • Be willing to work flexible shifts, including weekends and holidays when required.
  • Have previous care experience (preferred but not always essential, as training is often provided).
  • Hold or be willing to work towards a relevant Health and Social Care qualification.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay of £12.50–£14.50.
  • Full-time, stable employment with regular shifts.
  • Paid induction and ongoing training opportunities.
  • Career progression into Senior Care Assistant or other healthcare roles.
  • Holiday entitlement and workplace pension.
  • Supportive team environment and mentoring.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programmes (where available).
  • Uniform provided by the employer.
  • Opportunities for additional overtime and enhanced pay for certain shifts.
