Field Engineering Technician

Job Description

Technical Services Provider delivers installation, inspection, maintenance, and technical support services for engineering equipment and systems. The company is seeking a Field Engineering Technician to provide hands-on technical support at customer and operational sites. You will assist with equipment installation, testing, maintenance, troubleshooting, and repairs while ensuring all work is completed safely and to a high technical standard.

Responsibilities

  • Install, inspect, test, and maintain engineering equipment at customer sites.
  • Assist engineers with commissioning and technical service activities.
  • Diagnose equipment faults and carry out appropriate repairs.
  • Perform routine maintenance and equipment inspections.
  • Use technical tools and test equipment to identify faults and verify system performance.
  • Complete service reports, inspection records, and technical documentation.
  • Follow health and safety procedures when working on customer and operational sites.
  • Provide professional technical support and assistance to customers.

Requirements

  • Technical or engineering qualification, apprenticeship, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Previous experience in field service, engineering maintenance, or technical support is desirable.
  • Good understanding of engineering equipment and maintenance procedures.
  • Basic electrical, mechanical, or electronic troubleshooting skills.
  • Ability to read technical drawings, manuals, and equipment documentation.
  • Strong practical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Good communication and customer service skills.
  • Ability to travel to customer sites and work independently when required.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £30,000–£45,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive technical engineering salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Hands-on engineering and technical training.
  • Travel support for field-based assignments.
  • Opportunities to gain experience with different engineering systems.
  • Career progression and skills development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
