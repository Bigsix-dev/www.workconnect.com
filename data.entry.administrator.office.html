Data Entry Administrator

Job Description

Adecco UK Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading recruitment and workforce solutions providers, connecting businesses with skilled professionals across multiple industries. Adecco UK is seeking a detail-focused and reliable Data Entry Administrator to support accurate information management and administrative operations. In this role, you will input and maintain data records, process documents, update databases, and provide essential administrative support to ensure business information remains accurate and organised.

Responsibilities

  • Accurately enter and update information into company databases and systems.
  • Review documents and verify data for accuracy and completeness.
  • Maintain organised electronic and physical filing systems.
  • Process administrative documents and records efficiently.
  • Identify and correct data errors or inconsistencies.
  • Prepare reports and spreadsheets using collected information.
  • Support wider administrative teams with daily office tasks.
  • Maintain confidentiality when handling sensitive business information.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in data entry, administration, or office support is desirable.
  • Excellent typing accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Strong organisational and time management skills.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office applications, especially Excel.
  • Ability to work with large amounts of information accurately.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to prioritise tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Reliable, professional, and committed to maintaining high standards.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £24,000–£27,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Access to Adecco employee benefits and discounts.
  • Supportive and professional working environment.
  • Career progression opportunities within administration and business support roles.
