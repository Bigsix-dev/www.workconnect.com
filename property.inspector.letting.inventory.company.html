Property Inspector

Job Description

A Letting/Inventory Company is looking for a dependable Property Inspector to carry out residential property inspections and provide accurate condition reports. The role involves visiting properties, identifying maintenance or condition issues, recording observations, and ensuring reports are completed to a professional standard.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out scheduled residential property inspections.
  • Assess the condition of rooms, fixtures, fittings, and external areas.
  • Identify maintenance issues and potential property concerns.
  • Take photographs and maintain accurate inspection records.
  • Prepare clear and detailed inspection reports.
  • Report urgent or significant property issues promptly.
  • Communicate professionally with tenants, landlords, and property managers.
  • Complete inspection assignments within agreed deadlines.

Requirements

  • Good observation and attention-to-detail skills.
  • Previous property inspection or inventory experience is desirable.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to identify common property maintenance issues.
  • Comfortable using smartphones, cameras, and digital reporting systems.
  • Reliable and organised approach to appointments.
  • Ability to work independently.
  • Professional approach when dealing with tenants and property owners.

Working Hours

  • Part-time position.
  • Inspection appointments scheduled according to property requirements.
  • Flexible working arrangements may be available.

Salary

  • Salary not stated; dependent on experience and agreed working arrangement.

Benefits

  • Flexible part-time working.
  • Varied property inspection assignments.
  • Opportunity to gain residential property experience.
  • Professional working environment.
  • Potential for additional assignments and career development.
