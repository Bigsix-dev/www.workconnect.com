Receptionist / Administrator

Job Description

Mid and South Essex NHS Foundation Trust is one of the largest NHS healthcare providers in England, delivering a wide range of medical services to communities across Essex. The Trust is seeking a professional and compassionate Receptionist / Administrator to provide front-desk and administrative support within a healthcare environment. In this role, you will welcome patients and visitors, manage appointments, maintain accurate records, and support clinical teams with efficient administrative services.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome patients, visitors, and staff in a friendly and professional manner.
  • Handle telephone enquiries and provide accurate information where appropriate.
  • Schedule and manage patient appointments using healthcare systems.
  • Maintain accurate patient records and update administrative databases.
  • Process documents, correspondence, and confidential information.
  • Support clinical teams with general administrative tasks.
  • Manage incoming and outgoing communications efficiently.
  • Follow NHS confidentiality policies, data protection requirements, and service procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in reception, administration, or customer service is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Professional and welcoming approach when supporting patients and visitors.
  • Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to handle confidential information appropriately.
  • Confident using Microsoft Office applications and computer systems.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks in a busy environment.
  • Commitment to providing high-quality administrative support.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts depending on department requirements.

Salary

  • £24,000–£28,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive NHS salary package.
  • NHS pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave and public holidays.
  • Access to NHS learning and development programmes.
  • Employee wellbeing and support services.
  • Opportunities for career development within the NHS.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
  • Access to NHS employee benefits and discounts.
