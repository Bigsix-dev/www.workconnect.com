School Cleaner

Job Description

St John Payne Catholic School is committed to providing a clean, safe, and welcoming environment that supports excellent learning and teaching. The school is seeking a dependable and hardworking School Cleaner to maintain high standards of cleanliness across classrooms, offices, communal areas, and school facilities. In this role, you will help create a healthy and comfortable environment for students, staff, and visitors.

Responsibilities

  • Clean classrooms, offices, corridors, toilets, and shared school areas.
  • Maintain high standards of hygiene and cleanliness throughout the school premises.
  • Carry out dusting, vacuuming, mopping, and sanitising tasks.
  • Empty waste bins and dispose of rubbish safely.
  • Report maintenance issues, damages, or health and safety concerns.
  • Use cleaning equipment and products safely according to guidelines.
  • Follow school cleaning schedules and hygiene procedures.
  • Support a safe and welcoming environment for students and staff.

Requirements

  • Previous cleaning experience is desirable but not essential.
  • Good attention to detail and commitment to high standards.
  • Ability to work independently and manage cleaning tasks effectively.
  • Reliable, punctual, and responsible approach to work.
  • Knowledge of safe cleaning practices and procedures.
  • Ability to follow instructions and school policies.
  • Good communication skills.
  • Commitment to maintaining a safe environment for students and staff.

Working Hours

  • Part-time position.
  • 20 hours per week.
  • Flexible cleaning shifts, typically before or after school hours.

Salary

  • £12.85 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave entitlement.
  • Training and support provided.
  • Friendly and supportive working environment.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for additional hours where available.
  • Stable part-time employment within an education setting.
  • Rewarding role supporting the school community.
