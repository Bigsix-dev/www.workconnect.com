Kitchen Assistant

Job Description

Toby Carvery, part of Mitchells & Butlers PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's most popular carvery restaurant chains, renowned for serving freshly prepared roast dinners and traditional British cuisine. Toby Carvery is looking for hardworking and enthusiastic Kitchen Assistants to support its kitchen team. In this role, you will assist with food preparation, maintain kitchen cleanliness, organise ingredients and equipment, and help ensure that every meal is prepared to the highest quality and food safety standards.

Responsibilities

  • Assist chefs with preparing ingredients for daily food service.
  • Wash, peel, chop, and portion vegetables, meats, and other ingredients.
  • Maintain a clean, organised, and hygienic kitchen environment.
  • Wash kitchen utensils, cookware, and food preparation equipment.
  • Receive, unpack, and properly store food deliveries.
  • Monitor stock levels and report shortages to kitchen supervisors.
  • Dispose of waste safely and follow recycling procedures.
  • Comply with food hygiene regulations, health and safety policies, and company procedures.

Requirements

  • Good communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced kitchen environment.
  • Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Willingness to learn and follow kitchen procedures.
  • Ability to stand for long periods and perform manual tasks.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Previous kitchen or hospitality experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including mornings, evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £12.20–£13.40 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Discounted meals during shifts.
  • Employee discounts across Mitchells & Butlers restaurants.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment with opportunities for career progression.
