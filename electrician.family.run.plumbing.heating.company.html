Electrician

Job Description

A family-run Plumbing & Heating Company in Chelmsford and Essex is seeking an experienced Electrician to support electrical installation, maintenance and repair work. The role offers an opportunity to work within a close-knit team while delivering high-quality electrical services.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out electrical installation and maintenance work.
  • Diagnose and repair electrical faults.
  • Install and maintain electrical systems and equipment.
  • Complete work in accordance with relevant safety standards.
  • Work alongside plumbing and heating engineers when required.
  • Maintain a professional approach when working with customers.

Requirements

  • Qualified and experienced electrician.
  • Strong practical knowledge of electrical systems.
  • Good fault-finding and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a small team.
  • Strong focus on safety, quality and customer service.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours based on company and customer requirements.

Salary

  • £35,000–£45,000+ per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to join a family-run business.
  • Competitive salary with earning potential above £45,000.
  • Varied electrical work across Essex.
  • Supportive team environment.
