IT Helpdesk Specialist

Job Description

Rutherford Briant is seeking an IT Helpdesk Specialist to provide technical assistance and day-to-day IT support in Chelmsford. The successful candidate will help users resolve technology issues while ensuring support requests are handled efficiently and professionally.

Responsibilities

  • Provide first-line IT helpdesk support to users.
  • Respond to technical queries and troubleshoot hardware and software issues.
  • Log, track and resolve support tickets within agreed timescales.
  • Assist users with account, password and access-related problems.
  • Install and configure computers, software and peripheral equipment.
  • Escalate complex technical issues to senior IT teams when required.

Requirements

  • Previous IT helpdesk or technical support experience is beneficial.
  • Good understanding of computer hardware, software and common IT systems.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent communication and customer service abilities.
  • Organised approach with the ability to manage multiple support requests.

Working Hours

  • Part-time/temporary position.
  • Working hours will be based on the requirements of the role.

Salary

  • £30,000–£36,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to gain valuable IT support experience.
  • Exposure to a professional business IT environment.
  • Opportunity to develop technical troubleshooting skills.
  • Potential for further IT career development.
