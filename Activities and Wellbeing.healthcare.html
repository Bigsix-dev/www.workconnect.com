Activities and Wellbeing Lead

Job Overview

Cherry Wood Grange Care Home is seeking a compassionate and creative Activities and Wellbeing Lead to plan and deliver engaging programmes that enrich the lives of residents. In this role, you will organise meaningful activities that promote physical, emotional, and social wellbeing while fostering a welcoming and inclusive community environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Plan and coordinate daily, weekly, and seasonal activity programmes for residents.
  • Encourage participation in recreational, educational, and therapeutic activities.
  • Organise group events, celebrations, outings, and entertainment sessions.
  • Work closely with care staff to tailor activities to residents’ individual interests and abilities.
  • Monitor resident engagement and adapt programmes to support wellbeing and inclusion.
  • Build positive relationships with residents, families, volunteers, and external organisations.
  • Maintain activity records and ensure events comply with health and safety standards.
  • Support residents in maintaining independence, confidence, and quality of life.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in activities coordination, health and social care, or a similar role is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • A positive, enthusiastic, and caring approach to working with older adults.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Creativity in planning engaging and inclusive activities.
  • Ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a multidisciplinary team.
  • Flexibility to support occasional weekend or special events when required.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay of £13.00–£15.00.
  • Full-time, stable employment with opportunities for career progression.
  • Ongoing training and professional development.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Opportunity to make a meaningful impact on residents’ wellbeing and daily lives.
  • Paid holiday entitlement and workplace pension, subject to eligibility.

How to Apply

Interested candidates should submit an up-to-date CV along with a brief cover letter highlighting their relevant experience and passion for improving the wellbeing of care home residents. Applicants from the UK and overseas should ensure they have the appropriate right to work or clearly state their current work authorisation status where applicable.
