Customer Service Representative / Member Representative

Job Description

Saffron Building Society is seeking a friendly and professional Customer Service Representative / Member Representative to support members and provide an excellent standard of service. You will assist with enquiries, provide information about products and services, process member requests, and help build positive long-term relationships.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome members and provide professional customer service.
  • Handle member enquiries and provide accurate information.
  • Assist members with account and service-related requests.
  • Process transactions and administrative requests accurately.
  • Explain relevant financial products and services clearly.
  • Identify member needs and provide appropriate assistance.
  • Maintain accurate records and customer information.
  • Handle customer concerns professionally and escalate issues where required.
  • Support the team in maintaining high service standards.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service experience is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Confident dealing with customers face-to-face and through other channels.
  • Good numerical and administrative abilities.
  • Professional approach to handling confidential customer information.
  • Strong problem-solving and listening skills.
  • Ability to work effectively within a customer-focused team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £24,500 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional financial services environment.
  • Training and professional development opportunities.
  • Customer service and financial services experience.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
