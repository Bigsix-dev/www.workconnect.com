Service Desk Analyst

Job Description

Various Employers in Chelmsford are seeking Service Desk Analysts to provide first-line technical support to users. The role focuses on resolving IT incidents, answering technical queries and ensuring users receive an efficient and professional support service.

Responsibilities

  • Act as the first point of contact for IT support requests.
  • Log, investigate and resolve technical incidents and service requests.
  • Provide support by telephone, email and service desk systems.
  • Troubleshoot common hardware, software and access issues.
  • Escalate complex incidents to second-line or specialist teams.
  • Maintain accurate records of incidents and resolutions.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in a service desk or IT support environment is beneficial.
  • Good knowledge of desktop computers, software and basic networking.
  • Strong communication and customer service skills.
  • Good analytical and troubleshooting abilities.
  • Ability to prioritise and manage multiple support requests.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours may vary depending on the employer.

Salary

  • Around £21,483–£27,368 per year.

Benefits

  • Opportunity to develop IT service desk experience.
  • Technical training and professional development.
  • Potential career progression within IT support.
  • Experience working with a variety of business systems.
