Relief Support Worker

Full Job Description

As a Relief Support Worker, you will provide practical and emotional support to people with learning disabilities, helping them live as independently as possible while promoting dignity, choice, and inclusion. Relief workers often have flexible schedules and may work across different services based on operational needs.

  • Encourage independence and help people achieve their personal goals.
  • Assist with medication administration in accordance with training and policies.
  • Accompany individuals to appointments, social events, and community activities.
  • Maintain accurate care records and report any concerns.
  • Build positive relationships with service users, families, and colleagues.
  • Follow safeguarding procedures and health and safety guidelines at all times.

About You

  • Compassionate, patient, and respectful towards others.
  • Good communication and teamwork skills.
  • Reliable and flexible with working hours, including evenings, weekends, and holidays when required.
  • Able to remain calm under pressure and respond appropriately to changing situations.
  • Previous care experience is helpful but often not essential, as training may be provided.
  • A willingness to undergo background checks and complete mandatory training.
