Clinical Nurse Specialist (CNS)

Job Overview

A Clinical Nurse Specialist (CNS) is an experienced registered nurse with advanced knowledge in a specific area of healthcare, such as oncology, cardiology, diabetes, mental health, or respiratory care. The role focuses on providing expert patient care, supporting multidisciplinary teams, improving clinical outcomes, and promoting evidence-based practice.

Working in a specialist clinic, you will assess patients, develop personalised care plans, educate patients and families, and collaborate closely with consultants and other healthcare professionals to deliver high-quality care.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct comprehensive patient assessments and specialist consultations.
  • Develop, implement, and review individualised treatment and care plans.
  • Provide expert clinical advice and support to patients, families, and healthcare staff.
  • Coordinate care with doctors, therapists, and community healthcare providers.
  • Educate patients on managing long-term conditions and treatment options.
  • Monitor patient progress and adjust care plans where necessary.
  • Lead quality improvement initiatives and contribute to service development.
  • Maintain accurate clinical documentation and patient records.
  • Participate in audits, research projects, and evidence-based practice initiatives.
  • Mentor junior nurses and support staff development.

About You

The ideal candidate should have:

  • Current registration as a Registered Nurse with relevant professional credentials.
  • Significant post-registration clinical experience in a specialist area.
  • Strong assessment, communication, and decision-making skills.
  • Experience managing complex patient cases independently.
  • Ability to work collaboratively within a multidisciplinary team.
  • Excellent organisational and leadership abilities.
  • Commitment to delivering compassionate, patient-centred care.
  • A willingness to participate in ongoing professional development and training.
