Network Engineer

Job Description

IT Engineering Provider delivers network infrastructure and information technology engineering services to organisations across a range of sectors. The company is seeking a Network Engineer to design, configure, monitor, and maintain reliable network infrastructure. You will support network performance, troubleshoot connectivity issues, and help ensure secure and efficient communication across business systems.

Responsibilities

  • Configure, maintain, and monitor network infrastructure and equipment.
  • Support local area networks, wide area networks, and wireless networks.
  • Investigate and resolve network connectivity and performance issues.
  • Install and configure network switches, routers, firewalls, and related equipment.
  • Monitor network performance and identify potential faults or service interruptions.
  • Maintain accurate network documentation and configuration records.
  • Support network security and implement appropriate technical controls.
  • Work with technical teams to plan and implement network improvements.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Network Engineering, Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience in network engineering or information technology infrastructure is desirable.
  • Good understanding of networking concepts, protocols, and infrastructure.
  • Knowledge of routers, switches, firewalls, and wireless technologies.
  • Strong troubleshooting and analytical skills.
  • Understanding of network security principles.
  • Ability to document technical configurations and procedures accurately.
  • Good communication and customer service skills.

Working Hours

  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary
