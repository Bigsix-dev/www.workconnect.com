Teaching Assistant

Job Description

St Cedd's School is an independent school committed to providing a supportive, inspiring, and inclusive learning environment where pupils are encouraged to achieve academic excellence and develop essential life skills. St Cedd's School is seeking a caring and enthusiastic Teaching Assistant to support teachers and pupils throughout the school day. In this role, you will assist with classroom activities, provide individual learning support, and help create a positive environment where every child can succeed.

Responsibilities

  • Support classroom teachers with daily learning activities and lesson preparation.
  • Provide individual and small-group support to pupils who need additional assistance.
  • Help prepare teaching materials and classroom resources.
  • Encourage pupils' confidence, independence, and engagement in learning.
  • Support pupils with special educational needs where required.
  • Assist with classroom organisation and maintaining a positive learning environment.
  • Monitor pupil progress and provide feedback to teaching staff.
  • Follow safeguarding procedures and promote pupil wellbeing.

Requirements

  • Previous experience working with children in an educational setting is desirable.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Patient, supportive, and enthusiastic approach to working with pupils.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a teaching team.
  • Understanding of child development and learning support practices.
  • Knowledge of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Good organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Relevant Teaching Assistant qualification or experience is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £22,000–£28,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • School holiday periods and paid leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Supportive and friendly school environment.
  • Employee wellbeing support.
  • Opportunities for career progression within education.
  • Access to staff benefits and school facilities.
  • Rewarding role supporting children's development.
