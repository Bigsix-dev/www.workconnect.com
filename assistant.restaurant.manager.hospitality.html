Assistant Restaurant Manager

Job Description

PizzaExpress Restaurants Limited is one of the United Kingdom's leading casual dining restaurant chains, renowned for its handcrafted pizzas, Italian-inspired cuisine, and exceptional customer service. PizzaExpress is seeking an experienced and enthusiastic Assistant Restaurant Manager to support the Restaurant Manager in overseeing daily operations, leading the team, and ensuring every guest receives an outstanding dining experience while maintaining the company's high standards of quality and hospitality.

Responsibilities

  • Support the Restaurant Manager in the daily operation of the restaurant.
  • Lead, motivate, and supervise front-of-house and kitchen team members.
  • Deliver exceptional customer service and resolve guest concerns professionally.
  • Assist with staff recruitment, training, coaching, and performance management.
  • Monitor food quality, service standards, and restaurant cleanliness.
  • Support stock control, ordering, and inventory management.
  • Assist with scheduling staff and managing labour costs.
  • Ensure compliance with food hygiene regulations, health and safety standards, and company policies.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in a supervisory or management role within the hospitality industry.
  • Excellent leadership and team management skills.
  • Outstanding communication and customer service abilities.
  • Strong organisational and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to perform well in a fast-paced restaurant environment.
  • Knowledge of food safety and health and safety regulations.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • A passion for hospitality and delivering exceptional guest experiences.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £15.50–£18.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted meals during shifts.
  • Employee discounts at PizzaExpress restaurants.
  • Comprehensive management training and career progression opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive and inclusive working environment.
