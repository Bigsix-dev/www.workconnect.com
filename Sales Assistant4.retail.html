Sales Assistant

Claire's Stores UK Limited is a global fashion accessories and jewellery retailer specialising in accessories, cosmetics, and ear piercing services. Claire's is looking for energetic Sales Assistants who are passionate about fashion and delivering exceptional customer experiences in a fun and fast-paced retail environment.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers and provide friendly service.
  • Assist customers with fashion accessories and jewellery.
  • Operate tills and process transactions.
  • Maintain attractive product displays.
  • Replenish stock and organise merchandise.
  • Promote store offers and loyalty programmes.
  • Support ear piercing services after training.
  • Maintain store cleanliness and safety.

Requirements

  • Excellent customer service skills.
  • Passion for fashion and accessories.
  • Friendly and enthusiastic personality.
  • Good communication skills.
  • Ability to work in a busy retail environment.
  • Flexible availability.
  • Previous retail experience is an advantage.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35 hours per week.

Salary

  • £12.25–£13.40 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Generous employee discount.
  • Comprehensive training.
  • Career progression opportunities.
  • Supportive workplace culture.
