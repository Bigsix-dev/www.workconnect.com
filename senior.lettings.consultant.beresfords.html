Senior Lettings Consultant

Job Description

Beresfords is seeking an experienced and motivated Senior Lettings Consultant to support the successful letting of residential properties. The role involves managing landlord and tenant relationships, conducting property viewings, progressing applications, and helping achieve agreed rental and service targets. You will work as part of a professional property team while delivering a high standard of customer service.

Responsibilities

  • Manage residential lettings enquiries from prospective landlords and tenants.
  • Arrange and conduct property viewings professionally and efficiently.
  • Develop and maintain strong relationships with landlords and applicants.
  • Assist with property valuations, marketing, and rental recommendations.
  • Progress applications and liaise with tenants, landlords, and relevant parties.
  • Maintain accurate property and applicant records.
  • Support the achievement of branch and individual lettings targets.
  • Provide guidance to junior members of the lettings team where required.

Requirements

  • Previous experience within residential lettings or estate agency.
  • Strong customer service and communication skills.
  • Confident negotiating and relationship-building abilities.
  • Good knowledge of the residential property market.
  • Strong organisational and administrative skills.
  • Ability to work towards targets and deadlines.
  • Professional, proactive, and commercially aware approach.
  • Ability to work effectively within a busy property team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours based around branch and property viewing requirements.
  • Some weekend or extended-hour availability may be required.

Salary

  • £18,000–£20,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive property-sector salary.
  • Professional training and development opportunities.
  • Career progression within residential property.
  • Supportive team environment.
  • Opportunities to develop specialist lettings expertise.
  • Employee support and workplace benefits.
