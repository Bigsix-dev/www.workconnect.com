Police Community Support Officer

Job Description

Essex Police works with communities across Essex to prevent crime, improve public safety, and maintain confidence in local policing. The service is seeking a professional Police Community Support Officer to provide a visible policing presence, engage with residents and businesses, and support police officers in tackling local concerns. You will help prevent crime and antisocial behaviour while building positive relationships within the community.

Responsibilities

  • Provide a visible and approachable policing presence within local communities.
  • Engage with residents, businesses, community groups, and partner organisations.
  • Identify and report concerns relating to crime, antisocial behaviour, and community safety.
  • Support police officers with local policing activities and investigations where appropriate.
  • Conduct patrols and community visits within designated areas.
  • Provide crime prevention and safety information to members of the public.
  • Record incidents, observations, and community intelligence accurately.
  • Work with partner agencies to address local community safety concerns.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in policing, security, community work, public service, or customer service is desirable.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with diverse communities.
  • Strong observation and situational awareness.
  • Ability to remain calm and professional in challenging situations.
  • Good report writing and administrative skills.
  • Strong integrity, reliability, and commitment to public service.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a policing team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working schedule may include evenings and weekends.

Salary

  • £29,000–£34,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive police service salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Comprehensive police and community safety training.
  • Opportunities for specialist development.
  • Uniform and required operational equipment.
  • Career progression opportunities within policing and public services.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
