Unit Manager (Non-Clinical)

Job Overview

We are seeking an experienced and motivated Unit Manager (Non-Clinical) to join our team at Admirals Reach Care Home. This role is ideal for someone with strong leadership skills who can oversee the smooth day-to-day running of a residential care unit without direct clinical responsibilities.

You will be responsible for ensuring high-quality care standards, supporting staff performance, and maintaining a safe, welcoming, and well-organised environment for residents.

Key Responsibilities

  • Oversee daily operations of the care unit to ensure smooth service delivery
  • Supervise and support care staff, ensuring rotas and workloads are effectively managed
  • Maintain compliance with CQC standards and internal policies
  • Act as the main point of contact for staff, residents, and families within the unit
  • Monitor care quality and ensure consistent service improvement
  • Support staff training, induction, and ongoing development
  • Handle administrative duties related to staffing, records, and reporting
  • Promote a positive, person-centred culture across the unit

Requirements

  • Previous experience in a supervisory or management role within a care or healthcare setting
  • Strong organisational and leadership skills
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal abilities
  • Good understanding of care home operations and compliance standards
  • Ability to manage teams effectively and handle challenging situations professionally
  • Basic IT and administrative skills

What We Offer

  • Competitive hourly pay: £17.00–£18.50
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment
  • Opportunities for professional development and career progression
  • Paid training and ongoing staff development
  • Employee wellbeing support initiatives
  • Stable full-time position in a reputable care home setting
