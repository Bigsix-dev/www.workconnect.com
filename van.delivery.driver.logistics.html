Van Delivery Driver

Job Description

Amazon Logistics, part of Amazon UK Services Limited, is responsible for delivering millions of parcels to customers across the United Kingdom. Amazon Logistics is seeking a dependable and customer-focused Van Delivery Driver to join its delivery team. In this role, you will safely operate a company van, deliver parcels on time, and provide outstanding customer service while following company procedures and UK road safety regulations.

Responsibilities

  • Deliver parcels safely and efficiently to residential and business addresses.
  • Plan and follow assigned delivery routes using company navigation systems.
  • Load, organise, and secure parcels before starting each delivery route.
  • Complete deliveries within scheduled timeframes while maintaining high service standards.
  • Perform daily vehicle safety checks and report any defects immediately.
  • Accurately record deliveries using handheld devices and obtain proof of delivery where required.
  • Provide professional and courteous customer service during every delivery.
  • Follow company policies, road traffic laws, and health and safety procedures at all times.

Requirements

  • Valid UK Category B driving licence.
  • Good driving record with a strong commitment to road safety.
  • Ability to lift and carry parcels of varying sizes and weights.
  • Good communication and customer service skills.
  • Ability to work independently and manage delivery schedules efficiently.
  • Basic knowledge of using GPS navigation and handheld delivery devices.
  • Flexible availability, including weekends and peak seasonal periods.
  • Previous delivery driving experience is desirable but not essential.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts, including weekends and public holidays when required.

Salary

  • £13.50–£15.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Comprehensive training and onboarding programme.
  • Employee discounts on selected Amazon products and services.
  • Health and wellbeing support programmes.
  • Modern delivery vehicles and equipment.
  • Career development opportunities within Amazon Logistics.
