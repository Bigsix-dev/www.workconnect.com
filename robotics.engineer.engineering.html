Robotics Engineer

Job Description

Automation Company develops and delivers robotic and automated systems for manufacturing and industrial applications. The company is seeking a skilled Robotics Engineer to design, develop, integrate, test, and maintain robotic systems. In this role, you will work with mechanical, electrical, controls, and software engineering teams to deliver reliable automated solutions that improve productivity, efficiency, and safety.

Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and integrate robotic systems for industrial applications.
  • Program and configure robotic equipment and automated systems.
  • Carry out testing, commissioning, and validation of robotic systems.
  • Diagnose and resolve mechanical, electrical, software, and control-related faults.
  • Work with multidisciplinary engineering teams to develop automation solutions.
  • Develop technical documentation, system specifications, and operating procedures.
  • Optimise robot performance, cycle times, accuracy, and reliability.
  • Support the installation and commissioning of robotic systems at customer or production sites.

Requirements

  • Degree or equivalent qualification in Robotics Engineering, Automation Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • Previous experience with robotics, automation, or industrial engineering is desirable.
  • Understanding of industrial robots, automation systems, and robotic programming.
  • Good knowledge of mechanical, electrical, and control engineering principles.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to interpret engineering drawings and technical documentation.
  • Strong analytical skills and attention to detail.
  • Excellent communication and multidisciplinary teamwork abilities.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £45,000–£70,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Workplace pension scheme.

  • Robotics and automation training.
  • Opportunities to work with advanced robotic technologies.
  • Hands-on experience with industrial automation projects.
  • Career progression and professional development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
