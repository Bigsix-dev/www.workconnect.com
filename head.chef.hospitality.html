Head Chef

Job Description

Harvester, part of Mitchells & Butlers PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's leading family restaurant brands, renowned for its freshly prepared grilled dishes, famous salad bar, and welcoming dining experience. Harvester is seeking an experienced and inspirational Head Chef to lead its kitchen team. In this role, you will oversee all kitchen operations, maintain exceptional food quality, manage staff performance, and ensure compliance with food safety and company standards while delivering an outstanding dining experience for every guest.

Responsibilities

  • Lead and manage all daily kitchen operations.
  • Prepare and oversee the production of high-quality meals.
  • Recruit, train, mentor, and supervise kitchen staff.
  • Develop staff schedules and manage labour costs efficiently.
  • Monitor food quality, portion control, and presentation standards.
  • Manage stock ordering, inventory control, and minimise food waste.
  • Ensure all kitchen equipment is maintained and used safely.
  • Maintain compliance with food hygiene regulations, HACCP procedures, health and safety legislation, and company policies.

Requirements

  • Proven experience as a Head Chef or Senior Sous Chef in a busy restaurant.
  • Excellent leadership and people management skills.
  • Strong culinary knowledge and passion for quality food.
  • Ability to work efficiently under pressure.
  • Excellent organisational and problem-solving abilities.
  • Comprehensive knowledge of food hygiene and kitchen safety regulations.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Professional culinary qualifications are highly desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 45 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £20.00–£24.00 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted meals during shifts.
  • Employee discounts across Mitchells & Butlers restaurants.
  • Leadership training and professional development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programme.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within one of the UK's leading hospitality companies.
