Title Checker – Residential Property

Job Description

JS Legal Recruitment is seeking an experienced Title Checker to review residential property titles and support the conveyancing process. The successful candidate will examine title documents, identify potential issues, carry out relevant checks, and communicate findings accurately to the legal and property teams.

Responsibilities

  • Review residential property title documents and related information.
  • Carry out detailed title checks and identify potential issues.
  • Review title plans, registers, restrictions, covenants, and other relevant documentation.
  • Identify matters requiring further investigation or legal attention.
  • Prepare clear notes and reports outlining title findings.
  • Liaise with conveyancers, solicitors, clients, and other relevant parties.
  • Request and review additional information where required.
  • Maintain accurate records of title checks and outstanding matters.
  • Assist with the efficient progression of residential property transactions.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in residential conveyancing or title checking.
  • Good understanding of property titles and conveyancing processes.
  • Strong analytical and document-review skills.
  • Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Good organisational and time management abilities.
  • Ability to identify and investigate potential title issues.
  • Confident working with legal and property documentation.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • Working hours to be confirmed by the employer.

Salary

  • £35,000–£50,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package.
  • Professional legal and property environment.
  • Training and professional development opportunities.
  • Opportunity to develop specialist residential property expertise.
  • Potential career progression opportunities.
