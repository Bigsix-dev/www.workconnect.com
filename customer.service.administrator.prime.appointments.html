Customer Service Administrator

Job Description

Prime Appointments is seeking an organised Customer Service Administrator to provide administrative and customer support within a busy professional environment. You will handle customer enquiries, maintain accurate records, process documentation, and support the wider team with day-to-day administration. The successful candidate will have strong organisational skills, attention to detail, and a professional approach to customer communication.

Responsibilities

  • Respond to customer enquiries by telephone and email.
  • Provide accurate information and assistance to customers and clients.
  • Process orders, requests, documentation, and customer records where required.
  • Maintain accurate databases, spreadsheets, and customer information.
  • Handle customer complaints and escalate complex issues appropriately.
  • Coordinate with internal departments to resolve customer enquiries.
  • Prepare correspondence, reports, and administrative documents.
  • Provide general administrative support to the customer service team.

Requirements

  • Previous customer service or administration experience is desirable.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong organisational and time management abilities.
  • Good computer skills, including Microsoft Office applications.
  • Strong attention to detail and accurate data entry skills.
  • Professional and friendly customer service manner.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of an administrative team.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday.
  • 8 hours per day between 8:00am and 5:00pm.
  • Full-time working schedule.

Salary

  • £12.71 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly rate.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Customer service and administration training.
  • Professional office environment.
  • Opportunities to develop administrative and customer service skills.
  • Career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
