ICT Teacher

Job Description

Chelmsford Academy is committed to delivering high-quality education that equips students with the knowledge and digital skills needed for success in a technology-driven world. Chelmsford Academy is seeking an enthusiastic and innovative ICT Teacher to deliver engaging lessons in Information and Communication Technology. In this role, you will inspire students to develop digital literacy, computational thinking, and practical technology skills while preparing them for further education and future careers.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and deliver engaging ICT lessons in line with the national curriculum.
  • Teach topics such as digital literacy, computer systems, networking, programming, and cybersecurity.
  • Prepare lesson plans, practical activities, and assessment materials.
  • Assess student progress and provide constructive feedback.
  • Promote safe and responsible use of technology in the classroom.
  • Maintain ICT equipment and ensure effective use of digital learning resources.
  • Create an inclusive learning environment that encourages participation and achievement.
  • Participate in school meetings, parents' evenings, and professional development activities.

Requirements

  • Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) is required.
  • Bachelor's degree in ICT, Computer Science, or a related subject.
  • Previous experience teaching ICT at secondary school level is desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of current technology and digital learning practices.
  • Excellent classroom management and communication skills.
  • Ability to inspire students with practical and engaging lessons.
  • Commitment to safeguarding and promoting student wellbeing.
  • Strong organisational, planning, and assessment skills.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 32.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £34,000–£50,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive teacher salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme.
  • Paid school holidays and generous annual leave.
  • Professional development and continuous training opportunities.
  • Access to modern ICT facilities and teaching resources.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Career progression opportunities within education.
  • Supportive and collaborative teaching environment.
