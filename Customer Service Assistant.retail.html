Customer Service Assistant

Tesco, short for Tesco PLC (Public Limited Company), is one of the United Kingdom's largest supermarket and retail chains, serving millions of customers every week. Tesco is looking for friendly, reliable, and customer-focused individuals to join its team as Customer Service Assistants. In this role, you will help create a positive shopping experience by assisting customers, operating checkouts, replenishing stock, and ensuring the store remains clean, safe, and well-organised. This position is ideal for individuals who enjoy working with people and thrive in a busy retail environment.

Responsibilities

  • Provide friendly and professional customer service at all times.
  • Operate tills accurately and process customer transactions.
  • Assist customers with enquiries, product locations, and general support.
  • Restock shelves and ensure products are displayed neatly.
  • Monitor stock levels and report low inventory.
  • Maintain a clean, safe, and welcoming shopping environment.
  • Follow company health and safety procedures.
  • Work collaboratively with colleagues to achieve daily store targets.

Requirements

  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • A positive attitude with a strong focus on customer satisfaction.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced retail environment.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling skills.
  • Reliable, punctual, and able to work as part of a team.
  • Flexibility to work mornings, evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.
  • Previous retail or customer service experience is beneficial but not essential.
  • Full training will be provided.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 36.5 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts, including weekends and public holidays where required.

Salary

  • £12.45–£13.50 per hour.
  • Overtime opportunities may be available.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Tesco Colleague Clubcard with employee discounts.
  • Comprehensive training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Supportive and inclusive workplace culture.
