Emergency Call Handler

Job Description

Emergency Services provide vital assistance to members of the public during emergencies and critical incidents. The service is seeking a calm, focused, and professional Emergency Call Handler to receive emergency calls, gather essential information, assess the nature of incidents, and communicate accurate details to the appropriate emergency response teams. You will play an important role in helping ensure that emergency assistance is coordinated quickly and effectively.

Responsibilities

  • Receive and handle emergency calls in a calm, professional, and efficient manner.
  • Obtain accurate information about the location, nature, and circumstances of incidents.
  • Assess calls according to established emergency response procedures.
  • Record and update incident information accurately on computer systems.
  • Communicate relevant information to emergency response teams and control room staff.
  • Provide appropriate instructions and reassurance to callers where procedures permit.
  • Prioritise calls according to urgency and potential risk.
  • Maintain confidentiality and follow emergency service policies and procedures.

Requirements

  • Previous call handling, customer service, emergency services, or control room experience is desirable.
  • Excellent verbal communication and listening skills.
  • Ability to remain calm and focused under significant pressure.
  • Strong attention to detail and accurate information processing skills.
  • Good computer and keyboard skills.
  • Ability to make sound decisions while following established procedures.
  • Strong emotional resilience and professional judgement.
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a busy emergency services team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Shift-based working schedule, including nights, weekends, and public holidays.

Salary

  • £25,000–£30,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive emergency services salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Comprehensive call handling and emergency response training.
  • Opportunities for specialist control room training.
  • Supportive emergency services working environment.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
