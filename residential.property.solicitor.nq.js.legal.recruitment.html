Residential Property Solicitor NQ

Job Description

JS Legal Recruitment is seeking a newly qualified Residential Property Solicitor to join a professional conveyancing team. The successful candidate will manage residential property transactions, advise clients on legal matters, and work closely with clients, estate agents, lenders, and other professionals to progress transactions through to completion.

Responsibilities

  • Manage residential property sales and purchases under supervision where appropriate.
  • Handle freehold and leasehold conveyancing matters.
  • Review contracts, title documents, searches, and property information.
  • Raise and respond to legal enquiries.
  • Draft and review conveyancing documentation.
  • Liaise with clients, estate agents, lenders, and other solicitors.
  • Identify potential legal issues and provide appropriate advice.
  • Maintain accurate case files and case management records.
  • Keep clients informed about transaction progress.
  • Work towards exchange and completion deadlines.

Requirements

  • Newly qualified solicitor with relevant residential property experience.
  • Good understanding of residential conveyancing procedures.
  • Strong legal research, drafting, and analytical skills.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong client care and relationship-building abilities.
  • Excellent attention to detail.
  • Good organisational and time management skills.
  • Willingness to develop professionally within residential property law.
