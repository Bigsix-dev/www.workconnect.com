Lecturer – Business Studies

Job Description

Chelmsford College is a leading further education provider committed to delivering high-quality learning opportunities and helping students develop the knowledge and skills needed for successful careers. Chelmsford College is seeking an enthusiastic and knowledgeable Lecturer in Business Studies to deliver engaging lessons and support students throughout their academic journey. In this role, you will teach business-related subjects, develop learning resources, assess student progress, and contribute to the continued success of the college's business programmes.

Responsibilities

  • Deliver engaging Business Studies lessons to students across different levels.
  • Plan and prepare teaching materials, assessments, and learning activities.
  • Assess student performance and provide constructive feedback.
  • Support students with academic development and career progression.
  • Maintain accurate records of student achievement and progress.
  • Use a variety of teaching methods to support different learning styles.
  • Participate in curriculum development and quality improvement activities.
  • Work collaboratively with colleagues and support college initiatives.

Requirements

  • Relevant degree or professional qualification in Business Studies or related subject.
  • Teaching qualification such as PGCE, Certificate in Education, or equivalent is desirable.
  • Experience teaching Business Studies in further education or a similar environment.
  • Strong subject knowledge and passion for business education.
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills.
  • Ability to motivate and inspire students.
  • Strong organisational and assessment skills.
  • Commitment to safeguarding and student wellbeing.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during college term time.

Salary

  • £32,000–£42,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive lecturer salary.
  • Teachers' Pension Scheme.
  • Generous annual leave entitlement.
  • Professional development and teaching training opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Supportive academic working environment.
  • Opportunities for career progression within further education.
  • Access to college facilities and staff benefits.
