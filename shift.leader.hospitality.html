Shift Leader

Job Description

Greggs PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading bakery and food-on-the-go retailers, serving millions of customers every week with freshly prepared food, baked goods, and beverages. Greggs is seeking a motivated and dependable Shift Leader to supervise daily store operations, support the shop management team, and lead colleagues in delivering excellent customer service. This role is ideal for someone with leadership experience who enjoys working in a fast-paced retail and hospitality environment.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise the day-to-day operation of the shop during assigned shifts.
  • Lead, motivate, and support team members to deliver outstanding customer service.
  • Prepare and serve food and beverages in accordance with company standards.
  • Monitor product quality, food safety, and stock availability throughout the shift.
  • Handle customer enquiries and resolve complaints professionally.
  • Operate tills, process transactions, and complete end-of-shift cash procedures.
  • Assist with staff training, opening and closing duties, and daily operational tasks.
  • Ensure compliance with food hygiene regulations, health and safety requirements, and company policies.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in a supervisory or team leader role within retail or hospitality.
  • Excellent leadership and communication skills.
  • Strong customer service and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment.
  • Good organisational and time management skills.
  • Basic numeracy and cash-handling experience.
  • Flexible availability, including early mornings, evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • A positive attitude with a commitment to delivering excellent service.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35–39 hours per week.
  • Flexible shift patterns, including weekends and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £13.50–£15.20 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted food and drinks during shifts.
  • Employee discounts on Greggs products.
  • Comprehensive leadership training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive, inclusive workplace with excellent opportunities for career progression.
