Kitchen Supervisor

Job Description

Bella Italia Restaurants Limited, trading as Bella Italia, is a leading Italian restaurant chain in the United Kingdom, renowned for its authentic Italian-inspired cuisine and exceptional dining experiences. Bella Italia is seeking an experienced and motivated Kitchen Supervisor to oversee daily kitchen operations, support the Head Chef, and ensure food is prepared to the highest standards. You will lead the kitchen team, maintain food safety compliance, and help deliver an outstanding dining experience for every guest.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise the daily operation of the kitchen and support the Head Chef.
  • Lead, motivate, and train kitchen team members.
  • Prepare and cook menu items according to Bella Italia recipes and quality standards.
  • Monitor food quality, presentation, and portion control.
  • Manage stock levels, stock rotation, and assist with ordering kitchen supplies.
  • Ensure kitchen equipment is used safely and maintained correctly.
  • Support opening and closing kitchen procedures.
  • Maintain compliance with food hygiene regulations, health and safety legislation, and company policies.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in a supervisory kitchen or chef role.
  • Strong leadership and team management skills.
  • Excellent organisational and communication abilities.
  • Ability to work efficiently under pressure in a fast-paced kitchen.
  • Good understanding of food hygiene and food safety standards.
  • Food Safety Level 2 or equivalent qualification is desirable.
  • Flexible availability, including evenings, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Passion for producing high-quality food and supporting team development.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 38–42 hours per week.
  • Flexible shifts including evenings, weekends, and bank holidays.

Salary

  • £14.50–£16.50 per hour.

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly pay.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Free or discounted meals during shifts.
  • Employee discounts across Bella Italia restaurants.
  • Leadership training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Supportive working environment with excellent opportunities for career progression.
