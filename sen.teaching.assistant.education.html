SEN Teaching Assistant

Job Description

Moulsham Junior School is a welcoming and inclusive school dedicated to providing every child with the opportunity to succeed in a safe and supportive learning environment. Moulsham Junior School is seeking a caring and committed SEN Teaching Assistant to support pupils with special educational needs. In this role, you will work alongside teachers and support staff to help pupils overcome learning barriers, build confidence, and achieve their educational goals.

Responsibilities

  • Provide one-to-one and small-group support for pupils with special educational needs.
  • Assist teachers in delivering personalised learning activities.
  • Support pupils with communication, literacy, numeracy, and classroom participation.
  • Help implement individual education plans and support strategies.
  • Encourage independence, confidence, and positive behaviour.
  • Prepare classroom resources and maintain an organised learning environment.
  • Monitor pupil progress and provide feedback to teaching staff.
  • Promote safeguarding, inclusion, and pupil wellbeing at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous experience supporting children with special educational needs is desirable.
  • Knowledge of autism, ADHD, speech and language needs, or learning difficulties is beneficial.
  • Patient, compassionate, and supportive approach.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with pupils, staff, and parents.
  • Good organisational and observational skills.
  • Knowledge of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Relevant Teaching Assistant or SEN qualification is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Part-time position.
  • 25 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £20,000–£25,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • School holiday periods and paid leave entitlement.
  • Professional SEN training and career development opportunities.
  • Supportive and experienced school team.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for progression within education and special educational needs support.
  • Rewarding role helping pupils reach their full potential.
  • Inclusive and collaborative working environment.
