Learning Mentor

Job Description

Chelmsford Secondary Schools are committed to providing supportive and inclusive learning environments where students are encouraged to achieve their academic and personal goals. Schools across Chelmsford are seeking a dedicated and inspiring Learning Mentor to support students with their learning, confidence, behaviour, and personal development. In this role, you will work closely with students, teachers, and families to help young people overcome barriers to learning and achieve positive outcomes.

Responsibilities

  • Provide one-to-one and small-group mentoring support to students.
  • Help students develop confidence, motivation, and independent learning skills.
  • Support students with academic progress, attendance, and engagement.
  • Work with teachers to identify and address barriers to learning.
  • Develop positive relationships with students and provide guidance when needed.
  • Support behaviour improvement strategies and student wellbeing.
  • Communicate with parents, carers, and school staff where appropriate.
  • Maintain accurate records of student progress and support activities.

Requirements

  • Previous experience supporting young people in an educational, youth work, or mentoring environment.
  • Excellent communication and relationship-building skills.
  • Patient, supportive, and encouraging approach.
  • Ability to motivate students and build their confidence.
  • Strong organisational and record-keeping skills.
  • Understanding of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Ability to work effectively with teachers, families, and support teams.
  • Relevant education, mentoring, or youth support qualification is desirable.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 35 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during school term time.

Salary

  • £25,000–£31,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • School holiday periods and paid leave entitlement.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Supportive education team environment.
  • Employee wellbeing support programmes.
  • Opportunities for progression within education and youth services.
  • Rewarding role making a positive impact on students' lives.
  • Inclusive and collaborative working environment.
