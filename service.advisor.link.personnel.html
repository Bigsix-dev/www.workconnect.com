Service Advisor

Job Description

Link Personnel is seeking a customer-focused Service Advisor to manage vehicle servicing, maintenance, and repair requirements. You will provide a professional point of contact for customers, coordinate with workshop technicians, explain work and costs, and ensure vehicles move efficiently through the service process.

Responsibilities

  • Handle customer bookings for vehicle servicing, maintenance, and repairs.
  • Welcome customers and provide professional advice and assistance.
  • Record vehicle concerns and communicate customer requirements to technicians.
  • Prepare estimates and explain recommended servicing and repair work.
  • Coordinate with workshop teams to monitor vehicle progress.
  • Provide customers with regular updates regarding repairs and completion times.
  • Process job cards, invoices, and relevant service documentation.
  • Maintain accurate customer and vehicle records and support customer retention.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in an automotive service advisor or customer-facing dealership role is desirable.
  • Good understanding of vehicle maintenance and repair procedures.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong customer service and relationship-building abilities.
  • Good organisation and attention to detail.
  • Confident explaining technical information in a clear and understandable manner.
  • Good computer and administrative skills.
  • Ability to work efficiently in a busy workshop environment.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday.
  • Full-time position.
  • Standard weekday working schedule.

Salary

  • £30,000+ per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive automotive salary.
  • Workplace pension scheme.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Automotive service training.
  • Supportive professional working environment.
  • Opportunities to develop technical and customer service expertise.
  • Career development and progression opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and support programmes.
