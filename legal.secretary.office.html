Legal Secretary

Job Description

Birkett Long LLP (Limited Liability Partnership) is a well-established law firm in Essex, providing expert legal services to individuals, businesses, and organisations across a wide range of practice areas. Birkett Long LLP is seeking a professional and highly organised Legal Secretary to provide administrative and secretarial support to solicitors and legal teams. In this role, you will prepare legal documents, manage client communications, organise case files, and ensure the smooth day-to-day operation of the legal department while maintaining strict confidentiality.

Responsibilities

  • Provide administrative and secretarial support to solicitors and legal professionals.
  • Prepare, format, and proofread legal documents, letters, and contracts.
  • Manage diaries, appointments, court dates, and client meetings.
  • Handle client enquiries professionally by telephone, email, and in person.
  • Maintain accurate legal files, records, and document management systems.
  • Carry out digital dictation, document production, and filing duties.
  • Assist with billing, invoices, and other legal administrative tasks.
  • Ensure confidentiality and compliance with legal regulations and firm policies.

Requirements

  • Previous experience as a Legal Secretary or in a legal administrative role is desirable.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication abilities.
  • High level of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications including Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Experience using legal case management software is an advantage.
  • Ability to handle confidential information with professionalism.
  • Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to work effectively within a team.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday during normal office hours.

Salary

  • £25,000–£31,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee wellbeing and assistance programmes.
  • Supportive and collaborative working environment.
  • Modern office facilities and legal technology.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Birkett Long LLP.
