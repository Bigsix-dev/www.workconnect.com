HR Administrator

Job Description

Kier Group PLC (Public Limited Company) is one of the United Kingdom's leading infrastructure, construction, and property services companies, delivering projects that support communities across the country. Kier Group is seeking a highly organised and detail-oriented HR Administrator to support the Human Resources team with day-to-day administrative activities. In this role, you will maintain employee records, coordinate recruitment and onboarding processes, assist with HR documentation, and ensure all personnel information is managed accurately and confidentially.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain accurate employee records and HR databases.
  • Prepare employment contracts, letters, and HR documentation.
  • Support recruitment, onboarding, and induction processes.
  • Coordinate interviews, meetings, and employee training sessions.
  • Process leave requests, absence records, and personnel updates.
  • Respond to employee HR enquiries in a professional and timely manner.
  • Ensure confidential HR documents are securely maintained.
  • Support HR projects while ensuring compliance with employment legislation and company policies.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in Human Resources or office administration is desirable.
  • Excellent organisational and administrative skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Ability to handle confidential information professionally.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Word, Excel, and Outlook.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Strong teamwork skills with a proactive and professional attitude.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 37.5 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday working schedule.

Salary

  • £27,000–£33,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Professional training and career development opportunities.
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing programme.
  • Flexible working opportunities where applicable.
  • Supportive and inclusive workplace culture.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Kier Group.
