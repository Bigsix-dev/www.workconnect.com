Nursery Practitioner

Job Description

Busy Bees Nursery Chelmsford is part of one of the United Kingdom's leading childcare providers, delivering high-quality early years education and care for children in a safe, nurturing, and stimulating environment. Busy Bees Nursery Chelmsford is seeking a caring and enthusiastic Nursery Practitioner to support children's development through engaging activities, play-based learning, and personalised care. In this role, you will help create a positive nursery environment where children can develop confidence, curiosity, and essential early learning skills.

Responsibilities

  • Provide high-quality care and education for children aged from birth to five years.
  • Plan and deliver engaging activities that support children's learning and development.
  • Support children's social, emotional, physical, and communication development.
  • Build positive relationships with children, parents, and colleagues.
  • Observe and record children's progress in line with the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) framework.
  • Create a safe, clean, and welcoming nursery environment.
  • Support children's daily routines including meals, personal care, and play activities.
  • Follow safeguarding procedures and promote children's wellbeing at all times.

Requirements

  • Previous experience working in a nursery, childcare, or early years setting.
  • Level 2 or Level 3 Early Years qualification is desirable.
  • Passionate, patient, and caring approach towards children.
  • Good understanding of child development and early years learning.
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to create fun and engaging learning experiences.
  • Knowledge of safeguarding and child protection procedures.
  • Commitment to providing excellent childcare standards.

Working Hours

  • Full-time position.
  • 40 hours per week.
  • Monday to Friday with flexible nursery shifts.

Salary

  • £24,000–£29,000 per year.

Benefits

  • Competitive annual salary.
  • Paid annual leave.
  • Childcare discounts for eligible employees.
  • Professional training and early years development opportunities.
  • Company pension scheme.
  • Employee wellbeing support.
  • Supportive nursery team environment.
  • Excellent career progression opportunities within Busy Bees.
